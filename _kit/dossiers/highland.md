# Highland Instruments, Inc. — dossier (facts with sources)

## Company
- Neurotechnology company developing ElectroSonic Stimulation (ESStim™), a noninvasive brain stimulation technology that combines independently controlled electromagnetic and ultrasonic fields to focus and boost stimulation in neural tissue. ESStim couples transcranial ultrasound (TUS) with transcranial direct current stimulation (tDCS) to focus, amplify and steer transcranially applied currents. — https://www.highlandinstruments.us/ , /technology
- Founded by scientists trained at Harvard Medical School and MIT. Also develops supporting technologies: AI, imaging, wearable sensors and human movement kinematics; owns IP in these areas. ESStim protected by issued and pending patents. — /company
- Investigational device: US law limits the device to investigational use; not FDA-approved for a specific indication; not in commercial distribution. — site footer
- Six indications under investigation, said to affect 70 million Americans. — /indication
  - Parkinson's disease: three completed randomized double-blinded controlled trials (JANUS); ongoing JANUS 4 investigating balance and gait.
  - Opioid use disorder: multicenter randomized double-blinded controlled trial (RHEA) on drug use patterns and craving.
  - Knee osteoarthritis: three completed randomized double-blinded controlled trials (ALGEA).
  - Painful diabetic neuropathy: HERMES studies.
  - Chronic lower back pain: ATLAS 1 completed; ATLAS 2 ongoing.
  - Carpal tunnel syndrome: MINERVA 1 completed; MINERVA 2 ongoing.

## Funding (NIH RePORTER)
- 2R44NS110237-04, NINDS, SBIR Phase IIB, notice 2026-08-06, first-year award $1,496,687, PI Timothy Andrew Wagner: "ESStim for the Treatment of Postural Instability in Patients with Parkinson's Disease - Phase IIB". Project end 2028-08-31. — https://reporter.nih.gov/project-details/11409854
  - Multicenter, double-blind, placebo-controlled randomized trial, N=116 (29 per group): Standard (10 sessions, weeks 1–2), Distributed (3 sessions/week × 2 weeks), Short (5 sessions in week 1) vs 10-session sham.
  - Primary outcome: change in UPDRS Part III (time-standardized average from end of stimulation to 12 weeks), ON medication. Goal: minimal sufficient dosing achieving MCID (2.5 UPDRS-III points) for pivotal testing.
  - Secondary: kinematics from Highland's Integrated Motion Analysis Suite (IMAS), Mini-BESTest, falls diary, durability, PDQ-39. Safety: AEs, MoCA, EEG, mood, neuro exams.
  - Earlier PD studies with ESStim reported clinically significant improvements in UPDRS Part III, gait and balance, as monotherapy and with physical therapy.
- Other recent awards: 5SB1AG091887-02 (NIA, CRP: noninvasive brain stimulation for chronic pain, 2026-06-03, $1,916,568, PI Laura Dipietro) https://reporter.nih.gov/project-details/11395320 ; 1SB1AG091887-01 (2025-08-18, $2,055,669) https://reporter.nih.gov/project-details/11069588 ; 3R44DA049685-03S1 (NIDA, addiction supplement, 2024-05-17, $1,314,672) https://reporter.nih.gov/project-details/10999342 . RePORTER lists 31 Highland awards since 2008.
