# Cairngorm Capital Partners LLP (cairngorm)
Sources: https://www.cairngormcapital.com/ (home, /about-us/, /our-team/*, /portfolio/, /news/, /responsible-investing/, /contact/), fetched 2026-11-18.

## Firm
- Specialist private equity firm; invests in private mid-market companies with potential for further substantial growth (home).
- Thematic investors with a collaborative approach; partner with ambitious management teams to build enduring, industry-leading companies (home).
- Current portfolio companies: more than 7,250 employees, combined revenues greater than £1.2 billion (home).
- Committed equity capital in excess of £400m; typically deploys £10-40m per portfolio company (home).
- Majority positions in companies valued at up to £200 million; occasionally minority positions in very high-growth companies (home).
- Mostly UK-headquartered businesses; equally comfortable in North America and continental Europe; particular expertise in family and founder-owned companies (home).
- Organic growth levers: commercial optimisation, geographic expansion, service line extensions, digital transformation; expert support for acquisitive growth (home).
- Financial expertise: M&A execution and integration for buy & builds, recapitalisations, corporate carve-outs, management buy-outs (Andrew Steel quote, home).
- Senior staff invest in every transaction (about-us).
- Themes (notes for editors, 7 Jan 2026 release): ageing in place; repair, maintenance & improvement of properties; wealth management; sustainability.
- "Transformation Approach" with Operating Partners (Warren Gee quote, home).
- Cairngorm Capital Foundation established 2021; Scottish Highlands landscape and habitats (responsible-investing).
- Authorised and regulated by the FCA (footer). Copyright line: Cairngorm Capital Partners LLP.
