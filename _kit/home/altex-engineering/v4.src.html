About Altex

A full service provider to industry

As one of the UK’s leading sheet metal fabricators and experts in CNC machining, Altex offers design and re-engineering advice right from the start, so clients get best value for money without any compromise to product quality. Whatever the product needs, from initial concept over one-off prototypes to large batch production, our in-house services and technology offer the complete solution.

We have built an impressive UK-wide customer base in a variety of market places, and are approved suppliers to government agencies and several national and multi-national organisations.

Our services

From profiled blanks to complex assemblies

Our products range from the most basic of profiled blanks to large, complex assemblies and intricately machined parts and components.

  • Precision machining

    CNC milling and turning

    Milled and turned items from prototypes through to mass production, programmed with the latest 3D CAD/CAM packages.

  • Metal fabrication

    Laser, punch and fold

    Laser and punch technology produce high quality blanks that move through forming, inserting and welding.

  • Welding

    TIG, MIG, stud and spot

    Aluminium, mild steel and stainless steel, welded in dedicated bays and finished clean and smooth.

  • Finishing

    Powder coating in house

    Iron phosphate pre-treatment, three powder coating booths and three gas-fired stoving ovens.

How a job moves through the works

From drawing to despatch

  1. Design
  2. Cut
  3. Fold
  4. Weld
  5. Finish
  1. 01 · Design

    Design for manufacture

    SolidWorks Premium, Radan CAD/CAM and Bysoft bending software. Design and re-engineering advice from the start, so clients get best value for money.

  2. 02 · Cut

    Laser cutting and punching

    Two Salvagnini fibre lasers on 3000 × 1500 mm beds cut mild steel up to 20 mm and aluminium and stainless steel up to 15 mm. An AMADA punch handles forming up to 3 mm.

  3. 03 · Fold

    Folding and panel bending

    Press brakes up to 150 ton × 3 m, and a Salvagnini P2 panel bender (2500 × 1600 mm, up to 203 mm high) with fully automated tool changing.

  4. 04 · Weld

    Welding and fabricating

    TIG, MIG, stud and spot welding by skilled welders, plus a 6-axis TruArc Weld 1000 robot. Rough edges and colour in the welds are removed.

  5. 05 · Finish

    Powder coating and despatch

    Pre-treated, powder coated and stoved in house, assembled, then delivered by our own vehicles or a national courier service.

Our commitments

Quality and the environment

Quality

Altex runs a Business Operating System that meets the demands of the ISO 9001 Quality Management System standard. Every employee can affect the quality of product and service, so the system is communicated to everyone, its performance data is published, and its design is reviewed at regular intervals.

Altex is committed to preventing modern slavery and human trafficking across its operations and supply chain, reviewed annually by senior management.

ISO 9001 certified

Environment

Altex keeps investing in equipment that improves efficiency and lowers energy use:

  • Radan nesting uses as little material as possible on every sheet
  • An on-site nitrogen plant removed regular gas deliveries
  • The L5 laser can cut with compressed air instead of nitrogen
  • All paint shop materials and chemicals are RoHS compliant
