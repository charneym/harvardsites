Surface treatment

Corona treaters for every web

To obtain optimum adhesion when printing, laminating or coating, the surface energy of the substrate has to be raised. Corona treatment is a cost-effective, known and trusted way to do it, and our in-house design team builds a treater to suit each line.

  • Film manufacturers

    Blown, cast and sheet extrusion

    Guaranteed dyne levels, even at high slip additive levels. Cast film systems to 5000 mm wide, treating one side, both sides or either side of the web.

  • Converters

    Printing and lamination

    Universal systems for conductive and non-conductive substrates, with ceramic electrodes and removable cartridges. Widths to 3500 mm and speeds to 1500 m/min.

  • Digital print & finishing

    Narrow web label presses

    Compact cantilevered treaters, single, double or triple treat, installed on presses from HP Indigo, Bobst, Xeikon, Duplo, AB Graphic and others.

  • Ozone generators

    For extrusion coating

    Ozone applied to the melt curtain improves adhesion between extrudate and laminate, so coat weight and melt temperature can come down.

The system

Everything a treatment line needs

  1. Station
  2. Power
  3. Ozone
  4. Rolls
  1. 01 · Treater station

    Built for the line it joins

    Not every application can be solved with an off-the-shelf product. Our design team works with customers and their suppliers on a cost-effective, sustainable surface treatment solution.

  2. 02 · Power supply

    True power, on the display

    Variable-frequency IGBT power supplies from 0.3 to 20 kW. The 4 to 20 kW CSR range builds on Sherman Treaters reactive power generators and shows true and reactive power to the operator.

  3. 03 · Ozone

    Applied where it helps, removed where it harms

    Ozone generators feed the melt curtain on extrusion coating lines. Our destruct units use a manganese dioxide catalyst, not activated carbon, and guarantee exit levels below 0.1 ppm.

  4. 04 · Dielectric rolls

    The right covering on the base roll

    Silicone sleeves, ceramic and vulcanised coatings. The correct dielectric gives years of trouble-free treating; the wrong one gives many problems, so we help you choose.

Spares & consumables

Parts and test inks, from people who know the machines

Spare parts for our own treater stations and generators, genuine Sherman Treaters parts for GX and GXR power supplies, and parts for other manufacturers’ systems. Don’t know the part number? A description or a photo is often enough.

  • Dyne pens & inks

    Measure surface tension

    Quick Test, Jumbo and non-toxic Eco pens, plus test inks, from 32 to 71 dyne/cm. Jumbo pens conform to ASTM D2578-67 and DIN 53 364.

  • Silicone sleeves

    Dielectric coverings

    Made from a specific silicone rubber compound to close tolerances, for a wide range of roller diameters. Ceramic and vulcanised coatings too.

  • Treater & generator spares

    Ours, Sherman and others
