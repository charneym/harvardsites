Electrofusion wire laying

Wire laying machines for every fitting size

Our systems wire lay directly into the fitting, a simpler, more flexible and more efficient alternative to inserting wire coils during the initial moulding stage. Production licences are sold to each customer in line with worldwide patents.

  • EWL-P3

    Single spindle, horizontal

    Siemens 828D CNC. Designed for electrofusion pads for over-moulding into saddle bodies, plus small fittings and bifilar parts.

  • HWL 280S

    Long bed, horizontal

    For extra long fittings or pipe sections up to 280 mm internal diameter and up to 1.2 m long.

  • VWL 2000

    Vertical, 3-axis CNC

    Fittings typically from 250 to 2000 mm internal diameter (2000 mm SDR26).

  • SWL

    Saddle wire laying, 5-axis

    Machines and wire lays the curved profile of electrofusion saddle fittings.

Leather testing

Setting standards for leather softness and thickness

Precision instruments for the leather supply chain, used in automotive and furniture upholstery, footwear and fashion apparel.

The ST300 and SG300 measure leather and synthetic materials, in analogue or digital versions, and both can be fully calibrated to meet quality standard requirements. The ST300 was designed with BLC Leather Technology Centre.

  • ISO 17235

    ST300 Softness Tester

    Tests softness without defacing the hide or cutting samples, so supplier and customer can share one quality system.

  • ISO 2589

    SG300 Substance Gauge

    Measures leather thickness. Its triangular frame reaches a greater depth of softer material than conventional units.

  • 3 – 65 % water

    LM 6 Moisture Meter

    Non-destructive moisture readings in seconds, for wet blue as well as dry leather.
