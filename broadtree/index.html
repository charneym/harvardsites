01Approach

Growth, from the roots up.

Broadtree’s process is flexible and tailored to each owner. These five steps take a company from a first conversation to a signed purchase agreement and a plan for the transition.

Step 1 of 5 · Initial review

A first look, in confidence.

Broadtree identifies companies with growth tailwinds and completes an initial review of the organization, starting with a Non-Disclosure Agreement so information can be shared securely.

Step 2 of 5 · Collaboration

Your goals, first.

Broadtree works with you, the owner, on growth strategies and how it can be part of them. This often includes a virtual or in-person management meeting.

Step 3 of 5 · Letter of intent

Terms, agreed together.

Broadtree negotiates and signs a Letter of Intent with you and your company, with an investment proposal tailored to your objectives.

Step 4 of 5 · Due diligence

Verified, not assumed.

Due diligence confirms the financials and business details through a Quality of Earnings review, working toward the final purchase.

Step 5 of 5 · Purchase agreement

Rooted for the long term.

The Purchase Agreement sets the legal terms and the transition plan. After the deal, Broadtree invests in the critical infrastructure for the company’s continued growth.

  • Operators

    An operator-centric model

    Broadtree invests talent and time as well as capital. Owners work directly with a potential next CEO, a leader chosen through an extensive selection process.

  • Legacy

    Your legacy and culture, kept

    Broadtree prioritizes the company’s legacy and core values, rather than treating it as another acquisition or another branch on a family tree.

  • Proposals

    Tailored, flexible proposals

    Each investment proposal is customized to the owner’s objectives, built for a long-term partnership rather than a short-term strategy.
