Core Cutters

CCA automatic core cutter

A safe, cost-effective machine that needs minimal operator intervention. It grips the parent core, measures its length and works through the cut sequence on its own.

  • Up to 7 lengths programmed per sequence on the touch screen
  • Up to 60 cores a minute, depending on core material and cut width
  • Servo ball-screw indexing to micron accuracy that does not wear like belt drives
  • Cardboard, plastic and paper cores, with cut pressure set on screen
Salvage Winders

Universal X2 salvage rewinder

A roll doctor built for tension-sensitive substrates, with closed-loop Total Tension Control, a cantilevered design for easy loading and reversible running, so print direction can be corrected without unloading the roll. Up to 600 m/min.

  • Inspection
  • Defect removal
  • Roll profile correction
  • Spooling
  • Trimming
  • Slitting

About

Converting machinery from Ipswich since 1996

Universal Converting Equipment supplies slitter rewinders and converting machinery to the converting industry worldwide. We develop and build both the machinery and the control systems, which is why we can offer a high level of customisation and prompt support. All machines are designed and built in the UK, at our premises on Farthing Road in Ipswich.

  1. 1996

    Universal Control Equipment formed by former Atlas personnel.

  2. 2011

    500th installation worldwide.

  3. 2012

    Neighbouring buildings in Ipswich bought: 50% more manufacturing space.

  4. 2014

    CNC machine tools installed to make our own mechanical components.

  5. 2016

    Service and demonstration facility opened in Tampa, USA.

  6. 2018

    Assembly area expanded by 50%.

  7. 2022

    Large-format CNC mill added and a separate Technology Centre opened for customer demonstrations.

How we work

From our machine shop to your floor

  1. Machine
  2. Control
  3. Configure
  4. Support
  1. 01 · Machine

    Components made in-house

    Since 2014 we have made our own mechanical components on CNC machine tools, and in 2022 added a large-format mill. Frames are cross-braced and precision bored as a pair.

  2. 02 · Control

    Our own control systems

    We write the controls as well as building the machine: a large touch screen, recipes for repeatability, dynamic circuit diagrams with auto fault-find and an OPC server for SCADA.

  3. 03 · Configure

    Built around the application

    Automation options let each customer pick the version that suits the job, from laser perforation and inspection to turret rewinds and automatic knife and core positioning.

  4. 04 · Support

    Supported for the long run

    Same-day support by telephone, real-time remote diagnostics over the internet, and service engineers normally available within 48 hours.
