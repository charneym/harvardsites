## NIH funding (NIH RePORTER API, https://api.reporter.nih.gov/v2/projects/search)
First-year award amounts as reported by RePORTER. Fzata's own site states NIH non-dilutive funding "totaling of over $24MM".
- **1R44AI197301-01**, NIAID, award notice 2026-09-11, $313,095 — "Development of a Dual-Action Oral Immunotherapy for Clostridioides difficile Prevention". PI: Zhiyong Yang. Abstract: advances FZ002/FZ040, "a novel oral immunotherapy combining two engineered strains of the probiotic yeast *Saccharomyces boulardii*… FZ002 neutralizes the toxins TcdA and TcdB, the primary virulence factors of C. difficile, while FZ040 inhibits bacterial colonization and spore formation by targeting the conserved protease Cwp84." Abstract states CDI causes "over 12,800 deaths annually in the US" and that the approach "represents the first strategy to concurrently target C. difficile toxins and spores".
- **1R44DK146567-01**, NIDDK, award notice 2026-09-17, $372,153 — "Gut-Targeted IL-10 Delivery via Engineered Saccharomyces boulardii to Prevent Immune Checkpoint Inhibitor Colitis". PI: Zhiyong Yang. Abstract: FZ034 is "a first-in-class engineered Saccharomyces boulardii that secretes interleukin-10 fused with IgG-Fc (IL-10-Fc)"; it "delivers localized anti-inflammatory action to the gut mucosa via IL-10-Fc, mitigating colitis while preserving ICIs' antitumor immunity"; abstract states colitis occurs in "35–50% of patients" on immune checkpoint inhibitors.
- **4UH3NS135350-02**, NINDS, award notice 2026-09-19, $2,058,422 — "A novel oral immunotherapy for chronic visceral pain of irritable bowel syndrome". PI: Zhiyong Yang. Abstract: FZ006 is "an engineered probiotic yeast Saccharomyces boulardii that secretes a neutralizing antibody against TNF-α"; UH3 phase covers GMP manufacture, stability, GLP toxicology and IND submission.
- 1UG3NS135350-01A1, NINDS, 2024-08-29, $2,091,549 — UG3 phase of the same IBS programme.
- 1R44DK129133-01, NIDDK, 2021-04-27, $252,416 (Fast-track) — "Novel Oral Yeast Immunotherapies for Ulcerative Colitis"; abstract describes FZ006 delivering "a potent bi-specific neutralizing antibody against human TNF-a to the intestines".
- 1R44AI155277-01, NIAID, 2020-07-20, $169,461 (Fast-track) — "A Yeast-based Immunotherapy against Clostridioides difficile infection"; abstract describes Sb-ABAB, "a fusion protein of 4 VHHs targeting 2 distinct neutralizing epitopes in TcdA and 2 in TcdB", "broadly active against toxins from 64 clinical isolates".
- 1R01AI132207-01 and renewals, NIAID, from 2017-05-29 — "A Novel Humanized Tetra-specific Antibody against Clostridium difficile Infection".
- Earlier SBIR Phase I awards: 1R43AI129044-01 (2016-12-23), 1R43AI136108-01 and 1R43AI136176-01 (Jan 2018), plus Phase II continuations 2R44AI136176-02 (2021).
