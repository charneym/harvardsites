## Mission / technology
- "Engineered Nanoimmunotherapies for Cancer"; "ImmunoBlue engineers novel nanoimmunotherapies by combining the principles of nanotechnology and immunotherapy to treat cancer." — home page
- Our Technology: personalized, multi-targeted, tumor-specific T cells; T cell expansion platform using an FDA-approved nanoparticle activated with a laser; antigen-agnostic; autologous adoptive T cell therapy; brain malignancies and other solid tumors — https://www.immunoblue.com/about
- Process (IB-T cells): Prussian blue nanoparticle photothermal therapy on tumor cells excised at surgery → immunogenic cell death, antigens + adjuvants → expansion of tumor-specific IB-T cells with autologous dendritic cells → envisioned Phase 1: adjuvant into the tumor bed via an Ommaya reservoir. GBM 5-yr relative survival 7.1%. Phase I feasibility n=6 patients. Phase II: GMP manufacturing with GW, PDX efficacy, IND-enabling studies. — NIH abstract, https://reporter.nih.gov/project-details/11315832
