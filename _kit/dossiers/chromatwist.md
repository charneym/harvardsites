## The company
University of Birmingham spin-out developing patented large Stokes shift organic fluorescent dyes, excited across the
UV and the blue with tuneable emission across the visible spectrum, for flow cytometry (CT_UV series, 355 nm; CT_V,
405 nm, to come), multiphoton microscopy and other applications; also sells triphenylene chemical precursors.
Address: ChromaTwist Ltd, Lab PD206, Science Centre, University of Wolverhampton Science Park, Glaisher Drive,
Wolverhampton WV10 9RU. Sources: chromatwist.com (/, /about, /technology, /flow-cytometry, /contact, /news, /products).
