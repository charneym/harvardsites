## OXO-001
- First-in-class, non-hormonal, oral tablet that interacts directly with the endometrium to improve receptivity to the
  embryo (Clinical Trials Arena / Manufacturing Chemist, July 2024; IBEC 2021: "the first and only one of its kind").
- Passed the required preclinical safety studies to EMA and FDA quality standards (PCB, 1 Oct 2021).
- Embryo implantation (IVF/ICSI): Phase II completed (PR Newswire 2025).
  OXOART2 (NCT05076032), placebo-controlled; exploratory analysis of 96 women ≤40 with single embryo transfer and donor
  eggs, presented at ESHRE 40th Annual Meeting, Amsterdam, 7–10 July 2024. Early pregnancy 75.9 vs 52.4%; clinical
  pregnancy (heartbeat, 5 wk) 50 vs 35.7%; ongoing (~10 wk) 46.3 vs 35.7%; live birth 42.6 vs 35.7% (Clinical Trials
  Arena, 8 July 2024). Not designed to confirm statistical significance. Dosing twice daily from one cycle before transfer
  to five weeks after (Femtech Insider). Conducted at 28 centres in Europe (catalonia.health, July 2024). Phase III planned
  incl. women using their own eggs.
- PCOS infertility: preclinical, restores ovulation and reproductive capacity (Rutgers, 29 Nov 2021); "on track for
  Phase 2" (PR Newswire, 9 Apr 2025). Collaborators University of Córdoba–IMIBIC (Tena-Sempere), Rutgers (Babwah).
- Context: >3 million ART cycles worldwide a year, more than half do not result in a birth (PCB 2021).
