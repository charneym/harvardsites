## Company
- HQ: 1758 Tiffin Place, Victoria, BC, Canada (Wix business location on the site). Email: info@apricell.com (site footer; also the
  contact listed for CEO Amir Seyfoori in the Bionow/Tebubio release).
- Story (site): dedicated to reducing the attrition rate of new cancer drugs in clinical trials; Organoid-on-a-Plate technology developed
  in collaboration with the University; 95% of new cancer drugs fail during clinical trials; 2D cultures and murine models do not depict
  human biology (Eroom's law).
- Products: 3-in-1™ Platform (hydrogel insert per well of a six-well plate; spheroid formation, ECM, drug testing, downstream analysis;
  in situ FFPE slicing for IHC); TruSphere™ (up to 600 uniform spheroids from one pipetting step, 3500 per plate); HTSphere™ (96-well,
  6–12 spheroids per well, up to 1152 per plate, automation-compatible). Disease types, cell types, readouts as listed on /products.
- Clients shown: MGH, Brown, U of Toronto, U of Calgary. Supporters shown: NRC IRAP, Mitacs, New Ventures BC, Life Sciences BC,
  Coast Capital Innovation Centre, UVic, LiME lab.
