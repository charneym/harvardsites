## Investment criteria — exactly as published
Investment Focus page (https://snowpeakcapital.com/investment-focus/):
- INVESTMENT CRITERIA: "Lower middle market companies with headquarters in North America"; "Control-oriented investments with up to $30 million of EBITDA; positive EBITDA not a prerequisite"; "Flexible and creative capital structuring"; "Opportunity to create value through operational improvements and/or strategic M&A".
- INDUSTRY FOCUS: Business Services; Industrials; Technology, Media and Telecom.
- TRANSACTION TYPES: Owner Operator or Founder / Family Owned Businesses; Corporate Divestitures; Companies in Transition; Growth-Oriented Investments; Special Situations.

Fact sheet (Sept 2026):
- Financial Profile: Revenue up to $300M; EBITDA up to $30M; TEV up to ~$100M; Equity Check up to ~$50M; Balance Sheet "Big and Leverageable".
- Deal Dynamics: owner/operators looking for a transition plan; importance of speed, certainty & transition; structured deals to ensure everyone wins; assets in a state of flux; assets SPC knows well; reasonable valuation expectations.
- Strategic Angle: operating partner network leveraged during diligence and hold; deep relationships with experienced, hands-on operators who can oversee & advise portfolio companies; partner-oriented approach ensuring everyone wins.
- Value Creation Path: M&A Strategy; Margin Enhancement; Multiple Expansion → "Opportunities to Make a Good Business Better".
- Sector Focus — Industrials: Manufacturing & Industrial Products; Automotive & Mobility Solutions; Aerospace & Defense; Building Materials; Distribution & Packaging; Specialty Materials. Business & Tech-Enabled Services: MRO Services; Logistics & Supply Chain; Facility & Safety Management; B2B Distribution; IT, Value Added Resellers & Digital Services; BPO & Outsourcing. Plus "Other Opportunistic Sectors Where our Operating Partner Network has Experience and Provides us with a Compelling Angle".
