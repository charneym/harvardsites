## BRAND FIDELITY AND NO "AI TEMPLATE" LOOK (client, 2026-11-26 — overrides earlier hero/palette guidance)
The client found sites that read as "clearly AI": dark ink page, one floating object with a glow on the right, two-line H1 with an italic accent-coloured second line, and an accent colour that is not the company's (e.g. Tekno's red logo given a teal site). Rules from now on:
1. PALETTE = THE COMPANY'S. Sample the primary and secondary colours from their logo file and live site CSS and list them with hex values in your report. The main accent (buttons, links, highlighted words) must be one of those colours or a direct tint/shade of it. If their site is light, default to a light page; only go dark if their own brand is dark. No invented teal/cyan/lime accents.
2. HERO = THEIR REAL PRODUCT OR WORK FIRST. Use the company's own product photography, factory/lab photos or team-at-work photos whenever any usable image exists (≥ ~700px, can be cleanly cut out or shown full-bleed). Upscale modestly or crop rather than replace with a render. A crafted 3D render is allowed only when they genuinely have no usable images, and then it must match their actual product exactly — no generic glowing object. Say in the report which you used and why.
3. BREAK THE SILHOUETTE. Do not default to: dark page + floating object + glow + italic coloured second headline line. Choose a layout that fits the company: e.g. full-bleed photo hero with text over a calm area, light page with a product photo on a plain studio ground, split hero with a real factory photo, or a type-led hero with a strip of real product images. Headline in one weight/colour unless their brand calls for otherwise; italics only if their brand uses them.
4. In SITE DONE, include: "Palette: <hex list> from <source>", "Hero: own photo <url> / render (reason)", and "Layout: <which>".

## STRUCTURE v3: REMOVE THE TEMPLATE SKELETON (client, 2026-11-26 — overrides HOME_BRIEF, SPEC, PRINCIPLES and the LOADER section wherever they conflict)

## COMPOSITION BASICS (client, 2026-11-26 — checked by eye, every hero and every photo)
Things an ordinary visitor notices straight away:
1. Direction: the subject's front (face, ports, logo, working end) points INTO the page, towards the text. If it faces away, swap sides (photo left, text right), crop differently or pick another photo. Never mirror a photo that has a logo or text on it.
2. No confusing depth: no out-of-focus foreground blobs, no blurred parts crossing the subject, no fake reflections or floor shadows. Crop them out or choose a different photo.
3. The photo has its own defined area (a grid column, a framed crop or a full-bleed band). It never slides under the text or pushes the text into a corner. Centre the text block vertically against the photo. No large empty corners and no dead band under the hero.
4. Straight or gently angled subjects only. A diagonal that runs off the bottom edge looks accidental.
5. Sharp: never shown larger than about 1.2x its native pixels. If the only photo is small, show it smaller inside a frame, not stretched as a hero.
6. Before reporting, look at the hero at 1440 and 390 and ask: "Would a normal person think something is off here?" If so, fix it.
