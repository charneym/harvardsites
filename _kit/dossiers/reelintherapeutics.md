# Reelin Therapeutics (reelintherapeutics) — home-only build, batch 2026-11e

- Site: https://reelintherapeutics.com (WordPress "idea-flow" theme). HQ: San Diego, CA (NIH RePORTER); papers give La Jolla, CA 92037/92130.
- Mission (site): "Revolutionizing treatments for chronic inflammation." Target chronic inflammatory and autoimmune conditions with a biological molecule that decreases leukocyte-driven inflammation; modulate Reelin levels in plasma to restore immune balance without broad immunosuppression or compromising CNS function.
- Mechanism (site figure + NIH abstracts): plasma Reelin binds endothelial ApoER2, raising adhesion molecules (E-selectin, CAMs) -> leukocyte rolling, adhesion, diapedesis. Anti-Reelin mAbs deplete plasma Reelin stably and downregulate all major inflammation-driven adhesion proteins.
