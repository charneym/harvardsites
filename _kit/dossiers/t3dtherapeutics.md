## Facts used
- Founded 2013 to challenge prevailing thinking on AD; mission: a disease-modifying new drug for AD; also targeting other neurodegenerative diseases, emphasis on Huntington's disease.
- AD as a neuro-metabolic disease: poor energy metabolism and dysfunctional lipid metabolism -> brain "starvation" -> neurodegeneration and "strangulation" (plaques, tangles, inflammation).
- Brain = 2% of body weight; 20% of the body's oxygen; 25% of body glucose and 15% of cardiac output at rest.
- T3D-959: investigational, not approved; activates two nuclear receptors (PPAR delta, and PPAR gamma at 15-fold lower potency); first PPAR delta-activating compound developed for AD; oral, once a day; exclusive worldwide license for T3D-959 and structurally related small molecules; AD mild to moderate, potentially MCI.
- Phase 1: 96 normal subjects, up to 7 days, no drug-related adverse events, MTD > 200 mg. Phase 2a: 34 subjects, 4 doses, 14 days, 4 completers in a 22-week extension; AAIC 2018. Phase 2 PIONEER (NCT04251182): multi-center, double-blind, placebo-controlled, completed; results at AAIC 2024.
- Team, board and SAB names/titles as published (management, board, SAB pages).
