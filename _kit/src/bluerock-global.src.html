01Approach

Invest. Scale. Succeed.

BlueRock identifies high-potential businesses, optimizes operations and applies strategic investment to strengthen each company's position in its market.

01 · Components

The parts that are hard to find.

Direct Components, founded in 1998 by Managing Partner Aaron Nursey, is a stocking distributor of allocated, obsolete and hard-to-find electronic components for commercial, industrial and defense and aerospace customers.

02 · Assembly

Wired, built and boxed to print.

Paragon Manufacturing builds custom cables, harnesses, electro-mechanical assemblies and turn-key box builds to print. It was established in 2003 and is ISO 9001, ISO 13485, UL and ITAR registered.

03 · Optics

Light, shaped in Sarasota.

American Photonics makes precision infrared and laser optics for defense, medical and industrial systems, fabricated, coated and inspected under one roof in Sarasota, Florida. Founded in 2001, it joined BlueRock in 2026.

04 · The platform

One platform, on American soil.

Together they form a collection of American-owned manufacturing and technology companies built to keep critical capability at home, each one backed by BlueRock's capital and its experience in scaling operations.
