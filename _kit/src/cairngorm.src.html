Approach

More than capital.

We are thematic investors with a collaborative approach. Working with our management teams, we transform businesses, and our goal is to build companies that lead their fields.

What we look for

Profitable companies with room to climb.

We invest in profitable companies with the potential for transformative growth, and have particular expertise in family and founder-owned businesses. Most are headquartered in the UK, and we are equally comfortable investing in North America and continental Europe.

Structure

A majority partner, invested alongside you.

We take majority positions in companies valued at up to £200m, typically deploying £10–40m into each. Our senior staff invest personally in every transaction, so our interests are aligned with management's.

Transformation

Operating partners for the ascent.

With our Operating Partners, we work alongside leadership teams on strategy and operations: commercial optimisation, geographic expansion, service line extensions and digital transformation.

Buy and build

Leadership, acquisition by acquisition.

We provide expert support for acquisitive growth. National Timber Group was formed through nine acquisitions in five years; IBMG through more than twenty.

  • Aligned

    Our own capital in every deal

    Most of the capital we commit comes from institutional investors, but Cairngorm's senior staff also invest in every transaction, alongside our management partners.

  • Thematic

    Sectors with lasting demand

    We invest where our team has deep experience: ageing in place; repair, maintenance and improvement of properties; wealth management; and sustainability.

  • Responsible

    ESG from entry to exit

    ESG runs through our process, from finding and executing investments through stewardship to exit. Our Foundation, established in 2021, supports work to restore the landscape and wild habitats of the Scottish Highlands.

Investment criteria

Built for transformative growth.

We invest in strongly performing, private mid-market growth companies with long-term potential, and take an active role in their strategy and operational focus.
