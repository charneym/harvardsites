## History
- "For over a decade, Sallyport operated as a single-family office, executing direct investments on behalf of the Foshee family ... 96% of executed deals originating from proprietary relationships." Source: https://sallyportinvestments.com/about-us
- Sallyport Partners Fund established 2023; first close announced Jan 13, 2025: exceeded its $150mm target, closing with $160mm in total capital commitments. Foshee seeded the fund and sits on the Investment Committee. Investor base: "successful entrepreneurs, seasoned executives, and influential families primarily from Texas". Source: fund release above.

## Strategy / approach (their wording)
- "Sallyport is highly opportunistic, primarily focusing on founder and family-owned businesses and starting businesses from scratch." (release adds corporate carveouts). About page.
- "Sallyport is a very patient and selective firm. We have reviewed thousands of deals since inception." Strategy page https://sallyportinvestments.com/strategy
- Process: proprietary deal flow and investment theses from deep research, internal expertise and market trends → rigorous diligence "to validate or disprove our thesis" → after capital is deployed, network and in-house operational expertise: "executive coaching and recruitment to building sales processes and analysis". Strategy page.
- "Flexibility and creativity are core to Sallyport's DNA ... believe the first person to see a great deal will do it." Strategy page.
- Bethancourt: "We have a unique appreciation for the management team's perspective because we've been in their shoes." Fund release.

## Investment criteria (published PDF)
Source: https://sallyport.s3.amazonaws.com/assets/SPF-Investment-Criteria_2026-09-09-202504_veit.pdf
- Strike zone: $10–30mm equity investment (able to scale with co-invest); $5mm+ EBITDA; $25mm+ enterprise value; control & non-control ownership; "Nationwide approach, Texas-first mentality".
- Criteria: strong management team; product we like; we can add meaningful value.
- Capital structure: strong preference for equity, will consider structured debt with warrants.
- Size: primary focus lower to middle market, selective larger enterprise deals.
- Investment type: growth-stage or mature businesses, occasionally building new businesses from scratch (Founder / Growth / Buyout).
- Transaction focus: buyout, growth capital, equity recap, succession planning, non-core asset sale.
- Diversified industries (non-exhaustive): specialty finance, manufacturing, logistics, infrastructure, services.
