## Facts used (all from bitmos.de/de unless noted)
- Unternehmen: from graduates of Heinrich-Heine-Universität Düsseldorf; develops and sells high-quality medical products with own production since 1988; currently 7 staff on approx. 400 m² in Düsseldorf; most staff with the firm for more than 10 years; QM system per EN 13485 since 1998; full medical-device development service incl. design, documentation and CE certification; product optimisation, feasibility checks; core competence hardware and software development of electronic medical devices.
- Produkte: since 1988 solutions in neonatal monitoring, pulse oximetry, long-term oxygen therapy, sleep apnoea therapy, incontinence therapy; clearly structured operating concepts, innovative details, modern design, high cost-efficiency.
- Pulsoxymetrie: since 2000 around Masimo SET; current family sat 805 (stand-alone monitor; one-button operation) and sat 801+ (handheld with plethysmogram and interfaces).
- sat 805 page: Made in Germany; clinic (reliable data capture and alarms for fast reaction), sleep lab (integration into any polysomnography workstation), HomeCare (simple, safe operation). Home slider: Masimo SET, plethysmogram, Li-ion, remote alarm nurse call, analogue interface.
- sat 801+ page and slider: Masimo SET (motion and low-perfusion tolerant), longer battery, USB or SD data transfer; integrated protective collar.
- Masimo Sensoren page: RD SET and LNCS disposable adhesive sensors (adult, paediatric, infant, neonatal, NeoPt), reusable sensors, patient cables, accessories and spare parts; latex-free.
- Handelspartner: dealers across Europe (Belgium to Spain, incl. Dräger Medical in several countries) and worldwide.
- News: oxygen-therapy products discontinued 15.03.2024 (service continues); Medica 2022 note; donation arrived in Albania 23.06.2022; home page notice "Besuchen Sie uns auf der MEDICA 2025, Halle 11 - Stand B25".
