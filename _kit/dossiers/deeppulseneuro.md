## The company (deeppulseneuro.com, single page)
Montpellier medtech start-up. "DeepPulseNeuro will provide the standard technique for measuring electrical brain activity."
Device + endovascular approach (as used in cardiovascular procedures and stroke treatment with stents) → EEG measured deep in
the brain, as close as possible to the areas responsible for epilepsy; travels through the cerebral vessels without
perforating the skull; "safe, accessible, and clinically relevant channel". Epilepsy: >50 million people; one third
drug-resistant. Aims (*pending clinical evidence): faster, more accurate diagnosis; reduced patient uncertainty; refined
pharmaceutical or surgical treatment options; reduced medical costs. Europe and US access ambition. CSR purpose/goal text used.
Also (LinkedIn/HTX Index, not used on page): University of Montpellier innovation prize; HTX Index label.
