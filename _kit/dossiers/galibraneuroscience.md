## Facts used (all from their site, the PR Newswire release, NIH RePORTER or the cited journals)
- Home page: "Advancing gene therapy for GABA disorders"; founded in 2023 by a clinician and a scientist at Boston Children's Hospital affiliated with Harvard Medical School, founders with over 25 years of experience in clinical care and translational research. Science page: GABA is a major inhibitory neurotransmitter; genetic changes affecting its metabolism or signalling cause serious neurological disorders; research spans monogenic rare disorders and broader neurological conditions; each programme requires rigorous preclinical and clinical evaluation to establish safety and efficacy. "Why Galibra": partnerships with leading academic institutes and patient advocacy groups to streamline bench-to-bedside translation.
- Pipeline, read off their own chart (wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Slide6.png): Monogenic rare disorders — GAL005 / SSADHD (reaches preclinical, "AAV toxicology studies"), GAL101 / SLC6A1 (preclinical, "AAV efficacy testing in vivo"), GAL201 / GABA-A variants (discovery, "construct validation"), GAL301 / GABA-T deficiency (discovery, "mouse model validation"); Prevalent neurologic conditions — GAL401 / drug-resistant epilepsy (discovery, "proof-of-concept in vivo"), GAL501 / undisclosed neurologic condition (discovery, "concept validation"). Stage axis: Discovery, Preclinical, First-in-human, Pivotal, Post-approval.
- SSADH deficiency biology, from the August 2026 release: caused by pathologic variants in ALDH5A1, which disrupt GABA metabolism and lead to accumulation of toxic neuroactive metabolites; no FDA-approved disease-modifying therapy; treatment is symptom management; preclinical data from Boston Children's Hospital and Harvard Medical School; IND-enabling activities under way.
- Licences: option licence agreement from Boston Children's Hospital for AAV-mediated gene supplement therapy for SLC6A1-related disorders (May 2024 news); Apertura Gene Therapy CapX BBB-penetrating capsid licence for GAL005 (August 2025 news).

## Story
Galibra's own GAL005 mechanism as one engraved scene: a cerebral capillary across the top of the frame (the blood-brain barrier), a neuron filling the tissue below it. Step 1, the pathway GABA → succinic semialdehyde → succinate with the SSADH step crossed out and neuroactive metabolites accumulating in the soma; step 2, AAV vector particles travel the capillary and cross the barrier; step 3, the delivered ALDH5A1 reaches the nucleus, the blocked step opens, the accumulation clears, and the readout swings — SSADH activity up, accumulated metabolites down (direction only, no invented numbers).
