Past and present

A family-owned engineering business

Inca Geometric has all the advantages of a family-owned business, with the personal interest, dynamics and decision making to provide a round-the-clock engineering and production service. Subcontract operations now take the lion’s share of the business, built on a very loyal, technically demanding customer base with a high ratio of repeat business.

  1. 1956

    The company is founded to design and manufacture multi-drill heads.

  2. 1962

    Expansion into special-purpose machine tool development and build, with major projects for blue-chip companies in the UK and overseas.

  3. 1970s

    Subcontract machining is introduced, using the machine tool build facilities.

  4. 2019–20

    A new TOS W100A borer and a Quaser vertical machining centre add capacity.
