## Concept build notes (site: hidden-river/)
- Palette (BK): logo deep teal #0F4B66 → dark sections and text (#0f3e54); logo sage #639180 → --acc (#4a7565 for small text contrast on paper, #b9d3c6 on teal); warm paper #f2f1ea ground.
- Type: Spectral (a refined serif in place of Crimson Text), Schibsted Grotesk, Red Hat Mono. Official transparent logo hotlinked with a text fallback.
- Hero: the winding river of the logo's R as a topographic map, contour lines following its bends, a slow current along it.
- Story: the funding void between rigid debt and dilutive equity → structured capital runs through it → management still owns and runs the business → four uses (the firm's transaction types).
- Images used: all 8 headshots (portrait grid, two-column person sheets).
- Images not used: river hero photo/video (likely stock); generic icon set; portfolio logo tiles (1000px JPGs on white tiles; names set in type for one consistent treatment, rule BW); news thumbnails (badge, AI-generated cover).
- Fund size is not shown on the site: the firm's own posts give $245M, $248M and ~$250M.
