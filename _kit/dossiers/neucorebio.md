## Firm
- Neucore Bio, Inc., 1275 Kinnear Rd, Columbus, OH 43212 (Ohio State's Kinnear Road research campus).
- Platform: FixE / FieEV — fibroblast-derived engineered extracellular vesicles. Human dermal fibroblasts
  are transiently reprogrammed toward a Schwann-cell identity; the vesicles they release carry natural
  homing signals for diseased peripheral nerve cells, and are loaded after harvest with plasmid DNA or
  mRNA.
- Lead programme: FixE-NF1, plexiform neurofibromas in neurofibromatosis type 1. Second: FieEV for
  Charcot-Marie-Tooth disease type 1A (miR-381 / miR-871 to Schwann cells; PMP22 duplication).
- Research partner: The Ohio State University. Phase II plan (company's own statement in the NSF outcomes
  report): transfer manufacturing to Lonza, in vivo preclinical studies at Indiana University School of
  Medicine. Patient-community relationships: Children's Tumor Foundation, Gilbert Family Foundation,
  Dr. D. Wade Clapp.
