## Facts used
- Home page: a pre-clinical stage biotech that develops advanced tissue constructs and platforms for diverse applications. It is a spinoff of FORTH and the University of Crete. Five areas of expertise: precision biofabrication; biomaterial design and fabrication; stem cell culture and differentiation; cell and animal models of neurodegeneration and CNS injury; implantable device design and characterization. Team bios.
- /h-neuroimplants: "Novel treatments for Central Nervous System Injuries consisting of potent Stem Cells inside bioactive biomaterials". The H-Neuroimplants project is funded by an EIC Pre-accelerator grant and develops GMP-compatible neuroimplants for evaluation in preclinical models of chronic SCI. Three publications: Kourgiantaki 2020 npj Regen Med (doi 10.1038/s41536-020-0097-0); Georgelou 2023 Biomedicines (10.3390/biomedicines11041170); Georgelou 2026 J Biomed Mater Res B (10.1002/jbmb.70013).
- CORDIS 101310742 H-Neuroimplants, "Microfabricated Scaffold Carriers of human Neural Stem Cell Therapies for the Treatment of Chronic Spinal Cord Injury": EC signature 29 June 2026, runs 2026-08-01 to 2028-07-31, EU contribution EUR 488,600, under Widening participation. Objective: a precision-microfabricated bioactive scaffold housing hiNSCs of optimized lineage; scaffold laser microfabrication; previous prototypes delivered mouse NSCs and improved locomotion in a mouse SCI model; a roadmap to clinical implementation; strong IP; a pipeline of mouse experiments.
