## Science (RePORTER abstract)
- EpiMAX: drug-device combination; epinephrine encapsulated in microparticles embedded in a sodium alginate-based injectable hydrogel.
- Hydrogel compresses underlying vessels at the bleeding site; biphasic Epi release gives immediate and sustained hemostasis over 72 hours while tissue heals.
- In vivo proof-of-concept: stops GI bleeding within 5 minutes in a porcine ulcer model with no re-application; minimal inflammation; no significant immune reaction; complete re-absorption of hydrogel five days post-injection; no systemic uptake.
- Aims: (1) optimize Epi microparticle production for biphasic release; transferable method for CDMO Bravado Pharmaceuticals; (2) GMP EpiMAX at 100 g scale (1000 dosage units) for CMC data to support pre-IND FDA interactions; (3) GLP efficacy comparison in porcine deep-ulcer GI bleeding model vs hydrogel only, Epi microparticles, Epi in saline, with/without standard of care (cautery, clip).
- "poised to be the first FDA-approved Epi-based standalone hemostatic therapy" (company aim).
- BizWest: GI bleeding during endoscopic procedures estimated ~1 million cases annually in the US; procedures such as EMR / ESD.
