## Facts used (site, 2026-11-21)
- About: pioneering novel therapeutic platform targeting immune-modulation and fibrosis. Clinical candidate VN-1032: orally active, novel Selective Aryl Hydrocarbon Receptor Modulator (SAhRM) with significant efficacy as immunotherapy in pancreatic and colorectal cancer; potent enhancement of intratumoral cytotoxic T cells with promising compatibility for PD-1 inhibitor combinations; pharmacological profile distinct from existing AhR antagonists; platform modulates a pathway across cancer immunotherapy and fibrotic diseases.
- Timeline: 2020 incorporated as C-Corp; 2019-21 SBIR Phase I (PAH); 2020-21 SBIR Phase I (DDAH biotherapeutic, kidney function in cardiac surgery); 2020-21 NHLBI Catalyze; 2021 BARDA Blue Knight QuickFire winner (small-molecule DDAH modulator, diabetic kidney disease); 2021-23 NHLBI Catalyze II; 2024 SBIR Phase II (PAH oral therapy); 2026 FDA response to Pre-IND briefing.
- Science: AhR stimulates IDO in cancer cells → kynurenine (AhR activator); AhR induces PD-L1 and CD8+ T-cell exhaustion; AhR enriches Tregs in the TME; AhR modulates macrophage and dendritic cell activity. PAH: SAhRM and suppressor of vascular toxin ADMA; AhR activation essential for PAH progression in humans and animal models; VN-1032 targets inflammation, oxidative stress, fibrosis; disease-modifying efficacy in preclinical PAH and CKD models.
- Team (site): Jaipal Singh PhD (CSO & Founder); Brad Lawson (CEO); Anantha Shekhar MD PhD (Co-Founder, SAB); Young Lee PhD (Project Leader); Vijay Reddy PhD DABT (SVP Toxicology); advisors Raymond Kauffman PhD (Pharmacology), Srinivas Kasibhatla PhD (Chemistry), Douglas Balogh PhD (FDA Regulatory Affairs / CMC), Timothy Frankel MD (Scientific & Clinical, Oncology), Jamie Mills MD PhD (Clinical Investigator, PDAC), Andrew Dahlem PhD (Strategic).
- NIH RePORTER 5R44HL149496-03 (appl 11193989), NHLBI SBIR Phase II, $1,276,514, notice 2025-09-08, project end 2027-08-31. Abstract: PAH 2.8-year average survival, 5-year survival 60% on therapy; vasodilators treat symptoms not progression; VN-1032 reduced AhR activation and ADMA; inhibited PASMC proliferation, oxidative stress, inflammation and fibrosis; robust efficacy in Sugen-Hypoxia model; aims: non-GLP safety (dose-escalation and 1-week repeat-dose tox in rats and dogs), backup compound, 12-week dose-response efficacy in severe PAH; goal IND-enabling studies and IND filing.
- Other awards: 1R43HL149496-01 (2019, $217,901), 1R33HL154135-01/-02 (2020/2021, Catalyze), 1R43DK125142-01A1 (2020, $234,583), 2R44HL149496-02A1 (2024, $1,077,584).
