About FET

Extrusion equipment for high value textile materials

FET designs, develops and manufactures extrusion equipment for a wide range of high value textile material applications worldwide, with in-house expertise in design, process technology, electronics, manufacturing and after sales service. Installations are bespoke or standard, and range in value from £300k to £3 million.

Products

Laboratory and pilot spinning systems

Self-contained lines built for the most demanding research and development teams, for installation in a laboratory or a small scale process evaluation environment.

  • FET-100 Series

    Melt spinning

    Multifilament, monofilament and nonwoven platforms built around the FET-100 extrusion module, with mono, bi and tri-component options.

  • FET-500 Series

    Gel spinning

    UHMWPE fibre development from under 100 g of polymer, with a patent-pending supercritical CO₂ wash in place of hexane or DCM.

  • FET-200 Series

    Wet spinning

    Lab and pilot lines for solvent and polymer combinations, in multifilament, monofilament and staple fibre, including vertical spinning.

  • Nonwoven systems

    Meltblown, spunbond, composite

    Nonwoven melt spinning formats, including meltblown technology developed for biomedical grade resorbable polymers.
