- **HQ:** 1650 Tysons Blvd., Suite 810, McLean, VA 22102. Phone (703) 462-5600. General email info@bluestoneinv.com (https://www.bluestoneinv.com/contact). LinkedIn: https://www.linkedin.com/company/bluestone-investment-partners/. Time zone: Eastern. No other offices listed. Founding year not stated on site; first fund closed 2011 (Washington Post item dated 2011-11-20, https://www.bluestoneinv.com/news/bluestone-closes-first-fund).
- Managing Partners: John Allen (co-founder) and Bill Strang (co-founder).

## Investment criteria (published, https://www.bluestoneinv.com/about)
- Areas of Interest: U.S. Defense and Government Sector.
- Revenue: $15M-$150M. EBITDA: $2M-$10M+.
- Investment types: Majority Investments (Preferred); Management Buyouts; Co-Investments; Minority Investments; Recapitalizations; Growth Capital; Corporate Carve-Outs.
- No geography beyond U.S., no check size, no debt types published.
- Separate Apr 2024 release (PR Newswire): the Wilson/Weston partnership targets federal technology providers "with annual revenues surpassing $25 million" (a search-specific criterion, not the firm-wide one).
