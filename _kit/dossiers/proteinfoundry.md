## Facts used (all from proteinfoundry.com)
- Home: "Supplying the biomedical research community with the highest quality recombinant chemokines"; the catalogue contains nearly all human and mouse chemokines, each of which can be labelled with the customer's choice of fluorescent dye, biotin or C-terminal tag; custom chemokine variant production and stable isotope labelling for NMR, MS and other applications.
- About Us: "Over the last 20 years, our research programs have developed unique and specialized molecular tools optimized for the production and functional analysis of chemokines, with particular attention to preserving the native N-terminal amino acid sequence for full biological activity."
- Custom Production: wild-type chemokines (bench quantities and cost-effective larger lots for in vivo studies in animal models); mutant chemokines (sequence modifications to any catalogue chemokine); stable isotope labelling (15N, 13C) for NMR or mass spectrometry.
- Quality Assurance: expressed in E. coli from plasmid vectors engineered to yield native N-termini; ion exchange and RP-HPLC steps preserve purity and the native disulfide pairings; QC = purity/content (SDS-PAGE, RP-HPLC), authenticity (mass spectrometry), structural integrity (1D and 2D NMR), biological activity (calcium flux and/or chemotaxis), endotoxin (LAL); all proteins < 0.01 EU per 1 µg.

Rendered atom by atom by render.py (space-filling, wrap lighting + specular + rim, multi-scale SSAO, depth fog, true alpha) at 1200×1476: the two chemokine chains in the logo's red and orange, the receptor peptides in warm grey. Grepped the repo: no other concept uses 2K05 or this render. Slow breath/drift only, no pointer parallax (CZ).

## Story
One continuous scene, "From plasmid to proven protein": the plasmid engineered for a native N-terminus feeds the E. coli culture (cells fill the flask), material runs down the ion-exchange/RP-HPLC column into the fraction tube while the unfolded chain closes into the chemokine fold (three-stranded sheet, C-terminal helix, disulfides paired), and the verification bench draws a single sharp chromatogram peak, ticks the five QC checks and fills the vial.
