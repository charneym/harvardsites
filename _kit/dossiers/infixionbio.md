## Facts used
- Mission: "To vastly improve the lives of people living with NF1 by treating the root cause"; developing drugs for all patients regardless of their specific mutation.
- NF1: 1 in 3,000; 125K US, 250K Europe, 2.5M worldwide; 4,000+ unique mutations (NF1 Basics).
- Approach: autosomal dominance, haploinsufficiency, "NF1 Haploinsufficiency Correction Therapy" (HCT figure: 50% → 100% normal NF1 protein); NIH abstract: sbASOs against the NF1 3'UTR block miRNA binding to raise NF1 protein, normalize Ras; mutation-agnostic.
- Pipeline figure: Lead ASOs (increase NF1 protein expression in vitro; next animal models), Small Molecule (14K+ screened, lead classes; next lead optimization), New NF1 Scientific Tools (reporter assays, mAbs, ELISA, histopathology).
- 7 peer-reviewed grants from NIH, DoD and NF foundations (Investors & News). White paper: Frost M et al., J Transl Genet Genom 2022;6:403-28.
