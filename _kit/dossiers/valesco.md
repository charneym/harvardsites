Facts only, each with its source. Built 2026-11-18, batch 2026-11c.

## Firm
- Dallas-based investor making majority and minority investments in growth-oriented small and lower middle-market businesses in manufacturing, value-added distribution and business services. Source: https://valescoind.com/
- Investing in private and family-owned businesses since 1993. Source: https://valescoind.com/who-we-are/valesco/
- Types of investment: majority equity, minority equity, subordinated debt; situations: owner succession, management buyouts, recapitalization, growth financing. Source: https://valescoind.com/our-approach/investment-solutions/
- Valesco Fund III closed on $434M of commitments, above the $375M target; SBIC license Q4 2022. Source: https://valescoind.com/news/valesco-industries-announces-over-subscription-of-fund-iii-at-434-million/
- Actio: performance program for driving predictable company growth; four stages Action (strategy, Value Blueprint), Execution (team), Process (tools and systems), Performance (legacy). Source: https://valescoind.com/who-we-are/actio/
- Named to Inc.'s 2025 Founder-Friendly Investors list (also 2021 and 2022). Sources: https://valescoind.com/news/valesco-named-to-inc-s-2025-list-of-founder-friendly-investors-2/ and the site footer.
- Office: 1601 Elm Street, Ste. 4550, Dallas, Texas 75201; 214.880.8690. Source: https://valescoind.com/

## Criteria (https://valescoind.com/our-approach/investment-criteria/)
- $15–100M revenue; history of profitability with minimum cash flow of $4M
- Sectors: manufacturing, value-added distribution, business services
- Strong management, sustainable competitive advantage, reasonable growth prospects
- Majority, minority or debt investments; privately owned, preferably family and founder owned and operated; headquartered in the U.S.
- Avoided: VC/startup, real estate/financial services, turnaround/distressed
- Diversity statement as published.
