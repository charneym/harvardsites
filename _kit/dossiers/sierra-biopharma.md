## Science (site /science + 1R43NS145856-01 abstract)
- MG: chronic autoimmune neuromuscular disease, ~1.4 million people worldwide (site); ~60,000 cases in the US (abstract). Weakness in skeletal muscle, up to inability to walk, talk, eat or breathe (site); myasthenic crisis → respiratory failure needing ventilation (abstract). No cure; current therapies suppress the whole immune system.
- Antibodies attack the neuromuscular acetylcholine receptors (AChRs); 50–70% of anti-AChR Abs in MG sera bind the main immunogenic region (MIR) on the two α-subunits; anti-MIR Abs are a major, if not the main, pathogenic component.
- Sierra engineered a MIR mimic peptide of the conformation-sensitive MIR domain; shown to bind pathogenic anti-MIR Abs in EAMG (rat model) and in patient MG sera, and to clear them from EAMG rat sera in vitro.
- Therapeutic: novel Fc fusion protein = engineered MIR mimic (targeting domain) + human Fcγ1 (effector domain). Two prongs: (1) binds combining sites of anti-MIR Abs, forming immune complexes to neutralize and clear them; (2) binds B-cell receptors on anti-MIR B cells → destruction via complement-dependent cytotoxicity, Ab-dependent cytotoxicity and/or apoptosis.
- Site advantages: Effective (blocks and clears anti-MIR Abs), Targeted (only disease-causing Abs, not the wider immune system), Antigen-specific (does not downregulate the entire immune system).
- Phase I plan: produce, characterize, test in vitro and in EAMG rats for proof of concept; Phase II further preclinical → IND → clinical trial.
- Site diagram: "Engineered Targeting Domain — binds to MIR directed Abs and specific memory B-cells"; "Effector Domain (CH2, CH3) — elicits an immune response".
