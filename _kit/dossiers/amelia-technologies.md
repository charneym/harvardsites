# Amelia Technologies: dossier (home-only build, batch 2026-11e)

**Company:** Amelia Technologies LLC, a molecular biotechnology company (founded 2018) that develops and optimizes molecular diagnostics platforms measuring DNA damage and repair. Four research areas: carcinogenesis, aging and associated diseases, genome editing protocols, emerging diagnostics platforms.
