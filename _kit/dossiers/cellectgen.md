## Science (RePORTER abstracts + site)
- Periodontal disease affects ~47% of US adults over 30 (CDC, cited in abstract); ~60% unaware of their status.
- Disease course is discontinuous: periods of activity and inactivity, exacerbation and remission of tissue destruction. Challenge: identify patients with active/progressing disease, not just detect clinical features. Clinical and radiographic records reflect past history, not current activity.
- Biomarker: matrix metalloproteinase-8 (MMP-8), present in active and latent forms; hypothesis that the ratio MMP-8Active/MMP-8Total is a more meaningful measure of disease activity. Platform measures the "biological endpoint" (tissue destruction) and is agnostic to causes.
- Technology: Disease Activity Specific Monoclonal Antibody (DASMAB) against MMP-8; mAbs targeting total and active MMP-8.
- Phase II aims: (1) develop and validate the CELLIVA™ saliva collection device; (2) lateral-flow rapid tests with the MMP-8Total and MMP-8Active mAbs; (3) smartphone app; (4) validate the integrated platform with artificial saliva spiked with MMP-8 and whole saliva; (5) place the system in intended-use settings for usability feedback before design lock.
- Platform components (site): proprietary saliva collection and filtration device; lateral-flow POC test for biomarkers of bone and soft-tissue destruction; mobile app that quantifies results and tracks trends. Features: results in <15 minutes, quantitative, disposable/low cost, no shipping, room temperature.
