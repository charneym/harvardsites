# Post Oak Energy Capital — dossier (facts with sources)

- Firm: Post Oak Energy Capital, LP. Houston-based private equity firm "primarily focused on making investments in upstream oil & gas, mineral rights and the related infrastructure sectors of the energy industry"; seeks "outstanding energy entrepreneurs with well-defined business plans targeting the core producing basins in North America", and acquires and manages mineral interests in those basins "for long term yield and development appreciation" (home page, https://www.postoakenergy.com/).
- HQ: 34 S. Wynden Dr, Suite 300, Houston, Texas 77056; 713.571.9393 (site footer). Time zone: America/Chicago. Founded 2006 (press releases).
- Scale (Fund V release, 2025-06-17, https://www.postoakenergy.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/Post_Oak_Energy_Partners_V_Press_Release-FINAL.pdf): Fund V closed May 2025 with commitments of $600 million; $764 million raised for the strategy including a co-investment vehicle. Five portfolio companies committed across the Permian, Utica and Haynesville basins. Since inception, funds have invested in over 30 portfolio companies and raised approximately $3 billion across managed funds and related vehicles. Focus: lower middle market segment of the North American upstream oil and gas industry; equity for growth, development, acquisitions and recapitalizations. Quote, Frost Cochran: "We have captured exceptional opportunities in our lower middle market niche…"
- Approach (https://www.postoakenergy.com/about/): work closely with management teams to deliver strategic planning and corporate finance expertise while leveraging the operational backgrounds of the investment professionals; diversified portfolio of middle market energy investments; complementary, non-overlapping portfolio companies allowing cross-fertilization of experience; alignment by linking incentive compensation to equity value creation; minerals in core producing basins where they hold current and legacy positions, actively managed for accelerated development and current cash yield; ESG statement (environmental stewardship, safety and social responsibility).
- Post Oak Minerals: affiliated minerals platform (postoakminerals.com). Release July 2024: Post Oak Minerals acquires $475 million of Permian Basin focused minerals & royalties (press page title).
