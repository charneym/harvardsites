## Facts used (pretectdevices.com unless noted)
- Headline "A New Standard for Neonatal Critical Care"; tagline on empowering carers, safety of the smallest and most vulnerable, balancing resources and therapeutic outcomes.
- Data-driven neonatal IV monitoring; expertise in biomedical engineering, radio astronomy, neonatology and burns care; mission to protect the smallest and most vulnerable from preventable harm during IV therapy, "one of the most common yet primitively monitored interventions in the NICU".
- Continuous monitoring is the cornerstone of neonatal critical care (heart rate, oxygen saturation, temperature); IV therapy is not continuously monitored.
- 7–10 attempts, up to an hour, for neonatal cannulation; 7 in 10 neonatal IVs leak or infiltrate; 4–6% of leakages cause extravasation injury. (Not used: the "nearly 30% of neonatal deaths" sepsis figure.)
- Vedette: neonatal-specific continuous IV monitoring; detects early signs of infiltration before visible injury; developed with leading NICUs; integrates into workflows; quantitative, data-driven instead of subjective visual checks; balances safety, resource use and therapeutic goals in real time.
