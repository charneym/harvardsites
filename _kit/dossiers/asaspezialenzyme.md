## Facts used
- Founded 1991 by Dr. Arno Cordes (sole shareholder). Products: food processing, organic synthesis, analytics/biosensor technology, textile, paper, biogas, pharmaceuticals/adjuvants, small batch fill & finish (Ph. Eur. Annex 1). Cleanroom facility to GMP from 2020.
- Mixed microbial cultures for pond/lake restoration and water quality in aquariums, garden ponds and commercial aquaculture.
- Services since 1997: bioremediation, fermentation and purification processes, strain development and genetic engineering, enzyme technologies; lake restoration, soil remediation, heavy-metal soils; expression in E. coli and Pichia pastoris; partner HZI Braunschweig.
- Catalogue: 13 industrial enzymes (amylase FL/Thermo, cellulase, glucoamylase, invertase, lactase, lipase, pectinase, proteases A-01/A-06/S-02, transglutaminase); 20 special enzymes (incl. laccases A/C/F/PP, dehydrogenases, cutinases, chitinase, alcohol oxidase, glucose oxidase, carboxypeptidase B). News 01.09.2021: new fungal laccase with optimum at neutral pH.
- c-di-AMP: pharmaceutical work since 2018; GMP process via enzymatic conversion of ATP by DisA; toxicology studies; Phase I (HPV) since July 2023.
- Fill & finish: 0.3–1 mL in glass vials; APS-validated; up to 2,000 vials per week; PANHPVAX (EudraCT 2021-002584-22) with HZI; TherVac B (EudraCT 2022-000635-23).
- Team (About us): Dr. Arno Cordes (founder, sole shareholder; TU Berlin diploma, TU Braunschweig PhD); Petra Hoferichter (Head of R&D, deputy head of production, since 1996); Ana Pablos Ferreiro (Deputy Head of R&D, since 2008); Kai-Arne Klotz (CEO, since 2016); Merdan Urcanli (CEO, head of production, sales and consulting, since 2017); Valeria Korbel (since 2019); Dr. Corina Gryczka (since 2024).
