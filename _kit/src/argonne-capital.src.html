01Approach

One company, its own capital.

Argonne invests in stable, established businesses that stay relevant in a changing marketplace, and partners closely with the management team at each one.

01 · The company

We buy the business, deal by deal.

Argonne funds each investment on its own rather than from a single fund. That frees it to pursue transactions large and small and to shape each one around the owner's situation.

02 · The real estate

We read the ground it stands on.

Michael Klump co-founded a private REIT that came to own some 120 retail shopping centers. That background lets Argonne buy companies with significant real estate and set a property strategy that speeds their growth.

03 · The growth

Then we build, with no exit clock.

On sites like this one, working with each management team, Argonne has developed more than 100 new locations across its portfolio, and it holds every company for as long as the plan needs.

  • Price

    Disciplined on price

    Argonne aims for superior returns for its investors by staying disciplined on what it pays.

  • Protection

    Protect the downside

    It favours established companies that keep their relevance however the market shifts.

  • Patience

    A long-term view

    With no fund life to answer to, there is no fixed date by which a company has to be sold.
