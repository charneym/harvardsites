## Facts used
- Site (Who We Are): safest MRI contrast agents; iron-based agents for high-quality MR images of the brain, vasculature, liver and kidneys, and detection of tumors. Partners page: NSF grants 1746556 and 1951127.
- NSF 1746556 (STTR Phase I, $225,000, 2018-2019) abstract and outcomes: Fe(III) macrocyclic complexes; T1 relaxivity rivaling clinical Gd(III) at 4.7 T in vitro and in live mice; no reactive oxygen species even with ascorbate and peroxide; ancillary group steers renal vs hepatobiliary clearance; complexes patented by Dr. Morrow; collaboration with Roswell Park; first time Fe(III) complexes gave relaxivity in mice higher in certain organs than GBCAs; one complex promising as kidney-specific agent, another for the liver; Gd accumulation in brain, bone and skin; patients with impaired renal function restricted; iron cheaper and more available in the USA.
- NSF 1951127 (STTR Phase II, $899,899 incl. supplement, 2020-2023) outcomes: at least 40% of clinical MRI procedures use a GBCA; Gd foreign to the body, expensive, supply can be limited by international markets; small animal imaging at Roswell Park.
- NIH RePORTER 1R41CA301774-01 (NCI STTR Phase I, $305,253, start 2025-08-01): lead compounds anionic, renal clearance in mice; sulfonated pendants, imaging vs Gd(DOTA), 57Fe retention, MTD at 7 and 14 days.
- UB spotlight 2025-09-02: founded 2017 in a lab at the Incubator @ Baird; co-founders La Salle and Morrow; Snyder joined 2020; iron naturally regulated by the body; pre-clinical imaging and toxicity testing with Roswell Park.
- RF SUNY: company licensed UB patent applications for the first-generation agents.
- Snyder et al., Angew. Chem. Int. Ed. 2020, doi:10.1002/anie.201912273.
