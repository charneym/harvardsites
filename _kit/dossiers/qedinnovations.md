## Story
One fine-line coil cross-section: a ring of eight receive elements with preamps around the bore, with hydrogen spin arrows inside. Step 1: an RF pulse runs inward and tips the spins. Step 2: the spins precess, the signal ripples out and the elements light. Step 3: the channels route around the ring to a combiner and an image panel (a phantom) fills in.
