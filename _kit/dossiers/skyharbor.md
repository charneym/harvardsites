- Legal: "Skyharbor Capital is a trademark of Skyharbor, LLC." Disclaimer: "Skyharbor is not a registered investment advisor or broker dealer and does not provide legal, tax or investment advice." (site footer)
- Email for new opportunities: deals@skyharbor.co (https://www.skyharbor.co/team.html)
- Tagline: "Acquire. Implement. Scale." Thesis: acquires established legacy businesses and improves them through modern technology and information systems (efficiency, margin expansion, scale); brings in experienced AI implementers to embed operational AI into core workflows. Philosophy: "Most established businesses were built before AI existed. That's not a weakness — it's an opportunity." Partnership approach: AI operators "who understand that AI is a tool, not a strategy". (https://www.skyharbor.co)
- Investors (https://www.skyharbor.co/investors.html): large family offices, evergreen funds, former business owners and private investors; no traditional blind-pool fund, so no restrictions on opportunity type and no forced sale to raise the next fund; investors mentor the CEO and management.

## Criteria (https://www.skyharbor.co/criteria.html)
- Transaction: change of control acquisitions / management buyouts; retiring founder transitions; partner buyouts; generational family ownership transitions.
- Financial: small, well-established companies ($3–10M EBITDA); history of profitability (>20% EBITDA margins); long-term customer relationships (>80% renewal rate); capex light (<10% of EBITDA).
- Business model: B2B services; government services; industrial services; consumer services.
- CEO qualifications: integrity, business acumen and candor; verifiable track record; endorsement and support from company owner(s); MBA or graduate degree preferred, not required.
