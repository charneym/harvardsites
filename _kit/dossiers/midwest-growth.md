# Midwest Growth Partners (MGP): dossier
Facts only, each with source. Retrieved 2026-11-19.

## Firm
- Name: Midwest Growth Partners (MGP). Site: https://mgpfund.com/
- HQ: 1080 Jordan Creek Parkway, Suite 340N, West Des Moines, IA 50266. Omaha office: 2809 S. 160th Street, Suite 407, Omaha, NE 68130. T (515) 421-4800, F (866) 413-4806, info@mgpfund.com (https://mgpfund.com/contact). Time zone: US Central.
- Tagline: "The preeminent alternative asset manager serving overlooked geographies." (home)
- "Most private equity firms avoid the rural marketplace. We prefer it." Private equity fund manager "responsible for more than $510 million of private capital across multiple funds". Seeks "succession planning liquidity or growth capital investments in established companies in underserved rural markets which are managed by great people who share a cultural fit with our team." (https://mgpfund.com/investment-strategy)
- Seeks businesses that are profitable; have an owner who lacks an exit plan for retirement or a management team with a plan for significant growth; whose core values align with the MGP mission; whose product or service offers a defensible market position. Deal types: equity and debt investments in growth-oriented companies, management buyouts, internal recapitalizations, competitive roll-ups, distressed and turnaround situations. Value add: industry insight, senior leadership, executive mentoring, financial structuring expertise; "Our investment principals and operating partners have been CEO's, CFO's, and COO's of privately owned companies". (investment-strategy)
- Licensed RBIC and SBIC funds. Individual equity and debt investments up to $30,000,000; EBITDA $0–$12,000,000; prospective company annual revenues $4,000,000–$100,000,000; co-invests on larger opportunities; outright acquisition, majority or minority growth capital, partial or full management buyouts, internal recapitalization; also distressed/turnaround. Industries: manufacturing, food & agriculture, distribution & logistics. (https://mgpfund.com/investment-criteria). The home page also says business service companies with $10–200 million revenue (https://mgpfund.com/).
- "We believe relationships matter; finding a cultural fit with our partners is our number one priority." (home)
- "MGP re-invests in the businesses it partners with. New systems, retention and expansion of management teams, improved processes, complementary product lines... When MGP made its first acquisition in 2014, it added its first seven portfolio company employees. Today MGP has more than 2,400 dedicated and talented full and part-time employees." (https://mgpfund.com/team)
- Fund IV (2025-04-15): "Since 2013, MGP has been a trusted partner..."; "Between its four funds, MGP has invested in more than 40 private businesses"; Fund IV is MGP's first SBIC fund; plans 10–15 investments over five years; investors include commercial banks, rural electric co-ops, family offices, corporations, trade organizations and individuals. Quote by John Mickelson, Co-Founder and Managing Partner. (https://mgpfund.com/news-article/midwest-growth-partners-establishes-fund-iv--providing-opportunities-to-advance-rural-and-underserved-businesses-across-the-country)
