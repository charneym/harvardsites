About us

Thermal spray coating solutions since 1996

Extreme Coatings encapsulates complex industrial components, protecting them from wear and corrosion. We use HVOF thermal spray technology and proprietary coating formulations of hard carbides, ceramics and alloys.

Our coatings have become trade standards in the plastics and rubber industry for feedscrews, mixing rotors and tip assemblies, and in the oil and gas industry for downhole mud rotors and fracture pump plungers.

Applications

Parts that wear, coated to last longer

Most of our work is for the plastics and rubber industry and for oil and gas drilling. Every part is stripped, repaired, coated and polished to specification.

    For processing fiberglass, calcium carbonate, titanium dioxide, metals, ceramics and other highly abrasive fillers.

Our processes

The Extreme Coatings process

  1. Inspect
  2. Prepare
  3. Spray
  4. Polish
  5. Finish
  1. 01 · Inspect

    Check the part and the formula

    Every new or reconditioned part is inspected on arrival: dimensions, tool marks, coated area, materials and hardness. Then we confirm the processing details and the coating formula.

  2. 02 · Prepare

    Strip, repair and grit blast

    The part is stripped and repaired as necessary to OEM specification minus the coating thickness, then its surface is prepared by grit blasting.

  3. 03 · Spray

    Apply the coating by HVOF

    Rotation and traverse speed are combined to control heat input and the deposition rate of the chosen formula, keeping internal stresses low. The coating is sprayed and sealed as the application requires.

  4. 04 · Polish

    Finish with diamond abrasive

    The coating is polished with diamond abrasive while the polishing system is monitored, so the part meets the dimensional data needed to pass final inspection.

  5. 05 · Finish

    Inspect, measure, ship

    The part is measured again and leaves with a mirror finish, to a tolerance of +0/−.002″ (.0508 mm) on the specified coating.
