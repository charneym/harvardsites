## Company
- Medtech, Springfield, Ohio. Founded 2016 by Mubin Syed, MD. Products: the M-Y Sheath (Mubin-Y Sheath) family. — https://www.rammedicalinnovations.com/about
- Founding story (about page): "In 2016 Founder/CEO of RAM Medical Innovations INC., Mubin Syed, MD, imagined a bifurcated sheath with one end being a sheath tip and the other being a long fixed wire integrated into the sheath. The Mubin-Y Sheath (M-Y Sheath) for the prevention of amputations." Since then "12 US, 3 EU and 2 Chinese patents relating to the technology behind the M-Y Sheath have been granted."
- Home tagline: "M-Y ShEATH Making the Impossible, Possible! Leveraging integrated fixed wire technology to establish durable through and through access." Logo tagline: "Access Saves Lives".
- Products page: "The M-Y Sheath allows interventionists to conquer hostile arterial anatomy during difficult cases, previously not able to be performed with current/predicate sheath technology. The M-Y Sheath's patented fixed wire creates 2 points of control, giving interventionists the capability to not only push but also to pull a vascular sheath to a target site with a greater ease, safety, and reliability than seen previously with predicate technology." — https://www.rammedicalinnovations.com/products
- Address: 326 Braytonburne Dr., Springfield, OH 45503. Contacts: msyed@rammedicalinnovations.com, +1-937-215-0878; fdeek@rammedicalinnovations.com, +1-937-389-6979. — /contact

## Programmes (products page)
- 5F Peripheral M-Y Sheath — FDA 510(k) cleared (site says "K19369"; FDA record is K190369, decision 2019-04-17). https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/scripts/cdrh/cfdocs/cfPMN/pmn.cfm?ID=K190369
- 5F Peripheral FlexTip-88 M-Y Sheath — "FDA Traditional 510(k) Application in Progress".
- FlexTip-88 Coronary M-Y Sheath — "Concept & Development Phase"; "Coronary Patented Push-Pull Technology".
- 510(k) summary (via https://fda.innolitics.com/device/K190369): contralateral access guiding sheath with a proximal hemostatic valve; fixed wire attached to the sheath sidewall, extending beyond the distal tip; wire introduced through the ipsilateral femoral introducer, then snared and externalised through a contralateral introducer; fits through a standard 7F femoral introducer; lumen for 5F devices; peripheral vasculature (excludes coronary and neuro).
