## Science and product (home page, Fig. 4 image, RePORTER abstract)
- Target: MAPK13 in epithelial (basal-epithelial) stem cells drives airway inflammation and mucus production.
- MAPK13 inhibitor discovered by structure-based drug design; lead candidate NuP-4A (RePORTER abstract); inhaled product NuP-4B dpi (dry powder inhalation), "disease modifier": airway inflammation and mucus production "blocked, reversed, and did not recur after Rx stopped" (Fig. 4).
- Product claims (home): solution to airway inflammation and mucus production; inhaled delivery with potency, duration, selectivity, safety, low cost, broad use (including pediatrics), no needles; disease-modification and predictable benefit not found in current or pipeline therapies; advanced development finance-ready for IND approval and clinical trials in asthma.
- WU site handles discovery; the biotech handles development (safety toxicology, IND, first clinical trials). Lead compounds also for related pathways in breast, liver and brain cancers.
- RePORTER abstract (appl 11193773): licence from Washington University under a patent for composition and use of MAPK13 inhibitors; entrepreneurial resources of BioGenerator and Harrington Discovery Institute.
