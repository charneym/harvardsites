## Facts used
- "Olmeda BioSciences is a spinoff company from Children's Hospital Los Angeles and the University of Southern California that is focused on developing technology to use Aqueous Humor as a liquid biopsy for the diagnosis and therapeutic prognosis of ophthalmic diseases ... more accurate and efficient method for identifying and monitoring these conditions." (their home page)
- UM program (RePORTER 2R42CA291243-02 abstract): UM is the most common primary intraocular malignancy; nearly 50% develop metastatic disease; current risk prediction needs invasive intraocular biopsy + GEP; 21-protein biomarker panel by proximity extension assay with qPCR readout; Phase II aims: CLIA analytical validation (100 biobanked samples), CLIA-LDT protocols for AH collection/handling/transport, multi-center clinical validation with 50 prospective samples; partners USC/CHLA Ophthalmology and LuminoDx.
- RB program (RePORTER 1R42CA314557-01 abstract): RB often cannot be biopsied safely; AH tapping is clinic-feasible and contains abundant tumor-derived cfDNA; CLIA-grade ultra-low-input, capture-free WGS LDT, single tube, reports genome-wide CNAs, SNVs and indels; run at CHLA's CLIA-certified Center for Personalized Medicine.
- Funding total $1.88M = 396,161 + 55,000 + 1,028,948 + 398,880 (all NCI STTR, RePORTER).
- Garbled numbers in the RB abstract (".10%", ".1 ng", "7.10 day") were NOT used.
