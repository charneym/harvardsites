## Facts used
- About: based in Albuquerque; develops solutions for osteoarthritic joint replacement in an aging population; "Our why: We're driven to improve quality of life for patients through advanced solutions to problems in orthopedic medicine"; ZEPART conceived by Dr. Nathan Morrell with the engineering team.
- Home: "As the population ages, osteoarthritis is becoming a major driver of mobility loss"; "restoring function and improving quality of life"; AI-enabled design.
- Medical/patient overviews: stemmed device for complete replacement of the trapezium; replicates the hand's natural geometry to articulate normally with neighbouring carpal bones, no resurfacing or ball-and-sockets; prevents thumb shortening, maintains metacarpal alignment and natural resting position; alternative to trapeziectomy; 200,000+ patients a year seek surgical intervention in the U.S.; not FDA-cleared, not for sale.
- ZEPART page: "advanced, anatomically optimized thumb implant"; "distinctly differentiated design optimized for stability, strength and flexibility"; AI-enabled design, rigorous validation and clinical insight; not FDA-cleared nor CE-marked.
- Placement figure: "Designed for: 1. Normal grip strength 2. Normal range of motion 3. Eliminating symptoms".
- The need: up to 15% of adults, 24% of adults 60-92 (investors page; the-need page says 14% of adults 50-59). Trade-offs of current treatments; "A solution without compromise".
- Investors: 4,000+ ASSH members, est. 200,000+ U.S. procedures a year; ~40,000 CMC procedures a year each in France, Germany, Italy, Switzerland; CMC OA market CAGR 8.1%; 50+ population 1.9 billion → 3.2 billion by 2050 (70%); same number of thumb CMC joints as knees and hips; growth like knees/hips 20-30 years ago; design philosophy to be expanded to other joint implants; established regulatory pathway anticipated.
- Team (team page): Rick Thomson (President and CEO), Nathan T. Morrell, MD, FAOA (CMO, co-founder, co-inventor), Dimitri Madden (COO, co-founder, co-inventor), Ethan Darwin (Chief R&D Officer, co-founder, co-inventor; Palo Alto / Albuquerque), Ruba Sarris-Sawaya (Chief QRC Officer, Houston).
