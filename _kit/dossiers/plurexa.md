## Science (NIH abstract, R42DK136452)
- PKD affects 12M; tubules in kidneys, liver and brain vasculature expand into fluid-filled cysts; usually loss-of-function in PC1 or PC2; tolvaptan the only approved treatment, side effects make it unsuitable for many.
- Human organoids recapitulate PKD-specific cyst formation from tubules. NMII inhibitors increase cystogenesis in PKD organoids; genetic NMII deficiency causes kidney cysts in mice. NMII activators reduce PKD cyst growth in human organoids and mice; first-in-class, building on cardiac myosin II activators in clinical trials.
- Plurexa–UW team blueprinted a pipeline from in silico to in vivo. Objective: de-risk an NMII activator to justify IND-enabling studies, potentially with a pharma partner. Aim 1 SAR via computational-biochemical pipeline; Aim 2 cyst formation in vitro and in vivo. Expert advisory council.
