## Facts used (anoritmedical.com unless noted)
- "At Anorit Medical, we believe that anyone can be a lifesaver." 7,000,000+ cardiac arrests a year; survival depends on bystanders and Basic Life Support; innovative ventilation device for laypersons, high-quality air, simple, without direct contact.
- The Problem: 7 mill suffer a cardiac arrest each year; 8.8 % survive on average (refs 1, 2); ~350,000 out-of-hospital arrests in Europe a year (ref 1), survival 8–10 % (refs 2–3), lower with good neurological function; survival falls each minute; in Denmark survival has plateaued; "calls for a new tool in the resuscitation process".
- Our Solution: correctly providing air associated with improved survival (ref 3); mouth-to-mouth is the main option for laypersons, an intimacy barrier, complex, low success; device removes direct contact, simplifies, high-quality air, not interfering with compressions or AED shocks.
- Register interest (Google Form) and user-test sign-up (forms.gle); not CE-marked, under development.
- Team bios (condensed); formed in the BioMedical Design Novo Nordisk Foundation Fellowship Programme. Affiliations (logos on site): BioMedical Design, DTU Skylab, Innofounder, Mikrolegat; plus BII (news below).
