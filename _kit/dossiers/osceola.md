## Investment criteria (https://osceola.com/why-osceola/investment-criteria/)
- EBITDA $1M–$10M (demonstrated expertise in platforms below $5M); revenue $5M–$75M; equity up to $25M, with more via LP co-investment.
- Majority equity with potential for add-on acquisitions.
- Sectors: business, healthcare, tech-enabled and industrial services.
- Profitability, low concentration, talent, intermediaries (descriptions on the page).
