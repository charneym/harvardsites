## Facts used (expandinginnovations.com unless noted)
- X-PAC Expandable Interbody Cages replace the traditional inner implant drive screw with a patented lift-and-lock expansion mechanism designed to mitigate post-operative cage collapse and vertebral body subsidence (About; releases).
- Relocating the expansion screw to the inserter: larger, highly polished screw, less friction, more tactile feedback and lifting power; slow controlled expansion, start and stop; unidirectional locking teeth mitigate collapse; multiple inserters with incremental lifting force for different bone qualities; open architecture endplate to endplate, lateral windows for graft flow, bridging bone fusion; expands anteriorly and posteriorly, height restoration, indirect decompression (TLIF / LLIF pages).
- LLIF dimensions: widths 18 & 22mm, lengths 40–60mm, insertion height 7/8/9mm, max expanded height 13/14/15mm, max lordosis 4/8/12°.
- N-GAGE: 1-, 2-, 4-hole plates; NON-SCREW polyaxial coupler to X-PAC LLIF; coronal toggle ±6°, rotational ±7°; FDA 510(k) June 2025, the company's first fixation platform.
- Two most common complications of traditional screw-based expandable cages: post-op cage collapse and vertebral body subsidence (1 May 2025 release). 31% growth in 2024; Q1 2025 X-PAC revenue +22% on prior quarter (same release).
- 20,000+ cages implanted (Feb 2026 release). Founding team with over 65 years of combined spine-industry experience (News page). More than half a dozen new products scheduled over the next 12–18 months (Dec 2025 release).
- Team: Ron Sacher, Robert Jaramillo (Dec 2025 release); Timothy J. Scannell (Nov 2025 release: Stryker President & COO 2018–2021, non-executive chairman of Insulet).
