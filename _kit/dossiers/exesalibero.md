## The company
ExesaLibero Pharma, Inc. (NIH record: EXESALIBERO, INC., Morgantown, WV, UEI HVMJC4FQ8RM7) develops small-molecule drugs for
bone disease. Lead candidate ELP-004 (the haloanilide N-methyl-3,4-dichloropropionaniline), an ion-channel antagonist that
suppresses osteoclast maturation and bone erosion. Mission: complete preclinical toxicology and efficacy studies toward an IND.
HQ: 781 Chestnut Ridge Road, Suite 400, Morgantown, WV 26505 · 412-296-3200 (site footer).
