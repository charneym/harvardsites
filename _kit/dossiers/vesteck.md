## Company
- Clinical stage medical device company "dedicated to revolutionizing the aortic repair space through the development of an advanced endosuturing platform" (ESAR). Tagline: "Revolutionizing EVAR".
- Product: Suture-Tight™ Endovascular Suturing System. Catheter (physician designed, pre-loaded) delivers nitinol endosutures that secure EVAR/TEVAR endografts to the aorta, at the initial implant or at repair procedures. "Each endosuture working as a pair of surgical sutures." Fast, precise, repositionable (sutures can be retracted and repositioned). "With a single insertion the physician can implant up to 4 sutures."
- Goal: "eliminate graft migration and endo-leaks that lead to complex and expensive repair procedures."
- Milestones (about page): Oct 2021 R&D and manufacturing facility opens in Carlsbad, CA; Feb 2022 first pre-clinical study; Sep 2022 9th pre-clinical; Dec 2022 15th pre-clinical; Feb 2023 first in human (Latvia, EU); May 2023 7th clinical case (Canada, Latvia); Feb 2024 14th clinical case (Canada, Latvia, Australia). Jun 2023 USPTO allowed patent application (news page).
- Two-year results (release 2026-02-19): 14 first-in-human patients; no graft migration at two years; nitinol sutures remained in place; no suture fractures; no evidence of suture embolization; no evidence of endoleaks. Next: FDA 510(k) clinical trial.
- Disclaimer used on site: "Vesteck is a Clinical Stage Company. Suture-Tight™ is not available for sale in the USA or OUS."
- HQ: West Chester, PA 19380 (site footer and releases). NIH lists Malvern, PA. R&D/manufacturing: Carlsbad, CA.
- Emails: kenneth.stalker@vesteck.com (site contact); J.Rafferty@VESTECK.com (CEO, media contact on both releases). LinkedIn: linkedin.com/company/vesteck-inc.
