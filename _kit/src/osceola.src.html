01Approach

Operators who have run the business.

As former operators, Osceola's principals understand the challenges of managing, growing and selling a small business, and adapt to each one: guidance, gradual change, or hands-on direction from the day of closing.

Step 1 of 4 · Tampa

A Tampa firm, since 2014.

Michael Babb founded Osceola in Tampa in 2014. Its principals have led more than 100 corporate finance transactions, over $1 billion of capital, and served as CEO, CFO or president of more than 15 organizations.

Step 2 of 4 · Portfolio

Fourteen companies, nine states.

The current portfolio spans business, healthcare, tech-enabled and industrial services, from structural restoration in Miami to exterior facility services in Denver and biohazard remediation in Massachusetts.

Step 3 of 4 · Micro private equity

A bolt-on, built into a platform.

Osceola's Micro Private Equity strategy turns what other buyers would see as a bolt-on into a platform with its own strategy and brand. In July 2025 it recapitalized GC Restoration with its founders to form Fortify Restoration, which made its first add-on, Beach Contracting, in April 2026.

Step 4 of 4 · Add-ons

Then we build around it.

Valor Exterior Partners launched in September 2024 and is based in Cincinnati. After adding Pittsburgh, Delaware and New England roofers, it acquired Associate Roofing of Braintree, Massachusetts, in April 2026: its ninth acquisition.

For owners & managementWhy Osceola

  1. 01Experienced business operators

    Some businesses need guidance, others gradual change, others hands-on direction from day one. The team has the skill to create value in each.

  2. 02Trust and a common vision

    We look for owners who share trust and a common vision. Simply put, we sit at the same table, not across the table, from our business partners.

  3. 03A partnership model that accelerates growth

    With the roadmap agreed up front, deals close and move forward efficiently, hands-on where needed and shared with management where that works best.

  4. 04Broader solutions, better deals

    Versatility for succession plans, transitions and exits; flexibility in structure, whether an owner stays on or retires; and the vision to make a bolt-on its own platform.
