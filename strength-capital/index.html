01Approach

Many companies, one train.

Industrial Consolidation grows and diversifies a platform through acquisition. It works only with a strong management team at the front, and operations that run like clockwork.

Building partnerships

Management leads.

Strength partners with its management teams, empowering and motivating them to achieve successful outcomes for every stakeholder, and leans on them to find the next opportunities.

Industrial consolidation

Small companies, coupled.

Several small, concentrated businesses are joined into one large, diversified and resilient organization, combining Strength's transaction and financial expertise with management's operating know-how.

Operational excellence

The trains run on time.

Management teams have the autonomy to run operations their way. Strength interjects only where it is needed, and knows what it doesn't know.
