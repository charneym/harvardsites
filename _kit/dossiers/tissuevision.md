## Facts used (tissuevision.com unless noted)
- Mission "to help researchers develop life-saving therapies"; Boston based imaging solutions provider for pharma, biotech and academic organisations; experts in tissue imaging and analysis for more than 25 years; entire studies from tissue labeling through image analysis and final report; clients may send fixed tissues.
- Founded 2005 by experts from the Biological & Mechanical Engineering Departments at MIT (About).
- Three pillars (home): High Resolution 3D Imaging (Oh et al. Nature 2014, Okano et al. Neuron 2016), Scalable (tens to hundreds of samples), Automated Data Pipelines (terabytes; cloud-based analysis).
- STP tomography: serial sectioning + 2-photon multichannel imaging; simple agar embedding, no optical clearing; sections saved for secondary analysis (IHC) and mapped back; registration to the Allen Mouse Brain Reference Atlas; example dataset 1.2 micron in plane, 100 micron coronal spacing (Approach page).
- TissueCyte 1600FC: greater speed, higher sensitivity, first-in-class fully automated tissue section processing integrating with spatial biology; STP+ multi-foci imaging, organs imaged in hours; SlicePlacer module; feature list from /tissuecyte.
- Assays (What We Offer pages): Alzheimer's, Brain mapping (Allen Mouse Connectivity Project and Brain/MINDS built on TissueCyte; 10–15 business day turnaround), Biodistribution, Tumor imaging (SHG collagen), Liver fibrosis, Custom assays (tissue list).
- Publications rows link to DOIs listed on tissuevision.com/publications and the Approach page.
- NIH awards from NIH RePORTER (NIMH, NIA, NINDS, NIBIB programs since 2015).
