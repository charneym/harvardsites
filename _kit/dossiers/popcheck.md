## Company (source: https://popchecktechnologies.com/ , /about-us.php , /technology.php)
- PopCheck Technologies® (short for "Postoperative Check"), Inc.: healthcare technology company creating AI-enabled remote patient monitoring wearables with a connected digital platform that non-invasively predict the development of surgical complications such as venous clots in high-risk populations (total joint replacements).
- Home tagline: "Innovating Post-Operative Care. So you can concentrate on what matters most, your recovery." Footer: "Rest More Worry less".
- The problem (home): venous clots are one of the most common, potentially life-threatening yet preventable surgical complications. Incidence >1 million US per year; mortality "Up To 30% May Die"; cost burden over $12B per year.
- Solution: Prevent (external stimulation of calf muscles), Detect (abnormal biomarker patterns that predict venous clot formation), Alert (the healthcare provider for further management).
- Advantage: Portability (eliminating a major barrier to patient compliance); More effective prevention (muscle stimulation, a more effective strategy for generating blood flow than antiquated pneumatic compression); Real-time monitoring ("first of its kind" non-invasive monitoring platform for venous clots); Remote communication with care team.
- Products: VenaCheck (single-patient-use AI-enabled remote monitoring wearable; non-invasively provides real-time patient data); PopCheck® Digital Platform — Mobile App for patients (track recovery, report and document symptoms, schedule provider-requested studies/appointments) and platform for providers (real-time updates, signals concerns for complications, request patient-reported data, create orders for labs, imaging, consults, follow-ups).
- Disclaimer (site): "Our products are not available for sale or yet cleared for use in patients by the FDA or other regulatory bodies." (kept on the concept)
- Mission: providing patient-centric technologies that transform postoperative care by mirroring care models emphasizing safety, efficiency, equity and accessibility. Vision: to revolutionize the way postoperative care is delivered making recovery for all patients safe and stress-free.
- Founding story: Dr. Dillard co-led a study during medical training on inpatient care factors in managing venous clots in high-risk patients (spinal cord injuries); began PopCheck during the Patents 2 Products postdoctoral fellowship at the University of Memphis.
- Address: 460 S. Highland St, Memphis, TN 38111. Email: info@popchecktech.com.

## Funding / activity (latest first)
- 2026-09-20: NIH NHLBI SBIR Phase I, 1R43HL184749-01, "A Non-invasive Wearable Device for Monitoring and Predicting the Development of Venous Clots", PI Erika A. Dillard, FY2026 award $390,966, project 2026-09-20 to 2027-09-19. https://reporter.nih.gov/project-details/11316725
  - Abstract: VenaCheck uses light-based sensors (PPG) to detect changes in blood flow that signal DVT, combined with patient-reported symptoms via the PopCheck mobile app. Phase 1 tests a VenaCheck prototype at the Cleveland Clinic with 30 adults undergoing ultrasound for suspected DVT (15 confirmed, 15 without), during guided leg exercises, with app symptom tracking for 30 days and virtual check-ins at days 15 and 30. Goal: at least 80% sensitivity and specificity. Also analyses ~80,000 ultrasound scans to build two DVT prediction models (clinical-only and with VenaCheck data); success = AUROC ≥0.8 and ≥10% improvement with wearable data.
