## The company
Inventors and exclusive manufacturer of sustained acoustic medicine (sam®): wearable, multi-hour, low-intensity ultrasound
for soft-tissue healing and musculoskeletal pain. OZ Inside™ ultra-low impedance ultrasound miniaturisation. Facts from
zetroz.com home, /science-technology/, /our-team/, /sam-3-0/, /accolades/, /press-coverage-and-releases/, /company-news/.
- 48 patents (IP section of /science-technology/; the home page says 46); PCT: Europe, China, India, Brazil.
- 42 peer-reviewed publications; 20 Level 1-5 clinical studies (science page counters).
- cGMP, ISO 13485 (team page; the home page has a typo "13585"); four US manufacturing facilities.
- sam® 3.0 features: up to 8 h continuous therapy, faster charging, no buzzing, LED indicators, auto-adapting applicators,
  wear under clothing.
- Funding: NIH, NSF, DoD, NASA/NSBRI, CIMIT, Connecticut Innovations. DoD USAMMA Phase II with Univ. of Miami (Phase I:
  >50% faster soft-tissue healing vs standard of care). Insurance coverage for arthritis pain of knee, hip, shoulder, elbow.
