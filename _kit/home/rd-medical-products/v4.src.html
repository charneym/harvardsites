About us

A contract manufacturer for the patients of tomorrow

Our mission is pioneering the development of medical electrodes and hydrogels for our innovative O.E.M. partners. We have been at the same location, under the same ownership and with the same company name since 1997, and have kept the same management team and clients.

In recent years, new clients have replaced their foreign suppliers of hydrogel-based products with ours. We remain registered with the FDA as a contract medical device manufacturer.

Products

Electrodes, gels and patches for niche markets

Every product we develop is made in our own facility and can be supplied private label.

  • Electrodes

    An electrode for a niche market

    Neonatal and maternity, auditory brainstem, cardiovascular, cosmetic, intraoperative nerve monitoring, neurology, wound care, orthopedic, pharmaceutical and telemedicine.

  • Hydrogels

    Repositionable, reusable, durable

    Six types for skin surface mount, cosmetic, wound care, skin care, surface mount electrode and conductive uses, plus custom formulations.

  • Transdermal patches

    Prototype to high volume

    Transdermal gel patches for pharmaceutical, cosmetic and medical device brands, from first prototypes to high-volume production runs.

  • Cardiac mapping reference patches

    Surface Electrode Kits

    Locator patches for pulsed field ablation and patches for magnetic field and impedance mapping in the catheterization suite.

  • Hypochlorous acid

    Bulk, for private label

    Produced in its pure aqueous form at 200 ppm and stored in drums and totes, ready for your packaging.

  • Skin-adhesive wearables

    Interfaces and finished assemblies

    Peripheral adhesives that keep a device on for several days through swim and shower, and conductive adhesives that exceed AAMI EC12.

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

What products does R&D Medical Products manufacture?
We are a pain patch manufacturer, hydrogel manufacturer and industrial converting company producing transdermal patches, hypochlorous acid, hydrogel adhesives and skin-adhesive electrodes.
Where are your products manufactured?
Every product is made in the USA, at our FDA-registered facility in Southern California, as it has been since 1997.
Are you FDA-registered?
Yes. We operate an FDA-registered manufacturing facility and produce medical-grade products under regulated quality controls.
Do you offer private-label and OEM manufacturing?
Yes. We partner with healthcare brands, OEMs and contract manufacturers to produce private-label hydrogels, transdermal patches, hypochlorous acid and skin-adhesive wearables.
