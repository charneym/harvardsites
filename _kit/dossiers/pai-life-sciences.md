### SchistoShield® (https://pailifesciences.com/pipeline/schistosomiasis/ and press release)
- Defined molecular vaccine for intestinal schistosomiasis, developed by Dr. Afzal A. Siddiqui (Texas Tech University Health Sciences Center), Dr. Darrick Carter (PAI) and Dr. Steve Reed (IDRI). Based on recombinant antigen Sm-p80, "easily accessible to the host's immune system" and serving "a function which is vital for the survival of the parasite." Aim: lower adult worm and egg burdens, reduce morbidity, interrupt transmission.
- Four effects (preclinical): prophylactic adult worm reduction; resolution of egg-induced pathology in baboons and mice; therapeutic killing of established adult worms; cross-protection against urinary schistosomiasis. Low hypersensitivity risk (no prevailing Sm-p80-specific IgE in high-risk populations).
- Press release, May 31, 2022 (https://pailifesciences.com/2022/05/31/first-health-volunteer-schistosomaisis/): first healthy volunteer dosed in Phase 1, open-label, dose-escalation trial; 45 healthy adults aged 18–55; five groups of nine, three intramuscular injections; one group without adjuvant, four with. NCT05292391. Sponsored by NIAID; site Kaiser Permanente Washington Health Research Institute (VTEU); PI Lisa Jackson, MD, MPH. PAI licensed the vaccine from TTUHSC. Disease: "threatening nearly 1 billion people in 79 countries with more than 260 million people currently infected."
- Sean Gray bio: oversaw the manufacture and release testing of Sm-p80 for SchistoShield™, "the first ever human vaccine for schistosomiasis"; "currently in Phase 1b clinical trials in Africa."
- GeekWire, May 31, 2022: https://www.geekwire.com/2022/seattle-biotech-startup-backed-by-gates-foundation-launches-trial-for-snail-fever-vaccine/

### Others
- TB (https://pailifesciences.com/pipeline/tuberculosis/): reformulating two drugs as a combination therapy for multi-drug-resistant TB, delivered to the site of infection by "a convenient and low cost inhaler". Second platform: bioinformatics plus neural nets to identify antigenic epitopes.
- Onchocerciasis (https://pailifesciences.com/pipeline/onchocerciasis/): with The Onchocerciasis Vaccine for Africa (TOVA) Initiative; two lead candidates Ov-103 and Ov-RAL-2, protective in model disease, minimal risk of IgE responses in children; moving toward human testing with NIH support. River blindness endangers ~120 million people each year; >99% of cases in sub-Saharan Africa.
