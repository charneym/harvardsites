01Approach

Flexible capital, patiently held.

Eagle supports equity sponsors and management teams in change of control and growth-oriented transactions, in a variety of industries.

01 · The partners

Alongside owners and sponsors.

Eagle partners with management or other private equity investors to supply growth and change of control capital to middle market businesses, both privately-owned companies and sponsor-backed portfolio investments.

02 · The note

Subordinated debt, first.

Eagle invests primarily in subordinated debt, in amounts of $4 million to $15 million per company. Larger investments are made together with co-investors.

03 · The equity

With equity alongside.

To a lesser extent, Eagle also takes warrants, preferred stock and common stock, giving its partners a flexible capital structure for each transaction.

04 · The horizon

Four to six years.

Maturities typically range from four to six years: patient capital with a long-term investment horizon, so each company can meet its growth objectives.

Eagle invests in a broad range of businesses located in the United States, with proven management and a history of stable cash flow.
