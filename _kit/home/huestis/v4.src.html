How an air wipe works

Dry wire, less compressed air

  1. Manifold
  2. Jets
  3. Turbulence
  4. Venturi
  1. 01 · Manifold

    Air enters the body

    Compressed air is channelled through a manifold in the body and distributed to a ring of jets around the wire.

  2. 02 · Jets

    The jets meter the air

    Jet orifices restrict the flow, giving efficient drying at the lowest possible air consumption.

  3. 03 · Turbulence

    Turbulence lifts the water

    Controlling the air’s flow and direction creates turbulence that removes water from the surface of the material.

  4. 04 · Venturi

    The Venturi effect adds flow

    A Venturi effect inside the wipe draws in more air. Standard sizes are a starting point; custom openings go up to 7 in.

About us

A Bristol machine shop for more than a century

Huestis Industrial is a specialist in wire, cable and fiber optic machinery. Its reputation for dependability and rugged design comes from practical engineering and extensive in-house machining and assembly, with current CNC turning, milling, drilling and automated equipment.

The mission is plain: develop, manufacture and market reliable, cost-effective industrial and medical equipment, at a price that meets the customer’s full expectation.

  1. 1920

    J. Floyd Huestis founds Huestis Machine Corporation in Bristol as a specialty machinery supplier to local manufacturers.

  2. Early years

    Custom work for Sam Colt’s National India Rubber Co. and for Nat Herreshoff’s America’s Cup defenders Weetamoe and Enterprise.

  3. 1970s

    Asked to build radiation-therapy equipment, the start of Huestis Medical.

  4. 2006

    The Huestis companies join TeamBest®, acquired by Krishnan Suthanthiran.

Services

Parts, service and real people to talk to

Replacement parts

Smaller products such as the Air Miser™ use easily replaced components you can order and fit yourself. Ask for parts lists and technical help at sales@huestis.com.
