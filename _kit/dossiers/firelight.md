# Firelight Capital Partners (slug: firelight)

Facts only, each with its source. Retrieved 2026-11-18.

## Firm (source: https://www.firelightcapital.com/ , a single-page site)
- Private equity firm investing in branded consumer & retail concepts; helps them accelerate growth via brand management, digital transformation, operational scaling and bespoke, disciplined financing.
- Partnerships with founders and management teams: "understanding fully the fire they have lit in their category and culture."
- Investment professionals: over 70 years of collective investment and operating experience, deploying over $750 million of capital in over 20 consumer growth companies. (The GT release of 2025-08-07 says principals deployed over $1 billion in over 15 companies; the site's own figures are used.)
- Differentiated model graphic (site image): Private equity (growth capital, financial expertise, flexible capital structure, operational expertise, set of committed senior advisors, track record focused on consumer/retail, access to a suite of senior consumer/retail executives) + Branding & marketing (lifestyle/licensing, digital strategy/e-commerce, trend forecasting/consumer research, public relations, marketing/branding, endorsements/marketplace seeding).
- Office: 1700 East Las Olas Blvd, Suite 302, Fort Lauderdale, FL 33301. Time zone America/New_York.
