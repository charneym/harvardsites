## Activity (pre-screen: OK)
- **NIH NIA SBIR Phase II (fast-track), 5R44AG080951-04** — "At-home computerized assessment of normal cognitive aging and
  age-related cognitive decline in older African Americans, Hispanics, and rural non-Hispanic whites". Award notice 2026-07-10,
  $832,200; budget 2026-06-01 to 2027-05-31; project 2022-09-30 to 2027-05-31. Contact PI David L Woods (PRESIDENT);
  PI Peter Pebler (CHIEF TECHNOLOGY OFFICER). Org NEUROBEHAVIORAL SYSTEMS, INC., Berkeley CA 94704.
  https://reporter.nih.gov/project-details/11370594 . Earlier years: -03 $833,800 (2025-07-10), -02 $831,176 (2024-05-31),
  -01 $497,122 (2022-09-20). Abstract: longitudinal CCAB data from 300 healthy older (60-89) individuals in each of four groups
  (African Americans, Asian Americans, English-speaking US Hispanics, Spanish-speaking US Hispanics); 34 computerized tests and
  questionnaires; AI-enhanced text-to-speech voices; consensus automatic speech recognition; CCAB-Español; one-year intervals;
  "reduces the cost of cognitive assessment by more than 70%" (not used).
- 3R44AG062076-04S1 (2022-06-02, $250,000) supplement to "Computerized tests of cognitive decline in presymptomatic Alzheimer's
  disease": Spanish CCAB, norms from 256 older Spanish-speaking Hispanics tested telemedically at home; only 5.5% of US
  neuropsychologists fluent enough in Spanish. https://reporter.nih.gov/project-details/10408563
- ClinicalTrials.gov NCT04800588, "Computerized Tests of Cognitive Decline in Presymptomatic Alzheimer's Disease", sponsor NBS,
  RECRUITING, last update 2026-11-13. Contacts: Kathleen Hall, PhD (clinical_trial_coordinator@neurobs.com, 510-214-3176);
  David Woods (drdlwoods@neurobs.com, 510-527-9231).
- Not pathogens/toxins/biodefence: neuroscience software and cognitive testing.

## Publication
- Woods D, Pebler P, Johnson DK, Herron T, Hall K, Blank M, Geraci K, Williams G, Chok J, Lwi S, Curran B, Schendel K, Spinelli M,
  ... Baldo J. "The California Cognitive Assessment Battery (CCAB)". Front. Hum. Neurosci. 17 (published 2024-01-11).
  https://doi.org/10.3389/fnhum.2023.1305529 . Affiliations: NeuroBehavioral Systems Inc., Berkeley; Dept of Neurology, UC Davis;
  VA Northern California Health Care System, Martinez. Corresponding author email drdlwoods@neurobs.com.
  15 non-verbal and 17 verbal subtests normed for telemedical assessment; calibrated tablets over cellular or Wi-Fi, in the lab or
  at home; spoken instructions through headphones with naturalistic text-to-speech voices; verbal responses scored in real time
  and transcribed with consensus ASR combining seven commercial ASR engines (more accurate than any single engine); "the
  Examiner" web app; normative data from 415 older participants.

## Company facts (neurobs.com)
- About: founded in 2000 "with a single focus: to provide researchers with precise and powerful software tools to create innovative
  behavioral and physiological experiments. The result was Presentation®." SBIR-funded until 2004 (free), commercial since 2004.
  "115719 registrations and 264428 downloads" (live counters, 2026-11-21). Mission quote: "NBS vows to continually add new
  capabilities to its software products so that a scientist's imagination will not be held back by software limitations."
- Home: Presentation® "stimulus delivery and experiment control program for neuroscience that runs on any Windows PC and delivers
  auditory, visual and multimodal stimuli with sub-millisecond temporal precision"; fMRI, ERP, MEG, psychophysics, eye movements,
  single neuron recording, reaction time measures; "the world's most popular experiment control software"; tagline "Precise,
  Powerful Stimulus Delivery"; current version 25.1. Presentation Mobile (iPad, iPhone, Android; hosted experiments; encrypted data
  hosting). LabStreamer® ("offloads the difficult task of validating the performance of laboratory computers and equipment").
  Psych Lab 101 (30+ experiments, 11 adjustable visual illusion demos). Experiment Packs. Commercial: clinical trials, medical
  testing, cognitive training and assessment, neuromarketing; contact consultants@neurobs.com.
- Contact page: 2093 Addison Street, Suite 215, Berkeley, CA 94704-1151; 510-527-9231 (no phone tech support); support via
  forums; sales contact Nate Whitehill-Nigl; small business (under 100 employees). Jobs: "small and growing company located in
  downtown Berkeley".
