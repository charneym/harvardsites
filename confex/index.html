The process

How Continuous Rotary Extrusion works

  1. Wheel
  2. Shoe
  3. Die
  4. Product
  1. 01 · Wheel

    A rotating grooved wheel

    The heart of CRE is a grooved steel wheel. About a quarter of it is enclosed by fixed sealing segments, forming the extrusion chamber, and an abutment reaches into the chamber to block the groove.

  2. 02 · Shoe

    Held by the extrusion shoe

    The sealing segments sit in an extrusion shoe, held in the machine by high-strength clamps engineered to withstand the pressures that extrusion generates.

  3. 03 · Die

    Friction, heat and the die

    Copper or aluminium feedstock, usually rod, enters the groove. The turning wheel carries it to the abutment, where friction raises heat and pressure until the metal turns plastic and is extruded through the die.

  4. 04 · Product

    Continuous copper and aluminium

    Invented by the UK Atomic Energy Authority in 1971, CRE has been developed over 50 years for continuous production of both copper and aluminium extrusions.

Everything a CRE line needs to keep running

From stocked wear parts to new tooling designs, we support new and existing clients with proven expertise.

    We hold stock of extrusion wheels and wear parts such as feed plates and abutments for phased delivery or call-off, so you keep less on site.
