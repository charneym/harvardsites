## Facts used
- Home/The Solution: COPA = Computerized Oral Prescription Administration; dual biometrics (fingerprint and dentition) confirm the Authenticated Intended User (AIU™) before each dose; integrated cellular for real-time usage data and physician-driven dosage changes; cloud-based HIPAA-compliant DBMS; dashboards / event tracking; NCPDP Rx formats; setup about one minute; cellular not required for dose administration; "Delivering the RIGHT Amount of Medication, at the RIGHT Time, and Only to the Right Person"; under FDA review, not available for sale.
- Key-parts figure: dentition sensor, fingerprint scanner, security cap + smart cap, drug label window, standard amber bottle.
- The Problem: 50% fail to take medications as directed; non-adherence: >125,000 untimely deaths a year (U.S.).
- About: founded in Dallas in 2016 by Tommy Rouse and Susan Owen after Rouse's spinal surgeries; "Find a Better Way".
- Press: 2025-07-07 De Novo accepted for review (DHT3C; methadone HCl, oxycodone HCl, fluoxetine HCl oral solutions); 2025-06-02 NIH grant for clinical study program (take-home methadone, retention); 2025-01-16 Innovations in Clinical Neuroscience publication; 2023-06-20 two CPDD posters; 2022-12-07 Phase II of Fast-Track SBIR, just over $2.0M total (R44DA057185).
