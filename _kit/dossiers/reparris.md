## Science (NIH abstract)
- Novel class of SRC kinase recruitment inhibitors (SRis) that prevent cytokine receptor recruitment and activation of SRC kinases; bind an allosteric site, not the catalytic ATP-binding site, which "mitigates potential side effects". First-in-class; no SRC-targeting drug for OA exists.
- Preclinical: prevents protease activity, cell migration and systemic inflammation in joint cells and other tissues; strong anti-arthritic and analgesic effect in collagen-induced arthritis in mice.
- Aims: Phase I — SRi-15932 and backup SRi-15932N in a rat joint-injury OA model. Phase II — development plan + pre-IND meeting with FDA; canine oral PK (plasma and joint) + preliminary safety; preliminary efficacy in client-owned aged companion dogs with naturally occurring multi-joint OA and MSK pain. To be commercialized as a first-in-class oral therapeutic.
- Founders' published research: Shkhyan R … Evseenko D, *Sci Transl Med* 2023, PMID 36947594 (gp130 Y814 as cellular stress sensor; OA). Lee Y … Shkhyan R, Evseenko D, *Sci Rep* 2024, PMID 39732741. Note: the 2023 paper's compound R805 is linked in its COI statement to CarthroniX, so the site does not attribute R805 to Reparris.
