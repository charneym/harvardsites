# Eagle Private Capital (slug: eagle-private)

Facts only, each with its source. Retrieved 2026-11-19.

## Firm
- Fund manager of the Eagle family of funds; supplies growth and change of control capital to middle market businesses. Source: https://www.eagleprivatecapital.com/ and /firm-overview.html
- "With over $1 billion under management, Eagle Private Capital makes subordinated debt and private equity investments of between $4 and $15 million." Source: same.
- Partners with management or other private equity investors; principals have backgrounds in commercial and investment banking and public accounting and have worked with hundreds of middle market businesses as senior lenders, equity investors and strategic advisors. Source: /firm-overview.html, /team.html
- Patient capital with a long-term investment horizon for privately-owned companies and sponsor-backed portfolio investments. Source: home page.
- Stated on home page: 68 active portfolio companies, 87 exits to date, 155 total platform investments. (The portfolio page lists 67 active and 86 distinct exited names.)
- Partnering principles (home): serve as financial and strategic resource; provide patient capital; build long-term relationships. Value via flexible capital structures, relevant industry and transaction experience, and assistance with the evaluation of strategic opportunities.
- HQ: 8001 Forsyth Blvd., Suite 1000, St. Louis, MO 63105. Phone 314.754.1400. No email published on the site. Time zone: America/Chicago. Source: /contact.html

## Investment characteristics (source: /firm-overview.html)
- Generalists investing in a broad range of businesses located in the United States.
- Investment size $4M–$15M, larger amounts involving co-investors.
- Primarily subordinated debt and, to a lesser extent, warrants, preferred stock and common stock.
- Maturities typically four to six years.
- Portfolio companies: annual revenues $10M to over $100M; EBITDA in excess of $2M; proven management with long-term performance-based incentives; histories of stable cash flow and profitability.
