## Technology (aeonimaging.com Technology, LSDC, DLO, DLM, NIR Projector, Design Services pages)
- Rolling-shutter CMOS row exposure used as a flexible electronic confocal aperture, synchronised with an illumination
  line; no de-scanning, no pinhole or slit; aperture width adjustable in real time; offsets at pixel level and ~1 µs.
- LSDC: low-cost confocal line-scanning laser ophthalmoscope; multiply scattered light mode (aperture offset) shows
  drusen and edema. Not FDA approved.
- DLO: DLP projector as the source; DLO-LV, DLO-AO, DLO-AF variants; multiply scattered light. Not FDA approved.
- NIR Projector Module: 860 nm laser + TI DLP LightCrafter EVM; up to 80 mW, >2,000:1, 4 kHz patterns.
- DLM: Essentials Kit microscope (bright-field, dark-field, fluorescence, differential contrast).
- Design services: >15 years of confocal imaging systems, 10 with TI DLP kits (Zemax, SolidWorks, Eagle, CUDA, MATLAB).
- Patents: issued in US, Canada, Europe, Australia, Japan, Mexico (References page). US 7,331,669; 7,831,106;
  8,237,835; 8,488,895; 11,375,891 B1; 12,433,480 B2. Available for licensing / sub-licensing.
