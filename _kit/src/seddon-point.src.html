01Approach

An easy handover, done well.

We love working with owners who are collaborative and committed to a smooth transition. The aim is an easy, successful change of ownership for the owner and for every employee.

01 · The owner

Businesses built by the people who run them.

Seddon Point looks for owner-operator and management-run companies in the United States, often where the owner is looking toward retirement: an established brand with a reputation for quality.

02 · The deal

A structure that fits the owner.

We are open to a range of industries and flexible on deal structure, whether that means buying the whole company or a part of it, so the owner can step back on terms that suit them.

03 · The next chapter

Room to grow, for the people in it.

After the close comes engaged ownership: developing employees, improving operations and building for the long term, with careers, apprenticeships and the local community in mind.

  • Ownership

    Engaged, not absent

    Seddon Point acquires and supports high-quality companies through hands-on, engaged ownership.

  • People

    Employees come first

    Retaining and developing employees is central to the plan, with career paths and learning built in.

  • Operations

    Better every year

    Operational improvement and long-term growth, aimed at lasting value for employees, customers and communities.

        03Portfolio

        Precision, made in Georgia.

        Seddon Point's portfolio company carries forward a family business built over more than three decades south of Atlanta.

        Metal fabrication · Acquired 2025

        Metro Metal Works

        A precision sheet metal and welding company in McDonough, Georgia. It started in 1989 as a welding shop specialising in material handling and handrails, and has since grown into precision sheet metal with laser cutting and CNC fabrication, making high-tolerance, custom-engineered metal products for industries across the Southeast. The business grew under Harry and Barbara Phillips and their son, Justin Phillips.

        04Team

        Led by an operator.

        Seddon Point is led by its founder, who brings a background in technology, analytics and strategy to the companies it owns.

        Leadership

        • Biography

          Paul Callahan founded Seddon Point Capital and leads it as Managing Partner. He brings a diverse skill set in technology, analytics and strategy, built over a long career in consulting and advisory services that included senior operational leadership roles across Bain & Company, Accenture, IBM and Arden Lane Advisors.

          He set out to acquire a Georgia-based manufacturing business where he could drive employee retention, skill development, operational excellence and long-term growth, and in 2025 Seddon Point acquired Metro Metal Works in McDonough.
