## Concept
- Hero: the firm's own Milky Way panorama (header5.jpg from their site), graded toward the logo navy, with a live starfield continuing the sky above it that turns slowly about the pole (sidereal time); rare meteor. Footer echoes it.
- Story: engraved night elevation of a family-owned plant; the four approach elements ink layers (guiding star from their mark, dimensioned plan with dashed add-ons, add-ons inked and joined by a yard road, lit windows, people and a truck at the dock).
- Palette: logo navy #12244e and taupe #7e7260 sampled from the live logo PNG; champagne #cbb994 as a lighter accent of the taupe.
