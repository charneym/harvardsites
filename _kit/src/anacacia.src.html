Approach

Capital, and a seat beside you.

Anacacia supports leading SMEs through critical stages in their lifecycle. Experienced Non-Executive Directors back the management team that continues to run the business day to day.

The partnership

Investing alongside you.

Considering a staged retirement, a succession plan or a confidential sale? Anacacia can invest alongside owners and management, and sponsor the transaction when a leading manager is ready to become an owner.

Board level

Experience at the table.

Our Non-Executive Directors support management with strategic planning, executive hiring and governance, backed by our investment team and a Business Advisory Council of former executives from Australia's leading companies.

Growth and M&A

Growth, branch by branch.

We help portfolio companies execute their inorganic strategy: origination, due diligence, risk management, negotiation, structuring and financing of acquisitions, with group discounts and best-practice support behind the scenes.

The next stage

A full sale, or an IPO.

Owners can step away from the business and do more of what they love, while it is prepared for a longer-term sale or listing. Some companies have gone on to be backed by our public equities funds as well.

  • Private equity

    Partners for leading SMEs

    Anacacia's private equity funds invest in leading SMEs alongside founders, families and management teams, with capital to fund growth and shareholder sell-downs.

  • Listed equities

    From private to public

    The Wattle Fund and the Global Fund invest in listed companies, giving Anacacia the capacity to back SMEs on the journey from private to public.

  • Networks

    Business Advisory Council

    An exclusive advisory group of former executives from Australia's leading companies, plus regular meet-ups for the executives across our portfolio.

Investment criteria

What we look for.

We look for leading SMEs with strong cash flows, outstanding management and real growth opportunities, where our advice and capital can build long-term shareholder value.

  • $5M+Existing earnings: we look for strong cash flows
  • $25M+Revenue, typically, at the time we invest
  • AU & NZLocal management teams based in Australia or New Zealand
