## The company
Treysta Medical Technologies Inc., Gainesville, Florida. Developing TreystaCare, "a first-in-class topical drug" that
"works to guide the healing process, encouraging the deposition of smoother, healthier, more natural looking skin";
"advancing a new class of therapies that reduce post-surgical scarring". "The industry has historically approached the
problem of scarring from a remediative perspective. Treysta is thinking preventively." Mission: "We're on a mission to
safeguard quality of life." Sources: treystamedicaltechnologies.com (home), /investors (Cesarean Delivery), /blank (Serious Burn), /team, /contact-8.

Address (NSF award record, awardee): 747 SW 2nd Ave Ste 345, Gainesville, FL 32601-6283. Performance site: 3733 NW 53rd Lane, Gainesville, FL.

## Programmes
- TreystaCare – Acute™: "an ointment that interrupts the scarring cascade"; used "for the first week of recovery following cesarean delivery, seamlessly integrating into existing dressing change and wound care protocols". CD: "just under 1.2 million procedures each year", the most common US surgery. "Half of patients report being dissatisfied with the appearance of their cesarean section scar."
- TreystaCare™ System (burns): "a treatment technology for the care of serious second and third degree burns, currently under development"; used "in emergency settings and across the first two to four weeks of treatment"; reduces "scarring, fibrosis, and contracture".
- NSF SBIR Phase I award 2528240, "Development of a Burn Care Therapy to Prevent Fibrotic Pathologies", $304,984, 2025-07-30, expires 2027-06-30, PI Marcelo Buzzi (mbuzzi@treystamedicaltechnologies.com). Abstract: three-part sequence of topical anti-scarring therapeutics integrated with a purpose-built dressing; infection prevention, biocompatibility, dressing functionality; capstone porcine study. https://www.nsf.gov/awardsearch/showAward?AWD_ID=2528240
- Other indications shown on the home page with cited stats (mastectomy, brachioplasty, craniotomy, perineal tearing).
