“Aliter”, the Latin word for “a different way”, is a founding principle: our leaders are seasoned entrepreneurs who have walked the journey in your shoes many times before.

Aliter was founded in 2017 by a group of support services entrepreneurs and investors: Billy Allan, Greig Brown, Andy Galloway and Andrew Busby. We focus on the long-term creation of value and the alignment of every stakeholder’s interests, rather than financial engineering.

Approach

At your side, by your side.

The way we work with owner managers and investors is distinctly different. We are action-oriented, straight-talking partners with a strong belief in sharing risk and reward.

Buy and build, in practice

Orwins, the legal services group we have built with its management since 2024.

The platform · October 2024

Start with a business worth building on.

The journey began with a significant investment in BBS Law in Manchester: a resilient business model, a range of complementary services and long-standing client relationships.

First add-on · August 2025

Add strength, one firm at a time.

North London’s Carter Bond joined next, as Aliter supported both organic growth plans and a pipeline of target acquisitions to widen the firm’s regional coverage.

One brand · May 2026

A new name with room to grow.

The group launched its new identity, Orwins, alongside its investment in Clarkslegal, the Reading commercial law firm, serving clients across the Thames Valley.

National footprint · 2026

From one city to five offices.

Newcastle’s Clarke Mairs joined in July and Yorkshire’s Milners in August. Orwins now has almost 250 people working from Manchester, London, Reading, Newcastle upon Tyne and Leeds.

    Partnership promotes growth

    When we invest, we commit to working closely with the management team. That ethos aligns every stakeholder, as our shared approach to risk and reward shows.

  • Investment

    Investment creates value

    We don’t believe in financing with significant debt, so businesses can keep investing. And we deliberately back a handful of platforms at a time, so we really get to know them.

    We are straight talkers. We understand the stresses and strains of running a business, and we know how to help owner managers achieve life-changing outcomes.
