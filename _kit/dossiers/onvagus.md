## Science (RePORTER abstract)
- CAC: substantial weight loss / skeletal muscle atrophy; est. 0.5–1% of global population incl. 1.3 million Americans; no effective clinical treatment.
- VNS FDA-approved for other applications but never applied to CAC; vagal innervation of digestive tract, brown adipose, kidneys, liver, pancreas.
- OnVagus developed a non-invasive transcutaneous VNS device; established preclinical safety and vagal neuromodulation in swine and voluntary human subjects.
- Fast-Track with clinicians at UT MD Anderson Cancer Center: Phase 1 — improved device in porcine model + 10 patients under clinical supervision; Phase 2 — randomized pilot trials on 120 patients with metastatic pancreatic cancer, combined with standard-of-care, vs placebo device (no therapeutic output). Basis for de novo application with CDRH.
- Cell 2025 (Garrett A et al., "Vagal blockade of the brain-liver axis deters cancer-associated cachexia", Cell 188(21):6044-6063, doi:10.1016/j.cell.2025.07.016, PMID 40780194): cancer-induced systemic inflammation alters vagal tone in CAC mouse models; disrupts brain-liver vagal axis; depletion of HNF4α reprograms hepatic protein metabolism; targeting right cervical vagus (surgical, chemical, electrical or non-invasive transcutaneous) attenuated CAC; combined with chemotherapy improved outcomes in mice. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40780194/ ; release: https://terasaki.org/institute/news/pr/terasaki-institute-researchers-reveal-vagus-nerve-modulation-as-key-to-combating-cancer-associated-cachexia-featured-in-cell.html
