Products

From the controller to the encoder

Six product families for OEMs, retrofitters and end-users of coordinate measuring machines, CNC and PC-based measurement systems.

  • Automatic CMM solutions

    Complete hardware for the manufacture and retrofit of automatic coordinate measuring machines, compatible with many popular CMM software packages.

    Includes CMM system pack, 4 axis PCI Express CMM controller, 030 servo amplifier, 031 touchscreen joystick, 035 scanning probe interface, CMM I++ Server

  • Manual CMM solutions

    Complete hardware for the manufacture and retrofit of manual coordinate measuring machines.

    Includes encoder interface card and USB solutions, CMM retrofit cables, FC1 feature connector kit, Deva CMMCtrl DRO

  • Motion control

    Multi-axis servo and stepper controllers in PCI Express format, with a wealth of synchronisation and triggering facilities.

    Includes 4+ axis PCI Express servo controller, 4 axis PCI Express stepper controller, 3/4 axis PCI controller, 020 USB joystick interface

  • Encoder interfaces

    Position measurement in PCI, PCI Express and USB formats, for incremental and absolute SSI encoders: position, velocity and acceleration.

    Includes the 001 range (PCI and PCI Express) and the 037 range (USB, with or without power supply)

  • Optical

    Image acquisition and lighting for non-contact measurement and vision inspection.

    Includes 036 CMM vision probe (Autojoint or shank mounted), 028 3 megapixel camera module, 022 USB lighting controller

  • Signal conversion and generation

    Accessories that convert encoder signals from one standard to another, for most types and brands of encoders and linear scales.

    Includes 018 sinewave interpolator, 023 reader head pre-amp for Brown & Sharpe, 011 quadrature signal generator

Inside a CMM retrofit

How a point gets measured

  1. Move
  2. Count
  3. Touch
  4. Report
  1. 01 Move

    The controller moves the axes

    A Deva PCI Express servo motion controller drives the bridge, the carriage and the ram under PC control, with synchronisation and triggering built in.

  2. 02 Count

    Every scale, counted

    Encoder interfaces read the linear scale on each axis for position, velocity and acceleration; interpolators turn 11 µA and 1 Vpp sine signals into clean RS422 quadrature.

  3. 03 Touch

    One touch, one point

    The stylus meets the part, the probe triggers, and the position on every axis is captured at that instant. DevaCmmCtrl78 adds Renishaw SP600 scanning support.

  4. 04 Report

    Into the software you already run

    Point after point passes to your CMM software, natively as in PolyWorks|Inspector or through the CMM I++ Server, and the part takes shape.

CMM software compatibility

Works with the software you already run

End users and retrofitters can depend on an extensive range of third-party CMM software, compatible with Deva’s CMM solutions.

Since April 2026 Deva CMM controllers integrate natively with PolyWorks|Inspector 2026, with direct touch-probe control inside the software.
