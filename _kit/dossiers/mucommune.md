## Science (NIH abstracts + https://www.mucommune.com/technology-and-platform)
- Muco-trapping mAb platform licensed from UNC: "harnesses Fc-mucin interactions to immobilize viruses, bacteria and sperm in mucus"; plus a "highly multivalent Ig platform". "validated against a variety of pathogens across all major mucosal secretions, with proof-of-concept in both small and large animal models."
- Anti-sperm antibody (ASA) mechanism: agglutinates and traps sperm in mucus, preventing sperm from reaching the egg (1R44HD121291-01 abstract).
- LamH10: next-generation ASA targeting the same validated sperm epitope as the parent HC4 IgG, with 10 Fab domains per molecule, "picomolar agglutinating potencies under physiological conditions (e.g. at 37oC), exceeding the activity of the parent HC4 IgG by orders of magnitude"; "reduce progressively motile sperm by 99.9% in the sheep vagina in two minutes at a dose of just 33 ug per sheep" (same abstract).
- Capsule-IVR: "proprietary capsule-IVR system that stably released mAbs and provide virtually complete sperm agglutination in sheep at challenge for over 20 days" (same abstract).
- Earlier clinical work: Mucommune "was part of a consortium that advanced the very first ASA into exploratory clinical studies, where we showed a high dose of ASA (HC4 IgG) given immediately before intercourse was highly effective at eliminating progressively motile sperm in the endocervical canal" (abstract); first-ever clinical trial for a contraceptive mAb, ClinicalTrials.gov NCT04731818 (news, 3/11/2021).
- MM009: lactic-acid-releasing reservoir intravaginal ring for bacterial vaginosis; built on the observation that women with Lactobacillus crispatus-dominant microbiome resist BV, releasing lactic acid over at least 30 days (1R44AI192170-01 abstract).
- MM010 (Polyon): high-molecular-weight free PEG that saturates pre-existing anti-PEG antibodies, restoring circulation of PEGylated drugs and preventing hypersensitivity; validated in rodent and swine models (1R43CA310433-01 abstract; Technology page).
- In vivo cell engineering: in-licensed lentiviral platform using "the Nipah virus fusion protein with its native tropism ablated and retargeted via anti-CD3 scFv", formulated with transduction enhancers, transducing non-activated circulating T cells in vivo without lymphodepletion (1R44CA310402-01 abstract; Technology page). SLE program: dual CD19/CD138 CAR-T with a kill switch (1R44AI197810-01 abstract).
- MM004: bispecific inhaled "muco-trapping" mAb for RSV and MPV (5R44AI157661-03 abstract).
