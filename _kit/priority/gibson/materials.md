## The two points the client wants the site to convey above all
1. **Two commercially validated targets.** LMP400 (oral and IV), LMP744 (IV) and the next-generation azaindenoisoquinolines ("Azas") hit TOPO 1 (topoisomerase I) AND reduce overexpression of cMYC — the largest human oncogene, which drives approximately 80% of all human cancers.
2. **Proof of value from premier institutions.** Institutions that see a great many candidate cancer drugs have chosen to deploy their own resources on Gibson's drugs: NIH (NINDS), Mayo Clinic, Duke's Preston Robert Tisch Brain Tumor Center (adults) and Children's National (pediatric).
