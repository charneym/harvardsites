## Facts used
- From the site: 1 to 3 year diagnosis today; ~40% of severe psychiatric patients initially misdiagnosed, SZ ~50%, BD ~70%; stable trait blood RNA gene biomarkers; results to the ordering physician within "Days, NOT Years(TM)"; pivotal trial of 90 subjects at three time points, sensitivity and selectivity over 95%; UC Irvine and University of Iowa work, two NIMH SBIR grants; patents Aug 2023 and a second (Oct 2025); 2.2 million potential SZ/BD patients a year; early-detection protocol being designed (1-2 years before first psychotic episode, RAISE/NIMH); seeking pharma partners for companion diagnostics; commercialisation through a licensed CLIA lab partner; raising $5m seed funding.
- From the 28 Apr 2026 release (via CLP): the test aids differentiation of SZ and bipolar I disorder in symptomatic patients, gives an objective probability score from a venous blood sample, used alongside clinical assessment.
- Gene count and accuracy differ between sources (site: 20 genes, 92%/93%; 2026 coverage: 18 genes, 98.3% after reanalysis), so the concept gives neither figure.
- Team (Team page): Terry W. Osborn, Marquis P. Vawter (co-founder, CSO, UC Irvine), Richard G. Morris (VP Business Development), Michael R. Levin (CFO and Partner), Martin L. Osborn (CIO/CISO). Summaries shortened, nothing added.
