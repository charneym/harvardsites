## Funding (NIH RePORTER; amounts are first-year award amounts)
- 2R44OD037625-02, NIH Office of the Director, SBIR Phase II, $1,200,472, notice 2026-07-28, PI Andrew D. Alegria: "A computer-vision guided automated microinjection system with increased robustness, throughput, and generalizability to diverse species." Aims: automatic pipette change on clogging + multi-dish/multiplexed experiments; >500 injection experiments with Drosophila researchers at Baylor College of Medicine; software for users to train new ML models for new species/structures. https://reporter.nih.gov/project-details/11409842
- 1R42NS147759-01, NINDS, STTR fast track, $479,794, notice 2026-09-11, contact PI Daniel A. Surinach (PIs incl. Suhasa Kodandaramaiah, Aaron Kerlin): Bionic Imaging Platform (BIP), a robotic, animal-actuated optical system that follows a mouse's head through a 2D arena so benchtop imaging (mesoscopic 2P) can be used during free behavior. https://reporter.nih.gov/project-details/11316597
- 5R41OD039877-02 / 1R41OD039877-01 (2025, $416,925; 2026, $386,174), STTR, PI Kodandaramaiah with Surinach: mini-mScope with high-sensitivity CMOS, through-skull imaging; 5 behavioral assays. "Weighing 3.8g, the mini-mScope has been distributed to several laboratories globally." https://reporter.nih.gov/project-details/11405060
- 1R43OD037625-01A1, SBIR Phase I, $294,543, 2024-08-23, PI Alegria: Autoinjector for fruit fly embryos; "performing at a rate six times faster than humans"; aims on clustered embryo detection and pipette clogging. https://reporter.nih.gov/project-details/11013464

## Products (site pages)
- Autoinjector (/autoinjector): automated microinjection in a wide range of species; high-throughput transgenesis/mutagenesis; minimize training and hands-on time; "hundreds of embryos/hour"; "Elimination of uninjected embryos"; computer vision + ML, trainable for new organisms. Species shown: D. melanogaster, zebrafish (yolk, blastomere), medaka, black soldier fly, aphids, mosquito (under development), D. pseudoobscura.
- Injecta micropipettes (/micropipettes): pre-pulled, pre-beveled, individually QC'd; packs of 40; SKUs Fly-Mg, Fly-Ds, Fish-Zf, Fish-As, Bug-Ap, Bug-Dl, custom.
- mini-mScope (/minimscope): whole dorsal cortex mesoscale imaging; 8 × 10 mm FOV; 17–35 μm; high-sensitivity CMOS; < 4 g; magnetic clamping.
- SeeShell (/seeshell): 3D-printed implants; up to 8 × 10 mm; PET window; 1P/2P; stable > 1 year; head-fixed and freely behaving versions.
- Titanium headplates (/titanium-headplates): teardrop with handle, rectangular frame, teardrop with triangular mount, ring; custom.
- MagKine docking (/magkinedockingsystem): magnetic + kinematic docking, micrometer-scale repeatability.
- Robo-Comm (/robocomm): computer-vision guided robotic commutator.
