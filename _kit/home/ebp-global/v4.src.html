Services

Welded, tested and machined in-house

Two ways to work with us: as an approved special process provider for EB, TIG and NDT, quoted within a few days of your file, or as a make complete partner for the whole product.

  • Welding · EBW and TIG

    Electron beam and TIG welding

    Deep, narrow welds close to parent metal strength, made in vacuum with no filler, at 1–2 metres a minute and under 5% of the heat input of a comparable TIG weld.

    Details
  • Testing · X-ray and NDT

    X-ray and non-destructive testing

    Two X-ray bays with 225 kV systems and CR digital film, fluorescent dye penetrant, helium and air-in-water leak testing, macro sections and hardness.

    Details
  • Machining · 5 and 8 axis

    Hard metal CNC machining

    Regular production from Alloy 718, 625, C263, Ti 6Al-4V, Hastelloy and Haynes on ten machines, machined in the hard condition with an integrated shop-floor CMM.

Make complete

From drawing to finished assembly

  1. Design
  2. Machine
  3. Weld and test
  4. Deliver
  1. 01 Design

    Cost taken out at the drawing

    EBP reviews materials, features and tolerances to remove cost drivers without compromising integrity, while the design authority keeps control. One Alloy 718 part came out 50% cheaper.

  2. 02 Machine

    Machined in the hard condition

    Ten 5 and 8 axis machines run Alloy 718, 625, C263, Ti 6Al-4V, Hastelloy and Haynes as regular production, checked on an integrated shop-floor CMM.

  3. 03 Weld and test

    Welded in vacuum, then X-rayed

    The beam focuses 4 kW into a 0.5 mm spot and melts a deep, narrow keyhole, with no filler. Welds are then checked in two 225 kV X-ray bays on CR digital film, by macro section and hardness.

  4. 04 Deliver

    One order, one point of contact

    Materials, proprietary parts, machining, treatments, EB welding, post-weld machining, assembly and inspection, delivered as a finished, tested product.

Applications

Where the weld has to be perfect

Minimal distortion, dissimilar materials and exceptional repeatability make EB welding the answer for parts that conventional welding would ruin.

  • Aerospace

    Critical rotating parts

    Combustion chambers, blades and nozzle guide vanes, duct work, gear and shaft assemblies and bearing assemblies in dissimilar materials, plus component salvage and repair.

  • Instrumentation and medical

    Sealed and precise

    Pressure-sensitive devices, bellows and diaphragms, glass-to-metal feedthroughs, fabricated titanium sensor carriers, precision enclosures and replacement hip and elbow joints.

  • Oil and gas subsea

    Built for pressure

    Deep-section welds up to 20 mm in titanium and Inco 718, enclosures with heat-sensitive components inside, and “unmachinable” products fabricated from precision-machined parts.
