01Approach

Shoulder to shoulder with management.

Most buyers write a 100-day plan before they understand a business. Shorehill spends its first hundred days learning the people, the culture and the drivers of value, then builds the plan with management.

Human capital

It starts with people.

Investing in the people and the organization: coaching and support for the team, compensation aligned with strategy, value-added outside directors, and stronger safety and engagement programs.

Performance improvement

Measure what matters.

A metric-based management philosophy, better margins, working capital and fixed-asset utilization, and lean practices introduced or deepened on the floor.

Strategic growth

More than one road to growth.

Growth strategies with several avenues and the metrics to measure them, opportunities ranked by the value they add, and strategic add-on acquisitions.

Capital markets

Built to last.

A capital structure that supports the strategy, a company grown in size and scope, and a considered look at every exit alternative.

  • Equity partners

    Management owns alongside us

    Management is an equity partner in every platform investment, and Shorehill encourages ownership across a broad pool of key managers.

  • Common economics

    Common economics for all

    All investors' equity securities share common economics, with incentive equity and annual bonus plans tied to agreed objectives.

  • Discipline

    Capital preservation is king

    Stick to what we know, keep the portfolio narrow, and use leverage to enhance a return, never to justify one.
