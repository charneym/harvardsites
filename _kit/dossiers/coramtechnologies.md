- HL166051 abstract (used for the story and stats): MRI patients in VF are removed from the scanner, put on an MRI-safe
  stretcher and moved to a defibrillator; each minute of delay can reduce survival by 10–12%; >45 million MRI scans/yr in the
  US; pre-shock ramped High Frequency Alternating Current (rHFAC) reduces shock-induced skeletal muscle contraction (patient
  "jumping" into the bore); objective a clinical-grade MRI-conditional defibrillator for FDA 510(k) (FDA has indicated it is
  acceptable); exclusive license from Johns Hopkins University; patent filed on non-magnetic transformers; >14,000 US scanners.
- GM133243 abstract: interactive CPR film + "CPR Spring" device, Phase II adds a film with a female victim and wireless feedback.
