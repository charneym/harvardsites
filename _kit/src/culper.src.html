01Approach

Growth, not financial engineering.

Culper provides value-added strategic solutions to enhance returns and position companies for sustainable growth, using its own private capital alongside that of its business partners.

Step 1 of 4 · Capital

Capital, structured to fit.

Culper finds bespoke equity, debt or hybrid capital opportunities: control, minority, co-investment or secondary transactions, management-backed recapitalizations, corporate carve-outs and founder-owned businesses in transition.

Step 2 of 4 · Platform

A platform built from niche leaders.

In January 2022 Culper formed its first control platform, The Producto Group, acquiring Ring Precision Components and Dieco in a corporate carve-out from The PMT Group, and New Vision Industries from its majority shareholder.

Step 3 of 4 · Partnership

An extension of management.

Under Culper's "Extension of Management" strategy, the partners add value by providing resources, direction and advice across the legal, HR, insurance and CRM workstreams, alongside the management team.

Step 4 of 4 · Add-ons

Add-ons that complete the offering.

ThermoFab, a maker of heavy-gauge plastic enclosures since 1976, joined Producto in 2022. PolyCraft Tech then added cast urethane, the last manufacturing technology missing from the Innova platform.

02Investment criteria

Niche businesses, mission-critical products.

Asset-light businesses with strong competitive positions in fragmented industries, high barriers to entry and room to consolidate through add-on acquisitions.

  • 01 · Equity capital

    Buyouts & growth

    $5M+EBITDA, with revenue above $10 million and EBITDA margins of 20% or more
    Investment
    Management-backed leveraged buyouts, platform investments with add-on opportunities, growth capital
