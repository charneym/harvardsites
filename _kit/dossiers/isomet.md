## Facts used
- Founded 1956 growing and processing exotic crystal materials; one of the first commercial AO modulators into the laser market;
  customers from basic research to global OEMs; Ketterle (2001 Nobel) research used Isomet devices.
- AO devices: modulators, frequency shifters, deflectors, tuneable filters, Q-switches, special function, fiber coupled, AR coatings.
  Contemporary designs: ODXY1441, AOTF1110-VB, D1419-G50, OAM1059-T10S, IMAD-P80; quantum AOMs M1440 / M1451 (TeO2, quartz; UV/VIS/NIR).
- Electronics: frequency sources, RF drivers, power amplifiers, control electronics; iCS4 synthesizer; 555F dual-modulation driver; custom designs.
- Applications table (wavelengths, example devices) from acousto_optics_applications_home.html.
- AO mechanism text (transducer, photo-elastic grating, Bragg regime, RF frequency -> angle, RF power -> intensity) from acousto_optics.html.
