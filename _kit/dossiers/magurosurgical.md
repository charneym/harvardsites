## Facts used
- Site: "Redefining Kidney Stone Removal"; "Founded by urologists frustrated with existing tools."; Complete Stone Removal ("MagStone coats kidney stone fragments with magnetic polymer to simplify stone removal, delivering complete stone removal with fewer passes, faster procedures, and less trauma"); Streamlined Performance ("complete stone fragment removal for any size stone and integrates seamlessly with existing workflows and equipment"); Equalizes Surgery ("unlocks better outcomes for every patient"). Team: Kunj Sheth, MD (Founder, CEO); Daniel Massana, PhD (Founder, Director of R&D); Eric Johnson, MS (Principal Engineer); Jessie Ge, MD (Founder, Director of Regulatory Strategy, Consulting); Joseph Liao, MD (Founder, Director of Clinical, Consulting). Advisors: Andrew Cleeland (CEO, Fogarty Innovation), Mike Kujak (CEO, Francis Medical), Shan Wang, PhD (Stanford), Simon Conti, MD (Stanford).
- NIH abstract: fragments "often numbering in the hundreds"; residual fragments remain in ~40% of surgeries; the wire basket "generally removes only one fragment at a time"; MagStone coats fragments with a magnetic iron oxide-based hydrogel then retrieves them with a magnetic wire; fragments of any size coated en masse; more fragments per pass; prototype beat baskets in benchtop models with a good safety profile in preliminary in vivo porcine models; Phase I aims: delivery-catheter prototypes, ranking on a ureteroscopy simulation model with experienced endourologists, in vivo removal and clearance of the hydrogel.
- Stanford OTL HIT Fund page (https://otl.stanford.edu/researchers/high-impact-technology-hit-fund/hit-portfolio%23lifesciences/maguro-kidney-stone-removal): over 400,000 ureteroscopies a year in the US; HIT Fund aim of a design freeze via summative human factors testing and a preclinical/clinical testing plan with the FDA for De Novo; team Sheth (Assistant Professor of Urology), Daniel Massana Roquero, Joseph C. Liao (Kathryn Simmons Stamey Professor, Urology), T. Jessie Ge.
- Ge TJ, Roquero DM, … Conti S, Sheth K, Wang SX, Liao JC. "A magnetic hydrogel for the efficient retrieval of kidney stone fragments during ureteroscopy." Nat Commun 2023 Jun 22;14:3711 (PMID 37349287).
- US 11,903,666 B2, "Magnetic wire for retrieval and elimination of calculus from the urinary tract", granted 2024-02-20; inventors Ge, Conti, Liao, Sheth, Wang; assignees Stanford and the US Department of Veterans Affairs (https://patents.google.com/patent/US11903666B2/en).

## Story
One engraved scene, a kidney calyx in section with the ureteroscope in the infundibulum: (1) laser fragmentation scatters the stone; (2) the catheter injects the hydrogel, which coats every fragment dark; (3) the magnetic wire extends, draws the coated fragments onto its tip and withdraws them in bulk. Sources: NIH abstract and the 2023 paper. No invented numbers.
