## Company
- Medical device company, Santa Clara, CA, "dedicated to helping people with heart failure feel better and live longer".
- AccuCinch® Ventricular Restoration System: "the only completely transcatheter device designed to treat the enlarged left ventricle". FDA Breakthrough Device Designation. Investigational device (US law caution text required).
- Procedure (physicians page): Access (LV reached through the aortic valve with the AccuCinch Guide catheter; guide wire placed in the LV behind the chordae with the NavCath); Delivery (TracCath over the wire as template; anchors, each separated by a spacer to distribute force, delivered over a cable into the LV wall); Cinch (implant cinched and secured with a nitinol lock to reduce LV size).
- Goals: reduce LV size and wall stress; support and strengthen the LV; initiate reverse remodeling.
- About: ~26 million patients worldwide; up to 50% die within five years of diagnosis.
- CORCINCH-HF: prospective, randomized, open-label, multicenter, international; AccuCinch + GDMT vs GDMT; 400 randomized at up to 80 centers; PMA analysis after first 250 at 6 months, second at 12 months. NCT04331769. Other studies: CORCINCH-EU, -FMR, -HFrEF, -PMVI. Publication: J Card Fail 2023;29:1046-1055.
