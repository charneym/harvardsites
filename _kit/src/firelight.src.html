01Approach

Capital, plus the craft of a brand.

Firelight helps branded consumer and retail companies accelerate growth through brand management, digital transformation, operational scaling and bespoke, disciplined financing.

Step 1 of 3 · The founder

It starts with the fire you've lit.

Every partnership begins with the founders and management team: understanding what they have built in their category and culture, and where the brand can go next.

Step 2 of 3 · Private equity

The investor's toolkit.

Growth capital and a flexible capital structure, financial and operational expertise, committed senior advisors, and a track record in consumer and retail.

Step 3 of 3 · Branding & marketing

The brand-builder's toolkit.

Marketing and branding, digital strategy and e-commerce, trend forecasting and consumer research, public relations, licensing, and marketplace seeding.
