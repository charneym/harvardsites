About

A young company with decades of experience

Erwin Harter founded HARTER GmbH in December 2015. The team brings together more than 30 years of experience in machine building for the wire and cable industry and in custom machine building, along with long-standing relationships across the industry.

An international background and current 2D/3D CAD systems let the team take on new and upcoming projects, and HARTER sells its machines worldwide.
