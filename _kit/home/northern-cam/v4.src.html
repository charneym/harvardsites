About us

A family business that started with one CAM auto machine

Pritpal Singh Bhabra completed his engineering degree at Leeds University in 1963. When the chance came to buy a single CAM auto machine, he put all his savings into it, set up in a small workshop and named the company Northern Cam.

Today, as then, we are a family-owned business. Investing in the best technology, manufacturing quality products, employing knowledgeable people and insisting on high levels of customer service are, in our opinion, the four pillars of our success.

  1. 1977

    Northern Cam is founded in Leeds and incorporated, with Pritpal Bhabra as Managing Director.

  2. 1994

    His sons Satvinder and Parvinder Singh Bhabra join, and it truly becomes a family business.

  3. 1998

    A second manufacturing base opens in India, with an established supply chain from China.

  4. 2023

    After the founder's passing, Satvinder becomes Managing Director and Parvinder Finance Director.

Capabilities

From bar stock to inspected batch

  1. Turning
  2. Milling
  3. Inspection
  4. Batches
  1. 01 · Turning

    Turned components from 2 mm to 300 mm

    CNC fixed head and sliding head lathes with live tooling, cross drilling and milling. Automatic bar feed and un-manned night running significantly reduce costs, so we can give a highly competitive price.

  2. 02 · Milling

    3 and 4 axis machining centres

    Milling and drilling on several faces of a part. Twin pallet machines take high volume batches, alongside single pallet high speed drilling machines and 4th axis capability.

  3. 03 · Inspection

    Measured before it leaves

    A Mitutoyo coordinate measuring machine, a shadowgraph with projection up to 10x magnification, surface testing and high quality digital and conventional gauges, with full traceability.

  4. 04 · Batches

    From one prototype to large volumes

    Single prototype components through to large batch volumes, with our own manufacturing in the UK, India and China and delivery to customers throughout the world.
