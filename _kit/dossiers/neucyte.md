"Why partner" points: proprietary iPSC platform; SynFire neural cells; phenotypic and target-based screens; accelerated innovation; expert team.
- Technology: SynFire cells from proprietary methods; large homogeneous batches of multiple neuronal subtypes; pipeline Patient → Reprogram → iPS cells → Differentiate → pure populations → defined iN/astroglial co-culture → functional studies, phenotypic screening, neurotoxicity; assays (survival, axonal outgrowth, dendritic arborization; with glia: synapse formation, transcription, electrophysiology); human-specific phenotypes not identifiable in traditional models; 3D brain assembloids of SynFire excitatory and inhibitory neurons with astrocytes (TUJ1/GFAP/DAPI image).
