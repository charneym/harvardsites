## Story
One skin cross-section with an open diabetic wound: the wound stalls → cathepsin K enzymes gather and cut the collagen bundles around the bed → the inhibitor docks in each enzyme's cleft, collagen re-forms, new matrix fills the bed and skin cells migrate in until the surface closes. Schematic.
