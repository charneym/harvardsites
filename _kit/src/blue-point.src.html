Approach

Local partners, global reach.

Blue Point pairs the closeness of a regional firm with the resources of a much larger one: supply chain teams abroad, data and digital, human capital and hands-on M&A.

We're locals

Partnerships forged close to home.

From offices in Cleveland and Charlotte, Blue Point builds relationships with owners, advisors and local intermediaries. Nearly half of the portfolio is within driving distance of a regional office.

Globalizing the middle market

A supply chain team since 2004.

Blue Point built dedicated, in-house global supply chain resources in 2004. It has helped more than half of its portfolio companies sell internationally, source more cost-effectively or set up operations abroad.

M&A execution

Building industry leaders.

Blue Point often leads add-on identification, diligence, financing and execution, so management can keep running the business. In 2026 alone: Dumpsters.com, Shapley's for Weaver, and Wells Masonry for RSI.

  • Data & digital

    Data, not simply debt

    Blue Point helps management teams use their data better and take advantage of digital marketing and e-commerce, including more analytical, strategic pricing.

  • Human capital

    Stronger teams

    Over 100 new hires across current portfolio management teams, plus work on engagement, culture and finding outstanding executives and board members.

  • Operating Executive Group

    Seasoned operators

    Mentors to management teams, averaging nearly 30 years of operating experience, involved from initial screening through exit.

Investment criteria

Market leaders, ready to grow.

Blue Point invests equity in leading lower middle-market industrial, business services and consumer companies, and is particularly focused on being the first outside equity capital in a growing business.
