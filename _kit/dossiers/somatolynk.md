## Science (from https://www.somatolynk.com/approach and /alzheimer-s-disease)
- Somatostatin is a chemical mediator acting through several receptors to regulate brain function. Loss of somatostatin and somatostatin-expressing neurons contributes to pathology driven by amyloid-beta (Aβ) and Aβ oligomers (AβOs).
- SST4 (somatostatin receptor subtype-4) is heavily expressed in brain regions affected by Alzheimer's (neocortex, hippocampus, particularly CA1); activation linked to reduction of brain Aβ and AβOs; also identified in mitigation of neuropsychiatric symptoms, seizure activity and inflammation.
- Program: orally bioavailable small-molecule selective SST4 agonists.
- Target validation: SST4 agonist actions enhance learning and memory (T-maze, object recognition), with disease-modifying actions in AD mouse models (APPswe, 3xTg, SAMP8) across i.c.v., i.p. and oral administration. Hit agonist NNC 26-9100 increased learning and decreased toxic Aβ42 oligomers.
- Lead optimization: early lead SM-I-26: SST4 binding 12 nM, activity EC50 16.5 nM, reduced p-glycoprotein efflux vs hit; learning and memory benefit in SAMP8 mice; no negative motor or anxiety changes; neprilysin activity increased at 24 h after single i.c.v. injection, similar trend i.p.
- Advantages listed: avoids secretase-inhibitor toxicity and antibody brain-permeability limits; GPCR small-molecule targeting is druggable; SST4 in neocortex/hippocampus CA1; not expressed in pituitary, limited peripheral expression, no effect on glucagon, growth hormone or insulin release; low internalization and rapid recycling; enhances brain neprilysin, which degrades monomeric Aβ and AβOs; direct cognition enhancement; oral and nasal delivery focus; compound series novel, patented and exclusively licensed to Somatolynk.
- Alzheimer's context: ~7 million people in the USA (Alzheimer's Association 2024 Facts and Figures, cited on the site); global projection 152 million by 2050.
