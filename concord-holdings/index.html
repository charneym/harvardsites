How we partner

01 / The owner

It starts with what you built.

We partner with founder, family and operator-owned businesses with five or more years of stable, profitable operations and no institutional investors.

02 / Flexible liquidity

Capital that fits alongside you.

Flexible liquidity options for aligned owners, often a recapitalization in which existing leadership keeps a meaningful stake in the business.

03 / Hands-on help

No fixed clock, real support.

Hands-on operational assistance: technology, professionalization and add-on acquisitions, without the time-hold constraints of a traditional fund.
