## Firm
- Charter Oak Equity, LP. Private equity, lower middle market, control-oriented.
- "Charter Oak Equity and its predecessor funds have invested in private equity since 1992." Over $500 million in control-oriented capital invested in 30 portfolio companies through over 50 acquisitions.
- Focus: niche manufacturing, packaging, medical products & services, specialty chemicals, consumer products and financial services companies up to $150 million EV.
- Criteria: control-oriented investments, $25M–$150M EV; privately-held, often family-owned companies or non-core divisions of larger companies. Strategically attractive market, sustainable competitive position; products/services well established with a history of profitable operations; no start-ups or turnarounds; capability to grow via products/services and/or consolidating a fragmented market.
- Target industries: Packaging; Chemicals; Medical Devices and Engineering; Niche Industrial; Metal Processing; Pharmaceutical-Related; Value Added Distribution; Specialty Financial Services; Business Services.
- Approach: partners average nearly twenty years of PE experience; operational, financial, consulting expertise; partner with management; active line roles on a temporary basis where appropriate; general partners personally invest in all transactions; management teams invited to invest alongside; all partners have run companies, not their intent to run the companies.
- Slogan: "Focused. Committed. Driven."
- HQ: 274 Riverside Avenue, Second Floor, Westport, CT 06880 (contact page + SEC IAPD). Phone (203) 221-4752. (Site footer still shows an older 10 Wright Street, Suite 230 address and a fax.)
