# Eascra Biotech (eascrabiotech) — batch 2026-11g, home-only build

Facts with sources only.

## Company
- Nanomedicine company; precision therapeutic delivery for hard-to-penetrate tissues (articular cartilage, kidneys, ECM-rich solid tumors) using proprietary Janus base nanoparticles (JBNps), a DNA-inspired synthetic (non-lipid) molecule that fully encapsulates a cargo, room-temperature stable. Cargoes: siRNA, mRNA, Cas9, proteins, peptides, other drug agents. Source: https://eascrabiotech.com/
- Product areas: JBNp™ (delivery platform for mRNA therapeutics and gene editing), JBNm™ (cell-free injectable scaffold for cartilage repair/regeneration), JBNm-Tissue Chip™ (knee joint on a microchip). Source: https://eascrabiotech.com/
- HQ: 139A Charles St #315, Boston, MA 02114; lab in Worcester, MA. info@eascrabiotech.com, 401.286.9828. Sources: site footer; InSPA release.
- JBNp invented by co-founder Dr. Yupeng Chen, associate professor of Biomedical Engineering, University of Connecticut. Source: InSPA release.

## Activity
- 2026-08-27: $7.96M NASA InSPA Phase 2 contract (low Earth orbit manufacturing of JBNps); six ISS missions in under three years, six more anticipated over two years; aiming for FDA clinical trials by 2028; >$8M previous grants/awards/sponsored research (NASA, NSF, AFWERX, MassVentures, top-10 pharma partners). https://eascrabiotech.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Eascra-InSPA-Award-VF.pdf
