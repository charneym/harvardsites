## Technology (site /technology)
- HD64: high-resolution SCS; 60 stimulation contacts across the entire width of the spinal cord (NIH abstract); "two-times more pain fiber locations" (release).
- First Smart Programmable Electrodes: hermetic electronics within each array; 64–256 therapeutic electrodes using a fraction of the wires; proprietary chips to power, communicate, multiplex read-write signals.
- Shape-memory self-expanding electrodes: compress into a catheter, re-expand like a stent; 3–5x larger therapeutic area; arrays 2x4, 2x8, 2x12, 3x8, 4x6, 4x8.
- ASICs: 64-ch high-voltage multiplexer (24 in / 64 out, available for licensing by indication); 16-ch pulse generator ASIC with neural sensing (17 stim/17 sensing channels, 10 µA–25 mA, DC–50 kHz, 1.5 µVRMS noise floor).
- Stimulator platform: 24-channel trial stimulator, wireless inductive charger, iOS patient remote, Bluetooth LE; closed-loop sensing IPG, 1.3 mm lead bore; clinician programmer tablet.
- Contact page: IDE clinical studies performed; ISO 13485:2008 QMS. Applications: SCS, PNS, BCI; chronic pain, sensory-motor paralysis.
