- All NIH awards (RePORTER API, org "CG SCIENTIFIC"): 11 fiscal-year awards, 4 projects, $6,249,228 total: GM139391 PBMC isolation (2020 R43; 2025, 2026 R44); DK112468 islet isolation (2017 R43; 2021–2023 R44); EB027611 MSC extraction (2016 R43 via OD; 2018, 2019 R44); CA254490 CAR-T WBC enrichment (2020 R43). Co-PI 2016: Harun Elmasri.
- Science (from the abstracts only): HEDS = "High Efficiency Deterministic Separation", patented microfluidic platform. Phase I: ~97.2% PBMC recovery, ~99.97% RBC removal, ~99.86% platelet depletion vs Ficoll, no loss of viability, no immunophenotype change, no T-cell activation; COP chips by soft embossing; plastic prototype ~82.3% recovery. Goal: PBMCs from 10 mL blood in 15 min. Phase II aims: plastic chip >95% recovery; alpha prototype cartridge; beta prototype by soft embossing + injection molding; characterization (flow, functional assays, RNA-seq); multi-site benchmarking vs Ficoll and immunomagnetic methods. The chip's internal geometry is NOT published, so the site does not draw it.
