## Facts used on the page
- About: development-stage company restoring the lung's innate mucosal surface defense; founded on proprietary ENaC blockers;
  expanding into new mechanisms, indications and approaches (parion.com About).
- Idrevloride = P-1037 (PubChem CID 71502876, C30H49ClN8O7). Lancet Resp Med release (8 Sep 2023): CLEAN-PCD Phase 2,
  multinational, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled crossover, ages 12+; safe, significant improvement in lung
  function over 28 days vs hypertonic saline alone; presented at ERS 2023 ALERT; further PCD studies planned; mechanism text
  ("block the sodium channels on the airway surface, blocking the absorption of water and stimulating fluid secretion ...
  hydrate the mucus ... clearance of mucus by cough"); PCD: no clinically proven therapies to improve mucus clearance.
- FDA orphan drug, fast track, rare pediatric disease designations for P-1037 IS in PCD (release July 29, 2020); EC orphan
  designation (Nov 17, 2020); EMA PDCO positive PIP opinion (Apr 5, 2023); ENaC pulmonary rights reacquired from Vertex
  (Jan 23, 2020); P-321 rights regained from Shire/Takeda (Oct 31, 2019).
- P-1155 (NIH abstract): next-gen ENaC blocker with an osmolyte; builds on P-1037/HS; osmolyte for clinical activity, high
  protein binding for extra-renal excretion and safety; modified for faster onset in stable and exacerbating COPD/asthma;
  mucus plugs persist > 3 years in COPD; no agents dedicated to clearing mucus plugs.
- Pipeline page: P-321 dry eye Phase II; mucolytic preclinical (NIH 1R43HL112436 "Novel Mucolytic Agents", 2013).
  tPAD CF (Phase II on the page) left out: no current detail.
- Team (parion.com): Paul Boucher MBA, President & CEO (joined 2006); Karl Donn PhD (SVP Drug Development per 2023 release;
  site: VP); Brian Bray PhD, VP CMC (joined 2015). Board: Richard C. Boucher MD (Co-Chairman and Founder; Marsico Lung
  Institute, UNC CF Center), Robert Wills PhD, Paul Boucher.
- Funding: NIH and the Cystic Fibrosis Foundation (2023 release boilerplate). Values quote from Careers page.
