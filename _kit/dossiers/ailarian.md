## Story
Print → recover → reuse: tracks printed on a paper insert and LEDs light; magnets over a recycling line lift the ferromagnetic silver while the paper moves on; the recovered silver fills a jar of new ink and a loop runs back to print (Sustainability and Smart Packaging pages).
