## Facts used
- Site (home, about, story, technology, team, contact): inhibitory NIR wavelengths to prevent ischemia-reperfusion injury after cardiac arrest, stroke, TBI; ~$21M grants/awards (NIH, DoD, others) to Mitovation and founders; patents; Class II pathway; exclusive license via Wayne State OTC and U-M; ISO 13485 / FDA-compliant manufacturing partners; robust safety profile; waveguide technology (patent pending); no FDA-approved treatments; founded Nov 2016; discovery in Hüttemann lab, in vivo in Sanderson lab; indications incl. neonatal HIE; team bios.
