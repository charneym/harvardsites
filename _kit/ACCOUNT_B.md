## How to work (you are B's coordinator)
1. If your environment allows it, run 3–5 build sessions in parallel (one firm or a few per session, fresh sessions, each with an hourly resume task so they continue after usage-limit stops). Otherwise build them yourself one at a time.
2. Builders follow BATCH_BRIEF.md exactly. One change: instead of messaging a coordinator, each finished site is reported by APPENDING its full report (the §7 / "SITE DONE" format: URL, concept, gates, HQ, lead, emails, activity, bad-site evidence) to `_kit/reports/account-b.md`, committed in the same push as the site. Skipped firms get a line "SKIPPED: <slug>, <reason>".
3. Before starting a firm, `git pull --rebase` and check `_kit/sourcing/registry.csv`: build only firms in YOUR QUEUE below whose status is still `assigned`. Set the status to `building-B` in your first commit for that firm. Never touch account A's firms or files outside your own site folders, `_kit/src/<your slug>.src.html`, the registry status column for your firms, and the report file. Always rebase before you push.
4. Do NOT do outreach and do NOT email anyone. The client does that.
5. Account A verifies every reported site (gates plus a visual review) and either marks it `live` in the registry or appends a fix request under "Fix requests" at the bottom of `_kit/reports/account-b.md`. Check that section at the start of every work cycle and fix those first.
