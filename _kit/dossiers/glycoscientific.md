# GlycoScientific, LLC (glycoscientific)

Facts with sources only.

- **Company:** GlycoScientific, LLC, a privately held company developing and commercializing research reagents for glycobiology. Based in Athens, Georgia, next door to the University of Georgia's Complex Carbohydrate Research Center (CCRC). Funded by the National Institutes of Health and private sources, including the Georgia Research Alliance. Source: https://glycoscientific.com/pages/about-us
- **Domain:** glycoscientific.com (company name, About Us page, Shopify store). The 2015 press release lists ronorlando@glycoscientific.com. GRA company page links to it: https://gra.org/company/90/GlycoScientific.html
- **Products** (glycoscientific.com home and pages):
  - IgG isotope and glycoprotein quantitation standards; iGlycoMab, a murine IgG2b whose glycans are labelled with 15N; IgG1 standards in isotope light, amino-acid heavy, glycan heavy, and amino-acid and glycan heavy forms. https://glycoscientific.com/pages/igg-glycoprotein-quantitation-standards
  - Recombinant human IgG1, IgG2 and IgG4 standard proteins. https://glycoscientific.com/pages/recombinant-igg-standard-proteins
  - Site-specific antibodies to O-GlcNAc (including IRS2, HIPK1 and five sites on four histone proteins). https://glycoscientific.com/pages/custom-o-glcnac-antibodies
  - Antibodies to O-fucose (anti-FOS, anti-FOT, Notch1). https://glycoscientific.com/pages/fucose
  - Guinea pig and golden hamster purified proteins, antibodies and ELISA sets. https://glycoscientific.com/pages/guinea-pig-reagents
  - Custom antibodies and custom mammalian protein production. https://glycoscientific.com/pages/custom-o-glcnac-antibodies
- "Our research team has more than 25 years of experience in cutting edge mass spectrometry and glycan research." (IgG standards page)
