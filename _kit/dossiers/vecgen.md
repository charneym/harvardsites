Partners with pharmaceutical companies, physicians, patient advocacy groups and clinical collaborators.

## Science (abstract)
- GNE myopathy: progressive adult-onset neuromuscular disorder from recessive GNE mutations (rate-limiting enzyme in sialic acid biosynthesis); reduced sialylation, skeletal muscle degeneration; no approved therapies; AAV gene therapy showed preclinical promise but translation limited by severe side effects.
- Plan: clone and produce AAV vectors carrying all FDA-approved gene therapy sequences; establish immunogenicity benchmarks in healthy donor PBMCs; qualify Vecgen's PBMC-based IVD capturing cytokine responses predictive of post-treatment inflammatory biomarkers; correlate with donor measures; initiate regulatory engagement; expand to anti-AAV5 and anti-AAVrh74 antibody generation; multi-center PBMC testing from 100 prospective AAV recipients; PMA-enabling dataset; apply IVD to optimize the GNE cassette using CpG-minimized variants.
- Outcome: a qualified IVD for point-of-care assessment of AAV immunogenicity and a next-generation AAV-GNE candidate.
