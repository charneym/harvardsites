# Rock Hill Capital — dossier (facts with sources only)

- Site: https://www.rockhillcap.com · Slug: rock-hill · Track: private equity (spec §2b/§4b)
- HQ: 3355 West Alabama, Suite 850, Houston, TX 77098 · (713) 715-7510 · inquiries@rockhillcap.com (site footer). Time zone: US Central (America/Chicago).
- Founded 2007; Houston-based PE partnering with industrial products and services companies (infrastructure, energy, manufacturing, waste, business services); family and founder-owned lower-middle market businesses needing an active capital partner for succession and growth (https://www.rockhillcap.com/).
- About: "We Build for People"; matches entrepreneurs with capital, expertise and resources; helps partners grow organically, complete strategic acquisitions and resolve undercapitalized situations; six core values (Integrity, Partnership, Passion, Accountability, Humility, Adaptability); Stakeholder Impact (ESG) paragraph (https://www.rockhillcap.com/about).
