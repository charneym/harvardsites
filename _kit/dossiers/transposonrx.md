## The company
Clinical-stage San Diego biopharma developing therapies for neurodegenerative and aging-related disease, aimed at LINE-1
retrotransposons. Lead: TPN-101 (censavudine), a first-in-class nucleoside inhibitor of LINE-1 reverse transcriptase, once-daily
oral, brain-penetrant, more than 250 subjects exposed for about 48 weeks. Follow-ons: novel NRTIs and PKR inhibitors (discovery).
Phone (858) 535-4821. No street address published (releases are datelined San Diego).
Sources: transposonrx.com index, science, pipeline, tpn101, psp, als, ad, nrti, pkr, team, news, connect; press-release PDFs.

## Facts used on the page
- ARPA-H PROSPR award of up to $22M for TPN-101 in healthy aging, release 2026-03-04 (collaborators: Rochester, Brown, UConn, UTMB, UT Health, Nebraska).
- ADDF investment for a Phase 2 in Alzheimer's disease, release 2025-07-09.
- Selected for the Phase 2/3 HEALEY ALS Platform Trial (MGH, NEALS), release 2025-05-28.
- C9orf72 ALS/FTD Phase 2 final results, release 2024-07-24: VC decline −8.4% vs −16.5% at 24 weeks (~50% less); ALSFRS-R ~40% less decline than natural history over 48 weeks; NfL, NfH, IL-6 lower; pooled with PSP NfL p = 0.034. Design: n=42, 3:2, 400 mg daily, 24 wk DB + 24 wk OL.
- FDA Fast Track for PSP, release 2024-05-21; PSP Phase 2: first treatment to reduce CSF NfL vs placebo, dose-related IL-6 reductions.
- Nucleoside-analog oncology portfolio acquired from PrimeFour, release 2024-11-21.
- Team, board, scientific and clinical advisory boards from /team.html (summaries condensed). News items link to the release PDFs.
