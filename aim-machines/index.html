Company

Forming our future with yours

AIM Inc., also known as Automated Industrial Machinery, builds 2D and 3D CNC bending machinery and exports it from two manufacturing facilities, in North America and Europe. The machines are modular: change the frame modules and a 2D machine makes rectangular frames up to 72″ (1828 mm); add modules and 2D or 3D machines chamfer, butt weld, end form, drill, mark, thread and press in line.

Rebuild & upgrade

Rebuild the old machine, or replace it?

  1. Assess
  2. Strip down
  3. Controls
  4. Mechanics
  1. 01 · Assess

    How old is it, and is it compliant?

    With more than 1,300 machines installed, AIM gets at least ten rebuild enquiries a month. Age, condition, maintenance records and today's safety rules decide whether a rebuild makes sense.

  2. 02 · Strip down

    Everything comes apart

    Disassembly, inspection, clean-up and repainting come before any assembly, so a rebuild can take more labour than building a new machine.

  3. 03 · Controls

    A new controller, amplifiers and motors

    On a 15 to 25-year-old machine the servo controller is most likely out of production. A new controller needs new servo amplifiers, and those usually need new servo motors.

  4. 04 · Mechanics

    Or trade in for a new machine

    Gearboxes, bearings and worn parts go too. Often a trade-in discount on a new machine built to UL, CE, CSA and OSHA standards is the better deal; AIM's engineers quote both.

Software

SmartEditor®, designed and developed by AIM

The same software runs every AIM model, from the compact benders to the Gemini. The online AIM Academy trains operators around the clock.

  • CAD import: DXF, XYZ, STEP and IGES files load directly.
  • Animation simulator with collision detection, before the first bend.
  • Office version for off-line programming at a desk.
  • Dual processor: program the next part while producing.
  • Maintenance module that reminds you of every service task.
  • CMM link: FARO, ROMER and AICON arms correct programs automatically.
  • Robots: integrates with FANUC and Motoman (Yaskawa).
  • AccuTrack and AccuCAL automate production statistics and set-ups.
