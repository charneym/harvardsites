01Approach

Common sense, owner to owner.

What sets Thirty31 apart is a common-sense investment approach, rooted in operational expertise and strategic insight, and capital structured to fit the company.

Step 1 of 4 · The business

It starts with what you've built.

Thirty31 looks for established companies with strong fundamentals and room to grow: an experienced management team, strong cash flow and a growing share of its market.

Step 2 of 4 · Structure

Capital that fits the foundation.

Meaningful equity ownership, control or non-control, combined with subordinated debt with current yield. Thirty31 invests its own capital alongside that of its investors.

Step 3 of 4 · Involvement

A seat at the table, not your desk.

The partners serve as board-level advisors rather than day-to-day managers or operators, so the team that built the company keeps running it.

Step 4 of 4 · Growth

Room for the next addition.

Growth financings and acquisition financings help a company expand, as when portfolio company Innovative Technologies added Kubco Services in Houston.

  • Alignment

  • Experience

    Operators as well as investors

    The partners bring hands-on experience in both private equity and business ownership, from founding companies to preparing one for sale.

  • Transitions

    Built for ownership changes

    Management buyouts, recapitalizations, shareholder liquidity events and ESOPs: structures for owners planning what comes next.
