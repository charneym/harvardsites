## Science (RePORTER abstracts)
- Synaptic plasticity impairment is core to AD; Aβ and tau suppress synaptic potentiation; net synapse loss is an early event. Synaptic dysfunction = early, shared, targetable mechanism.
- NQ-013 (NQ-13): IGFBP2-mimetic with a growth-factor-like mechanism; first demonstration of IGFBP mimetics as potential AD therapeutics.
- Phase I results (Phase II abstract): oral dosing in rats → CSF level above EC100 for IGF2R binding/activation; good oral bioavailability; robust improvement in working and long-term memory in AD mouse models and aging mice; reduced phospho-tau with minimal effect on Aβ42; long-lasting increase in frontal-cortex QEEG alpha power in freely behaving rats; GLP 28-day IV rat study NOAEL ≥1,000-fold above in vivo EC50.
- Phase II plan: define PK profile, optimize formulation, complete IND-enabling studies, guide IND and first clinical trial of oral NQ-013 in AD/ADRD.
- Phase I abstract (JB2): binds IGF2R-containing spines in cortical neurons, synaptoneurosomes and recombinant IGF2R; raises intracellular Ca2+ in IGF2R-expressing HEK cells; facilitates synaptic strength via MEK/ERK, transcription/translation dependent, IGF2R- not IGF1R-dependent; LTP with increased NMDA and AMPA receptor activity and IGF2R surface expression; single dose reverses cognitive deficits in 5XFAD mice.
- Technology page (https://encueinc.com/technology/): proprietary QEEG biomarker to measure efficacy, optimize patient selection, monitor progression; NQ-13 induces structural plasticity; models: normal adult rats, Shank mice, 5XFAD mice, aged rats, TBI rats, chronic-stress rats; assessments: spine formation, synaptic AMPAR expression, in vivo/ex vivo electrophysiology; small-molecule candidates able to cross the BBB; fully synthetic and orally deliverable.
- Platform slide (ENCUE-new-slide-updated image on /technology/): IGFBP2 → JB2 platform → NQ-100 peptoid platform (high stability, tunable half-life), NQ-000 synthetic platform (small-molecule-like behaviour, scalable manufacturing, oral delivery), NQ-200 AI platform (AI-generated mimetics, rapid design iteration, optimized potency). JB2 provides manufacturing flexibility, delivery flexibility, IP moat, expansion loops, "three shots on goal". Pipeline: ASD (Rett, Fragile X), Alzheimer's, TBI, schizophrenia in 22q; future: schizophrenia, bipolar 1, sleep.
