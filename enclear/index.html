01·The barrier

Most CNS drugs never reach the tissue.

Between the bloodstream and the brain stand the blood–brain, blood–CSF and blood–arachnoid barriers. Specific regions are hard to target, higher systemic doses are needed to reach effective levels, and peripheral side effects and toxicity rise with them.

The amber drop stands for a systemic dose; the glass for the barriers. Sources: encleartherapies.com/csf-health-platform; Neuro-Oncology 27(S5) DDEL-15, Nov 2025.
02·The CSF circuit

The brain’s own river.

Cerebrospinal fluid fills the ventricles and moves through the third ventricle, the narrow aqueduct and the fourth ventricle, into the space around the brain and spinal cord. Intrathecal chemotherapy enters this fluid directly, but does not spread well into brain tissue.

03·EnTrega

Two ports. The pump sets the direction.

A catheter in a lateral ventricle and one in the lumbar thecal sac, or one in each lateral ventricle. A precision pump outside the body sets the rate and direction of CSF flow between them, so a drug given into the CSF is moved to its target rather than left to drift.

04·Hold

The dose, held over the target.

Rapid flow reversal can vary concentration over specific tissue. In eight healthy sheep, dye studies showed CSF flow could be controlled, with dye moving between the ventricles. Methotrexate distributed bilaterally in the brain and reached above the therapeutic threshold in deep structures such as the striatum.

  1. 1Dose given at the port
  2. 2Held in the cranial circuit
Concentration in the model is relative and illustrative. Sources: NIH R44NS135792 abstract; DDEL-15 (Kumthekar et al., 11 Nov 2025).
05·Monitoring

A loop that listens.

In the sheep studies, sensors followed pressure, respiration and heart rate, and vital signs stayed within safe limits. EnClear describes automated CSF sampling and closed-loop CSF control with integrated ML/AI. The authors report safety and feasibility in a large-animal model and plan a first-in-human study opening in Q2 2026.

Sources: DDEL-15; NIH R44NS135792; National Brain Tumor Society, 7 Jul 2025; encleartherapies.com.

Mission
Extending the lives of patients suffering from CNS disease.

Goal

To improve and extend the lives of patients suffering from central nervous system disorders, including neurodegenerative diseases and CNS malignancies.

Opportunity

To use cerebrospinal fluid for controlled, continuous and tailored delivery to the parts of the CNS that would benefit most from existing and next-generation therapies.

Strategy

Launch in neuro-oncology, addressing a $1.2B market need, then expand through partnerships into neurodegenerative, lysosomal storage and autoimmune diseases.

The platform

The EnTrega CSF Platform.

Closed-loop CSF control with integrated ML/AI. EnClear describes EnTrega as precision dosing of CNS therapeutics to reach efficacious levels in the targeted regions while reducing the risk of severe side effects and peripheral toxicity.

Monitoring and diagnostics

A sensor array follows the patient’s physiological response so the system can adjust infusion rate, CSF flow rate and flow direction. The same access allows CSF to be drawn: the company describes automated CSF sampling, non-invasive patient monitoring and machine-learning-enhanced safety protocols.

Safe, local access

EnClear describes moving CNS therapies from specialised hospital networks to local infusion clinics, making weekly, monthly or yearly dosing easier to manage by cutting trips to specialised centres.

Sources: NIH R44NS135792 abstract; funding release, 4 Sept 2024; encleartherapies.com/csf-health-platform.

First indication · ENC006

Leptomeningeal carcinomatosis.

Cancer that has spread into the pia and arachnoid membranes and the subarachnoid space between them, most often from breast cancer, lung cancer and melanoma. A late-stage complication with a poor outlook and few options.

110,000people a year in the US
2–7 mocurrent life expectancy
4–6 wksurvival untreated; about 3–6 months treated
$1.2Bmarket need cited by EnClear

Standard of care

Methotrexate given intravenously and intrathecally (IT/ICV), from twice a week to monthly. It brings peripheral toxicity, infection risk, bruising and bleeding, anemia and hair loss, and it does not adequately reach all areas of the CNS.

Figures from encleartherapies.com/elc1-program, citing De Oca Delgado et al., Front. Oncol. 2018;8:509.

We can do better

EnClear is developing EnTrega to deliver chemotherapy to the CNS regions that need it most. Within three years the goals are to reduce side effects, extend life and widen access to therapy.

In preclinical studies the company reports significantly higher methotrexate concentrations across the CNS than standard IT and ICV delivery. At SNO in November 2025 the team wrote that a first-in-human study was planned to open in Q2 2026.

The EnTrega CSF Platform is in development. Forward-looking statements have not been confirmed in human clinical trials and have not been assessed by the FDA. (Statement reproduced from encleartherapies.com.)
