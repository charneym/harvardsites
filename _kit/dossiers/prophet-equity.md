# Prophet Equity — dossier (facts with sources)

- Firm: Prophet Equity, "Seeing Future Value Today" (site title). https://prophetequity.com
- HQ: 1460 Main Street, Suite 200, Southlake, TX 76092 (team bio pages). Main line 817.898.1500, info@prophetequity.com (home page). Time zone: America/Chicago.
- Thesis (home): "a private equity firm that makes investments in strategically viable, asset-intensive, underperforming companies." Principals have invested in and managed entities with over $6 billion in revenue over twenty-plus years. Focus on lower middle market companies with strategically strong businesses and significant value creation potential, e.g. partnerships, corporate carve-outs, divestitures and non-strategic businesses of larger companies. Situations: special situations, succession planning, recapitalizations, reorganizations, turnarounds, acquisitions, bankruptcies. Holistic Value Creation™ (HVC™) strategy, "Fortune 500 tested and private equity proven". Management teams have clear, uncapped incentives based on realized business performance.
- Criteria (https://prophetequity.com/investment-criteria/): revenue up to $500M; enterprise values $10M–$250M; equity investments $5M–$50M. Industries of experience: Automotive & Transportation; Waste Collection & Disposal; Business and Consumer Services; Consumer Products; Aerospace; Distribution; Heavy Equipment Sales, Service, Rental and Leasing; Infrastructure; Healthcare; Manufacturing; Natural Resources and Energy; Defense. Situations: 363 Sales; Acquisitions; Corporate Carve-outs; Divestitures; Non-Strategic Business Units; Reorganizations/Recapitalizations; Special Situations; Stalking-Horse Bids; Succession Planning; Turnarounds.
- Office of Change Management: named in bios as the post-close body Prophet principals sit on at each investment.
