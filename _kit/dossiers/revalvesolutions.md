## Story
One fine-line heart in section (four chambers, native mitral valve, veins, aorta). Step 1: catheter from the femoral vein through the right atrium, across the septum. Step 2: the valve opens in the mitral annulus, four securement points light. Step 3: catheter withdrawn; the outflow path to the aorta draws in beside the low frame. Step 4: a future valve seats inside.
