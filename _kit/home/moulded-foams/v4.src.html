Construction systems

Part L thermal flooring and EPS for the building site

EPS helps cut both the embodied and the operational carbon of a building, and its expansion and moulding use only steam. Our construction range is built around the GDeck insulated beam-and-block floor.

GDeck combines pre-stressed concrete floor beams with lightweight EPS infill panels, giving a faster, lighter and more thermally efficient alternative to traditional beam and block. It was named Housebuilder magazine’s product of the month.

  • GDeckInsulated suspended floor, 98% lighter than traditional block and beam, Part L compliant
  • TDeckPart L thermal flooring for T-beam floors
  • PassivhausMember of the Passivhaus Trust
  • Underfloor heatingEPS underfloor heating panels
  • ICF buildingInsulated concrete formwork

BRE and Kiwa certified. GDeck panels laid between concrete floor beams.

EPS boxes

Insulated boxes for fish, food and pharma

Expanded polystyrene gives excellent thermal protection for goods in transit. Our euro-sized boxes are light, durable and stackable, do not support biological growth, and are available from stock.

    Euro-sized fish boxes and produce boxes, ideal shippers for fresh meat, fish and produce.

EPP foams

Expanded polypropylene that takes repeated impacts

EPP is a closed-cell bead foam with outstanding energy absorption, multiple impact resistance, thermal insulation and a very high strength-to-weight ratio. We mould it in densities from 15 to 200 grams per litre, and it is 100% recyclable.
