01Approach

From founder-led to fully built.

Our selection is company-centric rather than industry-focused: the characteristics of the company itself matter most in creating value. Then we work alongside management to take it across.

The company

An established business, ready to grow.

Private, established lower middle-market companies with strong cash flow and growth prospects, typically $10 million to $50 million of enterprise value, often founder or family owned.

The partnership

Management, committed.

We back a strong management team that is financially committed to the transaction, with the vision and desire to take the company to the next level.

Value creation

Five ways to grow.

Organic growth, new markets, broader product or service offerings, manufacturing efficiencies and acquisitions, with help from senior executives the partners have worked with closely.

The far shore

A larger, professionally-managed company.

We build entrepreneurial businesses into larger, integrated enterprises. Percheron, a 2010 investment, was merged with three related companies in 2012 and grew from the Northeast to the West and South before its sale in 2026.
