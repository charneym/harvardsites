## Science (sourced)
Site home: "Molecular Zip Code based therapeutics"; "unbiased screening approach to identify molecular 'zip codes' specific to
diseased regions or cell types in the brain"; neuroprotection, blocking neuroinflammation.
Approach page: "identified molecular 'zip codes' by employing in vivo phage display in animal models of brain diseases";
"panel of homing peptides"; TBI "impacts over 2.5 million people annually in the US"; delivery technology licensed from
Sanford Burnham Prebys (SBP); AC01 "first-in-class"; "strong safety and preclinical efficacy data on AC01 with direct effect on
glial activation and neuroinflammation"; AD probes for delivery and non-invasive early detection; biologic for vascular
function; "exploring AC01 application in different optic neuropathies including Glaucoma"; glaucoma "estimated 80 million
people worldwide".
TBI page: AC01 "repeated intravenous injections during the first days after TBI, strongly reduces the size of a brain lesion";
"Fluorescently labeled AC01 peptide accumulates only in the injured region of the brain"; list of independent publications
using the targeting platform (brain and spinal cord injury, 2018–2025).
NIA abstracts: CTGF found in the neurovascular unit of AD brain vessels by in vivo phage screening (Mann et al., 2017);
"expressed in the earliest stages of AD—before plaque formation and cognitive symptoms".
Papers (PubMed): Mann AP, Scodeller P, Hussain S et al. Nat Commun 2016;7:11980, PMID 27351915; Mann AP, Scodeller P,
Hussain S et al. Nat Commun 2017;8:1403, PMID 29123083.
