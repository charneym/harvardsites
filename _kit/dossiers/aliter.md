(https://www.alitercap.com/fund)
- Approach: UK services sector, ambitious support services SMEs; partnership with management; shared risk and reward; don't finance with significant debt; deliberately a handful of platform businesses at any one time; straight talkers. (https://www.alitercap.com/approach)

## Criteria (https://www.alitercap.com/strategy)
UK business services sector; predominantly B2B customer base; good national or regional presence; strong element of recurring or repeat work, or the ability to create this; large, fragmented, resilient markets; typically EBITDA of £1m-£5m, with an ability to invest in larger businesses.
