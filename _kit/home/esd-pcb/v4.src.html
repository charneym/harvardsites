About us

It all started with a customer request

Paul Winter set up Essential Services and Distribution in 1999 to source components for customers he had looked after as a distribution sales rep. When they asked for more, his wife Linda joined to assemble prototypes and small batches, and sub-contract PCB assembly followed. We still work for customers from the 1990s, and many have been with us for over ten years.

  • 2002Essential Services and Distribution becomes a limited company, trading as ESD Ltd.
  • 2014Move to much larger premises, with room to expand and more parking for visitors.
  • 2018A new high-volume surface mount line, installed in a second facility on site.
  • 2019ESD buys Integrated Technology Ltd, adding UL-approved wire looms and product design.
Services

Complete or partial electronic assembly

We can dispatch after any production stage: straight after surface mount, as a partial build, or fully built, tested and packed ready for your shelf.

  • Prototyping

    Quantities from one off

    Single or double sided, surface mount, through-hole or mixed, down to 0402 parts, with feedback on any production concerns.

On our shop floor

How a board moves through ESD

  1. Paste
  2. Place
  3. Reflow
  4. Inspect
  5. Insert
  1. 01 · Paste

    Set up from your files

    Your Gerber files program where each part goes, the BOM components are loaded, and solder paste goes onto the board through a stencil.

  2. 02 · Place

    Pick and place

    A conveyor carries the PCB through the machines. Each holds around 60 component values, and a camera orientates every part before it is placed.

  3. 03 · Reflow

    Several temperature zones

    Board material and component data set the temperature and speed through each zone, for gentle heating and cooling and sound joints.

  4. 04 · Inspect

    Automated optical inspection

    AOI checks the location, type, value and orientation of every component, and our inspection team checks each build before it moves on.

  5. 05 · Insert

    Insertion, hand fit and wave

    Heavier parts go through the board for a strong mechanical joint, ideal for power supplies and anything that vibrates.
