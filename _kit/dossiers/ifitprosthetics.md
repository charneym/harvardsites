## Facts used on the page
- Founded 2010 by Dr. Dillingham; U.S. veteran-owned; makes products in Milwaukee/Wisconsin; engineering partner Advanced Design
  Concepts (Jim Marschalek lead engineer).
- Fit and aligned in a single session with hand tools; no casts, scans or molds; 20 sizes; 7 issued US patents (8470050, 8491667,
  8845755, 10398577, 10806608, 11382775, 11766343); ISO 9001:2015; CE Mark; adapted ISO strength testing; waterproof.
- First and only company with a full line of pediatric and adult adjustable, immediate-fit prostheses (catalogue p.4).
- Products: TT200, TT250/TT250W, TTAC-S, TTAC-N, TF300, pediatric transfemoral advanced closure (catalogue pp.6-16, TF page).
- Publications: catalogue p.22. International: >120 individuals across Botswana, Zambia, Jamaica (catalogue p.27); Botswana 2021,
  2023, 2024 (53 persons over three trips) and Dominican Republic 2022 (site).
- Dillingham bio: Army in Persian Gulf War, NIH investigator, directed P&O clinic at Johns Hopkins (Background page); 2025 AAP
  Distinguished Academician Award, 2022 AANEM Innovation Award (Innovation page).
- Team (site): Dillingham, Marschalek, Ingram Dillingham, Liliana Pezzin, Sarah Spencer, Machele Brass, Jessica Kenia.
