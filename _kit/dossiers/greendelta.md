# Greendelta Corporate Investments (greendelta.nl), facts with sources

- Firm: Greendelta Corporate Investments (Greendelta BV), Sittard, Netherlands. Independent investment company focused on participations in mid-size and larger Dutch SMEs (MKB). Source: https://www.greendelta.nl/
- Positioning (their words): "een slagvaardig onafhankelijk investeringsbedrijf gespecialiseerd in bedrijfsparticipaties in middelgrote en grote MKB ondernemingen"; long-term focus; no specific sector focus; partners experienced in production, trade, services and IT; aims for about four new participations a year. Source: https://www.greendelta.nl/
- "Voor en door ondernemers." Source: https://www.greendelta.nl/over-greendelta
- AIFMD registration regime of the AFM (no licence required). Source: https://www.greendelta.nl/ , https://www.greendelta.nl/toezicht
- Werkwijze: full transaction support by Greendelta (sourcing, selection, valuation, negotiation, arranging acquisition finance, selecting management, management participation, steering due-diligence and documentation experts, assembling the investor fund); afterwards a supervisory role on behalf of investors; entrepreneurs lead; long-term holdings with no fixed exit; companies continue independently; buy & build supported where relevant; always with participating management (MBO, MBI or both). Source: https://www.greendelta.nl/copy-of-verkopers
- Toezicht: monthly reporting by portfolio companies, periodic meetings with management, sounding board, network, coordinates add-ons, refinancing and divestments; investors informed quarterly, at the annual general meeting and ad hoc. Source: https://www.greendelta.nl/toezicht
- Investors: new investors can co-invest with the regular investors; NDA, then information memorandum. Source: https://www.greendelta.nl/copy-of-greendelta-1
- Sellers: (partial) sale, pre-exit, growth partner; non-binding and strictly confidential. Source: https://www.greendelta.nl/copy-of-investeerders
- MBI/MBO: participating management with a proven track record; Greendelta advisory and supervisory. Source: https://www.greendelta.nl/copy-of-investeerders-1
- Criteria: large majority stakes in stable (mid-)size SMEs; all sectors except real estate, hospitality, primary agriculture, contractors, physical retail chains, transport companies; EBITDA (potentially) approx. EUR 1.5m to 3m; well-structured organisation; good prospects / "next level"; solid, proven business case (no start-ups or turnarounds); stable financial track record; full or partial sale (pre-exit), MBO, MBI or a combination; minority participation and financial commitment of management; hands-off; location: all of the Netherlands. Source: https://www.greendelta.nl/copy-of-toezicht
