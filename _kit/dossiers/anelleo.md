## Science (RePORTER abstracts; https://anelleo.com/anelleo-technology/; https://anelleo.com/anelleo-products/)
- Intravaginal rings (IVRs): non-invasive, self-administered, retrievable platform delivering drugs over days to months.
- Manufacturing: Digital Light Synthesis enabled by continuous liquid interface production (CLIP™), via relationship with Carbon, Inc.; rapid, cost-effective manufacture of geometrically variable rings (design, surface area, size, compression).
- Complex geometries give precise, tunable control over release kinetics; total release "can be > 90% of the total drug loaded".
- Patent-pending drug incorporation methods compatible with polar and nonpolar small and large molecules including biologics; library rings matched to pharma molecules "in months, not years"; scalable process.
- IP: expanding portfolio incl. license rights to IP generated at UNC-Chapel Hill.
- Published: unit-cell CAD architectures with DLS (Adv. Mater. Technol., https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1002/admt.202000261); post-fabrication absorption loading of a silicone-based DLS matrix (https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2468519422002075).

## Pipeline
- ANLO102 (AnelleO-PRO): progesterone IVR releasing ~28 days; 2–3 rings cover the 8–10-week ART period. Current options: vaginal gel (Crinone®) and inserts (Endometrin®) 2–3×/day, or daily IM injections. Infertility affects ~13–15% of reproductive-aged US couples; ~1.7 million women treated per year. Stage per CRP aims: IND-enabling studies → IND.
- 5% lidocaine mucoadhesive biodissolvable film for vestibulodynia (R44HD112245): VBD estimated in 11–28% of reproductive-aged US women; aims include pre-IND meeting, IND-enabling studies, IND.
- In research: HRT (postmenopausal low-dose hormones), infectious diseases (incl. STIs), oncology (gynecological cancers).
