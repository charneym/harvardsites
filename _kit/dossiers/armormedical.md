## Company
- Armor Medical Inc., founded June 2022; female-founded and female-led medical device company. "Est. 2022."
- HQ: Saint Louis, Missouri (NIH RePORTER organisation city/state). No street address is published. Phone +1 (314) 530-9239; email support@armormedical.us (the only published address).
- Mission: "to pioneer solutions that protect and empower women's health at every stage of life... rigorous research and empathetic, person-centered design." Maternal aRMOR is "the first commercial product" and "the first in a pipeline of technologies".
- Product: Maternal aRMOR, a wrist-worn sensor that gives providers objective, real-time insight into hemorrhage risk, even when blood loss is internal; reads peripheral blood flow independent of skin pigmentation; "patented technology"; equitable, low-cost. Product slide: automated quantified blood loss (QBL) and personalized compensation alerts. MedTech Dive: the wrist device "uses a laser and a camera to quantify flowing red blood cells"; the body clamps down on peripheral vessels to push blood to vital organs.
- Problem page: ~14 million people a year experience PPH; leading cause of maternal death globally; US: 5% of births, 11% of maternal deaths, $1.8B a year; 90% preventable with early detection and treatment.
- Funding (WashU 2025-10-21): Skandalaris Global Impact Award $25,000 (2023); NIH RADx Tech Maternal Health Challenge phase 3, cumulative $415,000 (2023–24); CDC SBIR Phase I (RePORTER 1R43DP006818-01A1, $295,892, FY2023); ~$700,000 pre-seed from angels, Laerdal Million Lives Foundation and Catalyst by Wellstar (2024–25); MedTech Innovator grand prize $350,000 (2025).
- Recognised by (site logos): SBIR, GIA, Catalyst by Wellstar, Equalize 2023, Hewlett Packard Enterprise, MedTech Innovator 2023, Femovate 2023-24.
