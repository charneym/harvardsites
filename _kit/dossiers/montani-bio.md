# Montani Biotechnologies — dossier (facts with sources only)

- Site: https://montanibio.com (GoDaddy Website Builder 8.0) · Slug: montani-bio · Track: biotech (SPEC §2c) · Format: HOME-ONLY (HOME_BRIEF, 2026-11-19)
- Legal name: Montani Biotechnologies, Inc. ("Montani Bio"). HQ: Morgantown, WV (site footer; BIO listing: Company HQ City Morgantown, West Virginia).
- Hero line (site): "Providing the Signal: Bridging Gaps in Immunity".
- Home (site): "Welcome to the future of immunotherapy. mRNA-LNP technology is transforming the development of next-generation therapeutics. By delivering messenger RNA directly into cells, lipid nanoparticles enable the body to produce precise proteins that can train the immune system to fight cancer and other diseases at their source, mimicking natural processes. Our platform is designed to accelerate the creation of personalized cancer therapies and disease-modifying treatments, combining speed, precision, and adaptability to address some of the most challenging medical needs."
- Platform page (site): "At Montani Bio we believe in the potential of mRNA to empower anti-cancer immunity." Three candidate technologies:
  - Genetic Adjuvants: expression of integral cytokines and chemokines in desirable inflammatory pathways; harness the innate power of the immune system to activate favorable responses to vaccines and immunotherapies. Preclinical: candidate genetic adjuvants suppress tumor growth in immunotherapy-resistant melanoma (VDC-MB-0001, 0002) and increase antibody response levels to antigen (VDC-MB-0002).
  - Autogene Therapies: personalized; the mRNA platform simplifies drug design and production; collaboration with "a world class neoantigen prediction algorithm" delivers potent tumor-specific neoantigens to drive durable anti-tumor immunity; being investigated in melanoma and breast cancer.
  - Targeted Antigens: "Canonical expression of mRNA-encoded payloads in vivo fail to harness the full potential of mRNA-LNP interactions in the host"; iteratively exploring how novel expression signals added in silico could improve presentation and processing of these proteins.
- About (site): incorporated in 2025 as a spinout of the Vaccine Development Center at West Virginia University Health Sciences Center; founded by Drs. Damron and Lee to commercialise their most exciting discoveries; goal: "continue to catalyze the potential of technologies from our academic space while building a research enterprise of our own."
- BIO listing (https://convention.bio.org/2026-sessions-and-courses/montani-biotechnologies-inc): "Montani Bio is a WV-based vaccines and therapeutics company focused on the expansion of the mRNA platform into new areas of disease. The company spun out of the Vaccine Development Center at West Virginia University in 2025 in order to advance exciting IP for cancer therapeutics towards the clinic." Start-Up Stadium session, BIO International Convention 2026, San Diego, June 23, 2026, room 5B; focus area Oncology; speaker Katherine S. Lee, PhD, CEO and Co-Founder. Year founded 2025.
