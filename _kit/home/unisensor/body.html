Light that tells plastics, gases and liquids apart.

UNISENSOR builds spectroscopic systems for process analysis in Karlsruhe: sorting machines that separate plastics regardless of their colour, and online monitors that find the smallest traces of contamination in CO₂ gas, beverages and water containers.

A family-run high-tech company for the process analysis of gases, liquids and solids.

Professor Dr.-Ing. Gunther Krieg founded UNISENSOR in 1990 on his long experience in opto-electronics and sensor technology. Since then a team of highly qualified staff, working closely with industry and technical universities, has developed systems that are used around the world and have set new standards in process technology.

One principle, four industries.

Every UNISENSOR system reads what light does as it meets a material. What changes is the material, the speed and the trace it has to find.

PlasticsCO₂ gasBottlingWater

Laser spectroscopy sorts plastics.

POWERSORT 200 separates previously inseparable plastics with the highest precision and speed, so valuable raw materials can be recovered for good. Europe's largest maker of food-grade rPET, APPE, secures its quality with it.

Absorption finds traces in CO₂.

CARBOSCAN 300 is a turnkey, factory-calibrated system for the online monitoring of CO₂ gas. Optical absorption spectroscopy identifies even the tiniest traces of contaminants, down to sub-ppb levels, from the light they absorb.

Online CO₂ control for bottlers.

CARBOSCAN 150 monitors CO₂ precisely and fully automatically during production, safeguarding continuous quality through carbonation.

Broadband spectroscopy checks every container.

AQUASCREEN inspects refillable water containers of 5 to 19 litres for diesel, petrol, engine oil, hydrocarbons or cleaning agents in the ppm or sub-ppm range, with gas-tight sampling and up to 4,000 containers an hour.
