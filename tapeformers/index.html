Standard range

Ten models, from 5 mm to 480 mm tape

Each model is a custom-made forming tray for a band of tape widths, built to the cable diameter, tape width and tape thickness you give us.

Tolerance

A TapeFormer exit is a fixed dimension. Use it with a larger cable or tape than it was made for and it will jam; a smaller one may work with a closing die, but needs testing.

How we work

Made for your cable, not off a shelf

  1. Specify
  2. Build
  3. Form
  4. Guarantee
  1. 01 · Specify

    Tell us the cable and the tape

    We need the core diameter, tape width and tape thickness. If a tape is not in our list, our engineers ask for a sample to test.

  2. 02 · Build

    Built to order in Mountsorrel

    We hold laser-cut and pre-machined parts, so our team can make your TapeFormer as soon as the order arrives. We keep no finished stock.

  3. 03 · Form

    On your line

    The tape enters flat and leaves closed around the cable. TapeFormers can be piggy-backed so more than one tape goes on in the same line.

  4. 04 · Guarantee

    Six months to get it right

    If a TapeFormer does not form just as you need, we alter it or replace it to your total satisfaction.

NM-B cable

USA building wire TapeFormer

Based on our Model C and D dimensions, it allows complete longitudinal paper tape wrapping during the manufacture of building wire. Two TapeFormers are fitted together with a ceramic guide for the copper ground wire. A version forms tape around the ground wire only.

  • Insulated core diameter, each core
  • Ground wire diameter
  • Ground wire tape width, normally 0.875″ (22 mm)
  • Assembly tape width, which we can recommend
  • Tape thickness

About us

A factory built for one product

TapeFormers Ltd is based in the Midlands, close to Leicester, in a factory specifically designed for the manufacture of TapeFormers. Our skilled and experienced team makes each TapeFormer to your requirements and aims to deliver it quickly, to help you meet deadlines.

We are members of the International Wire & Machinery Association and the East Midlands Chamber, with agents in Japan, Germany, the USA, South Korea, Thailand and South Africa.
