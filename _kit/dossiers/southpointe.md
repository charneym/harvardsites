# SouthPointe Ventures — dossier (facts with sources)

- Firm: SouthPointe Ventures, LLC ("SPV"), "a private equity holding company" (logo tagline). Site: https://southpointeventures.com
- Founded in Atlanta in 2003. Invests private equity capital and professional management expertise in established, lower middle market companies located within one time-zone distance from Atlanta. (https://southpointeventures.com/about-us/)
- Mission: "to make smart, fair, sustainable investments in partnership with talented and committed management teams in companies and industries with attractive, long-term investment return potential." Operating backgrounds; returns "the result of running companies better, not financial engineering." Can bring new leadership where a founder/owner is retiring, or augment an existing team. No set holding period. Pillars: Operations Focused; Management friendly; Long-term, patient partners. (about-us)
- Tagline: "building the future, one company at a time." (tag.jpg on every page)
- Values: "It Matters How We Do Business." Mutual Respect, Gratitude, Character, Accountability, Communication, Hustle (texts paraphrased/quoted from https://southpointeventures.com/our-values/)
- Criteria (https://southpointeventures.com/investments/): Geography within one time zone of Atlanta (except add-ons); Industries: Manufacturing, Distribution, Business Services; Revenue $5–50M (any size for add-ons); EBITDA $1–5M (any size for add-ons); Avoid: Real Estate, Oil & Gas, Turnarounds, Start-ups.
- HQ: Regions Plaza, 1180 West Peachtree St., NW, Suite 1910, Atlanta, GA 30309; (404) 249-6000. Contact page: Amy McGaughey, Office Manager, amcgaughey@southpointeventures.com (https://southpointeventures.com/contact-us/). Time zone: America/New_York.
