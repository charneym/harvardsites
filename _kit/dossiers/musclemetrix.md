## Story
Residual lower limb in fine line (shin, tibia, calf muscle) in a socket with pylon and bionic ankle-foot. Step 1 beads appear in the muscle; step 2 the external array reads them and the bead gap shortens as the muscle contracts; step 3 the signal runs to the ankle and the foot plantarflexes.
