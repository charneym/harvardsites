One injection that turns fat cells into lasting medicine factories

Remedium Bio is developing one-time gene therapies for obesity, type 2 diabetes and other diseases now treated with repeated protein injections. Fat cells under the skin make the therapeutic protein themselves, at a dose that can be adjusted.

Delivery

Lipid nanoparticles carry a gene to the fat layer

A familiar under-the-skin injection releases lipid nanoparticles (violet) among the fat cells. Each one carries DNA that encodes a therapeutic protein, switched on by a cell-specific promoter.

Expression

Inside the cell, the gene switches on

The DNA cargo reaches the nucleus and the fat cell starts producing the protein. Fat cells are long-lived, with many persisting in the body for years, so one treatment can keep working.

Secretion

The protein flows straight into the blood

Fat tissue is a highly vascularised endocrine organ. The protein (teal) leaves the cell, enters the neighbouring capillary and is carried throughout the body.

Result

A steady supply from a single treatment

Many cells produce at once, aiming for multi-year expression. Because individual fat cells can be lost without harming the tissue, the dose can be adjusted after treatment.

Many medicines for chronic disease are proteins that patients inject under the skin again and again. Remedium Bio wants to replace those repeat injections with one: a gene therapy that lets the body’s own fat cells make the protein, for years, at a dose that can still be adjusted.

The Prometheus™ platform

Five steps from one injection to years of protein

Prometheus is Remedium’s adjustable gene therapy platform. It turns fat cells under the skin (adipocytes) into durable sources of therapeutic proteins, with the aim of replacing many protein therapies that are injected today.

01
The cargo

Design the gene

A genetic construct (DNA, RNA or another nucleic acid derivative) carries a gene for a therapeutic protein, switched on by a tissue- or cell-specific promoter and other genetic elements.

02
The vector

Package it

The cargo goes into a delivery vector that can be viral, lipid or polymeric, with or without targeting. The obesity and diabetes work uses lipid nanoparticles injected under the skin.

03
Familiar and accessible

Inject under the skin

Subcutaneous injection is already the familiar, widely used route for peptides, proteins and antibodies, so treatment needs no new procedure.

04
Long-lived, well supplied

Fat cells become factories

Many adipocytes persist in the body for years. Fat tissue is a highly vascularized endocrine organ, so the protein it makes is carried throughout the body.

05
Renewable and adaptable

Adjust the dose

Individual fat cells can be lost without harming the tissue as a whole, which is what lets the dose be adjusted after treatment.
