## Science / product (sources noted)
- Phase II abstract: extraction device automatically combines a precisely controlled pH shock to the blood sample, human DNA elimination, filter-based pathogen capture and washing, ultrasonic disruption/DNA extraction, and automated elution of pathogen DNA; eluate used directly with a companion pathogen class-specific PCR test that runs on standard, open-platform instruments already available in 80% of U.S. hospital labs. Sepsis morbidity increases ~7% per hour of delayed correct treatment.
- Phase I outcomes report (NSF, modified 05/16/2025): device rapidly (~30 minutes) separated all nine tested common sepsis pathogens (and their DNA) from 6 mL whole blood; identified at 1–7 viable pathogen cells per mL with off-the-shelf PCR kits; species: Staphylococcus aureus, Listeria monocytogenes, Enterococcus faecium, Escherichia coli, Klebsiella pneumoniae, Pseudomonas aeruginosa, Candida auris, Candida krusei, Candida tropicalis; methicillin resistance (MRSA) detected at clinically relevant concentrations. Device mixes chemistry, mechanical shearing, filtration and ultrasonics. Sepsis: ~1.7M people/yr in the U.S., ~270,000 deaths, ~1 in 5 deaths worldwide; blood culture takes 12 hours to 7 days; empiric broad-spectrum treatment effective about 70% of the time. Conventional direct-from-blood extraction handles ~1/30th of the blood volume an effective sepsis test needs. Product named "Beta XB100 Blood Processor" with single-use extraction cartridge.
- Phase I release (17 Sep 2024): XB100 platform with 100x pathogen DNA concentration factor and 10,000x human DNA reduction factor; "just add water" PCR kits provided by BioGX. https://www.newenglandhemolytics.com/news/neh-news-new-england-hemolytics-awarded-a-national-science-foundation-sbir-phase-1-research-grant
- Products page specifications (*subject to change): input 4–7 mL whole blood in EDTA Vacutainer-type tube; output 50–100 µL (DNA in water) ready for PCR or sequencing; pathogen capture efficiency >90% for pathogen cells larger than 0.4 µm; lysing efficiency 50–90% depending on organism; human DNA removal 99.9%; total process ~15 minutes (1 minute hands-on); one-touch operation via touchscreen; active process monitoring; sensitivity depends on downstream assay, can be as good as 3 CFU/mL or better; instrument 10" × 5" × 7", 5 lb. https://www.newenglandhemolytics.com/products
