## Science (verbatim-sourced)
From the NHLBI award abstract (1R44HL182563):
- "Duchenne muscular dystrophy (DMD) is a severe genetic disorder caused by mutations in the DMD gene, leading to
  progressive skeletal and cardiac muscle degeneration."
- "existing approaches like micro-dystrophins provide incomplete restoration of function, particularly in cardiac
  muscle."
- The project develops and validates **KNA-123**, using the **Split Intein-Mediated Protein Ligation for Gene
  Therapy (SIMPLI-GT)** platform: "This approach enables the delivery of a larger, more functional midi-dystrophin
  transgene using dual AAV vectors, overcoming the size limitations of current gene therapies."
- Three aims: cardiac biodistribution, dystrophin expression and functional rescue after systemic AAVMYO
  midi-dystrophin in dystrophin-deficient rats; rescue of dystrophic phenotypes in human iPSC-derived DMD
  cardiomyocytes; a qualified functional potency assay using 3D engineered heart tissue from DMD patients.
From the earlier SIMPLI-GT award (1R44AR083801-01A1): AAV cargo capacity is ~5 kb; dual-vector recombination gives
large proteins locally but low levels after systemic delivery; "using AAV-Myo, we found that low dose
(2x10^13 vg/kg) generates large dystrophins in striated muscles with nearly complete physiological rescue."
From 4R44AR084978-02 (DM1): DM1 is caused by an expanded CTG repeat (50–4000 copies) in DMPK; the programme tests
interfering-RNA-loaded nanovesicles derived from human cells against toxic DMPK mRNA.

Platform, as described on kineabio.com/science:
- **SIMPLI-GT™** — "Split Intein Mediated Protein Ligation Gene Therapy" overcomes AAV packaging limitations by
  joining protein segments together to form large functional proteins.
- **MyoXpress™** — a proprietary library of muscle-specific expression cassettes that tunes therapeutic protein
  levels in skeletal and cardiac muscle while minimising expression elsewhere.
- **Myotropic capsids** — engineered AAVs for neuromuscular disease; naturally occurring serotypes such as AAV9
  require enormous doses, and better muscle targeting lowers dose, cost and liver exposure.
- **BioDrone™** — nanovesicles derived from human cells, enabling repeat delivery of genetic cargo without
  triggering significant immune responses (for gain-of-function indications such as DM1).

Indications described on kineabio.com/pipeline: Duchenne muscular dystrophy; congenital muscular dystrophy MDC1C
(the paediatric form of FKRP-deficient LGMDR9); dysferlinopathies (LGMD2B, Miyoshi myopathy, distal myopathy with
anterior tibial onset, asymptomatic hyperCKemia); myotonic dystrophy type 1; Duchenne cardiomyopathy.
Prevalence figures quoted on that page: DMD 4.8 per 100,000 (95% CI 3.6–6.3); LGMD2B about 1 in 300,000;
DM1 1 in 2,100 births (Neurology, PMID 33472919).

## Peer-reviewed publications (PubMed)
- McCormack NM, … Chamberlain JS, Mack DL, Childers MK, Jaiswal JK, Tasfaout H. "Use of split intein and myotropic
  AAV vectors enables effective preclinical gene therapy for dysferlinopathy." *Science Translational Medicine*,
  7 Oct 2026;18(870):eaei5938. PMID 42842721. (Childers' affiliation on the paper is Kinea Bio Inc., Seattle.)
  Dysferlin cDNA is 6.2 kb against AAV's ~4.7 kb capacity; split inteins reconstitute scarless full-length
  dysferlin; systemic dual AAVMYO1 improved muscle strength, increased contractile force and reduced
  histopathology at both presymptomatic and late stages, and outperformed the homologous-recombination dual-AAV
  strategy.
- Tasfaout H, Halbert CL, McMillen TS, Allen JM, Reyes TR, Flint GV, Grimm D, Hauschka SD, Regnier M,
  Chamberlain JS. "Split intein-mediated protein trans-splicing to express large dystrophins." *Nature*,
  Aug 2024;632(8023):192-200. PMID 39020181.
- Tasfaout H, et al. "Expression of full-length dystrophin reverses muscular dystrophy defects in young and old
  mdx4cv mice." *Journal of Clinical Investigation*, 1 Aug 2025. PMID 40493400.
