## Activity (pre-screen: OK; oncology cell therapy, no pathogens/toxins/biodefence)
- **NIH NCI SBIR Phase I, 1R43CA310292-01** (2026-09-10 to 2027-08-31; $398,039): "Development of a Universal Next Generation
  T Cell Receptor Cancer Therapy: Preclinical Proof-of-Concept and Optimization". Contact PI Lyndsey S Crump; Richard C Duke also
  listed as PI. https://reporter.nih.gov/project-details/11315490
  Abstract (source for the TCR programme copy): a "universal" TCR-T therapy; limitations addressed are T cell exhaustion, low
  persistence, weakly immunogenic tumors and the lack of a universal approach; tested against acute myeloid leukemia (AML);
  invariant natural killer T (iNKT) cells recognise non-classical MHC-like proteins such as CD1d with highly conserved TCRs;
  synthetic CD1d-reactive TCRs (TCR^CD1d) show cytotoxicity and cytokine production against CD1d-expressing tumors; aims:
  identify a lead TCR candidate, validate against AML in vitro and in vivo, optimise the MightyTCR-T regimen including with
  approved AML drugs, and assess biodistribution, PK and PD in mouse models.
- **NIH NCI SBIR Phase II, R44CA268313** (2022-09-01 to 2025-08-31; $1,000,000 in FY2022 under 1R44CA268313-01A1, PI Rene
  Gonzalez, and $1,000,000 in FY2023 under 5R44CA268313-02, PI Richard C Duke; $2,000,000 total):
  "Next Generation Autologous TIL Cancer Therapy: Development of GMP manufacturing process".
  https://reporter.nih.gov/project-details/10685604 and https://reporter.nih.gov/project-details/10472171
  Abstract (source for the platform and GMP copy): CD8α (extracellular) fused to MyD88 (intracellular), CD8α:MyD88, activates
  MyD88 signalling strictly upon engagement of the TCR with the MHC-antigen on tumor cells; increases responses to weak and
  suboptimal tumor antigens including neoantigens; reduces entry into an exhausted state; universal, usable regardless of HLA
  type. Aim 1: GMP g-retroviral vectors with the CDMO Vigene Biosciences. Aim 2: GMP coR8:Amp™ TILs with the Gates
  Biomanufacturing Facility and the Cell Therapy Operations Program at the University of Colorado Anschutz Medical Campus,
  leading to pre-IND and IND filings for a Phase I trial in immune-refractory solid tumors.
- Total NIH SBIR funding across the two programmes: $2,398,039.

## From their own site (quoted or paraphrased, nothing invented)
- Tagline (trademarked on their site): "Reviving the patient's immune system to destroy cancer—forever.™"
- "TrAMPoline Pharma is a cellular immunotherapy company that is developing personalized (autologous) therapies for solid tumor
  cancers." Lead candidate MightyTIL™: a gene-engineered tumor infiltrating lymphocyte targeting orphan indications (sarcoma,
  head and neck cancer) and melanoma; "significantly enhanced anti-tumor activity in multiple animal tumor models as compared
  with conventional unmodified TIL".
- Solid tumors "make up 90% of all cancers"; "~60% of cancer patients experience T cell immunotherapy failures (including
  checkpoint inhibitors, CAR T cells, TCR-engineered, TIL)".
- MightyTIL™ leverages the patented coR8:Amp™ technology: enhanced binding of the TCR to cancer cells plus enhanced TCR
  signalling domains. Claimed advantages: reactivity to multiple tumor antigens (vs a single antigen for CAR T, often lost);
  higher functional activity and more proliferation/survival receptors; increased persistence and reduced exhaustion through
  lower expression of key checkpoint receptors; less IL-2 required (IL-2 toxicity limits TIL efficacy); long-lived anti-tumor
  activity, responding months after transfer.
- Team page (portraits reused from their site, trampolinepharma/*.webp):
  - Eduardo Davila, PhD — Co-founder and Chief Scientific Officer; professor, Department of Medicine, Medical Oncology,
    University of Colorado, Director of Cancer Immunotherapy; his team dissected MyD88's role in T cell effector function and
    persistence; inventor of the two patents behind Living Pharmaceuticals, Inc., acquired by Miltenyi Biotec in 2017.
  - Richard C Duke, PhD — President; co-scientific founder and former CEO of GlobeImmune; involved in Newellink, ApopLogic
    Pharmaceuticals, Sierra Neuropharmaceuticals, MenoGeniX, PeptiVir and Aurora Oncology; those Colorado companies raised more
    than $200 million; 30+ years in biomedical research, 70+ publications, tenured faculty at the University of Colorado;
    McGill (BSc, MSc) and University of Colorado (PhD).
  - Lyndsey Crump, PhD — Senior Scientist (joined 2023); guides one of the adoptive cell therapy pipelines; cancer biologist,
    MS Biomedical Sciences (University of Wisconsin), PhD Cancer Biology (University of Colorado Anschutz, where she also did a
    post-doc). Contact PI on the 2026 NCI award.
  - Jing Liu, MD, PhD — Senior Scientist; four years in adenovirus gene therapy, six in immunotherapy; viral vector
    optimisation, homologous engineering, T cell engineering, adoptive cell therapy.
