## Facts used (all from plakoustherapeutics.com, its press-release PDFs, and NIH RePORTER)
- Mission: help patients overcome inflammation-driven diseases, from infancy to old age, using regenerative biotherapeutics.
- HPE: human placental extract, a biologic with anti-inflammatory and cellular proliferation capabilities; processing collects
  and preserves over 950 proliferative, developmental and anti-inflammatory proteins and chemokines from donated full-term
  placental tissue; inhibits inflammation directly and prevents over-activation of immune cells.
- Protego-PD™: orphan drug and rare pediatric disease designations for prevention of NEC (June 2020 release).
- July 2025 release: NIH renews year 2 of $2,925,000 SBIR Phase IIB (NICHD); IND-enabling preclinical safety studies; pre-IND
  FDA interactions; HPE "an orally delivered cell-free biotherapeutic ... from post-delivery placentas"; GMP-grade product;
  $20M Series A to complete IND and a Phase 1b/2a trial; >90% of 3,000 annual US NEC cases in babies <30 weeks; 30% mortality;
  ~$4 billion NICU and hospital costs; "accelerating intestinal maturation of premature infants".
- Value of HPE page: preclinical efficacy, mimicking nature, safety, lower hospital costs (texts restated).
- Future clinical targets page: NDI, BPD, ARDS, neurodegenerative (oligodendrocyte proliferation in vitro), osteoarthritis
  (cartilage protection in joint destabilization models).
- Pediatric malabsorption: preclinical weight recovery and weight gain in models of extreme prematurity.
- Team and board as listed on /leadership and /board-of-directors. Mayo Clinic know-how license (Aug 2020), NCBiotech >$300k
  (July 2022), Kalinovich board (May 2023).
- HQ address: 755 Highland Oaks Dr, Suite 103, Winston-Salem, NC 27103 (2020 press releases). Phone 908-625-3295 (press contact).
