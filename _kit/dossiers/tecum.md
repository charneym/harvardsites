## Investment criteria (published figures)
Two strategies shown on site (https://www.tecum.com/junior-capital , /control-equity); a third, the Tecum Essential Services Fund, appears only in a fact-sheet PDF.
**Junior Capital** (mezzanine debt, minority equity, opportunistic control equity): Investment range $5-$20MM; revenue $10-$100MM; EBITDA $3-$10MM; EBITDA margin >10%; gross leverage <4.0x EBITDA; geography "Primary operations in the U.S."; hold up to seven years. Transaction types: Acquisition Financing, Family Wealth Transfer, Independent Sponsor Buyouts, Leveraged Buyouts, Management Buyouts, Minority Recapitalizations. Target industries: Business Services; Capital Equipment with P&M; Consumer-Oriented; Food & Beverage; Healthcare Services; Industrial & Environmental Services; Precision Manufacturing; Transportation & Logistics; Value-Added Distribution. Characteristics: committed management team; defensible market position / differentiated product or service; high barriers to entry; market leadership in sustainable, non-cyclical growth niches; strong free cash flow; "No real estate ventures, start-up investments, project financing, or bridge loans". Home page card: "Subordinated debt and equity co-investment capital to support buyouts led by independent sponsors, family offices, committed funds, and management teams". Junior Capital fact sheet (PDF, July 2025): investment range $5 to $30MM; "Equity Co-Investment: Up to 50%". Release: average investment $5-$20 million per transaction. 
**Control Equity** (Tecum Equity Alpha Management, a multi-family office representing "several small to medium-sized family offices and high-net-worth investors"): revenue $10-$100MM; EBITDA $2-$6MM (fact sheet Jan 2026 says $2MM-$5MM EBITDA, Midwest Region, "Continuity with Management Team", flexible time horizon); margin >10%; gross leverage <4.0x; "Long-term investment horizon" ("without the timing pressure and restrictions of a limited-term fund"). Types: Family Wealth Transfer, Independent Sponsor Buyouts, LBOs, MBOs. Industries: Business Services, Precision Manufacturing, Value-Added Distribution (fact sheet adds non-discretionary B2B / Essential Services). "Control equity investment capital in buyout and management-led recapitalization transactions in growth-oriented businesses." Fact-sheet add-on criteria: "+$5MM revenue, +10% EBITDA margins".
**Tecum Essential Services Fund (TESF)** (fact sheet Nov 2026, https://www.tecum.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Tecum-Capital-TESF-Fund-Fact-Sheet-Nov-2026.pdf): "a committed fund that invests equity capital alongside leading independent sponsors in lower-middle-market essential services businesses critical to U.S. infrastructure. We are first time institutional capital in change of control deals." $3MM-$15MM EBITDA; US; continuity with management; 3-7 years; check size $10MM-$30MM (up to $50MM with co-investments). Focus: waste management and environmental services; grid maintenance and utilities; data, communications and connectivity infrastructure; specialty material handling and field logistics.

## Concept build notes
- Palette mapping (BK): logo charcoal #343333 → text/bands (#24272b); logo blue #4a90cd → --acc, deepened to #2f6fa8 for small text on paper (AA), deep blue #375f94 → --acc2; ground: warm paper #f3f1ec (the one light-ground site in this batch, distinct from Continuim, also Pittsburgh). Fonts: Hanken Grotesk (close to their Lato) and Fraunces italic for the meaningful word.
- Logo: raster only (216x72 PNG); redrawn as a vector of the split-circle T mark and TECUM / CAPITAL wordmark in their colours.
- Hero: two currents (charcoal and Tecum blue) meeting and running on together: Tecum = "with you", with the Ohio River Valley origin the firm cites.
- Story: Tecum's four-phase formula for success on one shared course (align, plan, support with experience/resources/flexible capital, execute and re-assess).
- Figures used: founded 2006; fourth SBIC fund (2025); 190+ platform and add-on acquisitions (junior-capital page). Fund IV size not stated (sources differ).
- Photos used: all 16 headshots (Josh Savacool's 364px file on the card only), founders group photo (Partnership page). Not used: skyline and generic imagery, portfolio logos (companies set in type).
