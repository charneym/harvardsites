Phase I 1R41NS143585-01, $454,437, 2025-09-23 (https://reporter.nih.gov/project-details/11186306), "Development and Validation of Efficacy Biomarkers for QTE Therapy in Friedreich's Ataxia". - Science (from the NIH abstracts): QTE therapy is multi-targeted. In FRDAkd mice it outperformed omaveloxolone: it enhanced FRDA target proteins, improved cardiac function, reduced iron overload and increased survival. The STTR Phase I covers proteomic and transcriptomic biomarkers in blood and muscle. Phase II plans cover cross-species validation including primates, human testing with industry partners, and progress toward an IND. The R21 covers PBPK dose and formulation, synergy, bioavailability and BBB penetration. The commercial strategy is planned with a patient advocacy group (orphan-drug benefits).
