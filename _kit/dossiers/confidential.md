- Form ADV (filed 03/27/2026): RAUM $161,889,433 (discretionary $161,095,240 + non-discretionary $794,193), 132 accounts; 18 individuals + 51 HNW individuals among client types; 1 employee. Schedule A: ADAMS, CRAIG, MAXWELL, President/Chief Compliance Officer, ownership E (75%+).
- Brochure (Part 2A, 3/27/2026): Personal Resource Management (PRM), "a holistic approach to optimizing the potential value of a client's personal resources using detailed financial forecasting models and analysis. Client-specific asset allocation models diversify across active, passive, tactical and dynamic management disciplines and across all major asset classes."
  - Process: meet and gather suitability information (objectives, risk tolerance, income, expenses, savings) → initial financial forecast model combining inflation, tax-rate and return assumptions ("While we do not expect any forecast to be correct, if we are fortunate, potential problems will show up in advance.") → asset allocation targets to (1) match requirements, (2) monitor and measure progress, (3) make changes as circumstances evolve → ongoing monitoring; model revisited periodically.
  - Third-party advisor screen: five-year history under same management; ranked on return vs risk; top ten ranked on up/down markets; five-year quarterly consistency of top two or three; work environment investigated. Investments expected to remain at least three years.
  - Non-supervisory: PRM "Executive Summary & Guidebook" reports (max flat fee $5,500); "Special Issue" reports hourly (max $450). PRM Strategic Planning Services to corporate employees through corporate benefit plan programs.
  - Fee schedule (max annual): $25,000–$250,000 1.50%; $250,001–$750,000 1.25%; $750,001–$1,500,000 1.00%; over $1.5M negotiable. No performance-based fees.
  - Clients: individuals, trusts, estates, beneficiaries, pension and profit sharing plans, charitable organizations. No account minimum in general.
  - Review: accounts directly managed updated daily; third-party-managed reviewed weekly; reviews by Craig M. Adams; quarterly reports (cost, income and performance per position), reconciled to custodial statements.
- Form CRS (2020): "PRM services include financial analysis/reviews, creation of specific investment allocation target models, investment management/selection services and continuing quarterly progress reporting. Investment allocation models are updated annually or more frequently."
