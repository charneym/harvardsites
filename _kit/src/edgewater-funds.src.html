Approach

Built by people who have run companies.

Most of Edgewater's Partners have been CEOs of public or private companies. They focus on executive-led, buy-and-build opportunities in fragmented industries. One recent platform shows how that works.

Nationwide Cooling Group · 1 of 3

Back an executive‑led platform.

Nationwide Cooling Group was formed in 2024 as a commercial and industrial HVAC/R coil platform, based in Brownsville, Tennessee, and led by CEO Hal Liller.

Nationwide Cooling Group · 2 of 3

Add the right pieces.

In April 2025 it acquired four coil makers and distributors: Precision Coils in Brownsville, Specialty Coils in Longview, Texas, Emergent Coils in Denver and JMG Thermal in Lexington, Tennessee. “Nationwide is now one of the largest independent coil manufacturers in the country,” said Liller.

Nationwide Cooling Group · 3 of 3

Widen what the platform can do.

Cooney Coil & Energy (King of Prussia, Pennsylvania) brought engineering in December 2025. Protecall (Scott Township, Pennsylvania) added coatings and indoor-air-quality services, with branches in four states, in March 2026. Metcam (Alpharetta, Georgia) added sheet-metal fabrication in July 2026.
