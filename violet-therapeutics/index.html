Medicines that protect synapses in Alzheimer's disease.

Violet Therapeutics maps how brain cells talk to each other. Its lead program, a small-molecule EphB3 inhibitor, aims to quiet the glial crosstalk that drives neuroinflammation and synapse loss.

Cell-to-cell signals

Brain cells are in constant conversation.

Astrocytes, microglia and neurons signal to one another all the time. Violet's C-to-C Engine builds 3D maps of these interactions, revealing thousands of them in a single tissue.

The target

One link drives the inflammation: EphB3.

Mapping a full neuroinflammatory cascade singled out EphB3, a receptor on astrocytes that mediates their contact with microglia and fuels neuroinflammation.

The medicine

A small molecule blocks the link.

VT-001 is Violet's EphB3 inhibitor, described as potent, highly selective and able to reach the brain. It is designed to reduce microglia-astrocyte inflammation.

The goal

Synapses preserved, cognition protected.

In two Alzheimer's mouse models, VT-001 prevented cognitive deficits and restored synapse markers in spinal fluid to normal levels.

Science

From a map of cell conversations to a medicine

The C-to-C Engine is Violet's platform for finding cell-to-cell interactions. It grew out of barcoded viral tracing of single-cell interactions in central nervous system inflammation, and builds maps from many tissue types.

BrainSpinal cordLungLiverGutSkinTumors
01

Map

Build C-to-C Communication Maps: detailed 3D maps of disease biology that show which cells are talking to which.

02

Identify interactions

Each map reveals thousands, or even millions, of interactions between individual cells.

03

Find the target

Mapping the full interactome of a neuroinflammatory cascade singled out EphB3, an astrocyte receptor tyrosine kinase that works with microglia to drive neuroinflammation. It is the first target the engine identified.

04

Block it

VT-001, a small-molecule EphB3 inhibitor described as potent, highly selective and able to reach the brain, aims to reduce microglia-astrocyte inflammation.

05

Protect synapses

The goal is to improve cognition and synaptic health in neurodegenerative disease, through glial-mediated synaptic preservation and repair.
