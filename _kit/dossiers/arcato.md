## The company
Arcato Laboratories, Inc., 1800 N Greene Street Ste E, Greenville, NC 27834 (site footer). Developing ARC-001 Gel, a novel,
proprietary, sustained-release single-dose gel combining benzocaine, lidocaine and bupivacaine (each with a different
onset, potency and duration of action), placed once into the tooth socket after third molar extraction; no needle; targets
pain relief over 5-7 days. Status (site): preclinical complete, CMC manufacturing scaled, IND approved by FDA, Phase I
completed. Engaged with FDA, aligned with its May 2026 draft guidance for non-opioid analgesics (release).
Phase 1 (release): primary safety endpoint achieved, supporting Phase 2/3; exploratory endpoints suggest pain reduction in
the first 24 h and relief for up to one week; placebo patients self-reported a need for opioids twice as often.
Note: an unrelated(?) 2011 "Arcato Laboratories" (OraWax, Golden Pine Ventures) appears in old listings; not used.
