## Activity (pre-screen: OK; cardiac drug and diagnostics, no pathogens/toxins/biodefence)
- **NIH NHLBI SBIR (Direct-to-Phase II), R44HL174138** "Nourexal™: A Novel Bioenergetic Drug to Increase Donor Heart Utilization",
  contact PI Salwa A Elgebaly, Nour Heart, Inc., Vienna VA:
  - 1R44HL174138-01A1, notice 2024-09-18, $1,658,923 — https://reporter.nih.gov/project-details/11003902
  - 3R44HL174138-01A1S1 (supplement), notice 2025-01-16, $50,000 — https://reporter.nih.gov/project-details/11209264
  - 5R44HL174138-02, notice 2025-08-29, $1,923,922 — https://reporter.nih.gov/project-details/11194294
  - Total $3,632,845 (shown as "$3.6M").
  Abstract (source for problem, story and pipeline copy): >20,000 US heart transplants needed a year, <4,000 performed, >12,000
  donors; 6 hearts from every 10 donors unused; rejected because transport is limited to 4 hours; CCrP granted FDA Orphan Drug
  Designation (DRU-2015-4951); enhances myocardial energy stores during ischemia, reduces injury, inhibits inflammation and fibrosis,
  restores function after extended cold storage of rat and dog hearts; 1.2 g/kg into rat donors protected hearts during harvesting
  and allowed 22 h storage with full recovery of grafted hearts for 3 days; aims: GMP CCrP + CMC, IND-enabling safety in rats, dogs
  and human unused cadaver donors, pilot pig study extending cold storage to 6 h overseen by Dr. O.H. Frazier at the Texas Heart
  Institute ("three-step clinical protocol", CCrP given once to donors, not recipients), Phase I protocol and IND (Pre-IND PIND 125867).

## From their own site
- Mission: "to develop and commercialize diagnostic and therapeutic products for the early detection, prevention, and treatment of
  myocardial ischemia in patients with ischemic heart disease."
- Nourexal® (CCrP): synthetic analogue of creatine phosphate, more stable, keeps synthesising ATP under acidic/anaerobic conditions
  where CrP stops; phosphorylates ADP under limited O2; only company producing it, under its issued chemical patent and a pending
  process patent. Applications: heart transplantation, cardiac surgery, interventional cardiology.
- Nourin® miRNAs (miR-137, miR-106b): rule-in/rule-out of myocardial ischemia before infarction, benefits for women; sensitivity
  >98%, specificity >97% vs non-ischemic controls with comorbidities; qPCR (clinics) and point-of-care (ED).
- Team page: Salwa Elgebaly PhD (Founder and CEO; FACC 2024), Charles Van Buren MD, Robert Poston MD, Michael A. Azrin MD,
  Hussien Rizk MD, Robert Christenson PhD, Nashwa El-Khazragy MD, Tamer Elbayoumi PhD, Joseph Fortunak PhD.
- Founded 2006 in Hartford, Connecticut (24-7PressRelease, 2019-11-27, datelined Vienna, VA). Phone (860) 680-8860.
- Publications used in News (DOIs checked): JACC 2024 ACC.24 abstract 10.1016/S0735-1097(24)03141-3; EHJ 44 Suppl 2 (2023)
