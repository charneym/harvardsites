Cell-to-cell signaling in the brain

Quieting the harmfulconversationsbetween brain cells.

Violet Therapeutics maps how brain cells signal to one another with the C-to-C Engine, to find the interactions that drive neurodegeneration. Our lead program is a small-molecule inhibitor of EphB3, the first target the engine identified.

The science →Our program
01 · Conversation

Brain cells talkconstantly.

Microglia and astrocytes, the brain's immune and support cells, signal to each other all the time. In neurodegenerative disease some of that crosstalk turns harmful and fuels neuroinflammation, which is “not only a result of neurodegeneration but also a crucial player.”

Zhang et al., as cited on violettx.com

02 · The C-to-C Engine

A map of who istalking to whom.

Violet builds 3D C-to-C Communication Maps of how cells interact in healthy and diseased tissue. The engine then mines those maps for the interactions that drive disease, and for targets drugs can reach.

03 · Target

One harmful exchange:EphB3.

EphB3 is a receptor tyrosine kinase that mediates the microglia–astrocyte interactions driving neuroinflammation. It is the first target the C-to-C Engine identified.

Shown: ephrin-B on microglia signaling to EphB3 on astrocytes. Schematic, not to scale.

04 · Inhibit

A small moleculequiets the exchange.

Violet's lead program is a small-molecule EphB3 inhibitor, designed to interrupt harmful glial signaling and preserve synapses. In a 2021 Science study from founder Francisco Quintana's lab, a CNS-penetrant EphB3 inhibitor reduced disease in a mouse model of multiple sclerosis.

The molecule shown is not Violet's compound or its binding mode.

Founding science

Built on research from Brigham and Women's Hospital.

Violet was founded in 2021 by Mass General Brigham Ventures with scientists from Brigham and Women's Hospital. Founder Francisco Quintana, Professor of Neurology at Brigham and Women's Hospital and Harvard Medical School, invented the C-to-C Communication Maps and SPEAC-seq. Michael Wheeler, Assistant Professor of Neurology at Brigham and Women's, co-invented the maps.

The work builds on methods such as RABID-seq, which traces cell-to-cell interactions at single-cell resolution. In that 2021 Science study, ephrin-B–EphB3 signaling between microglia and astrocytes promoted CNS pathology in a mouse model of multiple sclerosis.

Violet believes EphB3 inhibitors have the potential to reduce inflammation and improve cognition and synaptic health in neurodegenerative disease.
