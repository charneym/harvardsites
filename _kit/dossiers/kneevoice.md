## The company
Kneevoice, Inc., 1626 Montana Avenue, Santa Monica, CA 90403 (kneevoice.com /contact-us). Medical technology company
"pioneering objective auscultation technology for musculoskeletal care". Product: the Kneevoice Cartilage Evaluation System
(patented knee sensors + console + proprietary algorithm) that records joint sound, vibration and position during 8–10 knee
flexions and generates a cartilage score report plus a sound recording (/what-is-kneevoice, /why-kneevoice).
NIH RePORTER org record: KNEEVOICE, INC., Santa Monica CA 90403-1808, UEI Z4JTPHBE8L52.

## Facts used on the page
- FDA 510(k) clearance for the Cartilage Evaluation System, release dated Santa Monica, February 24, 2026; "now commercially
  available in the United States"; initial commercialisation concentrated on the knee; applications list (MSK evaluation,
  sports medicine, longitudinal monitoring, pre/post intervention, biologics, physical therapy). Pipeline: wearable technology
  and long-term diagnostic support tools. https://www.kneevoice.com/kneevoice-receives-fda-510-k-clearance-for-innovative-cartilage-auscultation-system/
- NIH NIA SBIR Phase I 1R43AG099200-01, "A non-invasive auscultation device for early detection of articular cartilage damage",
  $398,432, start 2026-09-17, contact PI Gustavo De Greiff. Develops "Wearable Kneevoice" (patellofemoral + medial).
  https://reporter.nih.gov/project-details/11308404
- Leal C, Rahman O, Hunter R, Bert J, Espregueira-Mendes J, Cugat R, Santos E, Radice F, Leal-Bernal C. "Comparison of
  Audioarthrography and Magnetic Resonance Imaging for Diagnosis of Patellofemoral Cartilage Damage", J Orthop Experience &
  Innovation 6(2), 2025-09-30. 90 patients; ICRS match 78/90 (87%) vs MRI 48 (53%); weighted kappa 0.91 vs 0.53;
  LR+ 8.43 / LR− 0.10 vs 2.80 / 0.38; sensitivity 90.32%, specificity 89.29% (Results). https://journaloei.scholasticahq.com/article/140763
- History (/why-kneevoice): Pre-seed 2019–20, patents granted, first multi-country clinical trial; partnership with
  Cedars-Sinai Kerlan-Jobe; FDA pre-submissions; Gold in "Most Fundable Companies" at Pepperdine University; collaboration
  agreements with Michigan Knee Institute and Samimi Orthopedic Group (2024).
- Team (/our-team): Gustavo De Greiff (CEO), Charlie Leal MD (CSO; site also shows "dr-carlos-leal"), James Rigby (CTO),
  Todd Davis, Jerry Chickillo, Brad Durcholz, Marianne Grunwaldt, Dr. Robert Hunter, Michael McBreen (advisor).
