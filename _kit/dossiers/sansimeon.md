## Science (RePORTER abstracts)
- Problem: SC-seq limited in clinical settings by poor reproducibility from variable sample prep, limited cell retention constraining throughput, and complex labour-intensive workflows hard to standardise across operators and sites; especially problematic for rare-cell detection (immunotherapy development, precision diagnostics, minimal residual disease). — 1R44GM163418
- Approach: a turnkey platform integrating high-efficiency, unbiased sample preparation with scalable, deterministic cell encapsulation. Unifies red blood cell and platelet removal, cell enrichment, multiplexed tagging and CITE-seq labeling in a one-step workflow, with no centrifugation and minimal hands-on time. High-speed deterministic encapsulation processes up to 100 million cells per run with significantly improved cell–bead pairing efficiency. — 1R44GM163418
- Phase I: one-step incubation protocol for SC-seq multiplexing, CITE-seq and cell enrichment in peripheral whole blood. Phase II: automated benchtop instrument + injection-molded microfluidic cartridge; validation across donors, operators and timepoints with commercial and in-house SC-seq platforms. — 1R44GM163418
- Mechanism: "rapid deterministic pairing of single cells with single beads in a microchannel prior to emulsification, without the need for multi-step labeling workflows such as cell-hashing or combinatorial indexing." — 1R43GM149003
- Throughput: existing methods do not support beyond ~1 million cells per experiment; Sansimeon targets >100M cells directly from crude, complex samples (whole blood, bone marrow aspirates) with a single-step automated workflow; incorporates in-line sample debulking previously commercialized for cell-therapy manufacturing and rare-cell isolation. — 1R43HG013446
- Applications (site): immune profiling (TCR repertoire), CRISPR screening, tailored drug therapies, next-generation cell therapies; data for AI in pharma: data analysis, clinical-trial design, biomarker discovery & target identification, integration & automation.
