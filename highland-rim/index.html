01Approach

Management first, by design.

A management-centric, consistent and repeatable approach: continuity of management, collaborative planning at the company level, and value-add work tailored to each business.

Step 1 of 3 · Continuity

The team that built it stays.

Highland Rim partners with management teams embedded in long-established businesses. The culture an owner instilled is part of why the company succeeded, and keeping it is critical to what comes next.

Step 2 of 3 · Ownership

Managers become owners.

Many management partners had limited ownership and little operating authority before Highland Rim. It changes that, with attractive ownership stakes and real autonomy over the business.

Step 3 of 3 · Growth

Energize, then elevate.

Support, resources and value-enhancement strategies built for each company: growth initiatives, operational improvements and add-on acquisitions of any size.
