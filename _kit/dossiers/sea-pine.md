# Sea Pine Equity Partners (dossier, facts with sources only)

- Site: https://seapineequity.com (pages: home, about, approach, partnerships, leadership, contact)
- HQ: 2215 3rd Street South, Suite 203, Jacksonville Beach, FL 32250 (site footer, contact page). Time zone: America/New_York.
- What: "Private equity for the service sector"; invests in lower middle market service-sector companies; takes controlling positions; prefers to partner with founders or management teams already in place; aims to provide capital and resources to accelerate growth while maintaining the culture created by management (home page).
- Core principles (home): Integrity ("We follow through on our commitments and value clear communication and transparency"); People First ("people are the most important part of any company"); Shared Success ("We structure deals so that everyone wins").
- Investment criteria (home): profitable, high-quality service companies that can benefit from a proactive approach to growth; consumer, healthcare, business, industrial and tech-enabled services including logistics; partnership with founders or existing management; preference for markets with a high degree of consolidation; EBITDA of at least $2 million for platform investments.
- Approach (/approach): invest in partnership with management; "We are not operators" (expertise in capital sourcing and structure, acquisition strategy and execution, general business approach; management runs operations day to day); focused on the service sector (secular tailwinds, adding locations, add-on acquisitions, investment in existing operations; high margins, low cyclicality, low capital intensity, broad customer diversification); acquisitions as a lever for value creation in consolidating markets.
- Partnerships (/partnerships): Concrete Conservation Holdings (wastewater infrastructure protection/rehabilitation; owner and primary applicator of SpectraShield; https://spectrashield.com/); Hi-Tek Data (managed IT services: 24x7 support, cybersecurity, cloud, Microsoft 365, application development; https://www.hitekdata.com/); Executive Exteriors (specialty roofing and exterior restoration; https://www.exeext.com/).
