## Company
- NeuroTherapia, Inc., a Delaware C-corporation, founded in 2015 by the Cleveland Clinic and two of its physician scientists; developing new drugs for diseases related to neuroinflammation, initial focus Alzheimer's disease (AD). Source: https://www.neurotherapia.com/history
- Clinical-stage, privately held; spun out of Cleveland Clinic; oral small-molecule drugs for neuroinflammatory CNS conditions (AD, Parkinson's, ALS, other CNS disorders). Source: press boilerplate, https://www.neurotherapia.com/press-releases
- Mission: "to develop treatments that will improve the course of the most debilitating and deadly central nervous system (CNS) diseases patients and their families face today." Source: same.
- Initial preclinical toxicology completed 2018; Tony Giordano, Ph.D. recruited as CEO. Source: /history

## Science (source: https://www.neurotherapia.com/research-and-development, /about, home)
- Microglia activation drives inflammatory response, a hallmark of AD, ALS, neuropathic pain, MS, Parkinson's.
- Two states: M1 predominantly neurotoxic, M2 neuroprotective; M2 help clear amyloid-β, M1 contribute to neuroinflammation and synaptic damage; M1 increased in AD. As microglia transition M2→M1, CB2 receptor expression is induced.
- NTRX-07: potent, highly selective, oral CB2 receptor agonist with demonstrated CNS activity; promotes M1→M2 transition: reduced neuronal damage, increased Aβ clearance, inhibited neuroinflammation.
- Research demonstrated NTRX-07 reduces neuroinflammation and improves neuronal function, improving learning and memory, "without any of the psychoactive side effects associated with cannabinoids." (/history)
- Combination potential with approved antibody treatments; plans to test synergy. (/research-and-development)
- Receptor context: target receptors increase in AD, ALS, MS, chronic pain syndromes (PRN releases).

## Clinical
- Oct 2019 first-in-human trials initiated; Dec 2020 Phase 1a completed; Oct 2023 Phase 1b data presented (press-releases page list).
- Phase 1b: 7-day study in healthy volunteers and an AD cohort; safety/tolerability at blood levels consistent with animal bioactivity; in AD cohort a trend toward reduced ADAS-cog errors and partial reversal of qEEG deficits. Source: NIH RePORTER 1R44AG102269-01 abstract.
- Feb 4, 2025: EMA approval of Phase 2 trial; 28 days, double-masked, randomized; CRU Global; sites in Hungary, Poland, Czech Republic. https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/neurotherapia-announces-ema-approval-of-its-phase-2-trial-for-the-treatment-of-alzheimers-disease-302366418.html
- Apr 7, 2026: Phase 2a completed; 48 subjects 1:1 across five sites; no treatment-related SAEs in active arm; MRI work with Oxford Brain Diagnostics (CDM Explore). https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/neurotherapia-announces-completion-of-its-phase-2a-trial-of-a-novel-treatment-for-alzheimers-disease-302735337.html
- Jul 13, 2026: AAIC 2026 London presentation; randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled; 48 participants with MCI or mild-to-moderate AD; 90 mg/day; exploratory diffusion MRI, NfL, Aβ42/40, IL-6, YKL-40 observations (hedged; warrant confirmation). https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/neurotherapia-to-present-phase-2a-alzheimers-disease-clinical-data-at-the-aaic-2026-conference-in-london-uk-302823358.html
- Six-domain Phase 2a summary (Safety, PK, Biomarkers, MRI, EEG, Clinical/functional) quoted near-verbatim from /research-and-development.
