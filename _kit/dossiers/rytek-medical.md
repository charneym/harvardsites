## Mission and science (rytekmedical.com home, about, technology)
- "Translating Technologies to Improve Lives"; "Enhancing clinical practice through innovation".
- Leading innovator in bioimpedance-based technologies to detect, monitor and treat disease; put accurate bioimpedance sensing and imaging devices in doctors' hands.
- Bioimpedance: tissues impede current differently by cellular architecture, cell types and health; sensed with small, imperceptible, safe alternating currents between electrodes.
- Expertise: Impedance technologies; Custom hardware development; R&D innovation; Software design and analytics.

## Programs (technology page; NIH RePORTER)
- Cancer: tetrapolar impedance biopsy needle (prostate sampling volume ×40, spatial guidance to high-grade cells); robotically actuated / laparoscopic surgical margin probe.
- Surgical: OsteoSmartSense™ dental implant drill (first implant drill with real-time impedance sensing; "Ensure 2mm surgical boundaries" per assurteksurgical.com); smart sensing surgical drill for orthopedics and neurosurgery.
- Neurological: NeuroZ non-invasive intracranial monitor (stroke progression, hemorrhagic onset); TBI impedance as co-modality to ICP.
- PulmoZ: wearable bioimpedance patch for remote pulmonary function in ALS (NIH R41NS143559).
- Site stats: <1% of prostate sampled in 12-core biopsy; 43% grade discordance; ~30% positive margins; 9.5M dental implants/yr; up to 40% iatrogenic injury; stroke 2nd cause of death.
