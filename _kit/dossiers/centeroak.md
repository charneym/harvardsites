## Investment criteria (https://centeroakpartners.com/strategy/)
- Industry focus: Business Services, Industrial Services, Consumer Services
- Enterprise value $50–500 million; EBITDA $7–35 million; equity investments $50–150 million
- Company attributes: strong, experienced management teams; potential for organic and acquisitive growth; opportunity for operational improvements; leading niche market position; sustainable competitive advantage; attractive relative value
- Geography: United States
- Transaction focus: buyouts and recapitalizations; closely-held or family-owned businesses; buy-and-build opportunities; non-core subsidiary carve-outs
- Sub-sectors: Business Services (facilities management & services; professional services; engineering & infrastructure services; environmental services; machinery maintenance & repair); Industrial Services (outsourced industrial services; specialty distribution); Consumer Essential Services (residential services; auto aftermarket)
- Value creation plans "often include": implementing operational efficiencies; facility expansions; logistics & supply chain optimization; product line & service extensions; geographic expansions; strategic tuck-in acquisitions; transformational mergers or combinations.
