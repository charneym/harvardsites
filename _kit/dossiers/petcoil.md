## Facts used (all from petcoil.com pages or NIH RePORTER abstracts)
- Home: "We make multimodality molecular imaging more accurate, sensitive, and affordable"; removable PET insert enables existing MRI scanners to achieve PET/MRI.
- PETcoil system page: "PET imaging for your MRI system"; integrated PET/MRI cost limits adoption; portable PET scanner placed within an existing MRI; first product a portable PET scanner for head imaging, "a fraction of the cost of conventional PET/CT and integrated PET/MRI".
- Company page: formed by imaging scientists, engineers and radiologists; core technology rooted at Stanford, where the founders created their first RF-penetrable PET insert for simultaneous PET/MRI.
- 1SB1MH139143 (NIMH CRP) abstract: integrated PET/MRI ~$5M + $1.5–2M room renovation; ~60 integrated PET/MRI vs 13,500 MRI systems in the US; insert into and removed from any MRI; 10-fold lower cost; >3x photon sensitivity, >4x finer volumetric resolution; Phase II built a functional prototype; CRP aims: industrial design for production, V&V with Stanford, FDA regulatory submission.
- 1R43DE035410 (NIDCR) abstract: Head PETcoil, portable high-resolution TOF-PET for brain, head and neck; nearly 6x finer spatial resolution, 3x photon sensitivity; small lymph node metastases in head and neck cancer; Phase I real-time motion correction.
- 2R44MH123253 abstract: removable from any MRI, can serve multiple MRI systems (clinical and research) or stand alone; collaboration with Dr. Craig Levin at Stanford.
- NIH total $7.4M = 494,652 + 497,108 + 1,106,983 + 98,508 + 1,026,717 + 851,630 + 1,596,935 + 1,320,291 + 409,779 (FY2020–FY2026, RePORTER).
