## Science (sources)
- Oxysterols: oxidised derivatives of cholesterol; activators or inhibitors of Hedgehog, TGF-β, Wnt/β-catenin and TLR signaling (https://maxbiopharma.com/our-approach/our-technology).
- Oxy210: oral, semi-synthetic oxysterol; derived from Oxy16 (20α,22(R)-dihydroxycholesterol, weak Hh antagonist); three-step synthesis from pregnenolone (https://maxbiopharma.com/pipeline/lead-indication). Room-temperature-stable small molecule, no cold chain (Stappenbeck quote, Technomark release).
- Inhibits TGF-β and Hh signaling in hepatic stellate cells; TLR signaling in Kupffer cells/macrophages; in humanized hyperlipidemic MASH mice reduced liver inflammation and fibrosis, cholesterol, atherosclerosis; reduces senescence markers (lead-indication page; PMIDs 34505423, 39404395; Cells 2025 doi 10.3390/cells14151191).
- Lung/kidney fibrosis: in IMR-90 and IPF-derived LL97A lung fibroblasts Oxy210 inhibited FN, COL1A1, CTGF expression and proliferation (PMID 36678611).
- Macrophages: TLR4/TLR2 inhibition (IJMS 2022, https://www.mdpi.com/1422-0067/23/10/5478).
- Origin: oxysterols activate Hh in mesenchymal cells (JBC 2007, PMID 17200122); Oxy133 osteogenic via Smoothened (JBMR 2014, PMID 24591126). PubMed: 29 records for Parhami F[au] AND (oxysterol OR Oxy210).
