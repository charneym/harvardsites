## Programme: MucoRegen (site "Current Innovations" + NIH RePORTER abstract)
- Product under development for periodontal soft-tissue regeneration; practical, cost-effective alternative to soft-tissue graft products (site).
- Gelatin-based matrix enhanced with other safe, natural, biodegradable materials; biomimetic and affordable; "to the best of our knowledge, no gelatin-based matrix is currently available on the market for gingival regeneration"; formulation extensively optimized by the PI's research group (RePORTER).
- Novel fabrication: dual-porosity scaffold with interconnected macro- and micropores mimicking native ECM; enhances nutrient diffusion, cell infiltration, proliferation; in vitro >3-fold increase in cell proliferation vs conventional scaffolds (RePORTER).
- Pilot in vivo: comparable to autologous grafts and slightly greater than Mucograft in increasing keratinized gingival width; ortho- and para-keratinization; organized collagen deposition and vascularization (RePORTER).
- Problem: FGG and CTG autografts are gold standard but invasive with donor-site morbidity; Mucograft, AlloDerm, NovoMatrix effective but expensive, limited in replicating native gingiva's elasticity and structure; commercial products $250–$800; MucoRegen projected under $100 (RePORTER).
- Phase I plan: in vitro and in vivo comparisons vs FGG (autograft), AlloDerm (human acellular dermal matrix, allograft), Mucograft (collagen-based xenograft), NovoMatrix (animal acellular dermal matrix, xenograft) (RePORTER).
- Market (abstract, not used on site): soft tissue graft revenue expected to reach $2.11B by 2034; CAGR 7.8%.
