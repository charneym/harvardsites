Perforating and punching machines, made in Pirmasens.

For more than a hundred years the Ring Group has built perforating, die-cutting and shoe machines, special machines and the tools that go in them, and perforates and embosses material to order for leather, automotive, furniture and fashion.

Machines, tools, design development, contract perforation and service, all from a single source and all made in Germany.

The Ring Group has specialised in perforation for more than a century. It is a regionally rooted, internationally active family business, run today by the third and fourth generations of the founding family.

From hole pattern to finished part.

How a perforation job runs through the Ring Group, from the first sketch to an automated line.

PatternToolMachineLine

The pattern.

Perfect perforation begins with design development. Our prototype team works out custom hole patterns with the customer, from the first concept to a pattern that is ready for production.

The tool.

The tool room builds punching and perforating tools matched to the material and the machine, from cutting dies in wooden plate to massive tools for technical industry.

The machine.

Perforating machines from ME and ME-Q technology to the HD XL, built in Pirmasens, alongside hydraulic die-cutting presses and machines for the footwear industry.

The line.

Automation around the press: picking, handling and pallet conveyors, rotary tables and camera inspection, designed and built to the customer's process.

Six product lines, one works.

Standard machines and machines built to your specification, with our own design department, tool making, service, spare parts and training.

  • Perforating

    Perforating machines

    ME and ME-Q technology, ME with automation, HD XL and HD technic, design perforating, One-Stroke, hole-pipe machines, perforating and embossing (ST-D) and AQC automatic quick change.

    Perforating machines
  • Die cutting

    Die-cutting machines

    High-speed ST 90D, hydraulic upstroke HTR, crosshead KBS, large-area GFS and OKS presses, roller, swing-arm and lifting-table cutting machines, with material supply and removal.

    Die-cutting machines
  • Tooling

    Cutting dies

    Punching tools matched to your material and press: cutting dies in wooden plate, dies for plastic trays, special cutting tools and massive tools for technical industry.

    Cutting dies
  • Footwear

    Shoe machines

    One of the few shoe machine makers left in Germany: upper production, lasting, sole handling and pressing, activating, drying, heel and toe forming, roughing and cementing.

    Shoe machines
  • Special machines

    Special machines and automation

    Embossing, cutting and handling machines built to order: assembly units, rotary table systems, inline printing, pallet conveyors and test systems with labelling and cameras.

    Special machines
  • Service

    Service, spare parts and training

    Service departments for perforating and for cutting, spare parts and tool sales from Pirmasens, and training for the people who run the machines.
