## Medical Device Identifier (MDI) — science (RePORTER abstract + site)
- CIEDs (pacemakers, ICDs) implanted in >3 million Americans; average implant age in the 60s, 40% ≥70, 28% ≥80.
- Each device can only be interrogated by its own manufacturer's programmer; patients often arrive without device info; standard of care to identify the device can take 30 minutes to hours (x-rays, database searches, calling family/clinicians).
- MDI: portable handheld device that identifies CIEDs from any manufacturer in the ED or in transit, by mimicking the "wakeup call" communication of the five major CIED programmers in sequence and analysing the device's response.
- Site: identifies the brand of an implanted cardiac device "in 10 seconds or less"; ~50% of the time patients don't have their device card.
- Indications for use (site): "The SmartRead(TM) Medical Device Identifier is intended to determine the manufacturer of an unknown cardiac implanted electronic device (CIED) manufactured by Abbott Laboratories, Biotronik, Boston Scientific Corporation, Medtronic, or MicroPort."
- FDA De Novo DEN260007 "SmartRead(TM) Medical Device Identifier (4100)", classification "cardiac device identifier", product code SJJ, regulation 870.3645, received 2026-03-24, decision granted 2026-09-29, contact John Walmsley. https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/scripts/cdrh/cfdocs/cfpmn/denovo.cfm?id=DEN260007
- Not yet available for sale; licensed to Cardiac Innovation, who will commercialize it (site). https://www.cardiac-innovation.com/
- Earlier MDI awards: 1R43AG059499-01 (2018), 2R44AG059499-02 (2019), -03 (2020), 2R44AG059499-04A1 (2023), -05 (2024).

## Other programmes (site + RePORTER)
- Blood volume / bio-admittance, with CardioVol Corp. (exclusive licensor of bio-admittance patents; tech mostly conceived by BSM scientists John Porterfield and Anil Kottam during PhDs at UT Austin); commercialized directly by BSM under a strategic alliance.
  - Impella Cardiac Output: real-time hemodynamic measurement (cardiac output) directly from a market-leading mechanical cardiac support device; for weaning from mechanical cardiac support. NHLBI R43/R44 HL145847 "Native Hemodynamic Measurement as Feedback Control for Mechanical Cardiac Support".
  - Smart Pericardial Drain: modified Blake drain measuring hemodynamics after cardiac surgery to clarify the cause of hypotension. NIBIB R44EB030973 "Post Cardiac Surgery Hemodynamics Diagnostic Device" (PI Kottam).
- Smart Nebulizer: adaptive nebulization changing particle size in response to inhalation flow; active mesh; pulmonary hypertension. NHLBI R43/R44 HL154960.
- Right heart catheter for PV loops in tricuspid regurgitation (new Phase I, 2026): PV loop analysis is the gold standard; incorporate PV measurement onto a standard right heart catheter.
- Technology-Enabled Financial Planning for Caregivers and Clients with Reduced Capacity, NIA R44AG074121.
