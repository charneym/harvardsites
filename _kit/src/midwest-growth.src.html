01Approach

Most firms avoid rural markets. We prefer them.

MGP invests in established companies in underserved rural markets, run by great people who share a cultural fit with our team: four funds, one strategy, since the first deal in 2014.

  • Relationships

    Cultural fit comes first

    We look for owners and management teams whose core values align with ours, and whose approach to business is very similar to our own.

  • Operators

    We've run companies too

    Our investment principals and operating partners have been CEOs, CFOs and COOs of privately owned companies.

  • Reinvestment

    Growth, as the name implies

    MGP re-invests in its companies: new systems, retention and expansion of management teams, improved processes and complementary product lines.

What our team adds to every company.

Our principals call on their own experience to help portfolio company leaders navigate the daily trials of operating a growing enterprise.

02Investment criteria

Capital where it's scarce.

As a lower middle market fund manager, MGP makes equity and debt investments in profitable, established companies, and co-invests on larger opportunities.

  • Up to $30MIndividual equity and debt investment
  • $4–100MAnnual revenue of prospective companies
  • $0–12MEBITDA, depending on the structure and nature of the investment
