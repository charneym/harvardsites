## Criteria (https://highlandrimcap.com/approach/)
- Transaction types: management buyouts, family succession, divisional spinouts, majority-control recapitalizations, founder transitions, corporate divestitures.
- Business characteristics: motivated, long-tenured in-house manager(s) who desire ownership; long and stable history of operating cash flows; diversified customer base.
- Size: revenue $10–100 million; EBITDA $2.5–10 million; add-ons of any size.
- Geography: nationwide, with a focus on the South and Midwest.
- Industries: light manufacturing, value-add distribution, business services.
- Sector-specific experience: water and wastewater equipment, capital equipment, specialty paper products, automotive equipment, material handling equipment, packaging equipment, medical device components, industrial sensors, marine equipment, sanitizing equipment, transformers, aerospace and defense components, recreational products, lab equipment, gears and gearboxes, promotional products.
