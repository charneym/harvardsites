## Firm (/cvwa, /cvwa/cvwa-our-philosophy, service pages)
- Tagline "Planning and investing for the goals of your life ™"; "We believe financial planning is not an event, but a process."
- Sells no products; low-expense, low-turnover, broadly diversified funds; "We don't play the market"; "We are investors; not traders"; "we believe an investor should BE the market."
- Four components: Financial Planning (Point A resources → Point B goals; roadmap; side trips, detours; taxes from CPA heritage; MoneyGuidePro), Investment Advice/Strategy (time horizons, Investment Policies with ranges per asset class; risk questionnaire critique), Asset Management ("where the rubber hits the road"; never sell investments), Performance Review (annual report vs asset-class benchmarks; quarterly reports; annual conference).
- Six principles (/asset-management): risk & return related; asset allocation is the key; diversification critical; market timing futile/fatal (Nick Murray quote); discipline reaps rewards (no hedge funds); costs matter, period.
- History: Bob Heaton began advice 2001 at Heaton Adams Financial Services (division of CPA firm Heaton Adams and Company); Karla Hoppes joined 2010; renamed/formed CVWA July 1, 2014.
- 401(k) rollover assistance; quarterly commentary to clients (no public link).
