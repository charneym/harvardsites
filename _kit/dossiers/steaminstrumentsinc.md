# Steam Instruments, Inc. (steaminstrumentsinc)

Home-only concept build (HOME_BRIEF, update 2026-11-20). Live concept: /steaminstrumentsinc/

## Company
- High-spatial-resolution imaging mass spectrometry start-up, Madison, Wisconsin. Develops ion detectors and ion imaging detectors, ion imaging optics and high-mass spectrometer designs; aims to enhance mass range, quantum efficiency and spatial resolution. Some technologies already shipped to early customers; first commercial-ready products ready for evaluation in 2026. SBIR recipient of DOE, NSF and CHIPS Act funding. Source: https://steaminstrumentsinc.com/
- Key areas (their site): direct-from-tissue biomolecule mapping and quantification, ultra-high-mass biomolecule analysis, sub-micron mapping of hydrogen and lithium, semiconductor device dopant analysis (a fifth listed area, nuclear forensics mapping, is left off the concept).
- Address: 931 E Main St, Suite 3, Madison, WI 53703. Phone 608-960-9000 (NSF awardee record). Personnel also in Pacific Grove, CA (company profile, https://inknowvation.com/node/179498).
- Founded 2018 by Thomas F. Kelly (MAS bio: https://the-mas.org/awards/presidential-science-award/2023-thomas-f-kelly/).
