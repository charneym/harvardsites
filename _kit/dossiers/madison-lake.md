## Firm
- Lower middle market private equity firm, Madison, Wisconsin. Office: 3821 Nakoma Rd. Ste C, Madison WI 53711. Email: info@madisonlakecapital.com (home). LinkedIn: linkedin.com/company/madisonlakecapital. Investor portal: investor.madisonlakecapitalapps.com. Time zone: Central.
- "We focus on lower middle-market companies and create partnerships with management teams to unlock value and drive growth." (home)
- "We operate in an independent sponsor model, enabling us to be very flexible in our partnerships." (home)
- Capabilities: Buyouts (buy from owners looking for a transition; post-acquisition, with management, put in place strategies to build a platform and work towards accelerated growth); Divestiture (divest a subsidiary or business unit into a stand-alone business); Recapitalization (majority or partial stake). (home)
- Deal criteria: EBITDA $3 MM to $15 MM; sector agnostic. (home)
- Industries of interest: Business Services (Utilities, Construction Services, Staffing Services, Maintenance Services, Other recurring services); Manufacturing (Core: products and services critical to customers; Value-add: seasonal or non-critical); Technology (B2B technology products and services); Supply Chain (Logistics, Private Label, Warehouse Management, Shipping & Packing, Distribution). (home)
- About (2026 release): specializes in lower-middle-market buyouts; works with management teams to achieve organic growth via tailored technology solutions and processes, irrespective of sector; honours each seller's desire for a seamless transition that preserves culture and safeguards people.
- About (2024/2025 releases): buyout practice targets family-owned, single-owner entities with a long history of stable performance; real estate practice targets properties with locked-in NNN tenants (2023: >80% occupancy).
