01Approach

A program for predictable growth.

Actio is Valesco's performance program for driving predictable company growth. It is built around four questions every owner of a growing business asks.

Action · Strategy

Do I have the right strategy?

We guide goal setting and action planning through our Value Blueprint exercise, so owners and management work toward one unified vision.

Execution · Team

Do I have the right team?

We help make sure the right people are in the right seats. We know what exceptional talent looks like.

Process · Systems

Are my tools ready for growth?

We identify the tools, systems and resources the business needs for its next stage of growth.

Performance · Legacy

A business built to last.

What will my legacy be? We believe in building businesses to last, and in building on what the owner has already started.

  • Majority

    Majority investment

    We buy a majority interest and partner with the existing management team. Valesco has access to all the funds needed for the buyout, structured with any combination of equity and debt.

  • Minority

    Minority investment

    Owners bring on a partner without selling the whole business or giving up control. We believe in partnerships: if a decision doesn't work for everyone, it won't work at all.

  • Debt

    Subordinated debt

    Growth capital as a supplement to a bank loan, to buy a competitor or add a production plant: patient, long-term capital with no amortization.
