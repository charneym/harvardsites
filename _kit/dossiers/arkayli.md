## Science (site + RePORTER)
- ARK001: proprietary topical gel of esmolol (ultra-short half-life beta-1 selective blocker), rapidly metabolized by red blood cells; no systemic beta blockade expected; pediatric-specific clear gel; 24-hour treatment, washes off with mild soap and water; less risk of hypoglycemia (no need to wake a baby to feed); intended to reduce bradycardia/wheezing and CNS effects. Soft drug design (STTR abstract); in-vitro data and preliminary animal studies showing no systemic beta-adrenergic antagonism (STTR abstract).
- IH facts (site, with references): ~170,000 US infants/yr; low birth weight top risk factor; 55% significant-to-severe sequelae (Baselga 2016). Release: ideal intervention in first 1–2 months; average >5 months to see a specialist.
