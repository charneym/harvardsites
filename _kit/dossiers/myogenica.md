## The company
Myogenica was formed in 2022 as a spin-out of the University of Minnesota to develop MyoPAXon, an induced pluripotent stem
cell (iPSC) platform for muscle regeneration. MyoPAXon has been effective in regenerating muscle and improving function in
models of Duchenne (DMD), limb girdle (LGMD) and facioscapulohumeral muscular dystrophy (FSHD). Sources: myogenica.com home,
/science, /our-team, /news.

Registered organisation (NIH RePORTER): MYOGENICA INC., Saint Paul, MN 55114-8612, UEI LH5TFUV5JJ39. No street address is
published on the site; the build shows "Saint Paul, Minnesota 55114".

## Science (myogenica.com/science) — the story
1. GMP iPSC cell bank, modified by lentivirus to express PAX7 (expressed only in muscle stem / satellite cells).
2. In vitro differentiation; purification on the proprietary CD54 marker with a cGMP-compliant process.
3. Expansion of CD54+ cells yields MyoPAXon (homogeneous muscle stem cells), frozen, available for clinical studies.
4. Transplanted cells improve existing fibres (restore missing proteins such as dystrophin), improve function, and create a
   pool of muscle stem cells that regenerates muscle after future injury; regenerative capacity at least 12 months after a
   single treatment. Development funded by over $10M in grants over 10 years.
