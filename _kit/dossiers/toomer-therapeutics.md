## Funding
- **NCI STTR Phase I, 1R41CA302048-01A1** — "Development of ARIH1 inhibitors as a novel targeted therapy in cancer".
  Award notice 2 Sep 2026; project 2 Sep 2026 – 31 Aug 2027; **$400,000** (FY2026). Org: Toomer Therapeutics, Inc.,
  Mount Pleasant, SC. PIs: Philip H Howe (contact PI), Breege Howley.
  https://reporter.nih.gov/project-details/11312958
  Abstract (used for the story, pipeline and "Why ARIH1"): ARIH1 higher in TNBC than normal tissue; high ARIH1 associated
  with reduced relapse-free and overall survival; silencing inhibits EMT and cancer stemness and blocks tumour progression
  in mouse models; silenced cells more sensitive to chemotherapeutics. Inhibitors found by molecular docking; hit compounds
  and derivatives of lead compound A3 inhibit ARIH1 ubiquitination in vitro and, like silencing, inhibit stemness and
  increase chemo sensitivity. Aims: lead optimization, ADME-PK, efficacy with chemotherapy in a mouse TNBC model.
  Phase II planned: IND-enabling studies.

- Hero: "Targeted Therapies for Aggressive Solid Cancers"; "Advancing first-in-class therapies that harness the ubiquitin system".

  minimizing off-target effects across the UPS); Clinical value (durable responses in aggressive solid cancers such as TNBC).
