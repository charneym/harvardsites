## Science used
- PAL-119: investigational oral small molecule, selective DYRK1A inhibitor; dual action (DYRK1A for proliferation; PKA signalling for
  glucose sensing and insulin secretion); improved selectivity and substantially reduced brain exposure vs harmine; from Icahn School of
  Medicine at Mount Sinai; exclusive worldwide rights incl. composition-of-matter patent families.
- Mechanism (Science page): DYRK1A phosphorylates NFAT and removes it from the nucleus; PAL-119 restores NFAT nuclear localisation.
- Trial design (11-02 release): SAD up to 6 cohorts, MAD up to 3, food effect (open-label crossover); Phase 2a T2D (two doses vs
  placebo) and T1D (vs placebo); C-peptide, insulin, fasting glucose, GLP-1.
- NIH abstract: Paulex-4 >10x more potent and more selective than harmine; normoglycemia within one week in human-islet-engrafted
  mice; ADME/PK/14-day GLP tox in rats and dogs; >1 kg GLP API; aims: PK/PD, second target, backup DYRK1A inhibitors.
- Diabetes page stats: ~828M worldwide, ~38.4M US; ~250M using insulin worldwide; life expectancy reduced >10 years.
