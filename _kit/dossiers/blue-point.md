## Criteria (https://www.bluepointcapital.com/how-we-invest)
- $30M–$300M revenue; EBITDA greater than $5M (no minimum for add-ons); leaders in their markets; strong fit with Blue Point value-add resources.
- Situations: recapitalizations of closely-held/family-owned companies; management-led buyouts; companies needing capital for internal growth or add-ons. "particularly focused on being the first outside equity capital in a growing business."
- Sectors: Industrial (Aerospace & Defense, Auto Aftermarket, Distribution, Engineered Products, Industrial Technology, Niche Manufacturing); Business Services (Commercial, Environmental, Facility, Industrial & MRO, Infrastructure & Utility, Residential Services, Specialty Consulting, Transportation & Logistics); Consumer (Apparel & Footwear; Beauty, Personal Care & Wellness; Consumer & Enthusiast Products; E-commerce; Food & Beverage; Pet).
