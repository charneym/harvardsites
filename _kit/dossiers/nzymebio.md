## Facts used (nzymebio.com unless noted)
- LPR reportedly affects ~10–30% of the U.S. population; symptoms chronic cough, throat clearing, post-nasal drip, hoarseness, globus, dysphagia; no effective medical therapy; ~10% of visits to otolaryngologists (home page "Why LPR").
- $26 billion/year spent on PPIs for LPR; PPI placebo-controlled trials failed to demonstrate efficacy (Investors page).
- Pepsin, detected in the airway in LPR, causes inflammation and promotes disease independent of gastric acid; a key therapeutic target. Fosamprenavir binds to and inhibits pepsin and prevents pepsin-mediated laryngeal inflammation and damage in an in vivo model; patients on HIV inhibitors have lower LPR incidence.
- Three pillars: accelerated development (repurposing a drug with a good safety and tolerability profile); improved drug delivery (prolonged-release oral formulation, dry powder inhaler for low local dose); corporate responsibility.
- Pipeline: NZYM-001 oral tablets, LPR, Phase 2 PoC; NZYM-003 oral suspension, GERD, preclinical; NZYM-002 dry powder inhaler, LPR, preclinical (pre-GLP studies confirmed safety and efficacy; GLP inhalation toxicology under way). The placeholder NZ-004 row was left out.
- Founders and advisory board as on the About page (summaries shortened, nothing added).
- Definition of LPR (About LPR page): retrograde flow of gastric contents into the larynx, oropharynx and/or nasopharynx.
