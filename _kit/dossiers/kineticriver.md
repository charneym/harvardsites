## Products (site)
- Delaware Flow NanoCytometer®: "the only flow cytometer on the market able to detect particles from 28 nm to 28 µm, including exosomes, liposomes, EVs,
  viruses, bacteria, and eukaryotic cells" (home). Up to five lasers (375, 405, 488, 561, 640 nm), up to 3 scattering and 6 fluorescence channels,
  proprietary high-NA collection lens; Shasta fluidics; Cavour flowcell monitor; Panama software. Services page: up to 68-nm sensitivity on liposomes, 6-nm resolution.
- Potomac Modular Flow Cytometer: 1 to 8 lasers, 4 to 20 detection channels; cage-cube optomechanics; installed at the National Cancer Institute.
- Arno Compensation-Free Flow Cytometer: 12-color, compensation-free with 2 lasers and 6 detectors; fluorescence lifetime (TRFC) separates two fluors per detector.
  Blog 2026-11-12: "supported by a portfolio of 13 patents to date".
- Danube Fluorescence Lifetime Flow Cytometer: time-domain lifetime, up to 1,500 cells/s, excitation events ≤4 ns, subnanosecond resolution.
- Modules: Shasta (fluidics control), Cavour (flowcell monitoring), Tuolumne (quad amplifier), Beam Genie.
- Nanoparticle analysis services on the Delaware: send samples, we measure and analyze, detailed report (culture medium, plasma, urine, purified EV preps).
- Consulting: expert witness, engineering consulting (M&A due diligence, design reviews), training seminars.
