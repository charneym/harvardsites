01Listen

The gut and the brain arein constant conversation.

Gut bacteria make and consume neuroactive molecules. Their signals can reach the brain by routes that researchers are still mapping.

  • iVagus nerve: a direct line from gut to brainstem
  • iiCirculating metabolites carried in the blood
  • iiiImmune signals from the gut
02Collect

The Microbiome Vault:human strains, in culture.

Holobiome is assembling what it describes as the world's most diverse, deeply profiled collection of human gut bacteria. Each strain is brought into culture one at a time, so it can be tested for what it actually does.

The company estimates there are about 10,000 known gut bacterial species. Public type-strain collections capture a few hundred of them, and about 50 are sold as probiotics today.NutraIngredients, May 2026

03Select

One strain thatmakes GABA.

The founding discovery was a "most-wanted" gut bacterium, KLE1738, that would only grow beside Bacteroides fragilis. The missing growth factor was GABA, a major inhibitory neurotransmitter. That finding led the team to gut bacteria that produce it.

Strandwitz P. et al., Nature Microbiology 4, 396–403 (2019). doi:10.1038/s41564-018-0307-3

04Signal

A fine signal,from gut to brain.

In a small study of people with major depressive disorder, faecal Bacteroides abundance was negatively correlated with depression-associated brain connectivity.

The finding is correlational and preliminary, which is why Holobiome is testing it further. Same paper, 2019.

05Develop

A next-generation probioticfor depression.

This is the lead program. The 2024 seed round is intended to advance it into human testing, and Holobiome says it will not sell anything until it has human data.

LaunchedOct 2026

GUT-FEELINGS:gut–brain scienceat population scale.

“The missing link is large-scale multimodal data from interventional studies, the kind this trial is designed to generate.”

Philip Strandwitz, PhD · CEO & co-founder

Holobiome and Radicle Science are running a randomized, placebo-controlled, decentralized study of food-derived prebiotics in people who feel anxious. The aim is to find which formulations improve mental wellness, and for whom.

Design
16 weeks, 20 arms: 19 prebiotic formulations vs. placebo
Scale
More than 6,000 Americans across 45 states
Outcomes
Anxiety, stress, mood, sleep and quality of life, plus stool sequencing, blood proteomics and wearable data
Support
An award from ARPA-H under its EVIDENT initiative
Readout
Initial results expected in the second half of 2027

Sources: NutraIngredients, 19 Oct 2026 · Nutraceuticals World, 12 Oct 2026
