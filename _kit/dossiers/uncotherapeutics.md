## About / Science (site)
- "Redefining GPCR drug discovery by creating new classes of therapeutics"; "building entirely new classes of therapeutics based
  on fundamentally different pharmacological principles."
- GPCRs: "more than 800 receptors located on the surface of cells throughout the body"; "molecular microswitches";
  "more than one-third of all approved drugs act by modulating GPCR activity."
- Opportunity: lack of efficacy a leading cause of clinical failure; only a small fraction of GPCRs targeted; chronic pain
  underserved; opioid risks; non-opioid alternatives lack efficacy.
- Pioneer program: Allosteric Uncouplers for difficult-to-target GPCRs in chronic pain and inflammation; block overactive
  receptors at the cell surface and from intracellular compartments; non-opioid therapies for arthritis, migraine, IBD, cancer.
- Platform: structural biology, AI-driven in silico screening, medicinal chemistry, translational pharmacology.

## Funding / activity
- NIH NIGMS STTR Phase I 1R41GM165099-01A1, "Development of Allosteric Uncouplers as Therapeutics for Chronic Pain",
  16 Sep 2025 – 15 Mar 2027, $350,000. https://reporter.nih.gov/project-details/11253679
  Abstract: proteases in cancer-associated, inflammatory and neuropathic pain activate PAR2 on sensory neurons by cleaving the
  N-terminus to expose a tethered peptide agonist (irreversible); traditional antagonists can't compete it off; PAR2 resisted
  small molecules, peptides, pepducins, antibodies. Allosteric uncouplers bind the intracellular G protein-binding site of the
  active receptor; PAR2–G protein interaction is transient so they can compete. Preliminary: computational/AI screening found
  several series; uncoupler function of some validated in ex vivo and in vivo pain models. Goal: optimise thiazolidine,
  naphthyl-diamine and pyrazolopyrimidine ("pyrazolopyramidines" in the abstract) series to potent, selective, drug-like,
  patentable compounds. Collaboration with the Thomsen Laboratory at NYU (site news).
- News (site /news, each links to /blog-news/...): 2026-06-01 Chitnis 3rd place oral presentation at AMPIRS (NYU College of
  Dentistry); 2026-03-01 AbbVie Golden Ticket (one year lab/office space at BioLabs @ NYU Langone); 2025-11-01 Chitnis joins as
  Co-Founder & Founding Scientist; 2025-09-01 STTR award.
