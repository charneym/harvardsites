## Hero
Option (c), a crafted render of their own product: the flexible, corrugated electrospun vascular graft shown on their site (Flexible-AV-graft.jpg) and in the JVS Vasc Sci papers, bent in a loose arc with open ends. It is ray-marched offline by `_kit/home/nanofibersolutions/render.py`: a torus-segment SDF with rib profile and slight irregularity, a thin hollow wall, matte white textile shading with fibre micro-texture in the normal, soft shadows, AO and a violet rim. It is baked with real alpha to hero.webp, so there is no stock photo and nothing is shared with another concept (DQ). It drifts in a slow Ken Burns breath with no pointer response.

## Story
How a graft is made, as one engraved scene: (1) a whipping electrospinning jet lays a nonwoven onto a rotating mandrel; (2) the wall is ribbed and the graft drawn as a U between the carotid artery and the jugular vein; (3) cells line the lumen as the scaffold resorbs, with "7 of 8 grafts open at one month · ovine" (JVS Vasc Sci 2020).
