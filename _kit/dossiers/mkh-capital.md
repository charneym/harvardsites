## Firm
- "MKH Partners for Growth. Creating stronger companies through flexible partnerships." (home, about)
- "MKH Capital Partners is an investment firm founded by entrepreneurial families and professional investors. MKH offers a strong combination of long-term family capital and operating expertise and resources." (https://www.mkhpartners.com/about/)
- "We actively partner with business owners and leading management teams to cultivate growth of niche industry players and create stronger companies that become regional/national leaders." (home, about)
- Initial capital came primarily from two families, the Motta and Kazma families; they have invested in and operated businesses across financial, telecom, retail, airlines, energy and distribution for the past 30 years and have partnered together for 20 years. They partnered with Miguel Heras, "a professional investor with over 30 years of experience". (about)
- Address on contact page and footer: 220 N Green St, Suite 209, Chicago, IL 60607. Email: contact@mkhpartners.com. (https://www.mkhpartners.com/contact-us/)
- About page also says "MKH is headquartered in Miami, FL and has an office in Panama City, Panama"; press releases call it "a family-backed, Miami-based private equity firm". The site's current address everywhere else is Chicago, so the concept uses the Chicago address only and avoids an HQ claim.
- DNA: "Flexible, Straightforward and Supportive Partners"; no "one size fits all" structure; strategic and on-the-ground support in human capital, acquisitions, financing, financial planning, procurement and technology; network of executives and experts. Values: Authenticity, Collaboration, Entrepreneurship, Excellence, Inclusion, Innovation, Integrity, Respect, Transparency, Trust. ESG policies and metrics. (https://www.mkhpartners.com/dna/)
- Referrals: "We welcome all introductions and reward intermediaries accordingly"; works with investment bankers, brokers, accountants and lawyers; "can act quickly, should the situation warrant swift diligence and decision-making." (https://www.mkhpartners.com/referrals/)

## Criteria (https://www.mkhpartners.com/investment-criteria/)
- Transaction types: control buyouts; significant minority partnerships; carveouts
- Target EBITDA $2–15 million; transaction size $10–200 million
- Qualitative: family/entrepreneur-owned businesses; partnership with leading management teams; consistent revenue growth; sustainable competitive advantages; diversified customer base
- Locations: United States, Canada and Latin America
- Current platforms looking for add-ons: European Wax Center franchisee; SUD / MH treatment provider
