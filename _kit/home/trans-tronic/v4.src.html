Products

Transformers, chokes and coil windings

These are a representation of what we make at our Chesterfield site. Most of our work is to customer specifications, or to new designs developed with our technical team.

  • PCB mounted, power, 3 phase, enclosed and encapsulated

    Laminated transformers

    From a standard basic transformer to a complicated custom specification. Thermal fuses and one-shot or resettable trips are available.

    Details
  • Up to 5 kVA

    Toroidal transformers

    A standard range from 15 VA to 1 kVA approved by UL and KEMA-KEUR, medical isolation transformers, and low-noise audio toroids for valve amplifiers.

    Details
  • RM, ETD, EFD, E cores and pot cores

    Ferrite transformers

    Transformers and chokes for switch mode power supplies: inverter, pulse and flyback transformers, boost and buck converters.

    Details
  • Laminated, ferrite and toroidal

    Chokes and inductors

    Common mode chokes for the suppression of EMI and RFI, and chokes for general purpose filtering.

    Details
  • Mains and output transformers, chokes, crossover coils

    Audio transformers

    For valve amplifiers, speakers and high end audio: low noise transformers and transformers with mumetal laminations.

    Details
  • Solenoid, self bonded and air coils

    Custom coil windings

    Coils for tattoo machines and water treatment, solenoid coils from car seat lumbar supports to missile firing mechanisms, and air coils for sensing.

From prototype to volume

How a Trans-Tronic part is made

  1. Design
  2. Winding
  3. Approval
  4. Volume
  1. 01 Design

    Your specification, our design help

    From a standard transformer to a complicated custom specification, the technical team helps with the design through prototype and development.

  2. 02 Winding

    Wound turn by turn

    Multiple primary and secondary windings where required. A 3D printer makes mandrels and jigs in-house, and a Solus skeining machine keeps wound joints to standard.

  3. 03 Approval

    Labelled, batched and approved

    A quality system to ISO 9001:2015, approved by LRQA, planned for right first time, with a toroid range approved by UL and KEMA-KEUR.

  4. 04 Volume

    One-off or in volume

    Enclosed and encapsulated assemblies, and more than 3.5 million components a year from the 11,000 square foot factory in Old Whittington.

Quality

Planned to be right first time

Our goal is to maintain and continually improve performance in every area, especially quality, cost and delivery, through an effective quality system and approvals our customers can check.

ISO 9001:2015LRQA
Quality system approved by LRQA, formerly Lloyd’s Register, certificate 10421237.
ULToroidal range
UL product approval of a range of toroidal transformers, licences XPTQ2 and XPTQ8 under file E179800.
KEMA-KEURDEKRA
KEMA-KEUR approval of a range of toroidal transformers, certificates 2149503.03 and 2149503.04.
Network RailProduct approvals
Network Rail product approvals, and a new range of Class II signalling transformers developed in collaboration with Network Rail.
Company

A family firm, grown by acquisition

Trans-Tronic is family run, with a multi-skilled and flexible workforce, and supplies customers across the UK and around the world from Chesterfield.

From a purpose built factory in Old Whittington the company brings more than 40 years of experience to a myriad of sectors.
