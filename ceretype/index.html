    Our mission

    Actionable individual and population insights, from detailed, commercially viable visibility into brain circuitry.

    Ceretype grew out of its physician-scientist co-founders' wish to translate years of academic work in psychiatric neurobiology and imaging methods into tools for drug development and clinical care, bringing new therapeutics to market faster and changing how psychiatric and neurological disorders are treated.

    The challenge in psychiatry and neurology care

    Biomarkers transformed cardiologyand oncology. The brain has waited.

    In nearly every area of medicine, biological evidence is imperative for diagnosis and treatment. Therapies for psychiatric and many neurological disorders have not benefited from brain-based biological evidence, despite years of research.

    Functional MRI can show neural activity, but its commercial translation had not been successful. Ceretype set out to change that.

    i  Solution

    Biological measurement of mechanism and markers of treatment effect

    Drug and device developers and clinicians often observe symptoms or rely on self-reporting to judge efficacy. A biological basis complements the patient interview with a fuller, patient-specific picture.

    ii  Solution

    Disease segmentation

    Psychiatric diagnoses vary widely and overlap. Biological evidence helps segment disease subtypes, and the ability to cohort patients enables better therapeutic solutions for underserved populations.

    How it works

    Brain circuit profiles acrossthe healthcare ecosystem.

    1. 01

      Protocol development

      Circuit maps are specified to the needs of the trial: specific regions, or whole-brain networks tied to the indication.

    2. 02

      fMRI before and on drug

      Subjects undergo fMRI before and while taking the study drug; longitudinal scans show treatment effects over minutes to years.

    3. 03

      Discovery in the Ceretype Cloud

      Biomarker discovery and measurement, with advanced processing that corrects artifacts for reproducible results across scanners and sites.

    4. 04

      Brain Insight Report

      An individual-level functional brain map designed by Ceretype's clinical neuroscientists, delivered across all trial phases.

    Precision

    Maps of brain activity and circuitry with resolution to the millimeter scale, measured precisely over time.

    Comparison

    Whole-brain, region-specific and circuit-specific measurements, compared against age-specific normal values.

    Specificity

    Neural circuit maps specific to the trial, such as dopamine circuits or the networks involved in treating depression.

    Integration

    Imaging integrates with behavioral, electrophysiology, structural and peripheral data, and with a sponsor's own algorithms.

    Offered directly and through Ceretype's partnership with Calyx, which describes the platform as an in-market, audit-cleared biomarker engine (April 2023).

    1. 01

      Patient presents with symptoms

      The starting point is the same clinical visit as today.

    2. 02

      Interview and next-generation fMRI

      The clinician interviews the patient, who undergoes Ceretype's next-generation fMRI.

    3. 03

      Clinician assesses key biomarkers

      Ceretype biomarkers help determine and monitor treatment, in conjunction with clinical evaluation.

    Broad applicability

    Disease- and patient-agnostic: usable for any person of any age with any disease.

    Decision support

    Clear metrics assess brain function in multiple dimensions, such as circuit activity over time and the number of active circuits.

    Platform

    A biomarker engine that continuously analyzes data and reports on brain health and treatment response.

    Integration

    Compliant integration into any RIS or DICOM system, with clear treatment-response reporting for clinics, studies, patients and payers.
