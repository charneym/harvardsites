## Activity
- 21.08.2026, EQS ad-hoc „KAP AG gibt Veränderung im Aktionariat bekannt“ (Fulda): Carlyle Group sold a block of shares to Tempus Capital GmbH; Carlyle's stake falls to 16,5 %; Tempus Capital GmbH acquires 29,0 % of KAP AG's issued shares; Carlyle remains economically exposed to the sold shares under the agreements. https://www.boerse-online.de/dpa-afx/eqs-adhoc-kap-ag-gibt-veraenderung-im-aktionariat-bekannt-deutsch-546638.html

- 16.09.2026, supplementary notice (sent 14.09.2026) with the aims of the holding: value-oriented strategy primarily aimed at profit from the holding; intends to contribute „unternehmerische Expertise aktiv und konstruktiv in die Governance der KAP AG“; willing to propose suitable candidates for supervisory board seats; seeks an open dialogue with the company's bodies; no further acquisition of voting rights intended within twelve months; no material changes to the capital structure intended. https://www.boerse-online.de/dpa-afx/eqs-stimmrechte-kap-ag-deutsch-551846.html
