## Criteria
- Equity (https://culpercapital.com/equity-capital/): management-backed LBOs; platform investments with add-on opportunities; growth capital; direct investments into PE funds; secondary PE transactions. Revenue > $10M*, EBITDA > $5M*, high gross margins with significant operating leverage, EBITDA margins 20%+. (*No minimums for add-ons.) Industries: Business/Industrial Services; General Industrial; Medical Device Contracting or Distribution; Niche Manufacturing; Machinery & Aftermarket Parts/Service; Healthcare Services. Target profile: asset-light niche businesses with strong positions in fragmented industries; high barriers to entry; mission-critical products with recession protection; consolidation via add-ons; recurring revenues, attractive cash flow, growth. Geography: United States. Home page: Control, Minority, Co-Investment or Secondary; management-backed recaps, corporate carve outs, founder owned businesses in transition.
- Debt (https://culpercapital.com/debt-capital/): unitranche, 2nd lien, mezzanine, subordinated, credit funds; Revenue > $10M, EBITDA > $3M; investment size $2–10M; industries: Business/Industrial Services, Niche Manufacturing, Value-added Distribution, Software, Real Estate, Other.
- Industrial Solutions (https://culpercapital.com/industrial-solutions/): control and non-control investments with investment partners in niche industrial companies, enterprise value up to $500M; U.S. or Western Europe; verticals: industrial distribution, test & inspection, automation products & services, Tier II aerospace components, HVAC products & services, precision machining, safety products, measurement & control products, building controls & solutions, machine tool solution manufacturing, medical device manufacturing.
