## Science used on the page (all from the award's summary and narrative)
CK2 as a target to block inflammation in glia and boost mitochondrial function in neurons. Talos designed a
brain-permeable CK2 inhibitor that is well tolerated and showed in vivo proof of concept in an AD mouse model after
3 weeks of daily treatment. Phase I aims: brain exposure, ADME/tox, selectivity, preliminary safety pharmacology,
and selection of an orally bioavailable, brain-penetrant lead.
"Further reading" link (independent, not claimed as Talos work): Gage et al., "CK2 inhibition suppresses glial
inflammation in the brain", bioRxiv 2025, doi 10.1101/2025.08.05.668554. The preprint's probe is named TAL606, but
no source links it to Talos, so the page does not mention it.
