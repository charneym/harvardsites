## The company
"a biotech company pioneering a machine learning-driven platform to decipher oncology drugs' true mechanisms-of-action"; "building the industry's first atlas of drug mechanisms and their impact on cancer biology"; "molecular fingerprints" for tens of thousands of compounds feed an inference engine that identifies small-molecule and target MOA pairs (Business Wire, 29 Sept 2022, $11M seed, led by HOF Capital and ZhenFund; with Obvious Ventures, Village Global, BrightEdge (American Cancer Society), Pebblebed, Axial, Olive Capital). HQ then given as Menlo Park, CA.
2024 boilerplate (Business Wire, 12 Feb and 17 Jul 2024): San Francisco and Cambridge-based machine-learning company discovering and developing small-molecule oncology therapies for high-value cancer targets with its proprietary platform AnchorOmics (multimodal, mechanism-of-action ML algorithm); $11M raised to date; drug discovery operations at Bayer's Co.Lab in Kendall Square. Founded 2021 by Jason Sheltzer, Joan Smith and David Li.
Address: Meliora Therapeutics, 238 Main Street, Cambridge, MA (affiliation in the Nature Communications paper; Bayer Co.Lab).

## Activity
- 19 Oct 2026: Nature Communications, "A genetic platform to deconvolve efficacy and toxicity reveals the on-target effects of CDK11 inhibition in vivo" (PMID 43045713, doi 10.1038/s41467-026-78814-0); Claudio Chuaqui, Meliora Therapeutics, among the authors; corresponding author Jason Sheltzer (Stanford). Findings: CDK11 is a core cancer dependency, needed for RNA splicing and HR gene expression; synthetic lethality with PARP inhibitors; 1p36 heterozygous deletion predicts sensitivity; GEMM separates on- from off-target effects; MEL-495R (oral CDK11 inhibitor) shows on-target toxicity and a narrow therapeutic index. The site states this plainly, without spin.
- May 2024: BioWorld reports Meliora patented CDK11 inhibitors / apoptosis inducers (breast cancer, melanoma). The site says "has filed patents".
