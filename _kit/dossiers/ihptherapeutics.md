## The company
IHP Therapeutics is developing IHP-102 for acute vaso-occlusive crisis (VOC/VOE) in sickle cell disease, aiming to be the
first self-administered rescue therapeutic and to move care to the home (ihptherapeutics.com About). IHP-102 is a novel dual
P-selectin and complement inhibitor (Science), glycan-based with pleiotropic activity (NIH abstracts).
Pipeline chart on the site: IHP-102 (lead; acute VOE in SCD) in IND-enabling studies, 100% of resources; additional
thrombo-inflammatory indications under investigation. IHP-101 (solid tumours with sialyl Lewis overexpression) through in
vitro validation, paused for resources, to be developed through partnership and grants.
