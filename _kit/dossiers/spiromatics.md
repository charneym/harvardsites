## Facts used
- Site: lung imaging (standard and proprietary CT biomarkers; phenotyping, progression prediction, stratification), spirometry (signal processing and AI, beyond FEV1/FVC), partners (pharma, device, academic), history (founded 2023; pulmonology, BME, ECE; publications and patents), "see us in person or on Zoom".
- NIH abstract: ~42 million US with obstructive lung disease; ~70% of spirometry-defined obstruction undiagnosed; diagnosis unchanged since the 1940s; FEV1/FVC threshold depends on demographics; portable handheld miniature spirometer with audiovisual coaching and embedded firmware; two-minute patient-friendly method; primary care, home monitoring, earlier exacerbation detection.
- UAB News: device could deliver results in ~30 s vs up to 30 min with a pulmonologist's reading; first startup supported by the Blazer Bridge Fund; preparing for FDA clearance; Phase II STTR and a Birmingham manufacturing partner planned.
- Harbert Institute: proprietary method analyses the shape of the spirometry volume curve; may identify ~10% more patients with COPD roughly five years earlier.
