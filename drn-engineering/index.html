Turning and milling

Turn, mill, drill and tap in one operation.

DRN offers the subcontract machining of precision components in a wide range of batch sizes, and specialises in the CNC machining of stainless steels 303, 304, 316 and 416.

Sliding head

Small diameters, long and slender

Star sliding head lathes with sub-spindle and driven tooling, fed from the magazine, for precision parts from 3 mm diameter up to 25.4 mm.

Fixed head

Up to 65 mm from the bar

Citizen Miyano and Miyano lathes with up to nine axes and a sub-spindle turn, mill, drill and tap a part in one operation.

Chucking

From 65 mm to 178 mm

Larger turned parts are held in the 7 inch chuck of the Doosan Puma GT2100 turning centre.

Second operations

Hexagons, slots and flats

When it is more cost-effective, milling is done as a second operation on four Haas vertical machining centres. Plating and heat treatment are subcontracted.

From a 3 metre bar to a finished part, on a sliding head lathe

  1. Fed from the magazine

    The magazine bar loader feeds the bar through the main spindle and out through the guide bush. A small batch of 100 is often the product of a single 3 metre bar.

  2. Turned beside the guide bush

    The headstock slides the bar forward while the tool moves in and out, so every cut is taken right next to its support. That is how slender parts stay accurate.

  3. Drilled by a driven tool

    Driven tooling cross drills, mills and taps while the bar is still held, so most parts need no second set-up.

  4. Parted off into the sub-spindle

    The sub-spindle grips the part, the parting tool cuts it free and the back is machined, all in one operation. More jobs in one operation is a cost saving for the customer.

History

A Luton family firm since 1982.

The company was registered by Don Newport in October 1982, and has grown on annual exhibitions, a loyal customer base and regular investment in new machines.

  1. 1982

    Don Newport registers the company in October. Gary Oakley joins from day one as Works Manager.

  2. 1985

    Paul Newport joins and builds up the customer base through annual exhibitions, from Subcon to Southern Manufacturing.

  3. 1997

    The move to a new factory, a modern 7,000 sq ft industrial unit with the floor area for more CNC lathes and machining centres.

  4. 2025

    A Star SP23 turn and mill machine, then a Citizen Miyano BNE 65 MYY nine-axis lathe, join the plant.
