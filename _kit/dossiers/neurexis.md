## Science (sourced)
Home: neurodegeneration is "a shared hallmark across major disorders of the central nervous system, including global cerebral
ischemia, spinal cord ischemia, stroke, and Alzheimer's disease"; "there are no approved therapies that directly prevent
neurodegeneration"; tatCN19o is "a first-in-class, optimized peptide therapeutic designed to target a key signaling molecule
downstream of glutamate excitotoxicity". "Surviving a brain disorder is only half the battle."
Technology page: 30 amino acid peptide, two functional domains: one crosses cell membranes and the blood brain barrier; the second is a
CaMKII-specific inhibitor. PK half-life 10–15 min in vivo. Protected by four issued US patents. Proof of concept — Rodent: neuroprotective
after cardiac arrest and CPR; after ischemic stroke; works with therapeutic hypothermia standard of care; prevented ischemia-induced loss
of synaptic plasticity and protected memory function. Pig: neuroprotective after ventricular fibrillation and return of spontaneous
circulation. Safety: no acute adverse effects in mice at 1,000x therapeutic dose, pigs at 20x; no off-target binding in vitro; no adverse
effects on memory. "even when the drug is administered hours after acute neuronal injury". Citations: Wu et al. JTCVS (2025); Rumian et al.
JBC (2023); Deng et al. Cell Reports (2017); Coultrap and Bayer PLoS One (2011); Buard et al. J Neurosci (2010); Vest et al. JBC (2010);
Vest et al. MBC (2007).
NIH R44NS120427 abstract: Ki 0.1 nM; selectivity >100,000x vs most closely related kinases; "only engages the CaMKII fraction that remains
hyperactivated after ischemic insult"; effective dose 0.01 mg/kg i.v.; cell- and BBB-penetrating 30mer; inhibits rodent and human CaMKII
equally; Phase II = IND-enabling: in vivo characterisation, formulation, pivotal safety studies, regulatory documents. GCI "afflicts over
700,000 Americans each year".
NIH U44NS132339 abstract: stroke, comparative testing in NINDS SPAN (rodent tMCAo); neuroprotection seen 0.5 h after GCI, 1 h after stroke
model, 6 h in neuronal cultures.
NIH R43AG094353 abstract (NIA, Sept 2026): Alzheimer's: restoring LTP in surviving neurons; tatCN19o restored LTP in 7–9-month-old 5xFAD
mice in vitro and after in vivo injection, lasting at least one week; learning effects mild and transient (<30 min).
