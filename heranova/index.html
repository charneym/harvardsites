HerResolve™ · Blood test for endometriosis

Years of uncertainty, resolved in a single blood draw.

HerResolve™ is a non-invasive blood test that confirms endometriosis in women with symptoms. It is ordered by healthcare providers and available now at select clinics nationwide.

01 · The wait

Six to twelve years.

That is the average delay to an endometriosis diagnosis. Because confirmation has relied on laparoscopic surgery, many patients wait while clinicians treat empirically.2

02 · One blood draw

All of it, in one drop.

HerResolve™ starts with a standard blood draw, ordered by a healthcare provider. No surgery is needed to take the test. The sample is analysed in the laboratory.

03 · The signals

Seven molecular signals.

  • Three circulating microRNAsqPCR
  • Three proteinsImmunoassay
  • One steroid hormoneImmunoassay

Combined with age and BMI. HerAnova has not publicly named the individual markers.3

04 · The model

Read together, not alone.

A random-forest model weighs all nine inputs. It was optimised for specificity and validated on an independent cohort with no overlap with its training data.3

05 · The answer

A clear answer.

Positive or negative, to aid diagnosis alongside clinical evaluation, imaging and/or laparoscopy. A triage tool, not a replacement for surgery.

Patients & clinicians03
For patients

Is HerResolve™ for me?

HerResolve™ is for people assigned female at birth, aged 18 to 49, with signs or symptoms that suggest endometriosis, such as pelvic pain, severely painful periods, abnormal bleeding or unexplained infertility. Your clinician will confirm whether the test is right for you.

  1. 01

    Talk to a healthcare provider

    HerResolve™ can only be ordered by a healthcare provider.

  2. 02

    Find a participating clinic, or bring yours in

    HerAnova can point you to a participating clinic, or contact your current provider.

  3. 03

    One blood draw

    No surgery is needed to take the test.

  4. 04

    A positive or negative result

    Your clinician reviews it with you and plans next steps.

For clinicians

Confirm earlier, less invasively.

An LDT-validated blood test that confirms endometriosis in symptomatic women, so personalised pharmaceutical or surgical management can begin sooner. Intended to assist diagnosis as part of a comprehensive approach that may include clinical evaluation, imaging (MRI, ultrasound) and/or laparoscopy.

Eligibility
  • Assigned female at birth, ages 18–49
  • Signs or symptoms suspicious of endometriosis: unexplained infertility, pelvic or chronic pelvic pain, abnormal bleeding, severely painful periods
Exclusions & washouts
  • Pregnant or lactating; miscarriage in the past 4 weeks
  • Known cancer, or undergoing cancer treatment
  • Antibiotics within 1 week of testing
  • GnRH, ovarian stimulation, IVF adjunctive or hormonal therapies within 4 weeks (oral contraceptives excepted)
  • Postmenopausal (≥ 1 year without a period)
