## The company
Neocor Therapeutics SAS (SIREN 980 496 970, created 2023-10-10, président Matthieu Metz; registered office 785 avenue du
Peymian, 13600 La Ciotat — recherche-entreprises.api.gouv.fr). Marseille biotech founded end of 2023, from academic research
at Marseille Medical Genetics (Inserm / Aix-Marseille Université); works from the La Timone research laboratory (Made in
Marseille, 2025-10-29). Mission (neocor.fr): "to uncover novel innovative therapeutic approaches for heart regeneration and repair".
Two approaches (neocor.fr): promoting cardiomyocyte renewal; preventing cardiac fibrosis; for ischemic and rare genetic cardiac disease.

## Mechanism (i-Lab 2025 press release, Marseille, 15 Oct 2025, eurobiomed.org PDF)
- Unique mechanism based on a protein identified and patented by the founding team; patent since 2018 (Made in Marseille).
- Delivered by a viral vector; three complementary modes of action: prevents cardiac cell death after injury; activates regeneration
  by reactivating their capacity to multiply; prevents formation of scar tissue. A single intravenous injection should durably restore cardiac function.
- Indications: heart after myocardial infarction; dilated cardiomyopathy (hereditary). >33,000 new DCM patients a year in Europe and the US.
- Plans: from 2026 large-scale production to pharmaceutical standards, then regulatory studies; first Phase I/II trial in 2027.
- GO4BIO accelerator (Eurobiomed). Supported by French Tech Emergence, SATT Sud-Est, Eurobiomed, Impulse. Site partner logos also: Bpifrance Deeptech, CAAP Capital Innovation, WAI BNP Paribas.
- Seeking €7.5M (Made in Marseille) — not used on the page.
