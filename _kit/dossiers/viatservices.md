## Activity (pre-screen: OK)
- **NSF SBIR Phase I, award 2528215** — "SBIR Phase I: Immersive Virtual Reality-Based Assessment Tool for Spatial Orientation".
  Award date 2025-08-04; start 2025-10-01, expires 2027-03-31; $304,998 (FY2025). PI: Maria Kozhevnikov (maria@viatservices.com).
  NSF TIP / Start-ups and Small Businesses; program Visualization & Virtual Design.
  https://www.nsf.gov/awardsearch/showAward?AWD_ID=2528215
  Abstract (used for hero, story, VR section, applications): first scientifically valid immersive VR tool for spatial orientation
  ("the capacity to imagine a reoriented self or a change of perspective within an environment"); essential in aviation, healthcare,
  public safety, where over 10 million Americans are assessed each year; existing computerized tools unreliable and fail to activate
  the sensory systems involved; current tests measure allocentric processing from a fixed viewpoint; instrument activates vestibular
  and proprioceptive input and constrains allocentric strategies; iterative development with behavioral testing, real-world navigation
  tasks and EEG-based validation; reorientation angles, pointing directions and response dynamics optimized to isolate egocentric
  processing; portable, scalable; annual licensing; at least three major industries by year three; applications across aviation,
  defense, healthcare and STEM education.
- Home page (2026): "Supported by an NSF SBIR Phase I award"; VR tool "with applications to aviation, aerospace, navigation, and other
  safety-critical environments". Uploads dated 2026/10.
- Not pathogens/toxins/biodefence: cognitive assessment.
