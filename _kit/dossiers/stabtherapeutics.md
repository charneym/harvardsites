## The company
Spin-off of the Research Institute Hospital 12 de Octubre (imas12), Madrid, developing next-generation immune-redirection
strategies for solid and haematological tumours. STAb immunotherapy combines adoptive cell therapy and bispecific antibodies:
engineered T cells secrete T cell-engagers (TCE) in vivo, giving effective serum TCE concentrations, expansion and persistence
of the STAb-T cells, and polyclonal recruitment of modified and unmodified bystander T cells at the tumour. Preclinically
validated in B-cell and T-cell haematological malignancies. Source: stabtherapeutics.com (About, Strategy).
Office: Calle Ferraz 78, 2º A, 28008 Madrid, Spain (site). Email: info@stabtherapeutics.com (site). Co-founders Luis
Álvarez-Vallina and Belén Blanco (BIO-Europe Spring sponsor profile, informaconnect.com).
