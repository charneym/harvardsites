# Argonne Capital Group (slug: argonne-capital)

Facts only, each with its source. Retrieved 2026-11-18.

## Firm
- Closely-held private investment firm, founded 2003; first acquisition 2004. Source: https://argonnecapital.com/about/
- Independently capitalizes each investment; indefinite time horizon for ownership; preferred acquirer for closely-held companies and unique deal dynamics. Source: https://argonnecapital.com/ and /about/
- "Since inception, Argonne has completed over 60 acquisitions"; portfolio generates over $1.8 billion in annual sales and employs over 15,000 people. Source: https://argonnecapital.com/
- Real estate expertise led by Michael Klump (co-founded the private REIT Equity Investment Group, ~120 retail shopping centers); over 100 new locations developed internally across the portfolio. Source: /about/
- Stated aim: "staying disciplined on price, protecting our downside and taking a long-term view of our investments." Source: /about/
- Over $2 billion of equity invested since 2003 (2023 release); $1B+ in continuation vehicles closed July 2023 (SRP / IHOP Florida, NFP / Planet Fitness). Source: https://argonnecapital.com/argonne-capital-group-announces-closing-1-billion-continuation-vehicles-four-executive-appointments-firm-pursues-new-era-growth/
- HQ: One Buckhead Plaza, 3060 Peachtree Rd NW, Suite 425, Atlanta, GA 30305. Tel (404) 364-2984, fax (404) 364-2985, admin@argonnecapital.com. Source: /contact/ . Time zone: America/New_York.

## Investment criteria (source: https://argonnecapital.com/investment-criteria/)
- Situations: divestitures of closely-held family business; operating companies with real estate holdings; broken or stalled auctions; undermanaged or troubled assets; management buyouts; corporate carve-outs; "end of fund" buyouts; public-to-privates.
- Metrics: $5 to $150 million of EBITDA; up to $300 million of equity per transaction; willing to pay buy-side advisory fees.
- Sectors: restaurants; retail (non-apparel); fitness; landscaping services; home services; quick service oil change; full-service automotive repair; dealerships (auto, agriculture, other); specialty manufacturing; light industrial; value-add distribution; franchise entities.
