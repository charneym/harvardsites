From the extruder to the bin.

Downstream of the extruder, a Gillard line pulls, cuts and collects the product without stopping.

PullGuideCutCollect

A caterpillar takes the line.

Twin belts, each driven by its own direct-drive AC servo motor through a sealed-for-life planetary gearbox, grip the extrusion and pull it steadily at line speed.

Into the cutter head.

The extrusion feeds into the cutter through a guide bush sized to the product, from medical tubing up to 300 mm outer diameter; a slide-away head opens for a quick change.

One sweep, one clean cut.

The Servo-Torq® fly knife slices across the bush face under Lenze servo control, at any rate from 1 to 2,000 cuts a minute, with blade lubrication as standard.

Collected, length after length.

A conveyor supports the extrusion out of the cutter for better length accuracy, then air jets or a plough move each cut length into the bin, with scrap kept apart.

Cutters, pullers and the whole line.

Every machine is custom made to order, with solid engineering and the best available components, from table-top cutters to ultra-high performance lines.

  • Servo-Torq® fly-knife rotary cutters

    From table-top versions to ultra-high performance machines: free-standing, Mini, Medi, Eco-Cut™ and XXHD.

  • Caterpillar haul-offs and pullers

    Twin-belt units with one direct-drive AC servo motor per belt, and belt lengths from 250 mm to 1800 mm.

  • Circular blade cutters and saws

    Including the Braid-Cut™ MT and HD, for PTFE hose covered with braided stainless steel wire.

  • Cuffed and bubble tube systems

    Camera, optical, laser and photo-electric sensing that finds each cuff, bubble or taper.

  • Conveyor collection systems

    Air-jet and plough conveyors that support the extrusion and separate scrap from good product.

  • Food extrusion equipment

    Cutters and pullers for toasted breads, croutons, breadsticks and dog chews, built to food hygiene standards.

  • Coiling machines and pay-offs

    Servo coilers and pay-off systems for the end of the line.

  • Options and accessories

    Slide-away cutter heads, SpeedCut™ up to 12,000 cuts a minute, blade lubrication and heating tunnels.
