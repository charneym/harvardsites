## Facts used (dembiopharma.com and the company's press releases)
- Privately held, Cambridge, MA, pre-IND; dedicated to discovering novel therapeutic targets for solid tumors and developing first-in-class cancer medicines; proprietary CRISPR-based functional genomic screening platform to attack tumor cells and the TME.
- Launch, June 23, 2022: $70M initial financing led by Longwood Fund and Alta Partners, with Insight Partners, Pfizer Ventures, Astellas Venture Management, Emerson Collective, UTokyo Innovation Platform and Alexandria Venture Investments. "Don't eat me" (DEM) and "eat me" (EM) signals; CHoMP™ platform (Co-culture with Human Myeloid Phagocytes), inter-cellular CRISPR screening with tumor cells, primary macrophages and other innate immune effector cells.
- DEM-TXX: novel plasma membrane-associated glycoprotein, primarily on mucosal epithelial cells; identified as a "DEM" signal in a CRISPRa screen of tumor cells; roles in cell adhesion and myeloid cell suppression; notably overexpressed in colorectal, gastric, liver, pancreatic, cervical and testicular cancers (the "6" stat); expression negatively correlated with disease progression.
- DEM301: first-in-class bifunctional ADC; afucosylated mAb against DEM-TXX conjugated to a clinically validated linker-payload; direct cytotoxic delivery plus immune-mediated mechanisms; potent, durable single-agent efficacy in preclinical colorectal/GI models; favorable safety in a humanized DEM-TXX mouse model; preclinical pharmacology/toxicology and CMC under way; Phase 1a/1b expected to begin 2H 2026 (as of January 2026; the site's pipeline page says IND mid-2026). The site's DEM301 figure shows the afucosylated Fc engaging FcγRIIIa.
- Team, board, co-founders and SAB from the About page (summaries cut to what the bios state). Dr. Merad and Dr. Quéva (older press) are not on the current About page, so they are omitted.
