## Facts used (all from lightel.com unless noted)
- About: designs, manufactures and supplies products and services to the fiber optic industry worldwide since 1999; started with coupler workstations for fused biconic taper products, expanded into fused fiber components, micro-optic passive components and video microscopes; Seattle-based HQ (sales and marketing, administrative, R&D, engineering, domestic production) with offshore engineering and manufacturing; design for small or volume manufacturing in house; many US patents awarded, several pending; customer-service recognition awards from manufacturing partners; worldwide distributor network.
- Home: ISO 9001 certified; "Components. Flexible. Custom."; CI-1100 (compact, easy to focus, rugged fold-up monitor, 100+ tips).
- Press release, 4 March 2020: incorporated in 1999; high-power tap couplers made by FBT, 1x2 or 2x2, 980–2000 nm, 100% tested at customer-specified optical powers up to 150 W; markets: optical communications, aerospace, sensors.
- CEO message, 27 March 2020 (signed Benson Shen, CEO): drop-shipments from the Shenzhen, China factory; Renton, WA facility.
- Passive components page: FBT and PLC couplers and splitters (single/dual/three window, PM, 1 µm and 2 µm), all tested before shipment; fused, filter, pump/signal WDMs, CWDM, DWDM; isolators, circulators, attenuators, VOAs, hybrids, PM patchcords.
- Inspection page: DI-3000 (embedded WiFi, autofocus probe), DI-2000 (one-touch autofocus), DI-1000 and DI-1000L-PRO (USB, ConnectorView Plus pass/fail), DI-1000MPO+ (X and Y scanning), CI-1100 (fold-up 3.5" LCD). News: SN and URM connector tips.
- Equipment page and product pages: hydrogen workstations for FBT devices, power combiners, WDMs, thermal core expansion; CW-7000 (end-pump combiners up to 37×1 / (36+1)×1, no glass tubes or capillaries, hybrid linear torch with micro nozzle); FiberForge (80–400 µm fibers, heating and pulling, 2-channel power meter 800–1700 nm, pulling motors); GPST-1100 (patented gliding-plasma stripping, up to 5 fibers, carbon and polyimide coatings, up to 100 mm); LTL-H2-500-2 hydrogen generator (>99.999%).
- Services page: OEM, contract manufacturing (clean rooms, environmentally compliant materials), component integration, ODM, ODM+.
- Contact: sales@lightel.com (sales), info@lightel.com (general), (425) 277-8000.
