## Story
A nerve-cell membrane holds a full-length 7-helix mu receptor (Exon 1) and a 6TM EM receptor (Exon 11), with pain pulses along the nerve. Step 1: pain signals. Step 2: opioids dock on the full-length receptor and its side effects appear. Step 3: CYT-1010 docks on the EM receptor, the pulses fade and the side effects clear.
