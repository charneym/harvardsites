## Awards
- NSF SBIR Phase I 2125984 (2021-08-15 → 2023-01-31, $272,082): co-presence; 24 pairs incl. 12 older-adult pairs; VRTogether interaction felt similar to sharing physical space; teamwork ratings most aligned with partners in VRTogether. PI email richie@ralphvr.com.
- NIA SBIR Phase I 1R43AG079739-01A1 (2023-09-20 → 2025-04-30, $452,016): VRSingTogether, networked VR singing/music/light rhythmic activity; feasibility with adults ≥65 with MCI and carers.
- NCCIH SBIR Phase I 1R43AT013038-01 (2024-09-17 → 2026-09-14, $254,892): Virtual PARTNER, multi-component VR mind-body intervention for ICU delirium. UK news 2025-09-23: UK pulmonary/critical care physician partners with VRTogether; UK HealthCare to pilot PARTNER in the ICU.
- NIA SBIR Phase II 2R44AG079739-02 (2026, $1,050,008): DUET (Dyadic Unity for Engagement and Therapy): music therapy + gamified engagement + immersive VR for older adults with MCI/ADRD and caregivers; four iterative design cycles with dyads; six-month study with 40 dyads; primary outcome NIH Toolbox Cognitive Battery; secondary depression, sleep, caregiver burden, neuropsychiatric symptoms. ">15 million older adults ... live at home with MCI or ADRD, more than 8 million supported by cohabitating family caregivers."
