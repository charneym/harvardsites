# Elaghmore (elaghmore)
Sources: https://elaghmore.com/ (home, /about/, /team/, /portfolio/ + company pages, /case-studies/, /category/news/, /contact/), fetched 2026-11-18.

## Firm
- "A firm of industrialists, business advisers and investors. From our £90m fund, we buy businesses in the UK that show real promise." (/about/)
- Buys companies from entrepreneurs, founders and family owners; supportive shareholders to managers, helping drive organic growth; typically aims to double a company's revenues within the first 3-to-5 years (/about/).
- What they look for: Leadership (talented, ambitious management team); Uniqueness (clear differentiation); Potential (significant growth potential realisable by a supportive owner with operational skills and a deep international network); Markets (fragmented markets with bolt-on opportunities). Values: integrity, respect, hard work, openness (/about/).
- Track record: began 2004 as a partnership between David Manning and Andy Ducker; over 17 years completed 14 investments and made a 14x return on invested capital; investing since 2016 from first fund of £60m with a £30m top-up in 2020 (/about/). Trutex release (28 Aug 2025): Fund 1 launched Dec 2016 with £60m; a further £40m raised from US investors to create Fund 1A.
- Team page: began from a base in the UK Midlands investing own money; first investment Dane Group (150-year-old family business), sold to its largest US competitor; in 2016 major financial institutions from the US and Europe asked them to invest on their behalf.
- Strapline: "Grow more. Build more. Be more." Home: "We buy companies. We buy into people."
- Ethics & conduct: human rights in the investment cycle, health & safety, supply-chain labour and modern slavery (/about/).
- Regulatory line (footer): Elaghmore Advisory LLP is an Appointed Representative of NCM Fund Services Limited (FRN 183732), authorised and regulated by the FCA.
