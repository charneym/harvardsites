## Positioning (their words)
- Home, "Who We Are": "We are an operationally-focused private investment firm that specializes in investing in and growing founder- and family-owned industrial, manufacturing and business-services companies. We invest in people and partner with management teams to help companies reach their fullest potential."
- Home slider headlines: "Stewards of Legacies / Partners for Growth"; "Long-Term Capital / Investing in People"; "Passionate Investors / Operators & Strategists".
- "How We Are Different": "Combining decades of operating and investing experience with a long-term focused and flexible investment approach, our team delivers superior value for all our stakeholders."
- "What We Believe": "a philosophy centered on people, alignment of interests and transparency, active operational involvement and flexible long-term capital."
- Origin (https://auxopartners.com/origin2.php): Αὐξώ (Auxo, "Increaser") was the Greek goddess of growth, personification of spring and protector of fertility; farmers prayed to her for correct timing in planting. "In scientific nomenclature, auxo is used … to indicate an agent of growth or acceleration." Auxo "makes investments in companies at transition points in their history, then instills infrastructure, processes and talent that nurture their success … as our name reflects, we are not merely investors, but partners."
- 2026 release boilerplate: "Michigan's leading lower middle market private investment firm"; "flexible capital model allows for long-term, growth-oriented decision-making without artificial deadlines … specifically designed for family businesses facing a generational transition or management teams seeking a partner to fuel growth."

## Investment philosophy (https://auxopartners.com/philosophy.php)
- **Long-Term Investors:** "We desire to own companies for extended periods of time. Our unique structure and investment model aligns investors with the company's interests, creating the flexibility to take a perpetual approach to our investments. This allows us to avoid excessive leverage and short-term decision making…"
- **Trusted Stewards:** "We care deeply about the employees, communities and customers of the companies we invest in…"
- **A Human Endeavor:** "We believe business is a human endeavor, not a spreadsheet exercise."
- **Supporting US Manufacturing:** "We believe domestic manufacturing makes America and American families stronger, so we strive to preserve and create US manufacturing jobs."
- **Conscious Capitalism:** integrate social and corporate governance concerns into investment and portfolio management.

## Why Auxo / approach (https://auxopartners.com/whyauxo.php)
- **Experienced Investors, Trusted Buyers:** "Our founders have over 80 years of private equity, investing and operating experience. We bring speed, certainty and creativity to transactions." Support with strategic plans, financial modeling, add-on acquisition analysis and negotiation, lender relationship sourcing and management.
- **Efficient Diligence, Flexible Capital:** "superior deal-execution expertise to the lower-middle market." Capital from "company principals and a select group of families and private investors" — no "predetermined investment timeframes or excessive debt."
- **Value Added, Aligned Partners:** "hands-on enough to know the business's people and understand its key drivers, while hands-off enough to empower the management team…" Model aligns with family offices.
- **Patience, Focus, Proven Success:** "We invest in the long-term success of each business, not short-term exit strategies."

## Investment criteria — exactly as published
Main site (https://auxopartners.com/portfolio_criteria.php):
- North America-based
- Manufacturing, industrial, value-added distribution, transportation or business services
- EBITDA (cashflow) of $2.0-$20 million
- Owner-operators seeking retirement or family succession planning solutions; or existing teams seeking to grow their businesses
- We prefer majority-control investments, and will consider select minority-partner opportunities

Michigan Opportunity Fund micro-site (https://auxopartners.com/michigan/index1.php):
- Michigan-based companies (Outside of MI for add-on acquisitions)
- Manufacturing, business services, value-added distribution, Healthcare, technology, food & beverage, consumer products
- Existing management teams seeking to grow the business or founder-led and family-owned businesses seeking retirement or family-succession planning solutions
- EBITDA (cash flow) of $2.0 - $20.0 million
- Flexible capital including majority-control investments, minority equity, subordinated debt and distressed situations

2026 release version: North America-based; manufacturing, industrial, or business services; EBITDA of $2-$20 million; owner-operators seeking retirement/family succession or teams seeking growth.
No revenue range or check size published.
