# MaxQ Medical, Inc. — dossier (facts with sources)

## Company
- MaxQ Medical, Inc., Sunnyvale, California. Private health-tech company developing an all-in-one transurethral imaging and therapy system for prostate care. Source: [PRNewswire, Aug 20, 2026](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/maxq-medical-raises-31-5-million-series-a-backed-by-olympus-to-advance-its-prostate-care-platform-302855815.html)
- Described as "a fully automated, urethra-preserving, outpatient ultrasound imaging and targeted therapy system for prostate care." (release, "About MaxQ Medical")
- Website copy: "The next frontier in prostate care"; "World's first transurethral all-in-one imaging & therapy system"; "See Better, Treat Better"; "The world's first fully automated, urethra-preserving, outpatient ultrasound imaging & targeted HIFU therapy system."; "Now urologists can deliver fully customized treatment plans tailored to each patient's anatomy, achieving optimal outcomes with minimal side-effects." Source: https://maxqmedical.com
- The MaxQ System is investigational and is not currently available for commercial use in the United States. (release)
- First spinout of Orchard Ultrasound Innovation (Sunnyvale), which commercializes semiconductor ultrasound technology developed at Stanford; Orchard co-founder and CSO Prof. Pierre Khuri-Yakub invented the Capacitive Micromachined Ultrasonic Transducer (CMUT). (release)

## Science / platform (from the release)
- Combines advanced imaging with tissue-selective therapy in a single transurethral procedure, giving urologists full visibility inside the prostate and the ability to treat what they see, without the anatomic limitations of approaches that work from outside the prostate.
- Designed to preserve the urethra and minimise side effects such as injury, swelling and bleeding, and impact on sexual function.
- Aim (CEO quote): "the durability of gold standard resective therapies with side-effects more comparable to tMIS solutions."
- Indications: first BPH; then focal therapy for prostate cancer; longer term broader prostate diagnostics incl. biopsy and tumor mapping.
- Clinical evaluations already underway (CEO quote).

## Funding
- $31.5M Series A, closed (announced Aug 20, 2026). Led by Atlantic Blue Ventures, S3 Ventures and Olympus Innovation Ventures, with participation from existing investor Hillside Capital. Use of funds: grow the team and advance the clinical program. (release)
