## Investment criteria (https://thirty31partners.com/investment-criteria)
- Involvement: serve as board-level advisors rather than day-to-day managers or operators
- Structure: meaningful equity ownership (control or non-control) combined with subordinated debt with current yield
- Characteristics: experienced management team, strong cash flow, established industry, growing market share
- Company size: EBITDA of at least $1 million
- Investment size: at least $3 million
- Industries: industry agnostic with a strategic focus on manufacturing and business services, open to other sectors
- Transaction types: management buyouts, recapitalizations, acquisition financings, shareholder liquidity events, growth financings, ESOPs
