## Science (from the RePORTER abstracts for the two awards)
- Objective: "simple, safe, potent, and cost-effective allogeneic (allo) off-the-shelf (OTS) T cell products for cancer."
- Problem: autologous CAR T has a notorious accessibility issue despite groundbreaking efficacy in certain B cell malignancies. Allo OTS T cell therapy faces two hurdles: graft versus host disease (GvHD), causing toxicity, and host versus graft (HvG) reaction, or allorejection, weakening efficacy.
- The widely adopted OTS approach uses gene editing to disrupt genes related to GvHD and allorejection; that adds manufacturing and quality-control complexity, drives cost significantly higher, decreases product yield and raises safety and efficacy concerns.
- Immunebro's donor engager T cell platform: engineered donor T cells that express and secrete an artificial modular protein that (1) targets the donor T cells to a desired cancer target and (2) curbs the donor T cells' alloreactivity of inducing GvHD. No gene editing and no CAR engineering required; manufacturing is well established and much less complex than for gene-edited OTS CAR T.
- Phase I results stated in the Phase II abstract: donor-derived CD19-targeting engager T cells had low xenogeneic GvHD risk and were efficacious in a xenograft CD19+ leukaemia mouse model; in a side-by-side comparison the first-generation engager T cells were inferior to 2nd-gen CD19 CAR T on efficacy due to lack of co-stimulation. Phase I therefore built enhanced versions by (1) co-expressing a CD19 CAR, (2) co-expressing IL15, (3) co-expressing 4-1BBL and CD80.
- IBT1116: a CD19-targeting OTS T cell product for refractory and relapsed (R/R) B cell leukaemia and lymphoma, which "threaten over 20,000 patients annually in the US". Built on Immunebro's 2nd-generation efficacy-enhanced donor engager T cell platform. In preclinical testing IBT1116 "cures leukemia-bearing mice without significant toxicity".
- Phase II aims: Aim 1 — develop a cGMP-ready manufacturing process and analytical testing to assure safety, identity, quality, purity and strength of IBT1116. Aim 2 — IND-enabling in vivo studies: dose range finding; toxicity and pharmacology in a xenograft leukaemia model; efficacy and durability in a humanised leukaemia model. Goal: submit an IND application for a first-in-human clinical trial.
- Long term: more OTS T cell products on the donor engager T cell platform, for cancers beyond B cell malignancies.
