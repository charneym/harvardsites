## Facts used
- Our Approach: metabolic surgery resolves T2D within days in a majority of patients; delivering a nutrient to the lower gut mimics it; BKR-017 butyrate tablet (sustained-release), BKR-021 (rapid-release), BKR-013 glutamine capsule (rapid-release); L-cells, GLP-1, PYY, GLP-2, oxyntomodulin.
- Technology Overview: oral; 505(b)(2) pathway; some newer drugs injectable, inconvenient and expensive.
- Formulation: Phloral™ coating in-licensed from University College London, dual mechanism (colonic amylase, pH > 7.4); no release in simulated gastric/intestinal fluid; butyrate released over 10 h from the tablet core in simulated colonic fluid.
- Results to Date: 1 g L-glutamine infused into the sigmoid colon significantly increased GLP-1 and insulin over placebo.
- News: 2025-11-15 $436K NHLBI Phase I SBIR (PK study at Pennington with Dr. Frank Greenway; BKR-019 next under Phase II); 2023-01-26 12-week CL-501 study complete (Duke Kannapolis, Duke Pickett Road, Pennington); 2022-04-30 4-week study, 14 T2D subjects, LDL 95.9→77.8 mg/dL (p=0.016) (−19% computed from their figures); 2019-10-01 $3.0M NIDDK Phase IIb SBIR; JDRF $500K SRA with Mayo Clinic (Adrian Vella PI, Szewczyk co-PI).
- ClinicalTrials.gov: NCT06556745 (BioKier, NHLBI; Phase 1 safety/PK of BKR-017 in individuals on statin therapy; completed 2026-06-04; 9 enrolled); NCT03999853 (Mayo; butyrate adjuvant therapy for T1D; completed 2025-12-01); NCT04279444 (4-week T2D study, 2020).
