## Firm

- Founded: 2020 by Joon Chang, Vijay Mehta and Pavel Chernyshov ("together with his two partners, founded Arkview Capital in 2020" — each co-founder bio, [TEAM]).
- HQ: Arkview Capital - Connecticut, One Stamford Plaza, 263 Tresser Boulevard, 9th Floor, Stamford, CT 06901 ([CONTACT]; site footer).
