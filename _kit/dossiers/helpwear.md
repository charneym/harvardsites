## Facts used (helpwear.ca unless noted)
- HeartWatch: first medical-grade ambulatory ECG monitor specifically designed for extended wear; novel dry-electrode (non-adhesive) design; comfort/accuracy/access bullets (non-invasive, non-adhesive, worn on bicep, can be worn in the shower; metal electrodes for medical-grade ECG; increased detection/diagnoses; without an implant; best economic value for patients and payors; HIPAA compliant); "can be worn for as many months as prescribed"; "covers patients from Day 0 through diagnosis"; "Monitoring for up to 180 days is critical".
- Comparison: Holter/patch/MCT 15–30 days, adhesive irritation, cannot rule out transient arrhythmias; implantable loop recorders 4–6 year commitment, invasive, costly.
- Detect / Treat (AF in cryptogenic stroke → oral anticoagulants; bradycardia/pause in syncope → pacemaker) / Protect.
- Stats (Conditions page, with their cited sources): 1 in 4 cryptogenic stroke patients have a recurrent stroke within 5 years; 1 in 2 syncope patients leave the hospital without a diagnosis; ~35% of strokes are cryptogenic.
- Caution statement: "Investigational Device, Limited by United States Law to Investigational Use Only. Not available for sale in the United States."
