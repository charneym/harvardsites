## Company (sources: crannmed.com pages and posts via the WP API, CORDIS, Enterprise Ireland, ThinkBusiness, ClinicalTrials.gov)
- Founded 2018; Galway. 6,000 sq ft facility in Galway Technology Park, Parkmore (moved in Dec 2021 from the GMIT Innovation Hub). It has a materials lab and a Class 8 cleanroom, where product is manufactured, lyophilized and packaged.
- Address on their contact page: Block 11, Galway Technology Park, Parkmore, Galway, Ireland, H91 VE0H. CORDIS lists the 2026 coordinator address as "Truskey Barna, H91XW6Y Galway", possibly the registered office.
- Email: Info@crannmed.com (contact page); sure@crannmed.com (SURE study); careers@crannmed.com. LinkedIn: https://ie.linkedin.com/company/crannmed
- SakuraBead: resorbable embolic microspheres made from organic biocompatible materials. An embedded enzyme degrades the base material, and resorption can be tuned from a few hours to six months. Patent US 12083242 B2. Name chosen for the transience of cherry blossom (musculoskeletal embolization was pioneered in Japan).
- Mechanism (The Science page): OA inflammation, then VEGF-driven angiogenesis, then leaky neovessels and neoinnervation. GAE with SakuraBead temporarily occludes the neovessels, so the vessels and nerves regress and the cycle is interrupted.
- FDA Breakthrough Device Designations: knee OA (May 2021), adhesive capsulitis (Nov 2023).
- First-in-human treatment Dec 2023 (Dr Sherzod Iskhakov; proctored by Prof Mark Little); 15-patient pilot study presented at CIRSE in Sept 2024.
- SURE study, NCT06550024: FDA-approved IDE study, 89 patients randomised 2:1 between GAE and steroid injection; first 4 patients treated Dec 2024; Global PI Dr Ari Isaacson.
- Lumbar pilot presented at SIR in April 2025 by Asst Prof Jessie Stewart (UCLA).
- Funding: DTIF €2.5m consortium in 2021 (ICS Medical, Creganna Medical, CREST TU Dublin); EIC Accelerator €3.5m in 2021 (EmboSure; CORDIS 190167448, EU contribution €2,488,572.80, May 2022 to Nov 2024); DTIF 2023 (Institute of Clinical Trials, University of Galway, and Synecco); EIC Accelerator up to €12.5m in 2026 (a €2.5m grant plus up to €10m equity). The 2026 award funds expansion to hand OA and plantar fasciitis, then commercialisation in Europe and the US; first commercial sales are expected as soon as the end of 2026.
- People: Liam Farrissey (CEO), Joan McCabe (COO), Helen Ryan (Chair), Andy Lewis PhD (Technical Advisor/Board Observer), plus staff and 8 clinical advisors as listed on the Our Team and Advisory Board pages.
