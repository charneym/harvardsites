## Science (1R44AG102216 abstract)
- NNMT upregulated in aging skeletal muscle; disrupts NAD+ biosynthesis, methionine cycle, muscle stem cell (muSC) regenerative function and muscle metabolism.
- Inhibiting NNMT rejuvenates muSCs; rapid repair and growth of injured muscle.
- Aged mice, once-daily RT-002 (first-in-class NNMT inhibitor): muscle peak torque +80%, post-injury muscle fiber size doubled. In translational models: prevented strength loss, improved muscle quality, reduced intramuscular fat.
- Completed pivotal GLP safety/toxicity studies; nearly complete IND package. Phase II aims: cardiovascular safety (hERG in vitro; radiotelemetry in minipig); cGMP immediate-release oral capsules; then IND and Phase I.
- Muscle loss begins by mid-30s, accelerates after 60. U44 abstract: ~300,000 older Americans hospitalized each year with hip fracture repairs; 1-year mortality ~10–30%.
- Publication: Dimet-Wiley AL et al., "Nicotinamide N-methyltransferase inhibition mimics and boosts exercise-mediated improvements in muscle function in aged mice", Sci Rep 2024. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38969654/
