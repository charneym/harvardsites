01Approach

Capital with a long horizon.

Three target sectors, one way of working: align interests with founders and management at the outset, then help execute the company's growth plan.

Sector 1 of 3 · Niche manufacturing

Niche makers with defensible positions.

National Metalwares, a maker of fabricated and finished tubular steel products, was held from 1995 to 2019. Precision Products Group, first acquired in 1992, was re-acquired in 2020, and minority stakes in Washington Metal Fabricators and EZ Fabricating followed in 2024.

Sector 2 of 3 · Business services

Services with recurring cash flow.

In June 2025 OM19 acquired Utility Revenue Services, an independent third-party auditor of utility billing vendors for multi-family real estate owners. Revco Solutions, a healthcare revenue cycle management company, was held from 2019 to 2024.

Sector 3 of 3 · Food & ingredients

Artisan quality, clean label.

Joseph's Gourmet Pasta, a maker of premium frozen pasta for foodservice and retail, joined in 2019. Sokol & Company, a wet-fill manufacturer of B2B ingredients and retail food products, was acquired in 2022 and exited in 2025.
