## Facts used
- Home/Overview: clinical-stage biotech developing ADCs that target immune cells; among the first to leverage ADC success from oncology for autoimmune/inflammatory disorders; investors ARCH Venture Partners, 5AM Ventures, Atlas Venture, Sanofi Ventures; pipeline payloads may include ASOs, siRNAs, small molecule inhibitors; experienced drug developers, strong partnering track record.
- Technology > LFD-200: GCs effective but off-target toxicity at high dose / long periods; VISTA selectively and highly expressed on immune cells incl. myeloid, T cells and plasma B cells; rapid internalization and lysosomal accumulation; short serum half-life (hours vs days for many ADCs); convenient subcutaneous formulation.
- Pipeline page: VISTA platform for rheumatology, gastroenterology, pulmonology, dermatology; diverse payloads incl. ASOs and siRNAs; long immune tissue exposure in vitro and in vivo; lead ASO ADC efficacy in multiple Th2-driven disease models, lead optimization; partnering.
- Clinical Trials page: Phase 1b MAD in moderate-to-severe RA, 12-week, prednisone / SC LFD-200 / placebo; stable methotrexate; sites Georgia, Moldova, Poland, Ukraine; NCT07207954.
- PR 2026-11-09 (ACR Convergence 2026): 8 GC payload molecules per mAb; antibody has no other biological function; SAD/MAD well tolerated; no effect on cortisol up to 12 mg/kg; serum half-life ~30 h; free payload exposure suggests ≥2 weeks in tissue; exposures support biweekly or monthly dosing; RA data expected by end of 2026.
- Leadership bios (condensed): Tzianabos, Bhasin, McClure, Nichols, McNeely, Rothstein, Williams.
