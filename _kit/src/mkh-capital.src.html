01Approach

Flexible, straightforward, supportive.

We find the best structure for each investment, with no "one size fits all" approach, then build alongside management. One platform shows how.

Step 1 of 3 · The platform

Start with owners, and a platform.

Clean Streak Ventures was established in November 2019 by partnering with car wash owners. MKH launched it as its express car wash platform, headquartered in Orlando, and hired Tom Welter as chief executive.

Step 2 of 3 · Buy and build

Add-ons, partners and new sites.

Top Dog Express and Blue Water in Orlando, a partnership with Clean Machine in Naples, Bay Breeze's seven sites in Tampa Bay and Sponge Spa in Fort Myers, alongside new washes the company built itself.

Step 3 of 3 · A Florida leader

Three sites became thirty-three.

Clean Machine opened from Tampa to Port St. Lucie and Cutler Bay, and Clean Streak built a leadership position in Florida. In December 2021 MKH sold it to Mister Car Wash for $390 million.
