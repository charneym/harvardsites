## NIH funding (NIH RePORTER API). First-year award amounts as reported by RePORTER.
- **2R44CA257783-02A1**, NCI, award notice 2026-09-17, $660,779 — "Development of dermal targeting formulations for squamous cell carcinoma". PI: Hugh David Smyth. Abstract: "Via Therapeutics has developed a novel topical therapy that addresses the primary obstacle limiting current topical agents: inadequate drug penetration into the epidermis and dermis. Through a proprietary high-throughput screening approach, we evaluated hundreds of formulations in ex vivo human skin and identified a synergistic combination of two compounds—delivered effectively into the dermis without significant systemic exposure. This lead formulation (VT103) demonstrated significant anticancer effects in a UV-induced photocarcinogenesis model (SKH-1 mice) during Phase I studies." Phase II aims: optimise and scale up manufacturing; in vivo efficacy studies; "Completing IND-enabling toxicology in line with FDA guidelines, positioning VT103 for a Phase I clinical trial." Abstract also states: "An estimated one in five Americans will develop skin cancer in their lifetime" and "Beyond immunotherapy for advanced or metastatic cSCC, there are currently no approved or recommended medical therapies for this disease."

## Brand mapping (PRINCIPLES BK), from the logo's pixel-sampled colours
Logo sampled with `_kit/photo-inventory.js` (https://static.wixstatic.com/media/41f917_1dec606712024cc4835d7b419b7ed33b~mv2.png): `#009090` 17%, `#004848` 15%, `#d8d8d8` 13%, `#187878` 6%, `#003030` 5%, `#189090` 4%.
- logo `#009090` → `--sin` (the accent)
- a deeper shade of it, `#0a6e6e` → `--sinD` (accent on the light base)
- a tint of it, `#4fc6c6` → `--sinL` (accent on dark sections)
- logo `#003030`/`#004848` deepened → `--ink #06272a`, `--ink2 #0a3034`, `--ink3 #10444a`, `--deep #041c1f`
- logo `#d8d8d8` warmed to paper → `--bone #f4f4f0`, `--bone2 #e8eae5`, `--bone3 #d5dad5` (the site's base, matching their white site)
- neutral `--liv #8a8278` / `--livL #b9b2a8` for the "held back at the surface" and systemic-circulation marks (a neutral, since their logo carries no second hue)
The served logo is a 145×105 raster of the mark on an opaque white box, which cannot be dropped out on either of this site’s backgrounds (the header is its own stacking context, so `mix-blend-mode:multiply` has nothing to blend into). The mark is therefore re-drawn as inline SVG/CSS from the full-resolution asset — the same "VIA" wordmark, the same eleven rounded brick bars beneath it and the same "Therapeutics" strapline, in the logo’s own `#009090` — and that re-draw is what the header carries on both light and dark sections.
