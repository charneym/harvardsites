## Hero
Option (c): a crafted render of their mechanism (render.py). It is a space-filling model from real atomic coordinates: a capture bead carrying intact IgG antibodies (PDB 1IGT) tethered by their Fc stems, some holding a native protein antigen (Ro60 autoantigen, PDB 1YVP chain A) at their tips. The model is shaded with ambient occlusion and has real alpha. It is unique: other concepts use 1IGT alone as a single antibody, but no other concept uses this bead-capture composition.

## Story
A fine-line IP-to-MS scene in three steps. Antibodies dock on a bead. Only gold antigens from the lysate bind at the antibody tips while the grey proteins stay put. The antigens are then released and a mass spectrum draws, with the antigen peaks in gold.
