## Science (verbatim-sourced facts)
From the 1R44DK148200 abstract (NIH RePORTER):
- "The core of this non-viral therapy is a safe hydrodynamic injection of plasmid DNA expression vectors directly
  into the liver through the biliary system."
- "Hydrodynamic injection mediates local high-pressure, creating pores in the hepatocyte membrane and allowing
  DNA delivery inside cells without the need for a viral vehicle."
- "By targeting the biliary system via a routine and safe clinical procedure, HydroGene can efficiently deliver
  naked plasmid DNA in 20x smaller volume compared to the vascular route."
- "naked DNA is 100x cheaper to manufacture than AAV strategies, lacks immunogenicity, has no DNA size
  limitations, and can be easily redosed."
- Wilson's disease: mutations in ATP7B cause early liver damage from copper accumulation; HydroGene proposes to
  deliver the **full-length** human ATP7B gene in a naked DNA plasmid, repeatably.
From the 1R44HL187020 abstract (NIH RePORTER):
- Hemophilia B affects one in 30,000 male live births; >3000 pathogenic F9 mutations; severe form <1% of normal
  factor activity. Standard of care is weekly prophylaxis of FIX concentrate.
- AAV gene therapy cannot be redosed, excludes pediatric patients with growing livers, and is limited by
  pre-existing anti-AAV antibodies.
From the 1R42DK141297 abstract: the endoscopic procedure "can be performed in under 30 min and has minimal
complications"; corrected hepatocytes repopulate an injured liver only if the gene is integrated into the genome.

## Peer-reviewed publications (PubMed)
- Kruse RL, Huang Y, Shum T, Bai L, Ding H, Wang ZZ, Selaru FM, Kumbhari V. "Endoscopic-mediated, biliary
  hydrodynamic injection mediating clinically relevant levels of gene delivery in pig liver."
  *Gastrointest Endosc* 2021 Dec;94(6):1119-1130.e4. PMID 34197834.
  Verbatim results: "Biliary hydrodynamic injection was well tolerated without significant changes in vital signs,
  liver enzymes, hematology, or histology. No off-target pDNA delivery to other organs was detected…
  50.19% of the liver stained positive for hFIX after hydrodynamic injection at 5.5 mg pDNA, with every hepatic
  lobule in all liver lobes demonstrating hFIX expression… significantly higher transfection efficiency than mouse
  vascular hydrodynamic injection at matched pDNA per liver weight dose (32.7%-51.9% vs 18.9%, P < .0001)."
- Huang Y et al. "Parameters of biliary hydrodynamic injection during endoscopic retrograde cholangio-pancreatography
  in pigs for applications in gene delivery." *PLoS One* 2021. PMID 33909609.
- "Successful liver-directed gene delivery by ERCP-guided hydrodynamic injection (with videos)."
  *Gastrointest Endosc* 2018 Oct. PMID 29018834.
