How we partner

01 / Alongside

Partners, not owners.

We provide management-friendly capital, typically holding common equity alongside proven managers who keep operating control and significant ownership. We are willing to be minority investors.

02 / Resources

The whole firm behind you.

When we invest, we commit our capital as well as the resources and broad experience of our entire organization, with value-add initiatives and limited financial leverage.

03 / Long term

Patient by design.

A long-term perspective and a preference for longer hold periods. Relationships built on trust, respect and a shared vision for success, since 1986.
