## The company
Swedish medtech: Multi4, a single-use instrument with 4 built-in functions (local anaesthesia, resection of tissue samples,
automatic transport of samples through the instrument, destroying cancer tissue / stopping bleeding), used through the working
channel of an endoscope to treat urinary bladder cancer in outpatient care, at the same visit as the control cystoscopy.
FDA 510(k) clearance (press release 2025-06-30); MDR technical file submitted 2025; U.S. soft launch 2026 (multi4.se timeline).
EIC Accelerator €2.5M (CORDIS 101188461, 2024-09-01 to 2026-08-31). Chairman: Erik Selin (press release).
