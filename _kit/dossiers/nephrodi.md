## Facts used
- Tagline (home): "Lack of concentration is our focus." Used as the H1.
- Disease facts: up to 20 L of urine a day; children must drink up to 20 L of water a day to avoid severe dehydration (home page). "Almost 1 L/h" and "there is no effective therapy for X-linked NDI" (NIH RePORTER abstract).
- Mechanism: V2R mutations "prevent activation of the cyclic adenosine monophosphate (cAMP)/protein kinase A (PKA) signaling pathway" (RePORTER abstract). AMPK can phosphorylate aquaporin-2 independently of PKA (Klein, Khanna, Pillarisetti, Hagan, LaRocque, Rodriguez, Sands, JCI Insight 2021, https://insight.jci.org/articles/view/146419). NDI-5001 "does not affect blood glucose levels in pre-clinical studies" (June 2024 release).
- Pipeline: Phase 1b open-label trial of NDI-5001 capsules in adult males with X-linked NDI, sponsored by Otsuka Pharmaceutical Development & Commercialization, start 2026-07-07 (actual), recruiting (ClinicalTrials.gov NCT07525960). SBIR Phase II aims: 39-week GLP juvenile dog toxicology, male rat fertility study, pediatric liquid formulation, to support the pivotal pediatric study (RePORTER).
- Designations: Orphan Drug Designation; Rare Pediatric Disease designation (Priority Review Voucher eligible) (June 2024 release).
- Partners and backers: MSRD (Otsuka family) agreement, June 2023 (Business Wire); Sound Bioventures Series A, June 2024 (BioSpace); NIH NIDDK SBIR; Georgia Research Alliance and Wallace H. Coulter Foundation early grants (about page). Shown as a type-only credit line.
- Team (team page, no portraits published, so the masthead list): Rachael Hagan; Jeff M. Sands; Janet D. Klein; Josh Knackert; additional founders Ish Khanna and Ram Pillarisetti. Mike Rose (VP Operations, 2023 release) is not on the current team page, so he is left out.
- News (all link out): ClinicalTrials.gov listing (July 2026 start); NIH RePORTER award (2025-08-28); Sands Berliner Award release (2025-04-25, via Santé log mirror of the GlobeNewswire release); Sound Bioventures (2024-06-20, BioSpace); MSRD agreement (2023-06-22, Business Wire).
