## Facts used
- ISO 13485-certified medical device company focused on cyclical aspiration for mechanical thrombectomy; CLEAR is a patented digital aspiration platform for removing thrombus from the peripheral vasculature; 510(k) and CE Mark (2026 release).
- Components: CLEAR Aspiration Pump (reusable); INSERA Catheter, INSERA Aspiration Tubing, INSERA Canister with Non-sterile Tubing (single use). Modes: cyclical, uniform, blood saver (blood saver is a form of cyclical vacuum aspiration) (2026 release; EVT).
- Indication: removal of fresh, soft emboli and thrombi from vessels of the peripheral arterial and venous systems (FDA). Testing listed in the 2026 release.
- 108 patent filings incl. 100 issued or allowed; priority to 2013; US 12,478,390; EP 3,620,203 (2026 release).
- Varies vacuum pressures and intervals within a safe range up to −98.2 kPa (−29 inHg); goal of increasing first-pass recanalization; optional iPad app (2019 releases). Arslanian et al., JNIS 2019 (as cited in the April 2019 release).
- CE Mark March 2019; first two stroke patients, Zagreb, October 2019 (first-pass eTICI 3 within six minutes); first PE patient, Zagreb, December 2019; NSF SBIR $221,700, October 2018.
- Mission (their site): "Our mission is to be the most competitive and innovative provider of cures for neurovascular diseases using minimally invasive therapeutics." Tagline "Neurovascular Innovations. Life Inspired."
- Left out: the CLEAR acronym (the 2019 releases spell it two different ways) and the SHELTER retriever (2018 only).
