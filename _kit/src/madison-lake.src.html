01Approach

Three ways to partner.

We operate in an independent sponsor model, which lets us be very flexible in our partnerships. Most of what we do takes one of three shapes.

01 · Buyouts

The whole company, from an owner ready to step back.

We buy businesses from owners looking for a transition. After the close, our team works with management to put the right strategies in place, build a platform and work toward accelerated growth.

02 · Divestitures

One unit, set up to stand on its own.

We help companies divest a subsidiary or a business unit, and set it up as a separate, stand-alone business with its own team and its own plan.

03 · Recapitalizations

A majority stake, or a partial one.

We can help a company recapitalize by taking a majority or a partial stake, shaped around what the owners want next.

  • Platforms

    Platforms, then bolt-ons

    Gilman Precision began our machine components platform and Milwaukee Cylinder our fluid power platform. In 2026, Perfection Industrial Finishing joined our industrial coatings platform.

  • Management

    Leaders stay in the lead

    Douglas Lacina stayed on as CEO of Milwaukee Cylinder, and Gilman's COO, Mike Weiland, took the lead there. Our partners sit on the boards beside them.

  • Transition

    A seamless handover

    Our acquisitions honour each seller's wish for a seamless transition: one that keeps the company's culture and safeguards its people.
