## 2-HOBA science (NIH abstracts)
- NHLBI STTR R42HL158393 "2-HOBA for Treatment of Pulmonary Hypertension" (PIs Rathmacher, James D. West): ROS → reactive
  lipids → adduct and inactivate mitochondrial SIRT3 → metabolic defects → more ROS (vicious cycle); 2-HOBA soaks up reactive
  lipids. Phase II aims: 2-week open-label mechanistic pilot in PH patients; RV under stress in sheep; mouse and large animals
  alone and with standard of care. Awards: FY2021 $256,580 (10257863); FY2023 $1,173,060 (10698621); FY2024 $957,913
  (10916207); FY2025 $957,913, project end 2026-08-31 (https://reporter.nih.gov/project-details/11180471). Total $3,345,466 → "$3.3M".
- NIA SBIR R44AG055184 (9970647): 2-HOBA is a natural product in buckwheat seeds, developed for human use by MTI; reacts
  1,600-fold faster with bifunctional electrophiles than lysine; SAD/MAD human PK and safety, brain penetrance, CSF markers.
- NCI STTR R41CA257262 (10257862), with Keith Wilson, VUMC: dicarbonyl electrophiles (isoLGs, MDA, 4-ONE, acrolein) adduct
  DNA/histones/proteins; 2-HOBA "highly bioavailable, with no toxicity, in rodents and in recent human Phase I clinical
  trials"; aims in gastric and colitis-associated cancer models. The page shows only the colorectal framing (no pathogen content).
- West et al., J Transl Med 2026-08-12, doi 10.1186/s12967-026-08716-8 (Rathmacher, MTI Biotech): three mouse PH models
  (AKR-HFD, LNAME-HFD, PAB); ~10% lower RVSP; reduced diastolic dysfunction; RV fatty acid oxidation normalised; lower plasma
  lipids/ceramides; restored mitochondrial function.
- Other papers used in News: Nutrients 2025 (MASH, doi 10.3390/nu17040610); ISSN HMB position stand (Rathmacher first
  author, doi 10.1080/15502783.2024.2434734); Inflammation 2023 (2-HOBA in healthy humans, doi 10.1007/s10753-023-01801-w).
- Not used: the 2025 first-in-human AF pilot (Circ Arrhythm Electrophysiol) reported higher AF recurrence; omitted rather than spun.

## Story

2-HOBA catches the lipids and lifts them away, SIRT3 relights (step 3). Source: R42HL158393 abstract; J Transl Med 2026.
