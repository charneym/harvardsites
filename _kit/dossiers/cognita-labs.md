## Science (2R44HL152912-04 abstract)
- Muco-obstructive lung diseases (subsets of non-CF bronchiectasis and cystic fibrosis): impaired mucociliary clearance, persistent *P. aeruginosa* infection.
- Airway clearance therapy (ACT) is essential, but current devices use non-adaptive, high-pressure vibration: fixed patterns (vests, OPEP) or subjective tuning (Frequencer, VibraLung).
- Effectiveness depends on matching input oscillations to the resonant frequency of obstructed airways; no current device optimizes this dynamically. No current device measures lung function to assess obstruction or progress.
- SonoHeal "will be the first closed-loop ACT device", adapting in real time to measured airway resistance; uses Cognita's patented airway monitoring technology to quantify lung response, localize mucus obstruction and dynamically adjust therapy frequencies. Proposal leverages airway modeling, ML-driven therapy optimization and transfer learning. Aims: adherence, comfort, rapid individualized therapy.
- Narrative: "optimize oscillatory pressure waves closer to resonant frequencies, reducing therapy time, intensity, and improving adherence."

## Products (cognitalabs.com home; pulmoscan.cognitalabs.com; capmedicinhaler.com)
- **PulmoScan**: smart portable airway assessment device (oscillometry / FOT), coaching tablet app, cloud support; "Class II FDA cleared"; zero-effort quick test. PulmoScan site: ">260,000 tests conducted", ">20 countries where deployed", "FDA cleared", "ISO 13485 Facility"; measures resistance and reactance during quiet breathing using oscillating pressure waves (5–30 Hz) (https://pulmoscan.cognitalabs.com/how-to-interpret-pulmoscan-oscillometry-data/); test under a minute, children as young as 4 (https://pulmoscan.cognitalabs.com/2024/05/10/).
- **CapMedic**: attachment for MDI inhalers; real-time audio-visual guidance; measures correctness, time and frequency of use; home spirometer (FEV1, PEF); "Class II FDA cleared". capmedicinhaler.com: "Study shows 3X better inhaler use with CapMedic" (Paronyan et al., ATS 2020); fits most MDI and DPI inhalers; assessment under 6 seconds.
- **UltraSpiro**: intelligent coaching spirometer; "highest flow sensitivity in the market with <1% error-rates"; mobile app + cloud.
- **Remote monitoring**: share medication-use and lung-health data with physicians; "Reimbursable under the new CMS telehealth policy."
