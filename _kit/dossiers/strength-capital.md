# Strength Capital Partners — dossier (facts with sources only)

- **Firm:** Strength Capital Partners, LLC — "a Birmingham, Michigan-based private equity firm that invests primarily in businesses in the Central United States", focused on "Industrial Consolidation" (site meta description, https://strengthcapital.com/). Founded in 2000 by Mark McCammon and Mike Bergeron (https://strengthcapital.com/mark-mccammon-bio/). Offices in Birmingham MI, Cincinnati OH and Denver CO (Grand Equipment release).
- **HQ:** 102 Pierce Street, Birmingham, MI 48009. Cincinnati: 2035 Madison Road, Cincinnati, OH 45208. Denver: no street address published. Source: https://strengthcapital.com/contact. Time zone: US Eastern (HQ).
- **Lead:** Mark McCammon, Co-Founder & Managing Partner (Denver; B.S. Finance, Miami University; M.B.A. Harvard Business School; formerly Goldman Sachs, T. Rowe Price). mark@strengthcapital.com, (248) 593-6872. Co-founder Mike Bergeron, Co-Founder & Managing Partner (Cincinnati), mike@strengthcapital.com. Source: https://strengthcapital.com/team and bio pages.
- **Criteria:** U.S.-based businesses with $4–25 million EBITDA; manufacturing, distribution, infrastructure and industrial services; no technology, software, healthcare or other "speculative" spaces; no minority investments; seeking new platforms and add-ons. Sources: homepage and https://strengthcapital.com/approach
- **Approach:** Industrial Consolidation Strategy, Building Partnerships, Operational Excellence ("The trains must run on time"), Industry Focus & Quantitative Criteria (approach page). Core values: Integrity, Accountability, Dialogue, Entrepreneurial, Passion, Courage, Resilience, Personable (https://strengthcapital.com/our-values).
- **Portfolio:** 10 current and 20 exited investments with descriptions and links as listed on https://strengthcapital.com/portfolio
- **Latest activity:** Truesdell Corporation acquired Beck-Reit and Sons (Tempe, AZ, March 31, 2026; https://strengthcapital.com/news/2026/4/21/truesdell-corporation-announces-the-acquisition-of-beck-reit-and-sons-ltd); investment in Grand Equipment Company (October 29, 2025); ESP Associates continuation vehicle (2025); Kelchner acquisition (May 2, 2025). Source: https://strengthcapital.com/news
