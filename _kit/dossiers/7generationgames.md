## Facts used
- Tagline "Games That Make You Smarter"; "no one learns from software they don't use"; start with what players should learn, then ten years of data on what keeps students playing.
- Stats (home): 30% student improvement in math scores; 3x improvement over control group; 51 games made; 200K+ users.
- Research page: average class gains 30% on tests of the standards taught, playing ~30 minutes, 2–3 times a week, for 10 weeks; three cycles of efficacy testing; Transmotion 4(2) 113–131 (Dakota culture/perseverance; two reservation schools, grades 4–5); USDA final report (Dakota Math Project, Spirit Lake pilot); SAS Global Forum 2015 paper.
- Games: Spirit Lake, Fish Lake, Forgotten Trail (Dakota Learning Project page); Making Camp Lakota, AzTech: Meet the Maya (Products); ages 8–14 (About).
- Custom development: $5,000–$50,000, typical $10,000–$30,000; teach, test and track.
- 7 Gen Blocks: learn to make a game in one hour; $250,000 cost of a good educational game; under-served communities, overlooked markets.
- Team (Our team page): AnnMaria De Mars PhD, President and Founder; Maria Burns Ortiz, CEO and Co-Founder; Dennis De Mars, CTO and Co-Founder; Diana Sanchez, Project Manager; Christy Hanson, Community Manager. Media: Maria Burns Ortiz in The Guardian "New Business School Series".
