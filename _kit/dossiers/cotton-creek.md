- Values: founded on the relationships of Brownlie & Braden, LLC, a trusted financial advisor to high net worth families across the Southwest for over twenty years; control investors; network across the South Central United States (https://www.cottoncreekcapital.com/values).

## Criteria (https://www.cottoncreekcapital.com/criteria)
- Solution capital situations: first round institutional capital, corporate divestitures, management-backed strategies; transitional events (generational transfers, asset diversification, capital constraints, need for management support or oversight, non-core divestitures, new financial partner for growth).
- US-wide, specific emphasis on the Southwest.
- EBITDA $5M–$15M with stable performance and attractive cash flow; niche market position; growth constraints; revenue enhancement + process improvement; proven or supplementable management; enhance the whole.
- Sectors: Manufacturing, Infrastructure, Specialty Chemicals, Food and Beverage, Transportation and Logistics, Business Services, Value-Added Distribution, Building Products, Industrial Services.
