## The company
Privately held, clinical-stage US oncology company (Neovia Oncology LLC, Delaware), founded 2012 by Trevor G. Blake. HQ: Newport Beach, California
(dateline of the 2 Sept 2026 release; the 6 June 2026 release is datelined Wilmington, Del.). Lead NEV-801: a patented drug-drug conjugate (DDC)
combining novel analogs related to irinotecan (Top1) and etoposide (Top2) in a plasma-stable architecture for controlled release in tumor
microenvironments. Independent TopoGEN studies: catalytic inhibition of human Top1 and Top2α, no confirmed interfacial-poison behavior.
Phone +1 866 636 8423. Sources: neoviaoncology.net /, /about, /press-release; releases below.

## Facts used on the page
- Phase I(a) (NCT02797795), release 2026-06-06 (BioPharma Dive): 33 patients, 20–600 mg/m², no treatment-related SAEs, no Grade 3/4 TRAEs, no neutropenia, ocular toxicity or ILD; 62% SD, 56% tumor inhibition, 22% PR (>30% reduction) in higher-dose cohorts. Site: no GI disturbance associated with the parent compounds; efficacy in patients who failed the parent compound and mAbs; results pending publication.
- Lab synergy with checkpoint inhibitors and mAbs (same release). ADC payload compounds; new patent filed (release 2026-09-02, PDF on neoviaoncology.net).
- Creatv Bio partnership (PR Newswire, 2026-06-02): LifeTracDx liquid biopsy in Neovia's upcoming MDR solid tumor studies.
- Team from /about (summaries condensed): Blake, Yang, Marulappa, Gerald Su, Raymond Su.
