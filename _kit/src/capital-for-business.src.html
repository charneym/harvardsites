01Approach

Midwest values, national reach.

CFB backs companies with strong management teams, niche market leadership and room to grow through product development, business development, capacity expansion and acquisitions. One platform shows how.

Step 1 of 4 · Partner · 2020

A cornerstone, 110 years in.

CFB invested in Entegra Attachments, alongside B12 Capital Partners, UMB Capital Corporation and CEO Michael Flannery, to acquire Bloom Manufacturing of Independence, Iowa: a maker of worm-drive and planetary winch systems, and the first cornerstone of Entegra's strategy.

Step 2 of 4 · Follow-on · 2021

Capital behind the plan.

A follow-on investment from CFB supported Entegra's acquisition of Hercules Machinery Corporation, a Fort Wayne, Indiana maker of hydraulic pile-driving and drilling attachments, and its sister machine shop, Titan Metal Worx.

Step 3 of 4 · Add-on · January 2026

Add-ons that extend the line.

Entegra acquired Leading Edge Attachments of Hubbardston, Massachusetts, a supplier of patented frost and rock ripping buckets, and integrated it into Entegra company B&D Fabricators.

Step 4 of 4 · Depth · July 2026

Depth where it counts.

TEI Rock Drills of Montrose, Colorado, a maker of drilling machinery for underground utilities, mining, tunneling and construction, joined Entegra's Pile Driving and Drilling Platform alongside Hercules.

For business owners

When you succeed, we succeed.

We partner with management teams and provide the resources required to succeed: board-level guidance, strategic insight, operating resources, capital and access to expertise across our portfolio of companies.

CFB only works with companies similar to yours, so our experience is built on guiding industrial growth companies through familiar opportunities and challenges.

For intermediaries

A fit for your clients.

We are a control investor focused on industrial growth companies across the United States, serving end markets from automotive and aerospace to medical and diversified industrials. We selectively consider minority investments.

With more than 120 deals completed over the firm's history, we pride ourselves on honest, responsive feedback and the highest certainty to close.
