About us

A trusted UK manufacturing partner since 1999

Printed Systems Limited is a UK-based specialist in PCB and LED assembly and contract electronic manufacturing. Since the company was founded in 1999, PSL has grown steadily to become a trusted partner for businesses that need precision, reliability and consistent quality.

Many of our staff have been with the company for years, building deep technical knowledge and genuine pride in their work. You deal with real people who know your project and stay involved from start to finish.

  • 50+people employed across our Hertfordshire facility
  • 140,000components per hour from our high-speed pick-and-place technology
  • 10,000sq ft dedicated LED facility, “LED World”, within our main premises

  2. 02 · Inspection

    AOI

    Automated optical inspection systems detect defects quickly and accurately during production, so issues are found early and quality stays consistent.

    Hand and unit assembly

  4. 04 · Test

    Test and dispatch

    Comprehensive testing that reflects real-world operating conditions, followed by final assembly, packing and dispatch.

LED Assembly

LED World: a dedicated 10,000 sq ft LED facility

PSL was among the first PCB assemblers to recognise the potential of the LED market and has since become a specialist in the field. Within LED World we manufacture boards up to 1.6 metres long, in materials from FR4 through to metal-core PCBs.

The service covers a wide variety of LED packages, plus testing, final assembly, packing and dispatch.

  • Placement

    High-precision LED placement with full BIN control for consistent output.

  • De-panel

    Clean, controlled separation of FR4 and metal-core boards.

  • Test

    Every LED board undergoes 100% light-up testing.

  • Final Assembly

    Wiring, cabling and mechanical assembly, packed for direct shipment.

  • Controlled Storage

    Moisture-controlled storage with full traceability.

Market Sectors

Who we work with

From safety-critical systems to high-volume production, our experience spans both public and private sectors.

  • Security / Safety

    Smoke detectors, fire alarms and intrinsically safe lighting for gasworks and oil refineries.

  • Medical

    Control boards for respiratory devices, laser equipment and circulatory systems.

  • Telecommunications

    Specialist text phones, helmet headsets and Bluetooth-enabled modules.

  • Broadcasting

    Colour mixing units for the film industry and complex sound-recording systems.

  • Computers / Peripherals

    KVM extenders, rack-mount units, gaming peripherals and battery back-ups.

  • Lighting

    Architectural, street and specialist LED lighting, indoors and out.

  • Retail

    Display lighting, 360° photography systems and card scanning devices.

  • Transport

    Vehicle lighting systems and electronic devices, including data recorders.

  • Aviation

    Cockpit lighting and energy management systems, including solar-powered drone technology.

  • Agriculture

    Specialist horticultural lighting and monitoring systems.

  • Construction

    Cable detection, measurement systems and building management controls.

  • Maritime
