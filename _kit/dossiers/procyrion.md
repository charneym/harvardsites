## Facts used (procyrion.com Home, Overview, Clinical Trials, Team, Contact; releases 17 Nov 2026, 2 Dec 2025, 9 Sep 2024, 4 Mar 2024, 5 Feb 2024, 30 Nov 2023, 7 Sep 2023, 21 Mar 2023)
- Aortix: pMCS device for heart failure patients "too sick for medication alone"; designed to reduce cardiac workload and increase renal perfusion; percutaneous deployment in < 10 min; "Resting the heart while perfusing the kidneys".
- Mechanism (Home, A-D): a portion of native flow enters the inlet; accelerated and exits in high-velocity jets downstream; jets entrain native flow, transferring momentum and energy; net increase in velocity and aortic flow. Overview: pumps blood from the aortic arch to the lower aorta; lowers aortic-root pressure (less resistance for the heart), raises lower-aorta pressure (renal perfusion, urine output, decongestion).
- Indications (Overview): ADHF with diuretic resistance; renal support to reduce AKI in cardiac surgery and coronary interventions (both in clinical evaluation); cardiac unloading to reduce infarct size; longer-term at-home therapy in chronic HF.
- Trials: DRAIN-HF pivotal IDE NCT05677100 enrolling (US and Eastern Europe), registry arm for end-stage HF; CRS Pilot NCT04145635 completed (THT 2023 late-breaker results); Perioperative Reduction of Kidney Injury NCT04999163 completed.
- Stats: over 6 million US adults with HF (Home); ~40% of ADHF patients have CRS (releases 2023); Breakthrough-designated therapy (17 Nov 2026); $57.7m Series E led by Fannin Partners (5 Feb 2024); new ICD-10-PCS codes (7 Sep 2023); Fierce 15 of 2023.
