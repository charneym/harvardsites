## Funding (verified)
- NIH NCI SBIR 1R44CA306595-01A1 (appl 11405218), notice 2026-08-24, first-year **$1,045,569**, PI Laura Taylor. "An Ultra-Precise Needle Delivery System for Cutaneous Cancer Treatment". Project 2026-09-01 → 2028-08-31. https://reporter.nih.gov/project-details/11405218
- Abstract facts: SoftTouch device; miniaturized voice coil actuator ±0.1 mm positional accuracy, depth 0.5–4 mm; rotary lead screw microvolume dosing ±5 µL, 10–200 µL/s; depth maintenance by vacuum stabilization (~10 kPa) / optical sensing; early prototypes: 85% reduction in injection pain vs standard needles, dose accuracy <5 µL, depth precision <0.5 mm; Phase II: handheld <1.5 lb, ex vivo porcine and human cadaveric testing (ultrasound, histology), toward FDA 510(k) under product code FMF. Skin cancer: >5M keratinocyte carcinomas and 100,000 melanomas per year in the US.
