How we partner
01 / Underwrite

A sharp view of the business.

Every partnership starts with rigorous financial underwriting: a sustainable, diversified business model, a strong management team and more than $1 million of EBITDA.

02 / Structure

Capital shaped to the deal.

A flexible approach to structuring. Unitranche, second lien, subordinated debt or equity, $3–25 million per company, for buyouts, recapitalizations and acquisition financing.

03 / Partner

Alongside sponsors and owners.

We back financial-sponsor-led and independently operated businesses, including independent sponsors, and bring an extensive industry network to the table.
