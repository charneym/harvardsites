## What they do (their words, home page)
- "Shining a New Light on an Old Problem"
- "a new line of wearable technology that provides the most accurate non-invasive, continuous monitoring of brain health, visual function, pain, opioid toxicity, and sleep stages."
- "a line of optical devices designed for both medical and consumer applications ... Whether in a clinical setting or at home"
- RSS description: "a series of optical monitoring devices sensitive to brain health, visual function, sleep, memory, and pain." (https://www.illuminationdiagnostics.com/blog-feed.xml)
- Applications (home page): Vision (patients waking up blind after surgery, "never" events); Sleep (four stages of sleep at home); Brain Health (ICU brain swelling and bleeding); Anesthesiology (narcotic toxicity in the OR).

- Podcast description (Apple Podcasts, Frameshifts): Schwartz "is building a MedTech startup focused on continuously monitoring a patient's pupils during surgery."

## Design
- Palette sampled from the live logo PNG (navy #10375D, sky blue #4FAEDB), set on a deep navy ground.
- Hero: a canvas-drawn human iris in macro (fibres, crypts, collarette, catchlight). The logo's two converging light beams rise to the pupil; every 9s they brighten and the pupil constricts and re-dilates (the light reflex). No pointer response.
- Story: an engraved eye cross-section behind the wearable. Light enters, then a signal returns and a continuous trace runs out along the optic nerve, then the trace serves the four applications.
- Fonts: Newsreader / Figtree / IBM Plex Mono.
