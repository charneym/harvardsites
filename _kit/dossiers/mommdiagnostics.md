## The company
Swiss startup (founded 2018, Basel) from a partnership between Avails Medical, Inc. and Empa. Developing préXclude, a rapid
sFlt-1/PlGF preeclampsia test, on its patented eFlow platform (single-use cartridge + compact low-cost handheld reader).
Sources: mommdiagnostics.com (home, /our-test, /about-momm, /partnerships, /investors, /contact, /impressum, /site-preeclampsia).

## Facts used
- >95% accuracy of sFlt-1/PlGF in centralized lab assays, refs 1–4 (our-test); 5–8% of pregnancies (site-preeclampsia).
- Electrochemical enzyme-linked lateral flow immunoassay, single-use cartridge, handheld reader (Gates grant text).
- Plasma separation device and cartridge system (Celine Gutmann bio). In development, not commercially available (our-test).
- Team, board and advisors from /about-momm. Partners: Empa, Avails, FHNW, CSEM, Swiss TPH (about); funders on /investors
  (Innosuisse, Swiss Nanoscience Institute, Venturelab, 5-HT) plus Gates Foundation.
