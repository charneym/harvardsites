# Japan Private Equity Co., Ltd. (日本プライベートエクイティ株式会社, JPE): dossier (home-only build, batch 2026-11e)

- **Site:** https://www.private-equity.co.jp/ (Japanese only). The concept site is built in Japanese (SPEC §2b); this report is in English.
- **HQ:** 〒101-0061 東京都千代田区神田三崎町2-15-6 K-STAGE 5F (Kanda-Misakicho, Chiyoda-ku, Tokyo). TEL 03-3238-1726, FAX 03-3238-1639. 四国オフィス (Shikoku office): 香川県高松市浜ノ町8-33.
- **Founded:** 24 October 2000. Capital ¥60M. Shareholders: J-Capital 36.1%, Development Bank of Japan (DBJ) 35.7%, others 28.2% (会社概要).
- **What it does:** manages buyout funds for small and mid-sized Japanese companies facing business succession (事業承継) or carve-outs (事業再編・MBO). It describes itself as a "succession fund", not a "buyout fund".
- **Size / scope check:** targets annual sales of ¥1–10bn and an enterprise value of ¥1–5bn (about US$7–35M). It invests ¥300M–2bn per company for a stake of one third to a majority, with board seats, and exits in 3–5 years (投資ターゲット / 投資対象 pages). Cumulative: 11 funds and ¥28.6bn (fund page). As of the 25th-anniversary message (Oct 2025): 37 companies backed, 29 exits. The portfolio page currently shows 9 current companies and 30 exits. This is lower-middle market and invests in operating companies. PASS.
