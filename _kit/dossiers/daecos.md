## Activity
- 2026-08-06: NIH NIDDK SBIR Phase I 1R43DK146600-01A1, $314,364, "Personalized Assessment and Care of Kidney Support in Critically Ill Patients (PACKS-ICU)". Contact PI Ravindra L. Mehta. https://reporter.nih.gov/project-details/11409014
  Abstract: AI-driven CDSS integrated with EHRs; validated on >100,000 ICU patients across five international centers; Phase I with UC San Diego Center for Health Innovation (Aim 1 refine on retrospective data from 3 UCSD hospitals; Aim 2 deploy in UCSD Epic via AWS + FHIR APIs; Aim 3 silent test mode across teaching/non-teaching settings); foundation for SBIR Phase II clinical trial. AKI-D: in-hospital mortality >50%, >$10B/yr, $1.7B avoidable.

## Science sources
- R. L.
