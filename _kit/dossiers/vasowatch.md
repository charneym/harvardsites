## Mission and science
- Tagline: "Saving mothers' lives through predictive technology" (home).
- Postpartum hemorrhage biomarker: the VIBRANT algorithm reads maternal heart rate during active labor for myometrial
  fatigue (Myometrial Fatigue Index), the leading cause of hemorrhage (home "Our Features"; /solution).
- 510(k)-cleared wearable sensor (ECG or PPG) streams heart rate; risk status updated continuously; alerts ahead of delivery;
  dashboards at bedside and charge nurse's station (home features).
- Cloud-based SaMD integrating with hospital hardware; works with Joint Commission-mandated risk assessment (AWHONN, ACOG,
  CMQCC); "Identifies 80% of life-threatening uterine atony missed by the current standard of care" (/solution).
- Paper: Trout, Modri, Watson, Lee, Sehdev, Weimer. "VIBRANT: Early Prediction of Life-Threatening Uterine Atony using
  Maternal Heart Rate". IEEE/ACM CHASE 2025 — https://jamesweimer.net/pdf/2025-chase.pdf (linked from /solution).
- Problem stats (vasowatch.com/about-2 "Problem"): hemorrhages account for 11% of US maternal deaths and occur in 5% of all
  births; 80% of hemorrhage incidents occur in "low risk" mothers; "Low Risk ≠ No Risk".

## Path to market (pipeline)
- Tested on observational study data from Pennsylvania Hospital; next a pivotal trial with a larger patient group expected to

  life-threatening bleeding; seeking FDA authorization for the algorithm, targeting mid-2028; system patented; works with
  heart-rate devices already in use (cost, rural hospitals) — Technical.ly, 2026-09-30:
