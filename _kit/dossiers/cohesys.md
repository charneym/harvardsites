## Facts used
- cohesys.ca: founded 2018, Toronto; developing a new solution for fracture repair under investigational testing; aims to disrupt a surgical-repair industry dependent on highly invasive plates and screws; BoneTape entirely resorbable materials; single piece unlike plates and screws in many shapes and sizes; designed to be cut to shape; address.
- Business Wire 2025-08-18: first participants treated; non-load-bearing craniofacial fractures; drill- and screw-free; handheld applicator affixes the resorbable device to bone surfaces; quotes Floros (data inform regulatory strategy and commercialization pathway), PI Dr. Jeffrey A. Fialkov (Head, Plastic and Reconstructive Surgery, Sunnybrook; Assoc. Prof., U of T), Dr. Janaina Bortolatto (VP Clinical Operations); risks monitored (infection, non-union, re-operation); Health Canada ITA; investigational, not licensed for sale; trademark line.
- ClinicalTrials.gov NCT06600854: official title; zygomaticomaxillary (midface) fractures often from car accidents or falls; feasibility / early efficacy / early safety outcomes; enrolment and dates.
- UHN Research News 2019-05-01: Santerre lab; translucent so surgeons can see alignment; bioresorbable, dissolves over many months, no follow-up removal surgery; mussel-inspired adhesive; metal plates must be bent and need tiny screws.
- Trent University News 2019-01-31: launched July 2018; concept conceived by surgeons, developed through a U of T and Sunnybrook Research Institute collaboration; adhesive inspired by marine adhesive proteins, works wet and dry without complex steps and drying of dental adhesives; Tessier COO; both co-founders did postgraduate materials science at Trent.
- Innovation Factory profile: leadership (Floros CEO and Founder; Tessier COO and Co-Founder; Bortolatto VP Clinical Operations and Co-Founder); CAMEDA programme.
