## Facts used (site copy)
- Tagline "life in nano ice". C80EZ = "Easy for −80 Celsius": biocompatible polymer combinations promote nanoscale cubic ice, minimise hexagonal ice and prevent recrystallization (international patent filing); anti-apoptosis agents and antioxidants; long-term storage in regular −70 to −80 °C freezers, no LN2; shipping in dry ice boxes. Variants: Standard, 2X, Tissue, Neuron, Insect, Competent. On the market since 2017.
- IcyEye: disposable ice-filtering device with nanostructured membrane + C80EZ-CORNEA; >95% post-thaw endothelial viability; −80 °C storage; compatible with Optisol / Life4°C; seeking FDA clearance.
- InstaVitria: >10^6 K/min on thin tissue surface, two orders of magnitude above plunging in LN2; LN2 and cold N2 jet arrays; vitrification without cell-permeating cryoprotectant; under R&D.
- OdinSol: "Hexagonal-ice Ablation on Nano-scale (HAN)"; skin banking (NIAID), CAR-T and NK cells without DMSO, higher post-thaw cytotoxicity (NCI).
- Dr. Han: founded 2012; Top Reviewer for Cryobiology; NIH SBIR reviewer; PhD mechanical engineering and cryobiology; postdoc in cryopreservation for comparative medicine; adjunct, School of Medicine, University of Missouri; Adjunct Member, Vision Research Center, UMKC; awards from NIH, USDA, Coulter.
- Publications page: C80EZ tested at Univ. of Missouri (Dalton Cardiovascular Research Center, School of Medicine, Vet Med), USDA ARS Columbia, Wayne State, Danforth Plant Science Center.
