## Activity (pre-screen: OK)
NIH NIGMS STTR, Org SYRACUSE BIOLABS, INC., Camillus, NY (NIH RePORTER):
- 5R42GM140530-03 — "Commercialization of a phylogenetic cell separation method for downstream purification of nucleic acids".
  Award notice 2026-06-30, budget start 2026-07-01, $534,278. Project 2021-09-15 to 2027-06-30. Contact PI Mark Patrick McPike;
  PIs Michael Marciano, Mark Patrick McPike. https://reporter.nih.gov/project-details/11369597
- 2R42GM140530-02 — same title (Phase II). Award notice 2025-07-09, $510,862. https://reporter.nih.gov/project-details/11065351
- 1R41GM140530-01A1 — "A novel method for improved separation of metagenomic samples for preservation and identification." (Phase I)
  Award notice 2021-09-13, project 2021-09-15 to 2023-03-14, $218,846, PI Michael Marciano. https://reporter.nih.gov/project-details/10324639
- Total $1,263,986 (Phase II to date $1,045,140).
Abstract (Phase II) used for copy: microbiome studies depend on effective purification; separating phylogenetic components, e.g.
removing human cells from the microbial fraction while preventing loss or degradation; patent-pending simple and inexpensive
approach separating and preserving host and microbial DNA; all organismal DNA preserved (not "sacrificed" or lost) during initial
separation, unlike protocols of existing kit manufacturers; higher sequencing coverage and sensitivity, higher confidence in
organismal characterization; SBL teamed with scientists in Syracuse University's Forensic and National Security Sciences Institute
(FNSSI); Phase I demonstrated superiority of the "Syracuse Method" to currently available kits (two named third-party kit brands,
not named on the site); Phase II has five aims toward fieldable cell/DNA purification modules; applications: human microbiome
research, forensic identification and geolocation, DNA-based ecological research, plant and microbial geosourcing, defense/homeland
security (environmental samples); narrative: kits for healthcare, microbial research and forensics; downstream qPCR or NGS.
Scope note: the science is host/microbe sample prep for sequencing (microbiome), not pathogen, toxin or biodefence work; the
abstract lists defense/homeland security among several application markets. That application is left off the site.

## Patent
US 12,344,834 B2 "Phylogenetic-based differential cell separation and DNA extraction". Assignee Syracuse University. Inventors
Michael Marciano, Molly Dunegan. Priority 2019-11-01 (62/929,216), filed 2020-11-02, granted 2025-07-01.
https://patents.google.com/patent/US12344834B2/en — 5 um wetted filters before any lysis (human epithelial cells retained; E. coli
pass), differential lysis buffers (lysozyme for bacteria, SDS for animal cells; claims: 2% SDS), third 5 um filtration; claims three
cell types (bacterial, plant, animal) in separate fractions.
