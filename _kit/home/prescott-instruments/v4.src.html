About us

A family business that builds machines to last

Prescott Instruments was founded in 1994 by Managing Director Philip Prescott and remains a privately owned family business in Gloucestershire. It began with instrument service and calibration, then developed its own electronics and Labline software. Today we make laboratory solutions for the rubber, elastomer and polymer industries, for tyre and automotive, nuclear, health and consumer goods customers.

Products

Rheometers, viscometers and more

The core range is centred on advanced rheometers, viscometers, tensile testers and dynamic mechanical analysers, with accessories and software to match. Every machine is manufactured and calibrated in the UK in line with international accreditations.

  • Moving Die Rheometers

    Rheoline MDR · MDR Smart

    Cure testing for rubber compounds, alongside our Oscillating Disc Rheometer.

  • Advanced Rheometry

    Rheoline Multi-Function · MF Lite

    Our move into advanced rheometry, launched in the 2000s.

  • Mooney Viscometers

    Mooneyline MV · MV Variable Speed · MV Smart

    Mooney viscosity testing, in production since the 1990s.

  • Dynamic Testing

    Dynamic Mechanical Analyser

    Linear motor technology: up to 1.75 kN static and 2.5 kN dynamic force at up to 100 Hz.

  • Tensile Testing

    Tensile testers

    Tensile testers for the rubber, elastomer and polymer laboratory.

  • Sample Preparation

    Laboratory Bale Cutter · Rheoline VSC · Mooneyline VSC

    Bale cutting and volumetric sample cutters for repeatable test pieces.

Also available: the Plastimeter, pre-used instruments, a 27" touchscreen for every instrument, Rheoline Auto-Load and Auto-Test automation, and parts and spares.

Software

Labline

Our flagship laboratory suite for rubber testing instruments. It synchronises test management, data acquisition and data analysis, and scales to operations of any size.

FlexiReports

Personalised, professional reports in a few easy steps, with full control of layout, content and design.

Universal Instruments

Merges data from Labline and non-Labline instruments into a single management console, for an unlimited number of instruments.
