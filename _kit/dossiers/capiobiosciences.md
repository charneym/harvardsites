## Facts used
- About: founded 2015 by Seungpyo Hong PhD (UW Madison) and Andrew Wang MD; CapioCyte captures CTCs from whole blood by biomimetic cell rolling and dendrimer-mediated multivalent cell capture; enriched CTCs for FISH, RNA-Seq, NGS. Vision: improve cancer patient outcomes by developing advanced oncology diagnostics.
- Home: Clinical Need text; 10–100× greater sensitivity than previously published data using other platforms; "currently under development and not for use in diagnostic procedures".
- Technology: proprietary chips in a specially designed flow chamber; controlled flow rate; rolling mimics CTC/vessel-wall interaction, slows and enriches; dendrimer-mediated immunoaffinity capture; DAPI+/CK+/CD45− identification; recovery for RNA-Seq or automated staining; flexible capture antibodies.
- Publications (site Featured Publications): JCO Precision Oncology 2023 (43 patients, CTC >15 OR 3.31, 26% preceded progression; EpCAM/HER2/EGFR antibodies); Cancer Letters 2023 (13 of 14 HPV+ HNSCC elevated, median 17 CTC/mL; cetuximab declines); Biosensors and Bioelectronics 2023 (GI cancer, median 50 CTC/mL, 3-month post-treatment counts predictive); Advanced Science 2025 (G7 dendrimer-peptide, ~2.48× avidity).
- Team page: founders, CTO Chang-Woo Lee, Michael Poellmann, Ju-Heon Lim; board David Chen, Li Mao (Betta Pharmaceuticals); SAB Langer, Jänne, George, Bailey.
- News: NIH SBIR grant effective 2018-08-01; Wisconsin State Journal 2021.
