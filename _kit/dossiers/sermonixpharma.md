## The company
Privately held Columbus, Ohio biopharmaceutical company focused on female-specific oncology, founded late 2014 by David Portman, MD.
Lead: oral lasofoxifene, an investigational nonsteroidal SERM for ESR1-mutated ER+/HER2- metastatic breast cancer (ELAINE studies).
Address: 250 East Broad Street, Suite 250, Columbus, Ohio 43215. 614-864-4919. info@sermonixpharma.com.

## Facts used on the page
- ESR1 activity discovered in the lab of Donald McDonnell, MD, Duke; Sermonix holds exclusive rights (/about). University of Chicago crystallography on the MOA (/lasofoxifene).
- Tissue selectivity, beneficial effects on vagina and bone in earlier studies (/about). 70% of breast cancers ER+; ER mutations a significant cause of resistance (/breast-cancer).
- ELAINE-1 (NCT03781063): 52 vs 51 randomised; median PFS 5.6 vs 3.7 mo; ORR 13.2% vs 2.9%; improved vaginal/vulvar symptoms (release 2025-01-29).
- ELAINE-2: 13 months PFS in heavily pretreated 2L/3L ESR1-mutated mBC (David Portman quote, release 2025-12-18).
- ELAINE-3 (NCT05696626): lasofoxifene + abemaciclib vs fulvestrant + abemaciclib; >50% enrolled, data mid-2027 (release 2025-12-18; /the-elaine-3-study).
- I-SPY 2 arm with Quantum Leap: 20 patients, well tolerated, promising Ki67 suppression (release 2024-11-02).
- Athira Pharma (now LeonaBio, Nasdaq LONA) exclusive global licence excl. Asia and certain Middle East countries; pre-funded warrant ~5.5M shares; up to $100M milestones + royalties; $90M placement + up to $146M warrants (release 2025-12-18; renamed 2026-01-09).
- Regor Therapeutics rCARD collaboration (release 2025-03-24).
- US patent, July 2024 (non-ESR1 aromatase-resistant ER+ BC). Registry also cites US 12,433,866 (2025-10-07); not used on the page.
- Team and chairman from the "About Sermonix" paragraph of 2025 releases.
