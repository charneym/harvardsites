## Facts used
- Home: founded to address arthritis, diabetic neuropathy, sports injuries and other ailments requiring long-term pain treatment; pipeline of proprietary analgesics; opioid-free; Capsadyn first drug, OTC topical cream. Arthritis #1 cause of disability in America, 54M+ afflicted; corticosteroids, NSAIDs, acetaminophen not considered safe for long-term use; HHS opioid emergency.
- Trial release (2024-03-15): double-blind randomized placebo-controlled, single site, Dermatology Department, Carilion Clinic; 44 healthy volunteers, all completed; VAS 0–10 every 10 min for one hour plus next-day interview; median peak burn 0 Capsadyn and placebo (p = 0.32), 5 for Zostrix 0.1% (p < 0.001) — shown on the site as "a 0.1% capsaicin comparator cream"; next day no burning with Capsadyn/placebo, 48.3% (14/29) with comparator; 0.25% is the FDA maximum for OTC topical analgesics; capsaicin is the molecule that makes chili peppers burn; safe to use with oral medications such as NSAIDs.
- Capsadyn.com: patented non-burning formula; 2.5x typical OTC creams; no NSAIDs, steroids, anesthetics or opioids; fast-absorbing, non-greasy, odour-free; gradually desensitizes overactive pain receptors in nerve endings; relief builds with continued use; fingernail-sized dab. News 2026-02-10: ~50 applications per jar; manufactured in the US per FDA regulations for external analgesics.
- Team (About Us): Iannello, Carliss, G. Robert Vaughan, Aileen Helsel; bios condensed from the page. RAMP regional accelerator graduate (2021-05-04 release).
