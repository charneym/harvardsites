## Facts used
- Vision (company page, verbatim gist); founders Ronald Crystal MD and Todd Rosengart MD, collaboration since the 1990s at Weill Cornell; exclusive license from Weill Cornell / Cornell.
- XC001 MOA (xc001-moa): altered adenovirus vector with the human VEGF gene; cannot reproduce; gene to nucleus; cell makes VEGF and ships it out; helps new blood vessels form; more blood and oxygen.
- EXACT-2 (exact-2-trial page): Phase 2b, multicenter, randomized, double-blind, sham-controlled; 106 subjects; ages 18–85; May 2025–June 2027; US, UK and EU; catheter delivery in the cath lab (Extroducer, SmartCella license 2024-07-10); CCS class II–IV, unsuitable for revascularization, symptomatic despite maximized medical therapy.
- EXACT-1 results (July 2025 release): 93% entered with chest pain markedly limiting daily activities; 43% no chest pain with ordinary activities at six months; no serious adverse events related to study drug; published Circulation: Cardiovascular Interventions (2024).
- Pipeline (pipeline page): XC001 refractory angina; XC001 adjunct to CABG (500,000 CABG/yr US, ~1/3 at risk of incomplete revascularization; double-blind Phase 2 per July 2025 release); XC002 discovery, cardiac regeneration. FDA Fast Track (news).
- Team and board: company page (names and titles as published). Investors: Jeito (Series B lead), Sofinnova and LSP (2018 Series A release).
