Approach

Industrialists, not bankers.

We are supportive shareholders to each company's managers, helping them drive organic growth, and we bring decades of running businesses ourselves.

We buy companies

Buying from founders and families.

We buy companies from entrepreneurs, founders and family owners: UK businesses that show real promise, with clear differentiation and significant room to grow.

We buy into people

Backing an ambitious management team.

Above all, we look for a talented, ambitious team who share our values of integrity, respect, hard work and openness, and we stand behind them as shareholders.

Grow more

Doubling revenue in three to five years.

Typically, we aim to double a company's revenues within the first three to five years. At SB Components, sales grew 70%, from £15m to £26m, backed by a best-in-class fleet centre.

Build more

Bolt-ons in fragmented markets.

We favour fragmented markets with room for bolt-on acquisitions. TVS Interfleet brought together SB Components, Priden and McPhee Mixers, then added Wilcox and Purpose Bodies.

  • Simple

    Quick, clear decisions

    We have the confidence to keep things simple. We trust our instincts, about business and about people, and we can make decisions quickly.

  • Experienced

    A bank of know-how

    Our experience of making companies successful lets us draw on a vast bank of knowledge, industry contacts and a deep international network.

  • Responsible

    Fair to everyone

    As owners, we make sure people who deal with our businesses are treated fairly: uncompromising on health and safety, and mindful of human rights and supply-chain labour.

Investment criteria

Real promise, ready to grow.

We buy UK businesses whose growth could be unlocked by a supportive owner with operational skills and a deep international network.

  • LeadershipA talented and ambitious management team
  • UniquenessClear differentiation in its market
  • PotentialSignificant growth that a supportive, operational owner can unlock
  • MarketsFragmented, with the opportunity to make bolt-on acquisitions
