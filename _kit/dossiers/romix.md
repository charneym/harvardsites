## Science (from the RePORTER abstracts)
- Kawasaki disease (KD): systemic vasculitis of unknown etiology, leading cause of acquired heart disease in children. Timely IVIG reduces risk of heart disease from 25% to 3%. No diagnostic test exists; clinical signs overlap with other febrile illnesses; IVIG is an expensive blood product.
- Plan: develop the first KD diagnostic using plasma cfRNA signatures, translated to a PCR platform (Thermo Fisher TaqMan Array Card). Steps: automate sample processing; large-scale biomarker discovery on pre-treatment plasma from KD patients and febrile controls; translate to qPCR; validate on prospectively collected samples from across the US. Outputs: diagnostic PCR panel, clinical data to engage IVD platform developers to license, automated workflow ready for regulatory review as an LDT.
- Collaborations named: UCSD/Rady Children's Hospital, Children's Healthcare of Atlanta, Children's National Hospital, Boston Children's Hospital, Children's Hospital Colorado.
- NHGRI project: unified library preparation capturing both short and long cfRNA (bridging exRNA and cfRNA research), helicase-active enzymes; targets 10x RNA yield, 1.5x biotype complexity, Pearson >0.98 reproducibility vs commercial kits; extend to urine. Applications: immunotherapy monitoring, infectious disease, inflammatory disease.
