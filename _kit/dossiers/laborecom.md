## About (site)
"Laborecom Therapeutics is a Chicago-based biotech developing LBRC-101, a first-in-class peptide therapeutic to prevent
reperfusion injury following heart attack by blocking the process that immune cells use to enter muscle and cause damage
following reperfusion with procedures such as percutaneous coronary intervention (PCI)."
"LBRC-101 has received NIH SBIR Phase I funding, validating the underlying biology of the LBRC pathway."

## Funding
- NIH NHLBI SBIR Phase I 1R43HL182534-01, "A cell-permeable peptide therapeutic to reduce myocardial reperfusion injury",
  15 Sep 2025 – 31 Aug 2026, $457,232. https://reporter.nih.gov/project-details/11254406
  Abstract: STEMI ~750,000 people/yr in US; PCI saves lives but as much as half of long-term damage is due to reperfusion
  injury; no approved therapeutic shown to significantly reduce infarct size; larger infarcts associated with reinfarction,
  heart-failure hospitalisation, all-cause mortality. Approach targets a motor protein uniquely expressed in endothelial cells
  to prevent transendothelial migration (vs. direct immune suppression or mitochondrial stress). Aim 1 optimise peptide for
  stability, permeability, plasma protein binding (in vitro TEM assay). Aim 2 head-to-head in mouse MI model (echo, infarct size
  histopathology). Next: Phase II incl. IND-enabling studies and swine MI model.
