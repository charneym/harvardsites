## The company
Clinical-stage Basel biotech tackling non-oncogene addiction in cancer: blocking transcriptional escape pathways that run parallel
to the primary oncogene axis. Phenotypic screening found CBP/p300 as an epigenetic master regulator of transcriptional resistance.
Lead: TT125-802, oral small-molecule inhibitor of the CBP/p300 bromodomain. Address: Hochbergerstrasse 60C, CH-4057 Basel. info@tolremo.com.
Sources: tolremo.com /, /ourapproach, /about-us, /news; press releases (PDFs under tolremo.com/s/).

## Facts used on the page
- Final Phase I (NCT06403436), WCLC 2026, release 2026-09-15: 45 patients, 24 NSCLC; 10/24 (42%) tumour shrinkage; 3 confirmed PRs (primary osimertinib resistance, acquired KRAS-G12C-inhibitor resistance, SOX2-amplified squamous); 60 mg QD RP2D, no food restriction; 98% TRAEs Grade 1–2; no thrombocytopenia. Alessandra Cesano quoted as CMO.
- Two FDA Fast Track designations, 2025-08-28 (EGFR ex19del/L858R NSCLC after EGFR inhibitor; KRAS-G12C NSCLC after G12C inhibitor).
- Series A USD 39M (CHF 34.1M), 2023-09-20, led by BioMedPartners, with Pierre Fabre Invest and existing investors.
- Innosuisse CHF 1.5M grant, Dec 2023 (news page). Co-founder Prof. Wilhelm Krek, ETH Zurich (in memoriam, about page).
- Preclinical: TT125-802 + KRAS/EGFR/AR inhibitors → deeper, more durable responses in NSCLC, CRC, prostate models (/ourapproach).
- Team, board and SAB from /about-us (summaries condensed). News items link to tolremo.com releases.
