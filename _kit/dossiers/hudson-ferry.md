# Hudson Ferry Capital (concept site: /hudson-ferry/, one-page format)

Facts only, each with its source. Built 2026-11-18, batch 2026-11c.

## Firm
- Lower middle market private equity firm founded in 2006, based in Stamford, Connecticut; control-oriented buyouts of established lower middle-market U.S. companies with enterprise values typically $10–50M. Source: https://www.hudsonferry.com/about/
- Partners collectively completed over 100 lower middle market investments before Hudson Ferry; "adding value beyond simply providing capital" is the guiding principle; draws on senior executives the partners have worked with. Source: https://www.hudsonferry.com/about/
- Hudson Ferry Capital III licensed as an SBIC (just over $200M of capital), September 3, 2025. Source: https://www.hudsonferry.com/news/hudson-ferry-capital-iii-licensed-as-a-200-million-sbic-fund/
- Office: 263 Tresser Boulevard, Suite 1601, Stamford, CT 06901; 212-308-3079; info@hudsonferry.com; opportunities to Finn Boed, fboed@hudsonferry.com. Source: https://www.hudsonferry.com/contact/

## Criteria (https://www.hudsonferry.com/investment-criteria/)
EV $10–50M; revenue $15–75M; EBITDA $3–7M; strong and growing free cash flow. Business model: specialty manufacturing, business services, outsourcing. Value creation: organic growth, new markets, broader offerings, manufacturing efficiencies, acquisitions. Market position and management attributes as listed on the page. Company-centric rather than industry-focused selection.
