## Facts used (all from luxmedsystems.com unless noted)
- About: first and only platform integrating real-time optical diagnostics and therapeutic ablation to detect and eliminate gaps; PFA fast and safe but no real-time insight into lesion durability; optical tissue interrogation diagnoses viability and gaps even in electrically stunned tissue; closed-loop instead of open-loop.
- Technology: green band = active viable muscle, yellow band = fibrotic/scar; proprietary optical sensing developed at The George Washington University; over $6 million in NIH SBIR/STTR funding; diagnostic catheter that fits existing mapping/ablation workflows; RF diagnostic catheters first, then PFA. Capabilities: contact stability, gap detection, lesion progression, catheter-tissue contact, catheter stability/movement, tissue composition.
- Progress: 11 patients with cardiac arrhythmias including AF ablated with the optically enabled RF catheter; preclinical PFA data in animal models; 61 issued patents, 20+ pending. Published data: HRS 2015, 2016, 2019 posters.
