01 · Approach

Built in partnership.

Partnerships with management teams create many paths to value. Hamilton Robinson values people and builds strong relationships, as engaged owners rather than day-to-day operators.

01 · Partnership

Engaged owners, not operators.

Hamilton Robinson brings strategic resources and guidance and builds a culture of collaboration with each management team. Warp and weft: the company's people and the firm's capital, worked together from the first day.

02 · Shared ownership

Ownership pushed deep into the company.

Skin-in-the-game investment and equity incentives reach well past the top team, so managers and key personnel think like owners. Over the years the firm has counted more than 500 management shareholders.

03 · Operational excellence

Take out the waste, not the cost.

LEAN is the continuous improvement and relentless elimination of waste across every part of an organisation. It isn't about cutting costs: it improves internal processes to create better outcomes for customers, on the shop floor and beyond it.

04 · Add-on acquisitions

One plus one equals three or four.

Add-ons bring new technologies, products, geographies or management depth. The firm doesn't pursue one unless it adds a strategic capability. Its latest: Sani-Matic's acquisition of Douglas Machines in 2025.

02 · Investment criteria

The lower middle market.
