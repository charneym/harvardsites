## Facts used (aquiluspharma.com unless noted)
- Mission: first-in-class medication for neurodegenerative diseases, primary focus ALS. Pre-Series A, funded via ALS Association, NIH and DoD grants.
- ALS: speak, eat, move, eventually breathe; life expectancy 2–5 years after diagnosis; loss of upper and lower motor neurons causing paralysis and muscular atrophy.
- MMPs: ~24 zinc-dependent endopeptidases; cleave virtually any ECM component; cell migration, signalling for proliferation, differentiation, death; process growth factors, cytokines, receptors etc.; MMP-9 and MMP-2 linked with severity and rate of progression of ALS; no FDA-approved MMP inhibitors.
- AQU-118: safe, orally bioavailable, semi-selective MMP-9/MMP-2 inhibitor; reduced nerve damage in rodent models of neuropathic pain and nerve injury; mitigated weight loss and reduced grip strength in the TDP-43 over-expressed mouse model. Henry 2015 (J Oral Facial Pain Headache 29:286–296); Kwan 2019 (IJMS 20:811).
- Clinical: IND-enabling complete; HREC approval for Phase 1 SAD/MAD (2024); IND filed 2024, cleared 2025; Phase 2 recruitment planned Q4 2026.
- Grants: Hoffman ALS Clinical Trial $1,000,000 (2023); DoD ALSRP $1,000,000 (2021); ALS Association Barnett $500,000 (2021); NIH SBIR $457,639 (2019). Sum $2,957,639 → "$2.9M".
- Team: Irving Sucholeiki PhD (President & CEO), Darrell J. Nix PhD (VP R&D), Roy Sucholeiki MD (CMO & VP Clinical Development); collaborators/advisors Bowser, Cudkowicz, Paganoni, Zarnescu, Sattler.
