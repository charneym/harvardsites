01Approach

Making strong businesses stronger.

CenterOak helps market leaders grow even stronger, with a value creation plan designed with management for each company and carried through every stage of the investment.

01 / 05 · Partnership

It starts with what is already strong.

CenterOak backs leading businesses with a niche market position: closely held and family-owned companies, founder-led and private equity-backed businesses, through buyouts, recapitalizations and carve-outs, with respect for each company’s legacy.

02 / 05 · The plan

A plan built with management.

Together with the management team, CenterOak designs a value creation plan for each company: operational efficiencies, logistics and supply chain optimization, and the systems that carry growth.

03 / 05 · Organic growth

New lines, new locations.

Product line and service extensions and facility expansions grow the core business from within.

04 / 05 · Buy and build

Tuck-in acquisitions.

Like-minded companies join a strong platform. Through CenterOak and across their careers, the team has completed more than 180 acquisitions.

05 / 05 · Reach

Into new regions.

Geographic expansions and, where they make sense, transformational mergers or combinations. Entomo Brands, for one, now runs 23 branches across seven states.
