## Science (abstracts + https://www.vasoptic.com/technology)
- LSCI: coherent light on tissue forms speckle; motion (pulsatile blood flow) blurs speckle over the camera exposure; contrast vs neighbourhood → blood flow velocity index (BFVi), pseudo-coloured, high spatial & temporal resolution, no contrast agents.
- Pulsatile metrics: dip and peak BFVi, fractional time-to-rise, volumetric skew, pulsatile index.
- Complementary to fundus/OCT/SLO (structure), FA/ICG (perfusion with dye), OCT-A (perfused vessel density).
- MIGS award: Phase I built VasoVUE prototype doing LSCI + high contrast reflectance imaging (HCRI) + fluorescence imaging (FLI); animal models showed IOP changes alter episcleral venous blood flow (EVBF) and EVBF differed after goniotomy. Phase II: clinical study of EVBF vs glaucoma status & MIGS (Aim 1); intraoperative EVBF feasibility and prediction of long-term IOP reduction (Aim 2); toward regulatory clearance.
- NIA 2026: XyCAM NV measures retinal blood flow response to flicker (retinovascular reactivity, RVR) as proxy for cerebrovascular reactivity (CVR); Aims: build clinical-grade XyCAM NV; validate vs MRI BOLD CVR; show RVR impaired in MCI.

## Products
- XyCAM RI — clinical retinal blood flow imager; FDA 510(k), Class II; non-mydriatic; soft IR illumination; workflow similar to fundus photography. https://www.vasoptic.com/clinical-use-ophthalmology-optometry ; clearance June 2020 (MPO).
- XyCAM CRE — custom research version, research use only. https://www.vasoptic.com/investigational-use-ocular-research
- VasoVUE G1 — lab multimodal speckle imager: high frame-rate LSCI, fluorescence (ICG) angiography, HCRI live view, synchronized/registered acquisition, long working distance; investigational, animal use only. https://www.vasoptic.com/laboratory-use-tissue-blood-flow
