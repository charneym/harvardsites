## Science (NIH abstracts)
- Coded ptychography microscopy (CPM) produces super-resolution quantitative phase images (QPIs) with well-visualized cellularity on unstained slides; multiwavelength compact prototype; AI object detection on unstained QPIs directs pathologists to areas of interest.
- ROSE performed in <10% of lung biopsies (2022 abstract) and <25% of thyroid biopsy procedures (2023). Lung FNA failure 20–40%; repeat biopsy delays up to 90 days. Survival 63.7% localized vs 8.9% distant.
- 2026 aims: next-generation Bioptic for clinical adoption; multi-center data on fresh unstained lung samples; refine autonomous adequacy algorithms → FDA submission.
