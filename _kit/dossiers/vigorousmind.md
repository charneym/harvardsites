## The company
Vigorous Mind, Inc. builds "Autonomous Robots and AI Transforming Health and Senior Care" (vigorousmind.com home).
Products: the Vigorous Mind Robot ("VMR"), its software on the temi autonomous mobile robot, for nursing homes and
senior living (scheduled virtual family visits via a website + Zoom, automatic dispatch to the resident's room,
telehealth, group activities for bed-ridden residents, deliveries up to 6 lbs, robot disinfection cabinet with a
6-minute UV cycle and no staff involvement); and the Vigorous Mind activities platform (group activities for senior
living / senior centres; brain exercises at home; community book, calendars/digital signage, attendance and reporting).
Sources: vigorousmind.com /, /copy-of-senior-mind-platform, /about-4, /senior-living-communities,
/copy-of-vigorous-mind-platform, /who-benefits-and-how, /about, /contact.

HQ (site, /contact): Vigorous Mind, Inc., 209 West Central Street, Suite #104, Natick, MA 01760; info@vigorousmind.com;
toll free (888) 769-6463. NIH RePORTER lists the organisation in Newton, MA (UEI DRN6GPSKR2J4). The build uses Natick.
