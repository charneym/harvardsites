## Facts used
- CORDIS, Nano4Rare (grant 101136772, EIC, Horizon Europe), https://cordis.europa.eu/project/id/101136772 : coordinator CSIC; EU contribution €2,498,563.75; 2023-11-01 to 2026-10-31. nanoGLA, patent-protected candidate for Fabry disease; EU orphan drug designation (EMA/EC) January 2021; Smart4Fabry (#720942) took it to an advanced preclinical stage incl. a first GLP toxicology study in rats; Phoenix (#953110) scale-up to GMP; Nano4Rare completes preclinical work for clinical entry and created the spin-off Delbios Pharmaceuticals SL to bring nanoGLA to the clinic and commercialise the nanoencapsulation platform; nanoGLA crosses the BBB and reaches the brain; relevance to other lysosomal storage disorders.
- NANBIOSIS (Jan 2025), https://www.nanbiosis.es/a-breakthrough-therapy-for-fabry-disease-nanogla-demonstrates-promising-results/ : peptide-guided nanoliposomes deliver GLA; greater efficacy than non-encapsulated enzyme in Fabry mice, reaching the brain; led by ICMAB-CSIC with CIBER-BBN; partners incl. VHIR, Nanomol Technologies, NANBIOSIS.
- Tomsen-Melero et al., Science Advances 10(50), 2024, DOI 10.1126/sciadv.adq4738: current ERT limited by poor biodistribution and inability to reach the brain; nanoGLA reduced Gb3 deposits more than non-nanoformulated GLA, including in the brain.
- Cabrera et al., Adv Healthc Mater 2016 (PubMed 26890358): GLA-loaded RGD-functionalised nanoliposomes prepared by DELOS-SUSP (depressurisation of a CO2-expanded liquid organic solution).
- Delbios tender notices (delbios.com): 2026-04-17 2-week DRF study in minipig (Nano4Rare WP1 T1.4); 2026-05-29 PK bridging study, liquid vs lyophilized nanoGLA, minipigs; 2026-06-05 IB, IMPD and Phase I synopsis for a future CTA (first-in-human); 2026-06-25 RFQ-FOR-002-2026 scale-up and production of a lyophilized nanoformulation.
