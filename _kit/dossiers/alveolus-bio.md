## Site copy (verbatim/paraphrase sources)
Home: "Pioneering biotherapeutic delivery for the prevention and treatment of chronic lung disease"; "First-in-Class
Inhaled Biotherapeutics"; "a team of scientists, clinicians, and engineers on a mission to disrupt the standard of care
for chronic and infectious lung disease. Our proprietary resMIT platform delivers safe, targeted bacteria directly to the
lungs to address fibroblast dysfunction in Idiopathic Pulmonary Fibrosis (IPF) and neutrophilic inflammation indications
(NCFBE, COPD)." Form interests: Investing / Partnering / Product Updates.
Science: "Changes in microbial signatures of the lungs and airways are associated with worsening chronic respiratory
disease and persistent inflammation." Focus on "harnessing the therapeutic power of commensal bacteria strains and their
byproducts for inhaled delivery straight to the target tissue: the lungs." Origin story: began in Dr. Vivek Lal's
Pulmonary Microbiome Lab and Dr. Amit Gaggar's Program in Lung Protease and Matrix Biology at UAB; published research on
the neonatal lung microbiome; "one of the first to conduct gain and loss of function studies on underlying mechanisms of
neutrophilic inflammation and the extracellular matrix of the lung." Infrastructure for preclinical drug development;
"a platform for respiratory drug development from discovery to clinical trials."
Publications listed: Neutrophilic inflammation during lung development disrupts elastin assembly and predisposes adult
mice to COPD (2021); Early airway microbial metagenomic and metabolomic signatures are associated with development of
severe bronchopulmonary dysplasia (2018); The Airway Microbiome at Birth (2016).
Pipeline: "Breakthrough Inhaled Live Biotherapeutic Products"; "over a decade of pulmonary microbiome research and
clinical data"; LBPs "that address fibroblast dysfunction and neutrophilic inflammation". Preclinical programs: IPF
(orphan disease); COPD (15M patients in the US); NCFBE (rare disease).
Press pipeline (Dec 2023 release): preclinical LBPs for COPD, BPD, pulmonary fibrosis, lung injury secondary to viral
infection, neutrophilic asthma; earlier-stage CF, PAH, ARDS. resMIT = "respiratory microbiota-based inhaled therapeutics".
Lead programme: COPD (all sources). Candidate name conflicts across aggregators (AB1000 / RB1000): NOT used.
