## The company
Female-founded medical device company in regenerative medicine. Designs biomimetic polymers that mimic the extracellular
matrix; tri-functional biomaterials (self-adhesive, anti-inflammatory, anti-fibrotic); adhesive, anti-inflammatory,
pro-regenerative wound fillers for complex wounds (ulcers, abscesses, fistulas). Lead/proof of concept MatriFlex, a
bioresorbable, liquid-to-solid skin substitute; second product MatriSoothe (Biotech Showcase 2024 post). B2B. Founded 2021
in Montreal by Dr. Fiona Simpson, Dr. Elle Edin and Benjamin Yam. Sources: biomimir.ca /home, /team, /contact, /news/*.
