## Science
- Target MCJ (Methylation-Controlled J protein, gene DNAJC15), endogenous negative regulator of the ETC at Complex I; upregulated in MASH livers. Removal is safe and raises respiration without more ROS (NIH abstract). Without MCJ, Complexes I/III/IV form a supercomplex (company technology page).
- Lead MITO-1041: proprietary siRNA vs human MCJ conjugated to proprietary GalNAc. In multiple mouse MASH models: steatosis/lipid −55–65%, fibrosis −75–85%. PK/PD and safety in non-GLP studies.
- NHP: ~70% MCJ suppression at four weeks with GalNAc-siRNA (PRWeb 2020-09-24).
- BIO 2023 (session 2420141): MT-101 expected once-quarterly subcutaneous injection; IND expected Q4 2024 (not met per 2026 award, which still funds IND submission); presenter Jack Talley, BS, CEO.
- Context (NIH abstract): ~5 million U.S. MASH diagnoses a year; resmetirom conditionally approved March 2024; five siRNA drugs FDA-approved.
- Pipeline (company site): MT-001 NASH; MT-002 APAP (siMCJ at 6 h and 24 h after APAP effective in mice, increased regeneration; NAC ineffective after 6 h); MT-003 kidney (UUO model in MCJ KO mice: less proteinuria and fibrosis; AKI after cardiovascular surgery; PKD); MT-004 immuno/cancer CD8 T cells (no text).
- Publications: Hatle 2013 Mol Cell Biol (PMID 23530063); Champagne 2016 Immunity (27234056); Barbier-Torres 2017 Nat Commun (29233977); Barbier-Torres 2020 Nat Commun (32620763). Rincón senior author on all; Martínez-Chantar co-senior on the Nat Commun papers.
